Open **Incident Reports** in the left menu.

## What Incident Reports does

Incident Reports gives your staff one place to write up injuries, illnesses, and behavioral incidents while the details are still fresh, and gives your health director one place to review them. Each report autosaves as a draft while it is written. Once it is filed, its wording is locked, and anything learned later is added as a dated note or follow up so the original account stays intact.

## Who can see it

**Incident Reports** appears in the left menu only when both of these are true:

- **Incident Reports Module** is turned on in **Admin → Site Settings → Modules** (under **Health and safety**).
- You are a camp administrator, or your role includes one of the two permissions in the **Incident Reports** category on the **Roles & Permissions** screen.

The two permissions are separate on purpose, because filing a report should not let someone read every report about every camper:

<ParamField body="File an incident report and edit your own drafts" param-type="permission" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Lets a staff member write and file reports. They see only the reports they filed themselves, in the list, in printed lists, and in PDFs. They can add follow ups to their own reports but cannot change a report's status, see **Trends**, or open **Settings**.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Read all incident reports, close them, and change settings" param-type="permission" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Everything above, plus every report your camp has filed, the status buttons on each report, **Trends**, and **Settings**.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Camp administrators always have full access to Incident Reports, whether or not their role includes either permission.
</Callout>

## The report list

The list shows the reports you can see, newest incident first.

- **Status buttons** across the top: **All**, **Drafts**, **Open**, **Under review**, and **Closed**, each with a count.
- **Filters** in the page header: a type filter (**All types**, **Injury or accident**, **Illness**, **Behavioral**, **Other**), a severity filter (**Any severity**, **Minor**, **Moderate**, **Serious**, **Critical**), a from date and a to date. Select **Filter** to apply them.
- **Columns**: the report number with a **Finish** button (drafts) or **View** button (filed reports), **Severity**, **Person** (linked to their profile when they were picked from your people), **What happened** (type, a short preview of the account, the place, and how many files are attached), **When**, and **Status**. A report with an open follow up shows its due date under the status.

Buttons at the top of the page:

- **New incident report** starts a new report.
- **Print** opens a PDF of the list as it is currently filtered.
- **Trends** and **Settings** appear only for people who can read all reports.

## File a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the report" icon="plus-circle" title-type="p">
    Select **New incident report**. The form saves as you type, and the save status shows at the bottom of the page. Nothing is filed until you choose to file it, so you can select **Leave for now** and come back later. Your draft shows in the list with a **Finish** button.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Say who it is about" icon="user" title-type="p">
    Under **Who is this about?**, choose **Camper**, **Staff**, or **Visitor**. For a camper or staff member, use **Search your people** and type at least two letters of their name. Picking someone fills in their name and age. Add **Phone** and **Parent or guardian** if you have them. For a visitor, type their name and address by hand.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Record what happened and when" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Choose the **Kind of incident** and **How serious was it?**, then the **Date** and **Time**. Fill in **What kind, more specifically**, **Where did it happen?** and **Exactly where**, whether they were in an activity and **Which activity**, and whether any equipment was involved.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tell the story" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    In **What happened**, write what you saw in order, in plain language. Use **Where people and objects were** for positions. Select **Attach a photo or document** to add photos or PDFs (up to 25MB each), and **Add a witness** for each person who saw it, with what they said.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Record care given" icon="heart" title-type="p">
    For **Injury or accident** and **Illness** reports only, a **Care given** section appears with **Part of the body**, what the person told you, what you observed, vitals (temperature, pulse, respirations, blood pressure, O2 saturation, and pain score), your assessment, what you did, what happens next, and **Where did they go afterward?**. Behavioral and other reports skip this section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Note the response" icon="shield" title-type="p">
    Under **What was done about it**, record any emergency steps and who carried them out, and what might keep this from happening again. Check **This needs a follow up** to set a **Follow up by** date and what needs to happen. Check **Let the family know when this is filed** if the family should be told.
  </Step>

  <Step title="File it" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Your name fills in as **Filed by**. Add **Your role** and **Your phone**, then select **File this report** and confirm. If anything required is missing, the form lists what to fix and highlights each field. Once filed, the report opens as a locked document with the status **Open**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A report needs these before it can be filed:

- The kind of incident and how serious it was.
- The date it happened.
- The name of the person it is about.
- A description in **What happened**.
- For an injury or illness, **Where did they go afterward?**
- A **Follow up by** date, if **This needs a follow up** is checked.
- An answer to any of your own questions marked as required for that kind of incident.

Each report gets its number when the draft starts, in the form `IR-` followed by the year the draft was started and a number.

## Work with a filed report

Select **View** on a filed report to open it. You see every section of the account, the attachments, who filed it, and whether the family was notified. A filed report cannot be edited. Use these instead:

- **Status buttons**: people who can read all reports can move a report between **Open**, **Under review**, and **Closed**. A filed report cannot go back to draft.
- **Follow ups**: add a dated follow up with **Follow up on** and **What needs to happen**, then select **Add**. On an open follow up, select **Record what happened** to close it out with the outcome, **Move** to change the date, or **Not needed** to drop it. Overdue follow ups are marked **Past due**. A follow up requested on the form becomes the first entry here when the report is filed. You can add follow ups to reports you filed; people who can read all reports can add them to any report.
- **Notes added since filing**: type in **Add a note** and select **Add it**. Check **Mark this as an addendum to the report** when you are correcting or completing the filed account. Every note shows its author and date.
- **Who was told**: a list of every notification the report tried to send, with **Who**, **How**, **When**, and **Result** (**Sent**, **Did not send**, or **Not sent**, with the reason when there is one).
- **PDF** opens the report as a PDF in a new tab. **Print** prints the page from your browser.

## Notifications

Notifications go out once, at the moment a report is filed. A notification problem never stops a report from being filed.

### Staff

Staff notifications follow the rules on the **Who gets told** tab in **Settings**. Each rule names a role or an email address, sends by email, text, or both, and fires only for reports at or above the severity you choose. Someone who matches more than one rule is told once per channel.

The staff message gives the person's name, the kind of incident, when it happened, how serious it was, where the person went afterward (if recorded), and the report number. It does not include the written account. A staff member with no email address, or no mobile number for texts, shows as **Not sent** in **Who was told**.

### Families

Families are told only when the person filing checks **Let the family know when this is filed** on that report. There is no setting that always notifies families. When it is checked:

- An email goes to the family on file for the person, with a short summary (name, kind of incident, when, how serious, and where they went afterward if recorded) and a request to call the camp office with questions.
- A text goes to the cell phone of the primary and secondary guardian, if one is on file.
- The report shows **Family notified** with the date and time.

The family can only be reached when the person was picked from your people. For a visitor typed in by hand, **Who was told** shows **Not sent** with "No family on file for this person."

## Settings

Select **Settings** on the report list. Only people who can read all reports can open it. It has three tabs.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Choices" icon="list">
    Edit the choices your staff pick from on the form, in four lists: **Places**, **Activities**, **Body parts**, and **Kinds of incident**. Rename a choice by editing it, add one with **Add a choice** and **Add**, or select **Retire** to stop showing it on new reports. A retired choice stays on reports that already use it, and **Bring back** restores it.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Your questions" icon="help-circle">
    Add questions the standard report does not ask. For each one, enter **The question**, pick an **Answer type** (**Short answer**, **Long answer**, **Choose one**, **Choose any**, **Yes or no**, **Date**, or **Number**), and choose **Shows on** (**Every report** or one kind of incident only). Add optional **Helper text**, enter **Choices** separated by commas for the choice types, and check **Must be answered before a report can be filed** to make it required. Select **Add this question**. Your questions appear on the form in a section called **Also for your camp**. Use **Retire** to stop asking a question.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Who gets told" icon="bell">
    Set up staff notifications. Under **Tell somebody else**, choose **Who** (**A role** or **An email address**), pick the role or type the address, choose **How** (**Email**, **Text**, or **Both**), and set **At least this serious**. Select **Add**. Rules you add here apply to every kind of incident. Select **Remove** on a rule to stop those notifications. If there are no rules, reports are still filed and saved, but no staff are notified.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Trends

Select **Trends** on the report list. Only people who can read all reports can open it. Trends counts filed reports only; drafts are left out. It covers the last 12 months unless you change the from and to dates and select **Update**.

- **Totals**: **Reports filed**, **Serious or worse**, **Still open**, **Need a follow up**, and **Family told**.
- **When they happen**: a chart of reports by hour of the day.
- **How serious**: reports by severity.
- **Over the range**: reports by week.
- **What kind**: reports by kind of incident, with each kind's share.
- **Where**, **During what**, and **What got hurt**: the top 10 places, activities, and body parts.

## Print and export

- **Print** on the report list opens a PDF of every report matching your current filters, with **Severity**, **Report**, **Person**, **What**, **When**, **Where**, **Status**, and **Filed by**. If you can only file reports, the PDF includes only the reports you filed.
- **PDF** on a filed report opens a full PDF of that report, including witnesses, care given, the response, follow ups, and notes added after filing.

## Start a report from the Health Center

If your camp also uses the **Health Center** and you can file incident reports, a health visit has a **Write an incident report** button. It starts a draft incident report filled in from the visit: the person, the time of the visit, the complaint, the assessment, the treatment, and the visit notes. Finish and file the draft in Incident Reports as usual. Once a visit has a report, the visit shows a link to open it.

## Notes

- Drafts can be edited until they are filed. After that the wording is locked for everyone, including administrators.
- Every time someone opens the list, a report, trends, or a PDF, it is recorded in an access log.
