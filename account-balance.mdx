## Overview

Use the **Accounts Receivable** report (**Financials → Accounts Receivable**) to see which families still owe money for a season and how those balances compare to their scheduled payments.

The report lists every family with a non-zero balance for the selected season, with contact details, registration information, revenue, scheduled payments, and current balance. Summary cards at the top show your **Overall balance**, **Overall scheduled**, and the **Difference** between them.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Use this report for a collections view by family: who owes money, how much, how much is already scheduled, and where those numbers do not match.
</Callout>

## What the report shows

The report is scoped by **season**. It includes only families with a **non-zero balance** for the selected season.

Each row represents a **family account** and includes:

- The primary contact and the users on the account

- Registration context (registration date and products)

- Contact information (email)

- Financial totals (revenue, scheduled payments, and outstanding balance)

- A per-family note you can edit inline

Use it with your scheduled payments and deposit reports to check that what is owed is scheduled to be collected.

## Filters

### Season / program group

The main filter is the season selector at the top of the page.

- Select the season you want to review, or **All Seasons**. The report shows families with non-zero balances for that selection.

## Summary cards

Above the family list, three summary cards total every row in the report. Click a card to filter the list:

- **Overall balance**: The combined balance across every family in the report. Click it to show the families with a balance.

- **Overall scheduled**: The combined total of future scheduled payments across those families. Click it to show the families with a schedule.

- **Difference**: **Overall balance** minus **Overall scheduled**, which shows how much of what is owed is not yet scheduled. Click it to show the families where balance and scheduled differ.

## Columns in the Account Balance report

Each family row has these columns.

### Family and registration details

- **Primary Contact**: The primary contact on the family account.

- **Users**: The users linked to this family’s registration for the selected season.

- **Products**: The sessions, add-ons, or other products on the family’s registration. The header uses your camp's term for products, such as **Sessions**. When the list wraps past two lines, the cell shows a **more** link; click it to expand the list, then **less** to collapse it. Search, sort, and exports use the full list.

- **Reg. Date**: The date of the family's most recent registration for the season.

### Contact information

- **Email**: The primary contact’s email address, with their phone number below it when one is on file.

### Financial totals

- **Revenue**: The family's total charges for the selected season, before payments.

- **Balance**: The family's current balance for the selected season, after payments and credits. The cell is tinted red when the family owes money and green when they have a credit.

- **Scheduled**: The total of the family's **future scheduled payments** that have not run yet.

### Notes and optional columns

- **Note**: A free-text note for the family. Click the cell (**Click to add note** when empty) to edit it. Use it to record collection status, promises to pay, or other context.

- **Profile-field columns** (optional): Columns for profile fields you choose with **Display Columns**. They appear only when you select them.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Compare **Revenue** with **Balance** to see how much of the family’s fees remain unpaid, and **Scheduled** with **Balance** to see whether future scheduled payments cover what is still owed.
</Callout>

## Choose which columns to show

Click **Display Columns** above the table to add profile-field columns.

- Check the profile fields you want (type in the box to filter the list), then click **Save Display Columns**.

- Fields you do not select stay hidden.

## Filter to mismatched balances

Click the **Difference** card to show only families whose scheduled payments do not equal their balance (the families with a red **Scheduled** cell). The line above the table changes from **Showing all** to the filter you picked. Click **Show all** to clear it.

While a filter is on, a note reminds you that the cards always total every row, while the column totals below follow the filter. The first column stays visible while you scroll sideways.

## Send an email from the report

The **Send Email** button at the top of the report lets you email families about their balances.

1. Optionally click a summary card so the email list matches the families you are working with.
2. Click **Send Email** and write your message.
3. Click **Send Email** again in the composer. Campium loads the recipient list for the selected season and filter, then asks **Send account balance email?**
4. Review the season name, the filter (**Mismatched only** or **Scheduled payments only**) when one is on, how many families and email addresses will receive the message, a short sample of names with balances, and any note about families skipped because neither guardian has an email address on file.
5. Click **Send Email** to send, or **Cancel**.

Only families with a positive balance in the current filter are emailed. If nobody qualifies, you see a warning instead of the confirmation.

## Red-cell mismatch warning

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  When a family’s scheduled payments do **not** equal their balance, that family's **Scheduled** cell is highlighted in red with a warning icon. Only that cell turns red, not the whole row. Hover the icon to see **Scheduled amount does not equal the balance.** The red tint on a **Balance** cell only means the family owes money.
</Callout>

When you see a red **Scheduled** cell:

- Click the **Difference** card to work only that list.
- Review the family’s scheduled payments and payment plan.
- Decide whether more payments need to be scheduled, or whether charges, discounts, or manual payments need adjusting.
- Follow up with the family if the plan does not fully cover the remaining balance.

## Run the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Financial reports menu" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    - Sign in to Campium.

    - In the sidebar, open **Financials**.

    - Click **Accounts Receivable**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the season / program group" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    - In the season selector at the top of the page, choose the season you want to review, or **All Seasons**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to use this report in your workflow

Use the report during your season to:

- Find families whose balances are **not fully covered** by scheduled payments (red **Scheduled** cells).

- Confirm that families on plans have enough future payments scheduled to cover their balances.

- Contact families who need updated payment plans or more payments before their sessions start.
