## Overview

Use the **Account Balance** report (Accounts Receivable) to see which families still owe money for a selected season or program group and how those balances compare to their scheduled payments.

The report lists all families with a non-zero balance for the selected season, along with contact details, registration information, revenue, scheduled payments, and current balance. Summary cards at the top of the report show your **Overall balance**, **Overall scheduled**, and the **Difference** between them.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Use this report when you want a collections view by family: who owes money, how much they owe, how much is already scheduled to be paid, and where those numbers do not line up.
</Callout>

## What the report shows

The Account Balance report is scoped by **season/program group**. It includes only families with a **non-zero balance** for the selected season or program group(s).

Each row represents a **family account** and includes:

- The primary contact and the users on the account

- Registration context (registration date and products)

- Contact information (email)

- Financial totals (revenue, scheduled payments, and outstanding balance)

- A per-family note you can edit inline

Use it alongside your scheduled payments and deposit reports to monitor whether what is owed is actually planned to be collected.

## Filters

### Season / program group

The primary filter is **Season / program group**.

- **Season / program group**: Select the season or program group you want to review. The report then shows families with non-zero balances for that selection.

If you use multiple program groups within a season, run the report once per group if you want to focus on a specific camp or program.

## Summary cards

Above the family list, the report shows three summary cards for the current selection:

- **Overall balance**: The combined outstanding balance across every family in the report.

- **Overall scheduled**: The combined total of future scheduled payments across those families.

- **Difference**: **Overall balance** minus **Overall scheduled**, so you can see at a glance how much of what is owed is not yet planned to be collected.

## Columns in the Account Balance report

This section describes the main columns you will see for each family row.

### Family and registration details

- **Primary Contact**: The primary contact on the family account.

- **Users**: The users linked to this family’s registration for the selected season.

- **Products**: The camp sessions, add-ons, or other products tied to the family’s registration. (Your column header may use your product plural label, such as **Sessions**.) When the list is long enough to wrap past two lines, the cell clamps and shows a **more** control; click it to expand the full list, then **less** to collapse again. Search, sort, and exports still use the full list.

- **Reg. Date**: The date the family completed registration for that season or program group.

### Contact information

- **Email**: The primary contact’s email address, so you can reach out about outstanding balances or payment plan changes.

### Financial totals

- **Revenue**: The total revenue for this family for the selected season or program group. This is what the family owes before payments.

- **Balance**: The current outstanding balance for the family for the selected season or program group, after payments and credits.

- **Scheduled**: The total amount of **future scheduled payments** for this family that have not yet been completed for the relevant charges.

### Notes and optional columns

- **Note**: A free-text note for the family that you can edit inline directly in the report. Use it to record collection status, promises to pay, or other context that stays with the row.

- **School** and **profile-field columns** (optional): You can add a **School** column and columns for your profile fields using the **Display Columns** selector. These appear only when you choose to show them.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Use **Revenue** vs **Balance** to see how much of the family’s total fees remain unpaid, and compare **Scheduled** vs **Balance** to confirm whether future scheduled payments will fully cover what is still owed.
</Callout>

## Choose which columns to show

Use the **Display Columns** selector to tailor the report to what you need to see.

- Turn on a **School** column to show each family’s school.

- Turn on columns for your profile fields to pull that data into the report.

- Columns you do not select stay hidden so the report stays focused.

## Filter to mismatched balances

Above the family table, use the **All** / **Mismatched** toggle to focus the list:

- **All** shows every family in the report (with the total row count on the button).
- **Mismatched** shows only families whose scheduled payments do not equal their outstanding balance (the same families with red **Balance** and **Scheduled** cells). The button is disabled when there are no mismatches.

When **Mismatched** is selected, a note reminds you that column totals below still reflect **all** rows, not only the filtered ones. The primary contact column stays visible while you scroll horizontally.

## Send an email from the report

The Account Balance report includes a **Send Email** action so you can contact families about their balances without leaving the report.

1. Optionally set the view to **All** or **Mismatched** so the email list matches the families you are working with.
2. Select **Send Email** and write your message.
3. Select **Send Email** again in the composer. Campium loads the real recipient list for the selected season and view, then asks **Send account balance email?**
4. Review the season name, **Mismatched only** when that filter is on, how many **families** and **email addresses** will receive the message, a short sample of names with balances, and any note about families skipped because neither guardian has an email on file.
5. Confirm with **Send Email**, or choose **Cancel**.

Only families with a positive balance in the current view are emailed. If nobody qualifies, you see a warning instead of the confirm dialog.

## Red-cell mismatch warning

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  When a family’s scheduled payments do **not** equal their outstanding balance, the **Balance** and **Scheduled** cells for that family are highlighted in red. Only those two cells turn red, not the whole row. Hovering over a highlighted cell shows the tooltip **Scheduled amount does not equal the balance.**
</Callout>

When you see red **Balance** and **Scheduled** cells:

- Switch the view to **Mismatched** to work only that priority list.
- Review the family’s scheduled payments and payment plan.
- Confirm whether additional payments need to be scheduled, or if charges, discounts, or manual payments need adjustment.
- Follow up with the family if the plan does not fully cover the remaining balance.

## Run the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Financial reports menu" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    - Sign in to your Campium admin portal.

    - Open the **Financials** navigation.

    - Select **Accounts Receivable**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the season / program group" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    - In the **Season / program group** filter, select the season or program group you want to review.

    - If needed, repeat the report for other program groups within the same season.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to use this report in your workflow

Use the Account Balance report regularly during your season to:

- Identify families with outstanding balances that are **not fully covered** by scheduled payments (red **Balance** and **Scheduled** cells).

- Confirm that families on plans have enough future payments scheduled to cover their balances.

- Prioritize outreach to families who need updated payment plans or additional payments before session start dates.
