Use **Attendance Sheets** when you need printable paper rosters for check-in and check-out away from a screen, such as at a trailhead, cabin, or bus stop.

<Callout kind="info">
  This builds paper PDFs. To record attendance online, use the live attendance reports instead. See [Attendance](/attendance).
</Callout>

## Where to find it

1. Open **Reports** in the left navigation.
2. Choose **System Reports**.
3. Under **Attendance**, select **Attendance Sheets (PDF)**.

You need the **Attendance Module** on in **Admin → Site Settings → Modules**, and your role must include the **Attendance** permission.

## Print sheets

The page title is **Attendance Sheets**. Pick a season in the header, then set the date range in the date picker. Campium defaults to the current week (Monday through Friday).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose how sheets are split" icon="layers">
    Under **One sheet for each**, pick **Session**, **Group**, or **Bus** (Bus appears when you have bus profile fields). Use **Only these** to limit which sessions, groups, or buses print, or leave it empty for all of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a layout" icon="layout">
    Under **Layout**, choose **Weekly, Mon-Fri**, **Weekly, 7 days**, **Daily**, **Daily sign-in / out**, or **Driver route** (Driver route is available when sheets are by **Bus**). Optionally turn on **Mark check-out too** so each day has IN and OUT circles. Choose **Page** portrait or landscape.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what each row shows" icon="list">
    Turn on extras such as sessions enrolled, **Group**, **Bus**, **Gender, age, grade, years**, **Health alerts**, guardian names and phones, and any extra profile fields you select.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print or save the setup" icon="printer">
    Click **Print PDF** to open the PDF in a new tab. Use **Save setup** to keep the options (except season and dates) under **Saved sheets**, then load them later with **Load a saved setup**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Season dates

If the selected season has no usable dates yet, Campium shows a notice that names the season and says it **has no dates yet**. Session and group sheets print empty until you set session dates (or Special Weeks dates when that is what the season uses). Administrators can open the linked **Sessions** or **Special Weeks** page from that notice.
