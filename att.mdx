## Overview

Use the Attendance sheet to print a paper PDF for taking check-in and check-out attendance by hand. It is handy when you want to track attendance away from a screen, such as at a trailhead, a cabin, or an off-site activity.

<Callout kind="info">
  This is a printable paper sheet. To record attendance online instead, use the online attendance reports for a single session or for a whole season.
</Callout>

## How it works

The page shows a full year of monthly calendars for the selected season. Pick a day, and Campium builds a printable PDF attendance sheet for that day.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a season" icon="folder" titleType="p">
    Use the season dropdown in the page header to pick the season you are taking attendance for. The export always targets a single season, so there is no "All Seasons" option here.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click the day you need" icon="calendar" titleType="p">
    In the calendar grid, click the date you want. Campium generates a PDF attendance sheet for that day and season, ready to print.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Print and take attendance on paper" icon="printer" titleType="p">
    Print the sheet and use it to check campers in and out by hand during the day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where to find it

In Campium, go to **Reports** &gt; **System** and select the Attendance sheet.

## Troubleshooting

- **No day exports anything**: Confirm a season is selected in the header dropdown before clicking a date.
- **Wrong campers on the sheet**: Check that the season in the header matches the season you are taking attendance for.
