## Overview

Use the Note Summary to review the notes your team has added to profiles over a chosen date range. It gives you one place to scan recent activity, follow up on flagged families, and see who logged each note.

## What it shows

<ParamField body="For" param-type="link" show-location="false">
The person the note is attached to, linked to their profile.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Note Type" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The category of the note, such as a custom note type or system events like Product Removed, Email Sent, and Email Opened.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Message" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The body of the note.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Date" param-type="date/time" show-location="false">
When the note was added.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Created By" param-type="link" show-location="false">
The staff member who added the note, linked to their profile when available.
</ParamField>

## Filters and columns

<ParamField body="Date range" param-type="filter" show-location="false">
Use the date picker at the top to choose which period of notes to include.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Note Type" param-type="filter" show-location="false">
Narrow the list to a single note type, including Product Removed, Email Sent, and Email Opened.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Display Columns" param-type="button" show-location="false">
Add extra profile fields as columns. Select **Display Columns**, choose the fields you want to see, and save. Your selection is remembered for next time.
</ParamField>

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Note Summary from Reports" icon="folder" titleType="p">
    Go to **Reports** &gt; **System** and select **Note Summary**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the date range and note type" icon="filter" titleType="p">
    Use the date picker and the Note Type selector to focus on the notes you care about.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

- **The report is empty**: No notes were added in the selected date range, or the chosen note type has no matching notes. Widen the date range or clear the note type filter.
- **A column you expected is missing**: Open **Display Columns** and confirm the profile field is selected, then save.
