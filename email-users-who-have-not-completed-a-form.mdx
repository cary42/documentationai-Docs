## Email users by form completion status

From the **Forms** list, or from a form's **Responses** tab in **Seasons** guided setup, email people on one form by their status (for example everyone still **In Progress**), then write the message in the shared **Email this group** composer.

You need **Manage Forms** to use the Forms list, and **Manage Email** to open **Email** and send. On guided season setup, open the form's **Responses** tab and click **Email** (same audience picker and composer).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Forms" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    - Go to **Forms** in the left navigation.

    - Find the form in the list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start Email" icon="mail" title-type="p">
    - Open the row **Actions** menu (vertical dots).

    - Click **Email**.

    - The modal title is **Email** plus the form name. Intro: **Pick who gets the email about** the form. **You write and preview it on the next step.**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Form status" icon="users" title-type="p">
    - Under **Form status**, check one or more:

      - **Not Started**

      - **In Progress** (checked by default)

      - **Pending Approval**

      - **Approved**

    - Each option shows a count in parentheses.

    - If the form has sessions, you can optionally narrow by session (your camp's product plural label). Leave sessions empty to include everyone with the statuses you picked. Helper: **Leave it empty to include everyone with the statuses you picked.**

    - Empty form: **Nobody is on this form yet, so there is no one to email.**

    - Click **Continue**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write and send" icon="send" title-type="p">
    - The shared **Email this group** composer opens with that audience.

    - Write the subject and message. Optionally click **Save as my default** so the next Forms **Email** for you fills in this subject and message (the form's name is swapped in for each form). Helper: **Next time, this subject and message are filled in for you. The form's name is swapped in for each form.**

    - Use **Preview** to see a filled-in sample, **Send this preview to** plus **Send preview** to mail a copy only to the addresses you type (never the whole group), then **Send Email** and **Yes, send it** when you are ready. See [Mass Emails: Preview before a group send](/mass-emails#preview-before-a-group-send).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View names under each form status

Open the form and use the status chips (for example **Not Started**, **In Progress**) to see who is in each status before you email.

## Notes

- Status filters apply only to the selected form.
- Sends use the Mass Emails queue, same as other **Email this group** sends.
- For reminder emails that Campium sends on a schedule from form settings, see [Forms](/forms#send-reminder-emails).
