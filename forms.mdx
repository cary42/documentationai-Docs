## Overview

Forms collect information from families and staff and keep each response connected to the person's profile. Use them for registrations, preferences, acknowledgments, and anything else you need to track over time.

Open **Forms** from the left navigation. It is its own top-level item.

On the parent portal, registration forms, standard forms, and reference forms use a stepped layout (sections, **Continue**, and the same preview strip you see in admin). That layout is on for every camp.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Some tabs and options depend on the **Form Type** you select. For example, Registration forms have a Products Layout tab, and Upload a File forms hide the Questions tab.
</Callout>

## Read the Status column on the Forms list

On the **Forms** list, the **Status** column shows whether each form is available right now. It uses the same badges as the form editor:

| Badge            | Meaning                                                                                                   |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Open**         | **Visibility** is **Visible** and today is between **Start Date** and **End Date**.                       |
| **Scheduled**    | **Visibility** is **Visible** and the **Start Date** is still in the future.                              |
| **Closed**       | **Visibility** is **Visible** and the **End Date** has passed.                                            |
| **No dates set** | **Visibility** is **Visible** but the form is missing a **Start Date** or **End Date**.                   |
| **Hidden**       | **Visibility** is **Hidden**. This wins over the dates.                                                   |

Under **Open**, **Scheduled**, and **Closed**, a line shows the timing, for example **Opens Tue 9:00 AM**, **Closes Jun 30, 5:00 PM**, or **Closed yesterday 11:59 PM**.

If a form shows **Scheduled** with an **Opens** line, families cannot see it on the portal until that time, even when recipients and other settings look correct.

<Callout kind="info">
  One family cannot find the form on the parent site, and the Forms list still says **Open**? Open their profile and click **Check Permissions**. The result tells you whether dates, Visibility, or a Recipients field is hiding it. See [Can't find a form on the parent portal](/user-cant-see-the-form-on-their-portal).
</Callout>

### Edit Open / Close on the Forms list

The **Open / Close** column shows when the form window starts and ends. Click the cell to edit without opening the form. Lines are labeled **Opens** and **Closes**. Empty cells show **No dates set**. Enter the date and time, then **Save** (or **Cancel**). After you save, the **Status** badge on that row updates to match the new window. You need the **Manage Forms** permission. You can also set **Start Date** and **End Date** on the form **Settings** tab.

### Recently deleted forms

Deleting a form from the Forms list, or removing one person's copy of a form from their profile **Forms** tab, moves it to trash instead of erasing it.

1. Confirm the delete. Whole-form delete asks: **Are you sure you want to delete this form? Every response to it is deleted too. You can restore it from Recently deleted for 30 days.** Removing one person's copy asks: **Are you sure you want to remove this form? You can restore it from Recently deleted for 30 days.**
2. After you delete, a toast can include **Undo** so you can restore it right away.
3. On the Forms list, click **Recently deleted**. On a profile **Forms** tab, use the **Recently deleted** link.
4. The **Recently deleted forms** dialog lists **Form**, what was deleted (the whole form with every response, or one person's response), **Deleted**, and **Restore**. Empty state: **Nothing was deleted in the last 30 days.**
5. Helper text: **Deleted forms stay here for 30 days, then they are gone for good.**

If you restore a person's response while they already have a newer copy of the same form, Campium asks whether to replace their current copy (the current copy then moves to **Recently deleted**).

## Read the availability badge in the form editor

When you open a form, an availability badge appears beside the form name on every tab (and as the leading chip on the status row in Guided Season Setup). It shows whether the form would appear on the Portal right now, based on **Visibility** and **Start Date** / **End Date**:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Hidden** | **Visibility** is **Hidden** (hidden from the portal). |
| **No dates set** | The form needs a start and end date to open. |
| **Scheduled** | The form opens on a future **Start Date** (the badge meta shows when). |
| **Closed** | The **End Date** has passed. |
| **Open** | The form is visible on the Portal through the **End Date**. |

After you click **Save Settings**, the badge updates without a full page reload.

## Create a new form

Use the **New Form** button on the Forms list to start a new form.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Forms list" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    - From the main Campium navigation, select **Forms**.

    You should see a list of forms and a primary action button labeled **New Form**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start a new form" icon="plus" title-type="p">
    - Click the **New Form** button at the top of the list.
    - Enter a **Form Name**, choose a season under **Select Season**, and pick a **Form Type**. In this dialog the types read **Registration (With Products For Sale)**, **Standard (Nothing for sale, just a form)**, and **Allow an upload of a File**. In the editor they read **Registration (With Products)**, **Standard (No Products for Purchase)**, and **Upload a File**.
    - When the form name looks like an immunization form (for example it contains "immunization" or "immunisation"), Campium offers **Include our immunization form?** Parents download Campium's blank fillable PDF, take it to their doctor, and upload the completed copy. Checking it sets **Form Type** to **Allow an upload of a File**. Use **Preview the form** to open the PDF. The same offer appears when you create a form from Guided Season Setup.
    - Click **Create**. Campium creates the form and opens the form editor with the **Settings** tab active.

    The editor shows additional tabs such as **Recipients**, **Questions**, and **Responses**, and may show **Products Layout** depending on the Form Type.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete required Settings fields" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    In the **Settings** tab, fill out the required fields (marked with a red asterisk). Items marked "registration only" appear only when **Form Type** is **Registration (With Products)**.

    - Core details
      - **Form Name** (required)
      - **Form Type** (required)
      - **Season** (required)
      - **Description** (optional; helper: **Shows up beneath the name of the form on the Portal.**)

    Who the form is for is set on the **Recipients** tab, not here.
    - Availability
      - **Start Date** and **End Date** (required; control when the form is available on the Portal)
      - **Cutoff Date** (registration only; optional age gate; helper: **If set, the user will not be able to continue if the child was born after the cutoff date (too young).**)
      - **Show Question Numbers?**
      - **Visibility** (**Visible** or **Hidden**; helper: **Setting this to hidden will hide access to the form.**)
      - **Lock Form After Completion** (helper: **Will prevent changes to a form once it has been submitted.**)
    - Statuses after completion
      - **Form Status After Completion** (required)
      - **User Status After Completion** (required)
    - Fees and payments (registration only)
      - **Application Fee** and the **Manage Fees** button
      - **Show # of spots left**
      - **Enable Payment Plan**
      - **Allow User To Pay Later** (helper: **No deposit necessary if set to Yes. Typically used for testing purposes.** When the Pending Registrations module is on, the choices are **No** and **Save for later (pending)**, which holds the spot as pending until you approve it from the profile.)
      - **Pay In Full Upon Registration** (helper: **If set to yes, minimum deposits will be ignored. You may want to set this to yes when camp is about to begin.**)
      - **Default Payment To** (helper: **When the user gets to the checkout screen, payment will default to this option.**)
      - **Minimum Deposit Override**, **Minimum Deposit Scope**, and **Charge Deposit On Approval**
    - **Due Date** (helper: **Displayed on Portal for user.**)
    - **Discount settings** (registration only)
      - **Allow For Discounts To Be Applied**
      - **Allow Multiple Discounts**
      - **Auto Apply These Discounts**
      - **Enable Pay In Full Discount**, with **Pay In Full Discount Type** and **Pay In Full Discount Value** to override the default amount or percent for this form
    - **Send Reminder Emails** (helper: **Users with a form status of "in progress" or "not started" will receive an email asking them to complete the form.** A warning under it says to avoid reminders on a primary registration form or on any form where a large audience could receive these emails.)
    - **Email settings**
      - **Send Admin Email Notification To** (pick staff users from the list)
      - **Email Template When Approved**, **Email Template When Pending**, and **Email Template When Rejected** (leave blank to send no email)
    - **Custom text** (registration and standard forms)
      - **Payment Button Text For Checkout Page** (required, registration only)
      - **Thank You Message**
      - **Form Header Text**
      - **Sessions Selection Header Text** (registration only, uses your product wording)
      - **Cart Header Text** (registration only)

    The value you choose for **Form Type** controls which tabs appear (for example, Products Layout vs. Questions) and how users interact with the form.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a Form Type" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">
    Select a **Form Type** based on what you are collecting:

    - **Registration (With Products)**: for registrations where families pick and pay for sessions or products. Shows the **Products Layout** tab.
    - **Standard (No Products for Purchase)**: for questionnaires, acknowledgments, and preference forms with nothing to buy.
    - **Upload a File**: for collecting documents. Hides the **Questions** tab.

    Changing the Form Type changes which configuration tabs appear in the editor.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save Settings" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    - Click **Save Settings**.
    - Campium saves your changes and keeps you in the editor so you can set up Recipients, Questions, and other options.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tabs inside a form

Each form opens in a tabbed editor. Some tabs appear only for specific Form Types.

### Settings tab

Use **Settings** to define how the form behaves.

- Sets the **Form Name**, **Form Type**, and **Season**.
- Sets the **Start Date** and **End Date** that control when the form is available, and on registration forms the optional **Cutoff Date** age gate.
- Controls **Visibility** (**Visible** or **Hidden**) so you can prepare forms in advance or take them down.
- Turns **Lock Form After Completion** on or off. When it is on, users cannot change responses after they submit.
- Sets **Form Status After Completion** and **User Status After Completion**, the statuses Campium applies after a user finishes the form.
- Includes fee, payment, discount, email, and custom text settings for registrations and payments.
- Ends with a sticky action bar with **Save Settings**. The bar shows **Your settings save automatically.** Changing a Settings control saves on its own. Text fields save when you stop typing (you do not need to leave the field). After a save the status reads **Saved just now.**, then **Last saved a moment ago.** While a save is in progress, the button reads **Saving...** Saves from typing update the status bar only and do not show a success toast. Errors still show a toast.

### Products Layout tab (conditional)

The **Products Layout** tab appears only when the **Form Type** is **Registration (With Products)**.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The label on this tab is dynamic. It reflects the product terminology configured for your camp, so it may read as **Sessions Layout** (or another product name) rather than **Products Layout**.
</Callout>

- Sets which sessions or products appear on the registration form and how they are grouped.
- Families select and pay for those items as part of completing the form.

On the live products selection page (list and grid layouts, and admin layout preview), sessions and products that have start and end dates show that range under the name (for example `Jun 22 - Jun 26, 2026`). Dropdown options can include the same range in parentheses after the name. Rows without dates show the name only. When Special Weeks is on, week choices can show their dates under each week label the same way. See [Special Weeks](/special-weeks#week-choices-on-registration).

If you select a different Form Type (Standard or Upload a File), this tab does not appear.

#### How a layout group appears

On the layout tab, use **New layout group** or **Edit Layout Group** to organize sessions or products into groups families see during registration.

Under **How this group appears**, choose:

- **List - every option is visible**: each option shows as a checkbox (the default).
- **Dropdown - options are picked from a menu**: families pick from a menu instead of scrolling a long checkbox list. Prefer this when a group holds many options.

Combine that choice with **Allow multiple selections in this group**:

- Dropdown with multiple selections off: families pick one option (**Select one**).
- Dropdown with multiple selections on: families pick several options from a searchable menu (for example **Select sessions**, using your product wording).

Other group options stay the same: **Do not display the heading for this Layout Group**, **Require the user to select at least one item from this group**, and **Save Group**. On the layout tab, groups set to dropdown show a **Dropdown** badge.

#### Continue on the product selection page

On a live registration form, the product selection step holds **Continue** closed until the family picks at least one available session or product (checkboxes, dropdown groups, or a food quantity greater than zero all count). A short hint under the button uses your product wording, for example **Choose a session to continue.**

When the page shows only one selectable session (not locked, not food-only, and not already unavailable), that session is checked automatically so the family can continue without an extra click. Multi-session pages are unchanged.

Food-only pages are not held closed: ordering no food is a valid answer. If every option on the page is already purchased, full, or wait listed, **Continue** stays enabled and a warning explains why nothing can be selected (for example that all sessions shown are full). Already-purchased rows that appear pre-checked and disabled do not count as a new choice.

Admin **Preview** of the product selection page keeps **Continue** review-only (nothing is saved), and shows a **Parent view** strip above the button. The strip reports **Continue closed** or **Continue open**, with the same parent hint families would see (for example **Select 1 more week to continue.** or **Choose a session to continue.**). When the gate is clear, the hint reads **Nothing is blocking Continue.** Preview still notes that the continue button does not work in preview. When Special Weeks is on, Preview (and Guided setup **Sessions selection**) also shows the **Which weeks will you attend?** block so you can check the week count gate without publishing.

### Recipients tab

Use the **Recipients** tab to set who the form is for and who can see it.

- At the top, choose who the form is about:
  - Registration forms: **Who is this registration for?** with helper text **Pick who attends, not who fills the form out.** (Your camper wording may differ.)
  - Other forms: **Who is this form for?** with helper text **Pick who the form is about. Add Guardians only if they are the ones attending.**
- Under **How to apply this form**, choose **To all the Active/Inactive/Pending user types selected above** or **Only people who match the filters below**.
- If you add a Parent or Guardian type, Campium asks **Will guardians be attending these sessions?** (or your product wording). Choose **Yes, they attend** to keep them, or **No** to remove that type.
- When the Season filter is exactly one season with a ready snapshot, **Profile answers as of** appears under it (**Today** or that season's **close** / **frozen**), the same control as on custom reports. It changes which profile answers the recipient filters use. Editing notes from reports are omitted here.
- When the recipient setup needs attention, a warning icon appears next to **Recipients** on the tab.

Set up recipients before you open the form or tell families about it, so the right people can see and complete it.

### Questions tab (conditional)

The **Questions** tab holds the questions users answer when they complete the form.

- Add, edit, and order questions.
- Question types include text fields, **Choose One**, **Choose Many**, and other input types.
- Toolbar: **New Question**, **Add Signature**, **Add from Library**, **Add Section Header**, and **Preview Form** (when it still appears on the Questions tab). Use **Add Signature** for signature questions.
- Each row starts with a drag grip (**Drag to reorder**), then the question number, then an **Actions** menu (vertical dots) with **Edit**, **Move to position**, and **Delete**. The question text is also a clickable **Edit** link.
- When a question has follow-ups, a chip such as **Shows 2 follow-ups** appears on the parent. Each follow-up shows a chip such as **Shown if Q4 is Yes**. Hover highlights the related rows; click jumps to the first related question.

#### Add from Library

Click **Add from Library** to open **Add from Field Library**. Search fields (placeholder example: allergies, doctor, emergency), pick packs and fields, then **Add Selected to Form**. Missing profile fields are created or matched automatically. The catalog filters packs by the form's user types (camper, parent, or staff). Packs include areas such as Camper Info, Parent / Guardian, Medical, Emergency Contacts, Transportation, Permissions, and Staff Application.

For **Upload a File** forms, Campium hides the **Questions** tab, because families upload a file instead of answering questions.

On the parent portal, file uploads show helper text **JPG, PNG, or PDF · up to 16 MB**. Files larger than 16 MB are rejected with an error that names the size limit. After a successful upload, the row status badge changes to **Received** without a page reload.

Profile photo questions on the parent portal show **JPG, PNG, or GIF. Maximum size is 15 MB.** Oversized photos are rejected with a message such as **This photo is X MB. The maximum size is 15 MB. Please choose a smaller photo.**

#### Move a question to a position

Drag the grip to reorder, or jump a question by number:

1. Open **Forms**, open the form, then open the **Questions** tab.
2. Clear the table search box if you used it. Moving or reordering while filtered fails with: **Clear the search box first, so every question shows in the list you are moving within.** (or the matching message when saving order).
3. Open the row **Actions** menu and choose **Move to position**.
4. In **Move Question**, set **Position** (the number this question should have) and/or **Or place it after** (**Place it after** for section headers). Use **Top of the form** to put it first.
5. Click **Move**.

#### Field Size on a text question

When you **Edit** a text question (not a signature), **Field Size** offers **Regular** or **Large**. Helper text: **Large shows a bigger box. This is the same size as the profile field, so changing it updates the profile and every form that uses this field.**

This is the size of the input box (a single line or a larger multi-line box), not a character limit. The same control lives on **Admin → Profile Fields**. See [Profile Fields](/profile-fields).

Click **Save Question** to apply.

### Responses tab

Use the **Responses** tab to track progress and work with submitted data.

- Groups users by response status:
  - **Not Started**
  - **In Progress**
  - **Pending**
  - **Approved**
- Lists each response to the form in a table.
- Shows who has completed the form, who still needs to respond, and which submissions you have approved.

#### Change status and open a response

On the responses table:

- Click the **Status** value on a row to change it. The menu includes **Reset to In Progress**, **Approve**, **Pending Approval**, **Reject**, and **Delete**.
- Use the **View** column (eye icon) to open that person's responses in a new tab. The row menu also includes **View Responses**.
- Use the filled primary **Print** dropdown for **Print All**, **Print Approved**, or **Print Pending**. **Bulk Approve** stays beside it. Those dialogs include a **Layout** choice and optional **Hide** checkboxes:
  - **Compact**: *Smaller type and tighter spacing. Keeps answers on fewer pages when possible.*
  - **Expanded**: *Larger type and more spacing. Each answer follows its question on the same line.*
  - **Hide** → **Section headers and instructions**: *Leaves out the form's headings and instruction text. Signatures still print.*
  - **Hide** → **Questions with no answer**: *Question numbers still match the form.*
  Campium saves your layout and hide choices to your admin account, so they follow you across browsers. Single-response print (**Print Responses** → **Open PDF**) uses the same **Layout** and **Hide** block, plus optional **Include uploaded files** / **Print files at full page size**. Profile **Forms** print and Staff Hub completed-form print use the same dialog.

When the form has reference submissions, the table also shows a **Reference For** column (the applicant, linked to their profile), and **Primary Email** shows the reference's invitation email.

### Question type labels

When you add a question, the multiple-choice types are labeled **Choose One** and **Choose Many**. The Field Library and other type lists use the same labels.

### Add a note on a form response

Use the **Note** column on the responses table for a short staff-only reminder about a submission (for example, a follow-up call or an exception).

1. Open **Forms** in the left navigation.
2. Open the form, then select the **Responses** tab.
3. In the **Note** column, click **Click to add note** (or the existing note text).
4. In the **Response note** dialog, enter the note, then click **Save**.

Clearing the text and saving removes the note. Parents do not see these notes.

## Print form responses

Use the **Print** controls on the **Responses** tab (or the print icon on a profile / Staff Hub completed form) to generate a PDF. Choose **Layout** and optional **Hide** options before you confirm:

- **Compact**: smaller type and tighter spacing when you want fewer pages.
- **Expanded**: larger type and more spacing; each answer follows its question on the same line.
- **Section headers and instructions**: omit headings and instruction text (signatures still print).
- **Questions with no answer**: omit blank questions; question numbers still match the form.

Campium saves your layout and hide choices to your admin account and uses them the next time you print. Confirm bulk jobs with **Yes, Generate PDF**.

You can also print from the dashboard **Quick Access Forms** card. Open the form's menu and choose **Print** (Registration and Standard forms only; **Upload a File** forms do not show **Print**). The **Print Responses** dialog lets you choose **Approved and Pending** (default), **Approved only**, or **Pending only**, plus **Layout** and **Hide**. Intro: **Build one PDF of this form's responses. You will get an email when it is ready.** Helper: **In Progress responses are not included.** Confirm with **Print**. This uses the same background job as **Print All** on the Responses tab.

When a form completion email attaches a PDF, each staff recipient gets the layout they last chose in the admin portal.

## What families see when a form save fails

On the parent portal, if answers cannot be saved, the form does **not** report success. Families see an error at the top of the page, their answers stay on the form, and the submit button changes to **Try Again**.

Typical messages include:

- **We could not save part of this form.** with the section that failed (for example **Guardian 1 information**, **Guardian 2 information**, or camper information), plus **Nothing you typed is lost.** and a **Reference** code for support.
- A generic **We could not save this form.** message when the portal cannot reach the server or gets an unexpected response (instead of leaving the button stuck on **Saving...**).

Families can press **Try Again**, refresh the page, or come back later and continue. The message also links to **contact the camp** if the problem keeps happening.

### Guardian 2 first and last name

If a family fills in any **Guardian 2** field, they must enter both a first and last name for Guardian 2 before the form can save. Without the name, Campium cannot create the second guardian, so the other Guardian 2 details would be lost.

The error reads: **Please enter both a first and last name for Guardian 2.** It explains that the name is required to save the other Guardian 2 details, and that families with no second guardian should clear the Guardian 2 fields and continue.

An empty Guardian 2 section stays valid, so single-guardian families are not blocked.

## Preview a form without saving

Use the orange **Preview** menu at the top of the form editor (same control as on a season's Guided setup Forms step) to walk through the form on the parent portal without creating a real submission. The menu header reads **Opens in a new tab**.

- **Questions** - what people see when they open this form
- On registration forms only: **Sessions selection** and **Cart**

You can still open previews from **Settings** links such as **Preview Form**, **Preview Sessions Selection Page**, and **Preview Cart Header**.

This is different from [Test Mode](/test-mode). Test Mode lets staff open the live form on the portal and can create real responses. Preview never saves answers.

### Open the preview

1. Open **Forms** in the left navigation.
2. Open the form you want to review.
3. Choose one of these entry points:
   - Click **Preview**, then **Questions** (or **Sessions selection** / **Cart** on a registration form).
   - On the **Questions** tab, click **Preview Form** when that button is shown.
   - On a registration form's **Settings** tab, under **Form Header Text**, click **Preview Form**.
   - On a registration form's **Settings** tab, under **Sessions Selection Header Text**, click **Preview Sessions Selection Page** (both labels use your camp's product wording).

The preview opens on the parent portal with a banner that says you are viewing a **PREVIEW** and that nothing is saved.

- **Registration** forms: **You are viewing a PREVIEW - nothing is saved. Click Continue to review the Sessions selection page.** (The product wording matches your camp, for example **Sessions**.)
- **Standard**, staff, and other non-registration forms: **You are viewing a PREVIEW - nothing is saved. Click Submit to see the thank you message.**

### Continue to the product selection preview

On a registration form preview:

1. Review the questions page.
2. Click **Continue**.
3. Campium opens the product selection preview so you can review that page too. Nothing is saved on either page.

On the product selection preview, selections and the continue control stay review-only. Use the **Parent view** strip to see whether **Continue** would be closed or open for a parent, and why. Use the separate **Preview Sessions Selection Page** link from **Settings** if you only need that page.

### Submit on a non-registration preview

On a standard or staff form preview, the control is **Submit**. Clicking it shows the thank-you message on the page and hides the preview banner. Nothing is saved, and you do not go to a product selection page.

### Parent view on Questions and Cart Header previews

Preview pages include a **Parent view** strip so you can check the same gates a family hits, without submitting anything:

- **Questions** preview (**Preview Form**): the strip shows **Continue closed** with a message such as **N required answers still to fill in:** followed by question names, or **Continue open** with **Every required answer is filled in.** Photo, file upload, and reference questions are only fully checked on a live form, so when those are required the open message notes that they are checked when a parent presses **Continue**.
- **Cart Header** preview (**Preview Cart Header**): the strip starts with **Try the checkout rules**. You can try sample **Payment plan** and **Paying today** choices (**Full Amount**, **Minimum Deposit**, **Other**). The strip reports **Checkout closed** or **Checkout open** with the parent's message (for example **Please select a payment plan before proceeding.**). **Submit Order** stays disabled in preview.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  If you need to submit answers for real while staff-only testing, use [Test Mode](/test-mode) instead of **Preview Form**.
</Callout>

## Form settings reference

Use the **Settings** tab to control how the form appears, what happens after submission, and how Campium handles payments and reminders. Who can complete the form is set on the **Recipients** tab.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Some settings appear only for specific **Form Type** values. Registration forms show fee, payment, discount, and deposit settings that do not appear on Standard or Upload a File forms. Standard forms keep **Thank You Message** and **Form Header Text** from the custom text settings.
</Callout>

### Basic information

The top of the Settings tab defines what the form is and which season it belongs to.

#### Form Name

**Form Name** is the name staff see in admin and families see on the Portal. Pick a name people recognize without opening the form, especially when a season has several forms.

#### Form Type

**Form Type** controls the overall behavior of the form and which tabs or settings appear in the editor.

| Form Type                               | Best used for                                                  | Questions tab | Products Layout tab | Payment-related settings |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Registration (With Products)**        | Enrollments, session signups, registrations with checkout      | Shown         | Shown               | Shown                    |
| **Standard (No Products for Purchase)** | Surveys, waivers, contracts, acknowledgments, preference forms | Shown         | Hidden              | Hidden                   |
| **Upload a File**                       | Document collection where the main action is file submission   | Hidden        | Hidden              | Hidden                   |

If you change **Form Type** later, Campium changes which tabs and fields are available. Review the Settings tab after any type change so you do not miss required fields.

### Immunization blank form (upload forms)

On an **Upload a File** form, **Settings** can show an **Immunization Records** block:

1. Turn on **This form collects immunization records** when uploads should be read for vaccine doses (paid immunization add-on). Leave it off for other uploads such as insurance cards.
2. Until Campium's blank PDF is attached, you also see **Include our immunization form?** with **Add our form** and **Preview**. **Add our form** attaches Campium's fillable immunization PDF so families can download it from the portal, complete it with a doctor, and upload the finished copy.

The blank PDF offer is available whenever you create or edit an immunization-style upload form. Turning on immunization *reading* still requires the paid Immunization add-on. See [Immunization](/immunization).

#### Season

**Season** connects the form to the seasonal setup used elsewhere in Campium. For registration forms, the selected season determines which products, fees, discounts, and payment plans are available to the form.

If a product or fee is missing from a registration form, confirm that the item exists in the same season selected on the form.

#### Required

**Required** appears on camper registration, re-enrollment, and update forms that have a season. Helper text: **Counts toward the "to do" number families see on the Portal, and toward Camper Hub's required forms.** The switch label is **Every camper in this season must finish this form**.

Turning it on adds this form to the same list you manage under **Admin → Camper Hub → Camper Hub Settings → Required forms by season**. Leave it off when the form is optional. See [Camper Hub](/camper-hub#choose-which-fields-and-forms-count).

#### Staff season assignment

**Staff season assignment** appears only when the form's recipients include a staff or volunteer user type. The label reads **Volunteer season assignment** when the form is for volunteers only. Helper: **When Yes, completing this form adds the Staff to their season on their profile.**

| Setting | Behavior                                                                                       |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Yes** | Completing the form adds the person to the form's season on their profile.                     |
| **No**  | Completing the form does not change the person's seasons. This is the default.                 |

The on-screen advice: **Use for your main hiring form; leave as 'No' for references or supplemental forms.**

#### Description

**Description** appears beneath the form name on the Portal. Use it to tell recipients what the form is for, when to complete it, or what happens after they submit. One or two sentences work better than a long paragraph.

### Scheduling

Scheduling settings control when the form is visible, when it is due, and, on registration forms, the age cutoff.

#### Start Date

**Start Date** is when the form shows up on the Portal, as long as **Visibility** is **Visible** and recipients are set. Helper: **When the form shows up on the Portal.** The field is a date and time. If you enter a date without a time, Campium asks you to add the time before you can save.

If families say they cannot see a form yet, check whether today is before **Start Date**. On the **Forms** list, forms that have not opened yet show **Scheduled** with an **Opens** line under **Status**. In the form editor, the availability badge shows **Scheduled** with the open date.

#### Form opening email for administrators

The day before a form’s **Start Date**, Campium can email a **Form opening** digest to users with the **Administrator** role. The subject looks like `{Form name} opens tomorrow` (or `{N} forms open tomorrow` when more than one form opens that day).

Each form card can include:

- **Type** and **Visibility**
- **Opens** (the start date)
- **Notes** such as **Ready**, **Hidden on Portal**, **Test form**, or **No items for purchase**
- Links **Open form settings →** and **View forms →**

Use those notes to fix **Visibility**, turn off Test Mode, or add products before families expect the form.

Administrators control this digest under your name (top right) → **Settings → Email Notifications** as **Form opening digest** (toggle **Email me this digest**). It is on unless you turn it off. Organization-wide on/off lives on **Admin → Email Notifications**. See [Account](/account#email-notifications).

#### End Date

**End Date** is when the form disappears from the Portal (helper: **When the form disappears from the Portal.**). Like **Start Date**, it is a date and time. If you enter a date without a time, Campium asks you to add the time before you can save.

Use **End Date** when you want a form to disappear completely after a deadline or event window passes.

#### Due Date

**Due Date** tells recipients when the form should be completed (helper: **Displayed on Portal for user.**), but it does **not** hide the form. A form stays visible after the due date until its **End Date**.

Campium also uses **Due Date** to schedule reminder emails. Reminders do not send without one.

| Configuration             | What recipients see                                                                               | Reminder email behavior                               |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **End Date only**         | Form stays available until End Date, with no due-date messaging                                   | No due-date reminders                                 |
| **Due Date and End Date** | Form can show as due or past due while still remaining available until End Date                   | Reminder emails can reference due and past-due status |
| **Due Date only**         | The form shows **No dates set** and does not open on the Portal                                   | Reminders are scheduled, but recipients cannot open the form |

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Use **Due Date** when you want accountability and reminders. Use **End Date** when you want the form to disappear.
</Callout>

### Visibility and editing rules

These settings control whether recipients can see the form and whether they can change it after they submit.

#### Visibility

**Visibility** is the manual on/off switch for the form.

| Visibility  | Behavior                                                                                             |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Visible** | Eligible recipients can see the form when the current date is within the form's availability window. |
| **Hidden**  | No recipients can see the form on the Portal, even if dates and recipients are set correctly. Helper: **Setting this to hidden will hide access to the form.** |

Use **Hidden** while building or testing a form. Switch to **Visible** only when content, recipients, and dates are ready.

#### Lock Form After Completion

**Lock Form After Completion** controls whether recipients can edit a form after they submit it. Helper: **Will prevent changes to a form once it has been submitted.**

| Setting | Behavior                                                                                              | Best use                                                                               |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Yes** | Recipients cannot reopen and change answers after submission.                                         | Registration forms, contracts, waivers, and any form that should become a fixed record |
| **No**  | Recipients can return and update answers after submitting, subject to the form still being available. | Surveys, preference forms, and forms where answers may change over time                |

For registrations, **Yes** is the safer choice, because families should not change enrollment details after checkout without staff review. For surveys and preference forms, **No** lets people update their answers.

### What happens when someone finishes

These settings set the status of the submission and, if you choose, update the person's status.

#### Form Status After Completion

**Form Status After Completion** sets the workflow state of the form response after the recipient submits it.

| Status               | What it means                                                                               | Common use                                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Pending Approval** | Submission is complete, but staff must review it before treating it as finalized.           | Staff-reviewed registrations, contracts, or approvals        |
| **Approved**         | Submission is treated as completed and approved immediately.                                | Self-service forms that do not need review                   |
| **In Progress**      | Submission stays marked unfinished after the recipient submits it.                          | Internal workflows that need more staff action               |

If staff approval is part of your process, set **Form Status After Completion** to **Pending Approval** so submissions do not count as fully approved the moment a family clicks submit.

#### User Status After Completion

**User Status After Completion** updates the person's status after they finish the form. Helper: **If this is a registration form, you'll want to set this to Active or Pending.**

| Status        | Effect                                                |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **No Change** | Leaves the user's current status unchanged.           |
| **Pending**   | Moves the user into a pending state after completion. |
| **Active**    | Marks the user as active after completion.            |
| **Inactive**  | Marks the user as inactive after completion.          |
| **Lead**      | Marks the user as a lead after completion.            |

Automatic status changes help in enrollment workflows, but they can confuse staff who are not expecting the update.

### Registration and payment settings

These settings appear only when **Form Type** is **Registration (With Products)**.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  If you do not see the payment and registration settings described below, confirm that **Form Type** is set to **Registration (With Products)**.
</Callout>

#### Application Fee

Use **Application Fee** to charge an upfront fee with the registration. Helper: **Automatically added to cart. This is not part of the deposit - it's an additional fee.**

Set it up in this order:

1. Create or confirm the fee item in the correct season.
2. Select that fee in **Application Fee**.
3. Review the fee list with the **Manage Fees** button.
4. Save the form and test the checkout flow with a sample user.

The fee is then added to the cart on every registration through this form.

#### Show number of spots left

**Show # of spots left** shows families how many spaces remain (helper: **Displays spots left on the Portal.**). Turn it on only when your capacity numbers are current.

#### Enable Payment Plan

**Enable Payment Plan** lets families pay over time instead of paying the full balance at checkout. Helper: **Display a list of your payment plans for the user to select from.** The plans come from **Admin → Payment Plans** for the form's season.

#### Require User To Add A Payment Method

**Require User To Add A Payment Method** appears only when your payment processor is Finix. Helper: **When the amount due today is $0, require a card or ACH account to be added and stored.** Turn on **Require payment method at $0 checkout** when you need a card or bank account on file for future scheduled payments.

#### Allow User To Pay Later

**Allow User To Pay Later** lets the user complete registration without paying at checkout. When the Pending Registrations module is on, the choices are **No** and **Save for later (pending)**: the spot is held as pending, with no balance and no enrollment, until you approve it from the profile.

On the parent checkout page, families choose how much to pay under **How much today?**:

- **Full amount** with helper **Pay the whole balance today.**
- Pay-later option (label comes from your portal pay-later text) with helper **Nothing is charged today.** when pay later is allowed
- Minimum deposit option (label from your minimum deposit text) with helper **The rest stays on your account balance.** when a minimum is offered
- **Another amount** when custom amounts are allowed (at least the minimum)
- **Credit card surcharge** line when a surcharge applies, then **Total today**

When payment plans are offered, a **Payment plan** card lists each plan as a selectable option. Families who can pick their charge day still see **Charge day of the month**.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Use **Allow User To Pay Later** carefully. Helper text: **No deposit necessary if set to Yes. Typically used for testing purposes.** Users finish registration without paying anything.
</Callout>

#### Pay In Full Upon Registration

**Pay In Full Upon Registration** requires the full amount at checkout. Helper: **If set to yes, minimum deposits will be ignored. You may want to set this to yes when camp is about to begin.** With **Preview new layout** on, setting it to **Yes** also hides **Default Payment To** and the minimum deposit settings, with the note **Users pay the full amount, so Default Payment To and the minimum deposit settings are hidden. Set Pay In Full Upon Registration to No to change them.**

Use this when you want registration to count only after the full amount is paid.

#### Default Payment To

**Default Payment To** sets which payment option, **Minimum Deposit** or **Pay in Full**, is already picked when users reach checkout. Helper: **When the user gets to the checkout screen, payment will default to this option.**

#### Cutoff Date

**Cutoff Date** is an age gate. Helper: **If set, the user will not be able to continue if the child was born after the cutoff date (too young).**

For example, if a program requires campers to be a certain age by a specific date, set that date in **Cutoff Date** so age validation uses the correct rule.

### Discount settings

The **Discount settings** box controls whether families can use discounts during registration and how Campium applies them.

#### Allow For Discounts To Be Applied

Set **Allow For Discounts To Be Applied** to **Yes** to add a discount box to checkout (helper: **Adds a discount box to checkout so codes can be entered**). Set it to **No** when the form should always charge the standard rate.

#### Allow Multiple Discounts

**Allow Multiple Discounts** lets more than one discount apply in the same checkout when your pricing rules allow it.

Stacked discounts can produce totals staff did not expect, so decide your stacking rules before you turn this on.

#### Auto Apply These Discounts

**Auto Apply These Discounts** lists discounts to apply automatically. Helper: **Applies these discounts to every registration.**

#### Enable Pay In Full Discount

**Enable Pay In Full Discount** adds a discount incentive when the payer chooses to pay in full.

| Setting                           | What it does                                                       |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Pay In Full Upon Registration** | Requires full payment at checkout.                                 |
| **Enable Pay In Full Discount**   | Rewards the payer for choosing full payment by reducing the price. |

One is a payment requirement. The other is a pricing incentive.

When you enable the pay-in-full discount, two fields let you override your default for this form. The helper under each shows your default.

- **Pay In Full Discount Type**: **Fixed Amount** or **Percent**.
- **Pay In Full Discount Value**: the amount or percent for this form. Leave both at the default to use your standard pay-in-full discount.

### Minimum deposit settings

The **Minimum deposit settings** box controls how much a payer must pay at checkout when full payment is not required.

#### Minimum Deposit Override

**Minimum Deposit Override** replaces the minimum deposit for this form. Helper: **This will override the minimum deposits set for the sessions.**

#### Minimum Deposit Scope

**Minimum Deposit Scope** sets whether the override applies per user or for the entire checkout.

| Scope                     | Behavior                                                             |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **For Entire Checkout**   | One minimum deposit applies to the whole order.                      |
| **Per User**              | The minimum deposit applies separately to each user in the checkout. |

Use **Per User** when each registrant should pay their own minimum. Use **For Entire Checkout** when one combined deposit is enough.

#### Charge Deposit On Approval

**Charge Deposit On Approval** (in the **Approval deposit settings** box) prompts you to charge a deposit to a payment method on file when you manually approve a response. Helper text: **When you manually approve a response, you'll be prompted to charge a deposit to a payment method on file. Only applies to manual approvals.** Check **Prompt to charge a deposit when approving**.

Set **Default Deposit Amount** so the prompt is prefilled. Helper: **Prefilled when you approve a response. You can change the amount or skip the charge each time.**

When you approve, the **Charge Deposit On Approval** dialog lets you:

1. Pick a payment method on file (primary is preselected), or see a warning if none are on file.
2. Edit **Deposit amount**.
3. Choose **When to charge**: **Charge now**, or **On a date** with a date and optional time.
4. Confirm with **Charge & Approve** (or **Schedule & Approve** when you chose a date), **Skip charge & approve**, or **Cancel**.

Helper under **On a date**: **The form is approved now and the deposit is charged automatically at this date and time. Leave the time blank to charge just after midnight.** You can schedule for today if the time is still later than now.

Use this when you review registrations before charging families, but still want a deposit collected when you approve.

### Send reminder emails

Reminder emails follow up with recipients who still need to complete a form, before or after its due date. Helper under **Send Reminder Emails**: **Users with a form status of "in progress" or "not started" will receive an email asking them to complete the form.** A warning below it says: **Avoid using reminders on a primary registration form or on any form where a large audience - potentially thousands of people - could receive these emails.**

Who gets a reminder depends on each recipient's form status:

| Form progress status | Included in reminder audience |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Not Started**      | Yes                           |
| **In Progress**      | Yes                           |
| **Pending**          | No                            |
| **Approved**         | No                            |

Once a submission moves to **Pending** or **Approved**, Campium stops reminding that recipient.

Reminders work best for deadline-driven forms people complete after registration or onboarding, such as medical forms, waivers, contracts, and staff paperwork.

#### How reminders are enabled

There is no on/off switch for reminders. You turn them on by picking days in two lists:

- **Before due date**: helper **Days before the due date to email users who haven't submitted the form.** Each day you pick sends a "due soon" reminder. Leave it empty for no pre-due reminders.
- **After due date**: helper **Days after the due date to email users who still haven't submitted the form.** Each day you pick sends a past-due reminder. Leave it empty for no past-due reminders.

Picking at least one day in either list turns reminders on for the form. When you save, Campium schedules one reminder per day you picked, counted from the **Due Date**. Days that already fall in the past are skipped.

Use **Preview Before-Due Email** and **Preview Past-Due Email** to see each message. **Edit Wording**, next to each preview, opens **Admin → Automated Emails** at **Form reminder: due soon** or **Form reminder: past due**. See [Automated Emails](/automated-emails).

<Steps>
  <Step title="How to turn on form reminder emails" icon="mail" title-type="p">
    1. Open **Forms** and select the form you want to manage.
    2. Go to the **Settings** tab.
    3. Confirm the form has a recipient list configured in the **Recipients** tab.
    4. Enter a **Due Date**.
    5. Pick one or more days in **Before due date**, and, for past-due reminders, one or more days in **After due date**.
    6. Click **Save Settings**.
    7. Reminders go out on each scheduled day when the daily reminder job runs. Review completions on the **Responses** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The reminder message changes depending on timing. Days chosen in **Before due date** send a "due soon" message. Days chosen in **After due date** send an overdue or past-due message.

Campium sends reminder emails from a job that runs once a day, so a reminder never goes out the moment you save. Plan for the next run when you test.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Important limitations for reminder emails:

  - **Due Date is required**. Without one, Campium keeps the days you picked but schedules no reminders.
  - **Registration forms are skipped** by the reminder job, even when reminder days are set.
  - **Large audiences** mean a lot of email. Think twice before turning reminders on for very large groups.
  - **Recipients still matter**. Campium only reminds people the form applies to.
  - **Unsubscribes apply**. People who unsubscribed from form notifications do not get reminders.
</Callout>

Use reminder emails for:

- **Medical forms**
- **Waivers**
- **Contracts**
- **Staff paperwork**

Avoid reminder emails for:

- **Main registration** forms
- Forms with **thousands of recipients** unless you have confirmed the volume is appropriate

### Email settings

The **Email settings** box controls which staff get notified and which emails Campium sends when a response changes status.

#### Send Admin Email Notification To

Use **Send Admin Email Notification To** to pick the staff users who get an email when someone submits the form. Helper: **The selected users will receive an email with a summary of responses and a copy of the invoice.** The helper also notes that sending health-related answers this way means you can no longer say you are HIPAA compliant.

This notification goes to staff, not families.

#### Also Notify When Additional Sessions Are Added

**Also Notify When Additional Sessions Are Added** appears on registration forms. Turn on **Email the same people when extra sessions are purchased later** to email the people above when someone who already completed the form comes back and buys more sessions. That email lists only the new sessions. Leave it off to be notified only when the form is first completed.

#### Email Template When Approved, Pending, or Rejected

Use **Email Template When Approved**, **Email Template When Pending**, and **Email Template When Rejected** to choose the email Campium sends when a response changes to that status. Leave a template blank to send nothing. Each has a **Click here** link to manage templates on **Admin → Email Templates**.

These templates are most useful when staff review submissions and the recipient needs to hear the outcome.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Be careful with health-related or other sensitive forms. Make sure your notification and status emails do not include medical or private details that should not go by email.
</Callout>

### Custom text

The **Custom text** box changes labels and instructions families see on the Portal. **Thank You Message** and **Form Header Text** appear on registration and standard forms. The checkout button, session selection, and cart text appear only on **Registration (With Products)** forms. Upload forms have no custom text.

#### Payment Button Text For Checkout Page

**Payment Button Text For Checkout Page** (required) sets the label on the button families click to pay at checkout. Use a short action phrase, such as confirming registration or submitting payment.

#### Thank You Message

**Thank You Message** appears after the form is completed and in the invoice email the family receives. Use it to confirm the submission went through and say what happens next. When **Preview Confirmation Page** appears under the label, use it to see the message.

#### Form Header Text

**Form Header Text** adds text at the top of the registration form. Use it for deadline notes, brief instructions, or reminders about required documents.

#### Sessions Selection Header Text

**Sessions Selection Header Text** (the label uses your product wording) sets the text families see while they pick sessions or products.

#### Cart Header Text

**Cart Header Text** sets the text families see on the final checkout page.

Under the field label, click **Preview Cart Header** to see that text on the checkout page. The preview is labeled as a preview, shows sample checkout content below your header, and includes the **Parent view** checkout-rules strip so you can check payment-plan and paying-today rules without signing in as a parent.

### Ask for References

Turn on **Ask for References?** when an application needs other people to fill out a reference form for the applicant.

Set **Ask for References?** to **Yes**, then choose one **Reference Form**. Everyone the applicant names fills out that same form. Use **References Required**, **Where References Appear**, **Reference Instructions**, and **Remind References After** to control the count, placement, intro copy, and reminder cadence.

If a form still uses the older two-form setup, Settings shows a yellow notice and will not send new invitations until you pick a **Reference Form**.

Full steps for applicants, the portal **My References** page, and the profile **References** card are in [References](/references).

### Question display

#### Show Question Numbers

Set **Show Question Numbers?** to **Yes** to number each question (1, 2, 3). Numbers help on long compliance forms, contracts, and multi-part questionnaires. Leave it on **No** for short forms.

## Saving and warnings

**Save Settings** stores the Settings values and can change which tabs and warnings appear in the editor.

On the **Settings** tab, **Save Settings** sits in a sticky bar at the bottom of the form that stays in view while you scroll. Next to the button, Campium shows **Your settings save automatically.** Changing a Settings control saves on its own. Text fields save when you stop typing, without leaving the field. Those saves update **Saved just now.** / **Last saved a moment ago.** on the bar and skip the success toast. Errors still show a toast.

After you click **Save Settings**, Campium keeps you in the form editor and refreshes the availability badge. Check that the tabs, payment settings, and warnings match the form you meant to build.

### Save Settings behavior

- **Updates conditional fields and tabs** based on **Form Type**. (The form itself is created when you click **Create** in the **New Form** dialog.)
- **Keeps you in the editor** so you can continue with Recipients, Questions, Products Layout, or Responses.
- **Sends nothing**: saving does not email the form to anyone.
- **Blocks incomplete date-times** when **Start Date** or **End Date** has a date but no time.

### Yellow warning banners

Campium shows a yellow **Note:** banner at the top of the Settings tab when the form is not visible on the Portal.

| Banner text                                                                                                              | What to fix                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **This form is not visible on the Portal because the Visibility setting below is set to Hidden.**                       | Change **Visibility** to **Visible**.                                                                                                               |
| **This form is not visible on the Portal because today's date does not fall between the Start Date and End Date listed below.** | Update **Start Date** or **End Date**. On the Forms list, a future **Start Date** shows as **Scheduled** with an **Opens** line under **Status**. |

These banners explain why a form with correct recipients still does not show to anyone.

### Form Type controls which fields appear

Changing **Form Type** changes which tabs and settings Campium shows in the editor.

| Form Type                               | Fields and tabs you should expect                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Registration (With Products)**        | Settings, Recipients, Questions, Responses, **Products Layout**, payment, discount, deposit, and custom text settings  |
| **Standard (No Products for Purchase)** | Settings, Recipients, Questions, Responses, **Thank You Message** and **Form Header Text**, no checkout settings        |
| **Upload a File**                       | Settings, Recipients, Responses, upload-focused behavior, no Questions tab                                             |

If a field seems to be missing, check **Form Type** first.

## Quick setup checklists

Use the checklist that matches the kind of form you are building.

### Registration form (camper)

1. Set **Form Type** to **Registration (With Products)**.
2. Choose the correct **Season**.
3. Enter a clear **Form Name** and optional **Description**.
4. Set **Start Date** and **End Date**.
5. Set **Visibility** to **Hidden** while building.
6. Configure **Application Fee**, payment plan, deposit, and discount settings as needed.
7. Confirm **Default Payment To** is correct.
8. Set **Form Status After Completion** and **User Status After Completion**.
9. Add recipients in the **Recipients** tab.
10. Add questions and product layout details.
11. Save, test, then switch **Visibility** to **Visible**.

### Deadline-driven form with reminders (medical, waiver, contract)

1. Set **Form Type** to **Standard (No Products for Purchase)** unless file upload is the primary action.
2. Enter a clear **Form Name** and a short **Description** explaining the deadline.
3. Set **Start Date**, **Due Date**, and **End Date**.
4. Pick reminder days in **Before due date** and **After due date**.
5. Add the correct recipients.
6. Set **Lock Form After Completion** to **Yes** if responses should not change after submission.
7. Choose the right admin notification and approval templates.
8. Save the form and confirm reminder settings are still present.
9. Make the form **Visible** when you are ready to publish it.

### Simple survey (no payment)

1. Set **Form Type** to **Standard (No Products for Purchase)**.
2. Enter **Form Name**, **Description**, **Start Date**, and **End Date**.
3. Payment, discount, and deposit settings do not appear on this form type.
4. Decide whether **Lock Form After Completion** should be **Yes** or **No**.
5. Set **Form Status After Completion** to match whether staff review is needed.
6. Add survey questions in the **Questions** tab.
7. Assign recipients.
8. Save, review visibility, and publish.

## Troubleshooting

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Why can't families see my form on the Portal?" default-open="false">
    Check the four most common causes:

    - **Visibility** is set to **Hidden**.
    - Today's date is before **Start Date** or after **End Date**.
    - The family was not added through the **Recipients** tab.
    - The family is using the wrong Portal URL or account context.

    If all four are correct, open the form and read any warning banner on the Settings tab. For one family, run **Check Permissions** from their profile. See [Can't find a form on the parent portal](/user-cant-see-the-form-on-their-portal).
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What's the difference between Hidden and End Date?" default-open="false">
    | Setting      | Effect                                                                  |
    | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
    | **Hidden**   | Manually turns the form off regardless of dates                         |
    | **End Date** | Automatically stops showing the form after the availability window ends |

    Use **Hidden** for manual control. Use **End Date** for schedule-based availability.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Does Due Date hide the form?" default-open="false">
    No. **Due Date** shows a deadline on the Portal and schedules reminders. **End Date** is what removes the form.

    If the form should disappear after the deadline, set **End Date** to the deadline.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Will reminders go out for my main registration form?" default-open="false">
    No. You can pick reminder days on a registration form, but the reminder job skips **Registration (With Products)** forms. Use reminders on follow-up forms such as medical forms, waivers, contracts, and staff paperwork.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What's the difference between reminder emails and admin notification emails?" default-open="false">
    | Email type                    | Audience                                       | Trigger                          |
    | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
    | **Reminder emails**           | Recipients who still need to complete the form | Daily automated reminder process |
    | **Admin notification emails** | Staff administrators                           | Form submission activity         |

    Reminder emails nudge people who have not finished. Admin notification emails tell staff a submission arrived.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What's the difference between Pay In Full Upon Registration and Pay In Full Discount?" default-open="false">
    | Setting                           | Purpose                                     |
    | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
    | **Pay In Full Upon Registration** | Requires full payment                       |
    | **Enable Pay In Full Discount**   | Offers a discount for choosing full payment |

    One setting enforces payment timing. The other changes pricing.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I require staff approval before a registration counts?" default-open="false">
    Set **Form Status After Completion** to **Pending Approval**. That keeps the submission from being treated as approved immediately after the family submits it.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I automatically activate campers when they register?" default-open="false">
    Set **User Status After Completion** to **Active**. Campium marks the person active when they finish the form.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I prevent families from editing after they submit?" default-open="false">
    Set **Lock Form After Completion** to **Yes**. Use it for registrations, contracts, and other forms that should become a fixed record after submission.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can families opt out of form reminders?" default-open="false">
    Yes. People who unsubscribe from form notifications stop getting reminders, so not every recipient gets every reminder.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="A family says the form failed to save. What should they do?" default-open="false">
    Ask them to read the error at the top of the form. If it asks for Guardian 2's first and last name, have them add both names or clear the Guardian 2 fields. Otherwise, have them press **Try Again**, or refresh and continue. Their typed answers should still be on the form. If it keeps failing, ask them for the **Reference** code in the message and contact Campium support with that code.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
