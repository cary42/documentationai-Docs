Campers upload vaccine records the way they already do on forms. Campium reads the pages, lists doses for your health director to confirm or correct, and compares what is on file to your state's requirements.

Immunization is a paid add-on. Open it from the left navigation item **Immunization**. You need an **admin** login or the Immunization permission on your role.

## Preview before you buy

If your camp has not purchased immunization reading yet, **Immunization** opens a preview titled **Immunization records, read for you**. The sample roster is made-up data so you can see how the screen works.

### What it costs

On the preview:

- **Five cents per page read.** You pay for what gets read and nothing else. There is no minimum.
- Campium estimates a season total from how many campers are enrolled and a typical page count.
- Set **Stop after** to a dollar cap. Reading pauses when you reach it, and you can raise the limit later.
- Click **Turn on immunization reading**. You will be asked for a card or bank account to bill after the season.

Only an administrator can purchase. Staff with the Immunization permission can open the preview, but they see **An administrator needs to turn this on.** instead of the purchase form.

## Review campers and documents

After purchase, **Immunization** opens the review queue (default **Campers** view). Use **Documents** to work from uploaded files instead.

On **Campers**:

1. Choose a **Season**.
2. Filter by status: **All**, **Needs review**, **In process**, **Meets**, **No record on file**, or **Awaiting**.
3. Open **Review** on a camper who has a document.
4. Confirm or correct each dose. Campium presents the evidence; you make the compliance call.
5. Use **Export CSV** or **Recalculate all** when you need a download or a fresh pass over the season.

Usage tiles show **Used this season**, **Stops at**, **Left**, and **Expected for the season**. If reading is paused at your cap, use **Raise the limit**.

## Document page limit

Campium reads up to **5 pages** per uploaded immunization document. A file with more pages is not read and shows status **Over 5 pages, not read**. Ask the family for a shorter upload, or split the record, then review again.

## Setup checklist

If compliance is not fully configured, the queue can show **Finish setting up immunization compliance** with steps such as linking forms. Campers stay blank until those steps are done. Use the form links in the checklist, or open **Forms** to finish setup.
