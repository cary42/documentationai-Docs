## What the report shows

Use the Form Status Summary report to see which participants have completed the forms for a season, and which forms are still pending or not started.

The report lists one row per participant and one column for each form in the selected season, with a color-coded status in each cell.

## Permissions and access

The Form Status Summary report is in the **Misc** group of **Reports → System Reports**.

- You need the **Access To The Report Module** permission (in the **Reports** category).

- The report does **not** require financial permissions.

- The roster includes inactive participants as well as active ones. Anyone who already has a response to one of the season's forms stays on the report even if their account later became inactive.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  If a staff user cannot see the report in the Reports menu, review their permissions using the steps in [Allow a staff user to see a report](/allow-a-staff-user-to-see-a-report).
</Callout>

## Run the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Form Status Summary report" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">
    - Go to **Reports → System Reports** and, under **Misc**, choose **Form Status Summary**.

    The report opens for your default season, with the filters at the top of the page. The page title reads **Form Status Summary For** and the season name.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the season (required)" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    - Use the season picker to choose the season you want to review.

    After you pick a season, the page reloads with one column for each form in that season and one row for each person the forms apply to or who has a response.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Filter by session (optional)" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    - Use the session picker (placeholder **Select a Session**, using your product wording) to narrow the report to participants enrolled in one or more sessions.

    - Leave it empty to include everyone in the season.

    The report reloads after you change the selection.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Filter by sub-session (optional)" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    - First pick exactly one session.

    - Then use the sub-session picker (placeholder **Select a subproduct**) to narrow to one sub-session of that session.

    If you do not pick a sub-session, the report shows participants for every sub-session of the chosen session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Filters

These filters control which participants and forms appear in the table.

### Season (required)

- **What it does:** Chooses the season whose forms and participants you want to view.

- **Behavior:**

  - The report opens on your default season.

  - There is one form column for each form in the selected season that has recipients set, whether or not the form is open right now.

  - Participant rows include everyone each form applies to, plus anyone with a response to one of the forms, including inactive accounts.
  - The season list includes past and inactive seasons that other season pickers hide.

### Session (optional)

- **What it does:** Limits results to participants enrolled in the sessions you pick.

- **Behavior:**

  - When empty, shows everyone in the season.

  - You can pick more than one session. A participant appears if they have any of them.

  - When exactly one session is picked, the sub-session picker lists that session's sub-sessions.

### Sub-session (optional)

- **What it does:** Narrows results to one sub-session of the selected session.

- **Behavior:**

  - The picker appears after you pick a session, and lists choices only when exactly one session is picked. Otherwise it reads **Select one session first**.

  - When set, the report shows only participants with an active purchase of that sub-session.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  If the sub-session picker has no options, make sure you picked exactly one session first. Sub-session choices come from that session. A session with no sub-sessions shows **No subproducts available**.
</Callout>

## What you see in the table

The table shows one row per participant and one column per form in the season.

### Participant column

- **Name:** Displays as `Last name, First name`.

- **Profile link:** Click the name to open the participant's profile.

### Form columns

For the selected season, the report adds:

- One column for each form in that season that has recipients set.

- Each column header is the form name.

- Each cell shows the participant's status for that form as a word, with a background color.

If you add or remove forms for the season, the columns change the next time you run the report.

## Status colors and codes

Each form cell shows the participant's current status for that form as a word and a color.

- **Approved**

  - Color: **green**

  - Meaning: Staff have approved the form.

- **Pending Approval**

  - Color: **purple**

  - Meaning: The participant submitted the form and it is waiting for staff approval.

- **In Progress**

  - Color: **orange**

  - Meaning: The participant has started the form but has not submitted it.

- **Not Started**

  - Color: none (white cell with the words **Not Started**)

  - Meaning: The form applies to this participant, but they have not started it.

- **Rejected**

  - Color: none (white cell with the word **Rejected**)

  - Meaning: Staff rejected the form.

- **Blank cell**

  - Color: none, no text

  - Meaning: The form's **Recipients** settings do not include this participant.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  A cell with no text means the form does not apply to that participant. A participant who should fill out the form but has not started it shows **Not Started**.
</Callout>

## Exporting and working with columns

Use the buttons above the table to export what you see.

- **Copy:** Copy the table to your clipboard.

- **Excel** and **CSV:** Download the table for analysis or sharing.

- **PDF:** Download a landscape PDF of the table.

- **Print:** Open a print-friendly version.

Exports use your current filters (season, session, sub-session).

## Common uses

Use the Form Status Summary to:

- Identify families or participants who have not started or finished required forms.

- Follow up on forms that are **Pending** approval and need staff review.

- Confirm that all approved forms are in place before the start of a session.

- Track completion across many forms without opening each profile.

- Export a list of participants missing a particular form for targeted reminders or outreach.

For a roster of enrolled campers where you can change form statuses, set required forms, and send reminders, see [Camper Hub](/camper-hub).

## Troubleshooting and common questions

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Why do some participants not appear in the report?" default-open="false">
    - The report includes only people at least one of the season's forms applies to (through its **Recipients** settings), plus anyone who already has a response. Check the form's **Recipients** tab.

    - Check that you picked the right season.

    - If you filtered by session or sub-session, confirm the participant is enrolled in that session or sub-session.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Why is a form column missing?" default-open="false">
    - Confirm the form belongs to the season you picked. The form's **Season** is on its **Settings** tab.

    - Confirm the form has recipients set on its **Recipients** tab. A form with no saved recipients has no column.

    - If you recently created or changed a form, reload the report.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I tell if a blank cell means not started or not applicable?" default-open="false">
    - A participant who should complete the form but has not started it shows **Not Started**, not a blank cell.

    - A blank cell means the form's **Recipients** settings do not include that participant.

    - To find out why, open the participant's profile and click **Check Permissions**. See [Can't find a form on the parent portal](/user-cant-see-the-form-on-their-portal).
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I email families who have not completed a specific form?" default-open="false">
    Yes. Use this report to find who has not started or is still in progress on a form, then follow [Email users that have not completed a form](/email-users-who-have-not-completed-a-form) to send a reminder.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
