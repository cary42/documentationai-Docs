## Overview

Use the **Pickup Report** to see who each family has authorized to pick up a camper, which guardians are blocked from pickup (with the staff-only reason), and which families still have no authorized pickup people listed. Needs Attendance report access. Reminder emails also need the **Pickup Module** on under **Admin → Site Settings → Modules**.

Open **Reports → System Reports**. Under **Attendance**, open **Pickup Report**.

The report has three views:

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Authorized pickups** | People families listed as allowed to pick up each camper |
| **Blocked guardians** | Guardians marked **Not allowed** for pickup, with staff reason and history |
| **No pickups listed** | Families with a registered camper who has no authorized pickup person besides parents and guardians |

Use the season and session filters at the top for any view.

When the **Pickup Module** is on, the toolbar also shows **Remind everyone to check** (emails every family registered in the season to review their pickup list). When the module is off, reminder buttons stay hidden and the missing view explains that reminder emails are off because families cannot see **Pickup privileges** in the parent portal.

## Authorized pickups

Each row lists an authorized pickup person for a camper.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Camper | The camper being picked up (link to the profile) |
| **Pickup Name** | The person the family authorized |
| **Relationship** | How that person is related to the camper |
| **Phone** | Contact phone for the pickup person |

Select **Display Columns** above the table to add extra profile fields (cabin, group, bunk, and so on). Your selection is remembered.

<Callout kind="info">
  Authorized pickup rows appear after families have entered at least one authorized pickup person on their profile. See [Pickup privileges](/attendance#pickup-privileges).
</Callout>

## Blocked guardians

Click **Blocked guardians** to list guardians who may not pick up a camper in the season and sessions above. Helper text: **Guardians who may not pick up a camper in the season and sessions above. The reason is for staff only. Open a camper's history to see every change.**

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Camper | The camper the block applies to |
| **Guardian** | The guardian who is not allowed to pick up |
| **Reason** | Staff-only reason entered when pickup was turned off |
| **Turned off by** | Who recorded the block |
| **When** | When pickup was turned off |
| **History** | Link **Pickup history** to the camper's pickup change log |

Empty state: **No guardians are blocked from pickup.**

To turn pickup off or back on for a guardian, use **More → Pickup Info** on the profile. See [Block a guardian from picking up a camper](/attendance#block-a-guardian-from-picking-up-a-camper).

## No pickups listed

Click **No pickups listed** to see families who still need an authorized pickup person for a registered camper in the season and sessions above. Helper text explains that parents and guardians alone do not count as the authorized list for this view.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Family** | Family name |
| **Parents and guardians** | Linked contacts and emails. Badge **No parent email** when nobody has an email on file |
| Camper with no pickups | Campers who still need an authorized pickup person |
| **Last reminded** | When a pickup reminder last went out, or badge **Never** |

When the **Pickup Module** is on, select families (or use select all), then **Send reminder email**. That opens the shared group email flow with a default subject such as **Please add who can pick up your camper**. Families without an email address cannot be selected.

Empty state: **Every family has someone listed for pickup.**

## Filters

  <Step title="Season" icon="folder" titleType="p">

## Troubleshooting

- **No authorized pickup data yet**: Families have not entered any authorized pickup people. Ask them to add at least one on their profile, or use **No pickups listed** and **Send reminder email**.
- **A camper is missing from Authorized pickups**: Check the season and session filters, and confirm that family has entered a pickup person. Otherwise they appear under **No pickups listed**.
- **Blocked guardians is empty**: No guardians are marked **Not allowed** for the filters you chose. Blocks are set on **Pickup Info**.
- **Reminder buttons missing**: Turn on the **Pickup Module** under **Admin → Site Settings → Modules** so families can see **Pickup privileges** and you can send reminders.
