## Overview

Use the Pickup Privileges Report to see who each family has authorized to pick up their child, so staff can verify identity at pickup time.

<Callout kind="info">
  Data only appears once families have entered at least one authorized pickup person on their profile.
</Callout>

## What it shows

Each row lists an authorized pickup person for a camper.

<ParamField body="Camper" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The camper being picked up. This is a link to the full profile.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Pickup Name" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The name of the person the family has authorized to pick up the camper.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Relationship" param-type="text" show-location="false">
How the pickup person is related to the camper, for example parent, grandparent, or family friend.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Phone" param-type="text" show-location="false">
A contact phone number for the pickup person.
</ParamField>

## Filters

<Steps>
  <Step title="Season" icon="folder" titleType="p">
    Use the season dropdown in the header to focus on a single season, or leave it on all seasons.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Session" icon="filter" titleType="p">
    Narrow the report to campers in a specific session using the session dropdown below the season selector.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add profile field columns

Select **Display Columns** above the table to choose extra profile fields to show as columns, such as cabin, group, or bunk. Type in the filter box to find a field quickly, check the ones you want, and save. Your selection is remembered for next time.

## Where to find it

In Campium, go to **Reports** &gt; **System** and select the Pickup Privileges Report.

## Troubleshooting

- **No data yet**: Families have not entered any authorized pickup people. Ask them to add at least one on their profile.
- **A camper is missing**: Check the season and session filters, and confirm that family has entered a pickup person.
