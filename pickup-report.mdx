## Overview

Use the **Pickup Report** to see who each family has authorized to pick up a camper, and which guardians are blocked from pickup (with the staff-only reason). Needs the **Pickup Module** and Attendance report access.

Open **Reports → System Reports**. Under **Attendance**, open **Pickup Report**.

The report has two views:

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Authorized pickups** | People families listed as allowed to pick up each camper |
| **Blocked guardians** | Guardians marked **Not allowed** for pickup, with staff reason and history |

Use the season and session filters at the top for either view.

## Authorized pickups

Each row lists an authorized pickup person for a camper.

<ParamField body="Camper" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The camper being picked up. This is a link to the full profile.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Pickup Name" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The name of the person the family has authorized to pick up the camper.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Relationship" param-type="text" show-location="false">
How the pickup person is related to the camper, for example parent, grandparent, or family friend.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Phone" param-type="text" show-location="false">
A contact phone number for the pickup person.
</ParamField>

Select **Display Columns** above the table to add extra profile fields (cabin, group, bunk, and so on). Your selection is remembered.

<Callout kind="info">
  Authorized pickup rows appear after families have entered at least one authorized pickup person on their profile. See [Pickup privileges](/attendance#pickup-privileges).
</Callout>

## Blocked guardians

Click **Blocked guardians** to list guardians who may not pick up a camper in the season and sessions above. Helper text: **Guardians who may not pick up a camper in the season and sessions above. The reason is for staff only. Open a camper's history to see every change.**

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Camper | The camper the block applies to |
| **Guardian** | The guardian who is not allowed to pick up |
| **Reason** | Staff-only reason entered when pickup was turned off |
| **Turned off by** | Who recorded the block |
| **When** | When pickup was turned off |
| **History** | Link **Pickup history** to the camper's pickup change log |

Empty state: **No guardians are blocked from pickup.**

To turn pickup off or back on for a guardian, use **More → Pickup Info** on the profile. See [Block a guardian from picking up a camper](/attendance#block-a-guardian-from-picking-up-a-camper).

## Filters

<Steps>
  <Step title="Season" icon="folder" titleType="p">
    Use the season dropdown in the header to focus on a single season, or leave it on all seasons.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Session" icon="filter" titleType="p">
    Narrow the report to campers in a specific session using the session dropdown below the season selector.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

- **No authorized pickup data yet**: Families have not entered any authorized pickup people. Ask them to add at least one on their profile.
- **A camper is missing from Authorized pickups**: Check the season and session filters, and confirm that family has entered a pickup person.
- **Blocked guardians is empty**: No guardians are marked **Not allowed** for the filters you chose. Blocks are set on **Pickup Info**.
