## Overview

Use the Scheduled Payments Report to see all upcoming scheduled payments and their expected income so you can follow up with families and manage cash flow.

The report lists each scheduled payment with key contact details, amount, timing, and type, and lets you send targeted payment notifications directly from the results table.

<Callout kind="info">
  You need access to Reports to open and run the Scheduled Payments Report.
</Callout>

## Open the Scheduled Payments Report

Access the Scheduled Payments Report from the dashboard Payment Issues card (not from **Reports → System Reports**).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the report" icon="folder" titleType="p">
    On the dashboard, open the **Payment Issues** card and select **Scheduled Payments**. You can also follow a **Scheduled Payments** link from **Financials → Revenue Summary** when that stat is shown.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the page title" icon="check-circle" titleType="p">
    Check that the page title shows **Scheduled Payments Report** at the top. You should also see the **Expected Income** chart, filters, and the results table. The header also includes **Upcoming Charge Emails**, a **Preview Email** action, and a **Help** button. **Upcoming Charge Emails** opens the [Upcoming Charge Emails](/system-report-upcoming-charge-emails) report so you can see who will get each reminder. If there are no upcoming scheduled payments to preview, **Preview Email** still opens a sample version of the upcoming-charge email using your branding, a placeholder amount and date, and a placeholder card ending in **4242**, instead of an empty message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Filters and display settings

The Scheduled Payments Report includes season and payment-type filters, a month filter on the chart, and a column picker for profile fields.

### Season and Payment type

Use the season control and the **Payment type** dropdown in the header to narrow the list. Payment type options include **All Payments**, **Credit Card Only**, **ACH Only**, **No Account on File**, **Held by pending camper**, and **Cleared by a payment (last 7 days)**. Changes update the table and chart right away.

**Cleared by a payment (last 7 days)** lists missed charges that a later successful payment covered in the last week. Those rows can show a **Cleared by a payment** badge with the date.

### Held by pending camper

Open **Held by pending camper** from the dashboard **Payment Issues** card (with a count badge when there are any), or choose that option in **Payment type**.

Those rows wait when **Admin → Site Settings → Payment Info** has **Run scheduled payments normally when there is a pending enrollee** turned off, and every camper the installment pays for is still pending. A payment for an approved camper still runs even if a sibling is pending. They show a **Held by pending camper** badge, who they are **Waiting on**, plus **Approve camper** and **Open profile**. After you approve the camper, the row can show **Camper approved** / **Not charged yet** with **Charge now**. Approving a pending camper can also open **Charge the held payment?** (or **Charge the N held payments?**) with **Charge now**, **Remove it** / **Remove them**, or **Decide later**. Helper text: **Remove keeps the amount owed and moves it to the remaining payments.** You need **Sales** tab access and **Process Payments** for those charge actions. Held rows do not re-run on their own until you charge them.

Open ACH returns of plan charges can also appear on this report as display-only failed rows with an **ACH returned** badge. Their email icon opens the ACH return notice.

### Decline reminder timeline and Pause reminders

When **Send Email To User When Scheduled Payment Fails** is on under **Admin → Site Settings → Payment Info**, and the Automated Emails switch **Send it again 3 and 6 days later if the payment is still unpaid** is on for **Scheduled payment failed**, declined rows can show a reminder timeline such as:

- **Reminded** on a date, **next** on a later date
- **Reminded** on two dates
- **Next reminder** on a date
- **Reminders paused** on a date (alone or after an earlier reminder)

Use **Pause reminders** to stop the day-3 / day-6 follow-ups for that installment, or **Resume reminders** to turn them back on. You need **Process Payments** for those links. See [Automated Emails](/automated-emails#payment-and-receipt-notices).

### Profile Fields Display Settings

Use Profile Fields Display Settings to choose which profile fields you want to see as additional columns in the results table.

You configure these with the **Display Columns** control.

#### Display Columns

Choose one or more profile fields to include as extra columns in the results table. Selected fields appear as columns in the table, with each field name shown as its display name.

<Callout kind="info">
  Filters and profile field selections apply only to this report view. They control which scheduled payments you see and which columns appear; they do not change any underlying profile or payment data.
</Callout>

## Expected Income chart

At the top of the report, Campium shows a chart labeled **Expected Income** that summarizes upcoming payments by month.

#### Expected Income

See the income expected from the scheduled payments currently included by your filters. Click a month column on the chart to limit the table to payments due in that month. A banner shows **Showing payments due in** the month name; click **Show all months** (or click the same month again) to clear the filter. Use this to understand short-term cash flow based on pending scheduled payments.

## Results table columns

The main table lists one row per scheduled payment. Each row includes a blank Actions column, core contact and payment details, and any extra profile fields you selected.

### Core columns

#### Actions

Use this first column for row-level actions such as **Send Payment Notification**, **Retry**, and **Dismiss** when a declined scheduled charge is resolved (for example after a later successful payment covers what was owed).

#### Payment Since

**Payment Since** shows whether a later successful payment covers this missed charge:

- **Covered by $X on** a date (green check) with **Dismiss**. Tooltip: **Payments since this failed cover it.**
- **Partial: $X of $Y** (amber) with **Email parent**. Tooltip: paid since the failure, but not enough to cover it.
- No later payment: red mark / **No successful payment since**.

**Dismiss** appears only when the amount is fully covered. Confirm with **Dismiss this declined charge?** (or the ACH wording when the row is a return).

#### Name

Shows the primary person or student name associated with the scheduled payment. Click the **Name** header to sort by name.

#### Campers

Lists who each scheduled payment covers (your camp's wording for registrants, often **Campers**).

#### Phone

Shows the contact phone number pulled from the associated profile, used for follow-up if needed.

#### Guardian 1 Email

Shows the email address for the first guardian or primary contact on the profile tied to this scheduled payment.

#### Guardian 2 Email

Shows the email address for the second guardian or additional contact, if available.

#### Amount

Shows the scheduled payment amount for this row.

#### Season

Shows the season, term, or program label that the scheduled payment belongs to, such as Summer Session or Fall Season.

#### Type

Shows the payment method for this scheduled payment, such as credit card, ACH, or no account on file. This value matches the options in **Payment type**. Rows can also show chips **Expired** or **Expires before charge** when the on-file card has already expired or expires before that installment (within 45 days of the charge date).

#### Message

Shows any note or message associated with the scheduled payment, which may help explain why the payment was created or what it covers.

#### Date

Shows the scheduled date when this payment is expected to process.

### Dynamic profile field columns

In addition to the core columns, the table can show extra columns based on your Profile Fields Display Settings.

#### Dynamic profile field columns

For each profile field you select under **Select Profile Fields to Display**, the table adds a column using that field's display name. Each row shows the value of that profile field for the profile tied to the scheduled payment.

<Callout kind="info">
  Dynamic profile field columns update immediately when you change your selections. If you deselect a field, its column disappears from the table; selecting it again restores the column and values.
</Callout>

## Run the Scheduled Payments Report

Use these steps to configure and view the Scheduled Payments Report for your current needs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report page" icon="monitor" titleType="p">
    In Campium Money, go to **Reports** &gt; **System** and select **Scheduled Payments Report**. Confirm that the page title reads **Scheduled Payments Report** and the **Expected Income** chart appears above the table.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the Filter by Type value" icon="settings" titleType="p">
    Use the **Filter by Type** dropdown to choose which scheduled payment types you want to review. The table below updates automatically to show only payments that match your selection, and the **Expected Income** total recalculates to match.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose profile fields to display" icon="users" titleType="p">
    In the **Profile Fields Display Settings** section, use **Select Profile Fields to Display** to add or remove profile-related columns from the table. The helper message reminds you that selected fields appear as columns in the table below.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review expected income and rows" icon="bar-chart" titleType="p">
    Check the **Expected Income** chart to see the amount expected from the visible scheduled payments. Then review the table rows, including **Name**, **Guardian 1 Email**, **Guardian 2 Email**, **Amount**, **Season**, **Type**, **Message**, **Date**, and any dynamic profile fields.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the table and totals look correct, use the Actions column to send payment notifications if needed.

## Send payment notifications

From the Scheduled Payments Report, send a payment-related email for a specific scheduled payment using the **Send Payment Notification** modal.

### Open the Send Payment Notification modal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the payment row" icon="search" titleType="p">
    Scroll or filter the table to find the scheduled payment you want to email about. Confirm that the **Name**, **Amount**, and **Date** match the payment you intend to reference.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use the Actions column" icon="arrow-right" titleType="p">
    In the blank **Actions** column for that row, click the available action, such as a button or link, to open the **Send Payment Notification** modal for that specific scheduled payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Modal fields

The **Send Payment Notification** modal loads the real email for the type you pick so you can edit it before it goes out.

#### Email Type

Choose which notice to send. Options: **No Payment Account on File**, **Payment Issue/Error**, **Card Billing Address Did Not Verify** (Finix billing-address follow-up; steers the family to **Payment Methods** → **Edit**), **Payment Reminder**, and **ACH Payment Returned** (used for open ACH return rows). With **Site Settings** permission, **Edit this email** opens the matching row on [Automated Emails](/automated-emails#sent-by-you-from-scheduled-payments).

#### Subject and Message

Edit **Subject** and **Message** in the modal. Helper: **You can change any of the wording before you send it.** With **Site Settings** permission, a link can also say **edit your camp's default wording** and open [Automated Emails](/automated-emails) for that notice. Links in the message point families to update payment methods when that fits the notice type.

When the family has an active **Guardian 2** with a different email, check **Also send to** that person's name and email. The main send still goes to the account-holder email shown in the modal. Success can read **Email sent successfully to** both addresses. The Guardian 2 copy is logged too. In **Previous Emails Sent**, a dual send can show **Sent to** both addresses **on** the date and time.

A declined charge on this report can show a plain-language reason that looks like a link. Open it for next steps, then **Email parent** or **Text parent** when a cell number is on file (same actions as on a profile **Sales** decline).

<Callout kind="alert">
  Review the **Email Type**, **Subject**, and **Message** carefully before sending, especially for time-sensitive or high-value payments. If the account has no email, Campium asks you to add one on the profile first.
</Callout>

### Send the notification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure the email content" icon="edit" titleType="p">
    In the **Send Payment Notification** modal, select the appropriate **Email Type**, then edit **Subject** and **Message** as needed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send and confirm" icon="send" titleType="p">
    Click **Send Email**. After sending, close the modal and return to the table to continue reviewing other scheduled payments or sending additional notifications.
  </Step>
</Steps>
