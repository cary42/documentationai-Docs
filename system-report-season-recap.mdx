Use **Season Recap** when you want a short season summary without opening every chart report. It shows enrollments, payment mix, top sessions (or your product wording), recommended next steps, and links to the full chart reports.

## Where to find it

1. Open **Reports** in the left navigation.
2. Choose **System Reports**.
3. Under **Enrollment**, select **Season Recap**.

You can also open it from the shared chart reports button row on other chart pages (for example **Stats Dashboard** or **Registration Patterns Report**). The button label is **Season Recap**.

You need the **Reports** permission to open this page. The **Revenue** column and net booked amounts appear only when your role also includes finance access.

## Load a recap

1. Choose a **Season**. Campium selects the latest season by default.
2. Optionally turn on **Compare to prior season**, then pick a **Compare season**.
3. Click **Load recap**.

While it loads, the page shows **Loading…**. If no season is selected, Campium asks you to select one first.

## What you see

After the recap loads:

- Snapshot cards for **Campers**, **Families**, **Waitlist**, and **On a payment plan**. With finance access you also see **Net booked**. When you compare seasons, deltas show as **vs prior**.
- **When families registered** - busiest day and peak hour (Eastern) when enough activity exists.
- **How families paid** - share of successful ACH vs card payments, and dollar volume when you have finance access.
- **What sold** - a table of top sessions (or your product plural) with **Campers**, **Fill**, and **Revenue** (finance access only).
- **Recommended next steps** - short tips for your season.
- **Open the full report** - links to related chart reports for a deeper look.

If this recap was emailed before, a note shows when it last went out and how many administrators received it.

## Email the recap

1. Load a recap for the season you want.
2. Click **Email recap**.
3. Confirm **Email this recap to your administrators?** (or the re-send prompt if it was already emailed), then choose **Send email**.

Campium emails the recap to users with the **Administrator** role. You need the **Reports** permission to send.
