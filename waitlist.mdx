## What the Waitlist Report does

The Waitlist Report shows who is waiting for a full or closed session, so you can decide what to do when spots open up. Every wait-listed camper is in one list, with their details and the actions to invite, accept, or remove them.

## Where to find the Waitlist Report

You can open the Waitlist Report from the **Reports** menu or straight from a session's wait list count. The report and the wait list links appear only when the **Wait List Module** is on under **Admin → Site Settings → Modules**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open from Reports" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    - In the left menu, open **Reports → System Reports**.

    - Find **Wait List Management** in the **Enrollment** group.

    - Click the report name to open it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open from a session Wait link" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    - In the left menu, open **Seasons** and click the season name to open its **Sessions** step.

    - Find the session you want to review.

    - Click the number in the **Wait listed** column to open that session's wait list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open from an option or subproduct" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    - If your session has subproducts, open the session's **Subproducts** panel.

    - Find the option you want to review.

    - Click the number in its **Wait listed** column. The report opens filtered to the parent session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The **Wait listed** column shows on every session and subproduct while the **Wait List Module** is on, including sessions with nobody waiting.
</Callout>

## Choose what you are viewing

The page title reads **Waitlist For** followed by the season (and session, when you pick one). Use the dropdowns at the top of the page to narrow the list.

- **Season**: Pick the season you want to review, such as Summer 2025.

- **Session**: After you pick a season, choose one or more sessions.

When you change a dropdown, the page reloads to show only the campers waiting for what you picked.

## What you will see on the list

Each row on the Waitlist Report shows one wait-listed registration, oldest first.

The columns are:

- **User**: The camper, linked to their profile.

- **Status**: The current situation for that session:

  - **Full**: the session (or subproduct) has no open spots.

  - **Open Spot**: at least one space is open for someone on the wait list.

  - **Closed**: the session's **Status** is set to **Closed**, which sends every new registration to the wait list.

  - **Invite open until \[date]**: you sent an invitation and the family can accept until the listed date.

- **Session** (your term for sessions): The session the camper is waiting for.

- **# Waitlisted**, **# Subproduct Registered**, **# Total Registered**, **Subproduct Capacity**, and **Session Capacity**: How many campers are waiting, how many are enrolled, and the capacity, so you can see how tight the session is. Subproduct columns show `--` when the registration has no subproduct.

- **Season**, **Sibling** (Yes or No), and **Email** (the family's primary email).

- **Registration Date**: When the camper joined the wait list, so you can see who has been waiting longest.

To add profile fields as extra columns, click **Display Columns**, check the fields you want under **Profile Fields Display Settings**, and click **Save Profile Field Settings**.

## Common actions on the Waitlist Report

From the Waitlist Report you can reach out to families, invite campers into the session, and keep the list up to date.

### Email everyone on the list

Use **Send Email To All Wait Listers** to send the same message to everyone on the current wait list view (your **Season** and **Session** filters apply).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Email all waitlisted families" icon="mail" title-type="p">
    - Open the Waitlist Report and set your **Season** and **Session**.

    - Click **Send Email To All Wait Listers**.

    - The shared **Email this group** modal opens with recipient preview, Guardian 1 / Guardian 2 routing, and the full editor. Default subject is **Wait List Update** unless you change it.

    - Review who will receive the message, then click **Send Email**. Confirm with the sample message preview and **Yes, send it**. **Open in full editor** keeps this wait list audience locked in Mass Emails until you **Replace with a filter**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you do not see the button, confirm you have permission to send mass email, or compose from **Communications → Mass Emails** and filter to the same wait list group.

### Text everyone on the list

Use **Send Text To All Wait Listers** (next to the email button) when you want a short SMS for the same wait list view.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Text all waitlisted families" icon="message-square" title-type="p">
    - Open the Waitlist Report and set your **Season** and **Session**.

    - Click **Send Text To All Wait Listers**.

    - The modal title is **Text Waitlist**. Review how many families and phones are included, edit **Message**, then click **Send Text** and confirm with **Yes, send it**.

    - **Open in full editor** continues in **Communications → SMS**. See [SMS](/sms#text-families-from-a-report).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Get notified when someone joins a wait list

Use the **Email me when someone joins a wait list** checkbox in the report toolbar to opt in to a notification whenever a new camper is added to a wait list.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on wait list notifications" icon="bell" title-type="p">
    - On the Waitlist Report toolbar, find the **Email me when someone joins a wait list** checkbox.

    - Check the box to start receiving an email each time someone joins a wait list.

    - Uncheck it at any time to stop receiving those notifications.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  This opt-in controls notifications for your own account, so each admin who wants these emails needs to check the box for themselves.
</Callout>

### Accept or invite someone when a spot opens

When the status shows **Open Spot**, use the **Wait List** pop-up to accept, invite, or remove someone from the wait list.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Wait List pop-up" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    - Open the Waitlist Report for the season and session you want.

    - Find the camper you want to work with.

    - Click the arrow icon (tooltip **Accept / Reject User Into Session**) on that camper's row to open the **Wait List** pop-up.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose an action" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    - In the **Action** dropdown, choose **Add / Accept** when you are moving the camper into the session or sending them an invitation to accept a spot.

    - Choose **Remove / Reject** when you are taking the camper off the wait list because they are not attending.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Update the camper status" icon="users" title-type="p">
    - Use the **Change User Status (Current Status: ...)** dropdown if you want to update the camper's overall status, such as moving them to Active.

    - Leave it as is if you only want to change their wait list state.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set a response window" icon="clock" title-type="p">
    - In **Amount of time to allow user to respond**, pick how long the family has to reply. Helper text: **The user will be informed that they have a timeframe to accept or reject.**

    - Choose **No Limit** (the default) to accept the camper into the session now. Choose **1 Day** through **7 Days** to send an invitation instead.

    - With a response window, the camper stays on the wait list, the family receives an invite email, and the row's status shows **Invite open until \[date]** until they answer or the window ends.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview the email" icon="mail" title-type="p">
    - Under **Email to the family**, click **Preview Accept Email** to see the email a family gets when you accept them.

    - Click **Preview Invite Email** to see the version that invites them to claim an open spot.

    - To change the wording of these emails, go to **Admin → Automated Emails** (the pop-up links there).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Control who gets emailed and save" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    - When you accept with **No Limit**, the **Send email to guardians about acceptance.** checkbox is on by default. Leave it on to send the acceptance email, or turn it off to accept the camper without notifying guardians.

    - The checkbox is hidden when you choose a response window, because the invite email always goes out, and when you choose **Remove / Reject**.

    - Click **Save** to apply your choices, or **Close** to leave without changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Invite emails include **Accept Spot** and **No Thanks** buttons the family clicks to respond.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  When a family clicks **Accept Spot** or **No Thanks** in the invite email, their wait list entry updates based on their choice. Refresh the report to see the latest state.
</Callout>

### Cancel an outstanding invitation

When a camper has an invitation that has not been answered, a **Cancel Invitation** icon appears on their row. Use it to withdraw the open invite without taking the camper off the wait list.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cancel an open invite" icon="x-circle" title-type="p">
    - Find a camper whose status shows **Invite open until \[date]**.

    - Click the **Cancel Invitation** icon on that camper's row.

    - Confirm when asked. The open invite is withdrawn and the accept and decline links in the family's email stop working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  **Cancel Invitation** is different from **Remove**. Canceling only closes the outstanding invite and keeps the camper on the wait list, while **Remove** takes the camper off the wait list entirely. **Cancel Invitation** only appears while an invitation is still open.
</Callout>

### Remove someone from the waitlist

If a family tells you they are no longer interested, or you want to clean up old entries, remove the camper from the wait list.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Remove a camper from the waitlist" icon="trash" title-type="p">
    - Open the Waitlist Report and filter to the right season and session.

    - Find the camper you want to remove.

    - Click the trash icon (tooltip **Remove User From Wait List**) on that row.

    - Campium asks **Remove this user from the wait list?** Click **Remove** to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Removing someone permanently deletes their wait list entry and cannot be undone. If you add them again, they go to the end of the list, because the list is ordered by when each entry was created.
</Callout>

### Let families remove themselves

Turn on **Allow users to remove themselves from wait lists** under **Admin → Site Settings → Modules** when you want families to leave a wait list from the parent portal.

With that setting on, a wait-listed session on the parent home or **Account** page shows **remove from wait list** next to **Wait List**. The family confirms, then sees **Session removed from wait list.** If the spot is no longer wait-listed (for example staff already accepted or removed them), they see **This session is no longer on the wait list. Refresh the page.**

Families can remove only wait list spots this way, not enrolled sessions. See [Withdraw a camper](/withdraw-a-camper).

### Download or print the waitlist

The table has **Copy**, **Excel**, **CSV**, **PDF**, and **Print** buttons.

- Use **Excel**, **CSV**, or **PDF** to save the current wait list to a file you can work with outside Campium.

- Use **Print** to create a paper copy for staff meetings or offline review.

## Troubleshooting

### The report is empty

If the Waitlist Report shows no campers:

- Check that you picked the correct **Season**.

- Make sure you selected the right **Session**.

- Confirm that the session is full or its **Status** is **Closed**. Registrations only go to the wait list in those cases.

- Try another season or session to see if you have any waitlisted campers at all.

If the list is still empty and you expect names there, contact support to review your session settings.

### I cannot find the email button

If you do not see **Send Email To All Wait Listers**:

- Confirm that you are on the **Wait List Management** report, not another report. The button sits at the top of the table.

- Ask an account owner if your role has permission to send mass emails.

You can also go to **Communications → Mass Emails** and filter to the same season and session.

### An invited camper still shows on the waitlist

After you invite or accept a camper, you may still see them on the wait list.

This can happen when:

- You sent an invitation with a response window and the family has not answered yet.

- The camper has enrolled, but the page has not been refreshed.

Try these checks:

- Refresh the page to pull in the latest status.

- Look at the **Status** column to see if it shows **Invite open until \[date]**.

- Check the camper's profile **Sales** tab to confirm whether they are enrolled.

If the camper is enrolled but still on the wait list after a refresh, contact support to review the record.
