Welcome to Campium. Campium is an all-in-one platform for running your camp or program. You manage registration and forms, seasons and sessions, billing and payments, attendance, communication, health records, transportation, and much more from a single admin site, while families and staff use a connected parent portal and mobile app.

<Callout kind="info">
  New to Campium? Start with the [Quickstart](/quickstart). It walks you through creating a season, adding sessions, and building a registration form so families can start signing up.
</Callout>

## What you can do

Campium brings the day-to-day work of running your camp into one place:

- **Registration & forms** — build the forms families complete to enroll, sign waivers, and provide the details you need.
- **Seasons & sessions** — organize everything you offer into seasons, then sell sessions as purchasable products.
- **Billing & payments** — collect payments online, offer discounts, and let families pay over time with payment plans.
- **Attendance** — check campers in and out and keep an accurate daily roster.
- **Communication** — reach families by email, SMS, and push notifications.
- **Health & more** — track health details, medications, bunking, transportation, staff, and reporting.
