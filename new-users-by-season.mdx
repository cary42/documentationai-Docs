## Overview

Use this report to see the people who registered for the first time in the selected season. It helps you understand how many families are new to your camp and who to welcome.

## What it shows

Each row is a person who is new in the selected season.

<ParamField body="Name" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The person's name, linked to their profile.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Age Group" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The person's age group, when age groups are in use.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Sessions" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The sessions the person registered for.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Location" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The person's location, when shown.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Status" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The person's current status.
</ParamField>

## Choose the season

Use the season dropdown in the header to pick the season, or leave it on all seasons.

## Where to find it

In Campium, go to **Reports** &gt; **System** and select the new registrations by season report.

## Troubleshooting

- **No one listed**: No first-time registrations exist for the selected season yet. Once someone registers for this season, they appear here.
- **A returning family shows up**: This report counts people new in the selected season; someone new to this season may still have history in other seasons.
