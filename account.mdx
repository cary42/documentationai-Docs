## Account overview

Use the Campium account section to manage your own login, security, and communication preferences. All settings here apply only to your staff account.

You can access these pages from the admin navigation:

- Click your name in the top right, then click **Settings**.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Changes you make in the Account section affect only your user login and messages. They do not change other admins or family accounts.
</Callout>

---

## Account Settings

Account Settings at `/account/` cover:

- Changing your password

- Choosing your default season

- Turning two-factor authentication (2FA) on or off

- Managing passkeys

- **Email Notifications** (optional staff digests and alerts)
### Change your password

Use this form when you know your current password and want to update it.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  If you do not remember your current password, use the reset-password link on the login page instead of the in-account change form.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the password form" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    - Go to `/account/` from the admin navigation.

    - Find the **Change Password** section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your current and new password" icon="lock" title-type="p">
    - Enter your **current password** in the `Old password` field.

    - Enter your **new password** in the `New password` field.

    - Re-type the same new password in `Confirm password`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review password requirements" icon="info" title-type="p">
    - Make your password long and unique to Campium.

    - Use a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters.

    - Avoid common phrases, names, or passwords you use on other sites.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and confirm" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    - Submit the form to save your new password.

    - If the current password is correct and the new passwords match, you see a success message and your new password takes effect immediately.

    - If there is an error (mismatch or invalid current password), fix the highlighted fields and submit again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  After changing your password on a shared computer, sign out of Campium when you are done.
</Callout>

### Set your default season

Your default season controls which season Campium shows first in many admin screens (for example, financials or reporting). You can still switch seasons on the reports; this setting just picks your starting point.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open default season setting" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    - Go to `/account/`.

    - Locate the **Default Season** dropdown.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your preferred season" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    - Open the dropdown to see the available seasons.

    - Select the season you work in most often (for example, `Summer 2025`).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your choice" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    - Save or apply the change (the page may auto-save when you choose a season, depending on your setup).

    - On pages that support it, Campium now opens with this season pre-selected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Changing your default season affects only what you see when you first open pages. It does not change the current season for families or your public portal.
</Callout>

### Manage two-factor authentication (2FA)

Two-factor authentication adds a second check at sign-in using your mobile phone. When enabled, you enter your password and then confirm a code or prompt sent to your phone.

2FA sessions last **about 12 hours**. During that time, you usually do not need to re-enter a code on the same browser. Standard non-2FA sessions are shorter (about 90 minutes).

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Use 2FA on accounts with access to payment data, refunds, or staff scheduling to reduce the risk from stolen passwords.
</Callout>

#### Requirements for 2FA

- A **mobile phone number** on your user profile

- Access to that device when you sign in

- Ability to receive calls or messages, depending on your 2FA method

If your account does not have a mobile number, the 2FA section shows a warning and you cannot turn it on until you add one.

#### Turn 2FA on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm your mobile number" icon="users" title-type="p">
    - Go to `/account/`.

    - In the **Two-Factor Authentication** section, verify that your mobile number is displayed and correct.

    - If the number is missing or wrong, update your user profile first, then return to this page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable two-factor authentication" icon="shield" title-type="p">
    - In the 2FA section, switch the **Two-Factor Authentication** toggle to **On**.

    - Follow any on-screen prompts to confirm the change.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in with 2FA" icon="lock" title-type="p">
    - On your next sign-in, enter your email and password as usual.

    - Complete the second step sent to your phone.

    - After you finish, Campium keeps your session active for about 12 hours on that browser before asking for 2FA again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  If you sign in from a new browser or device, Campium asks for 2FA again, even if your 12-hour window is still active on another device.
</Callout>

#### Turn 2FA off

Only disable 2FA if you are changing devices or have another strong security control in place.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open 2FA settings" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    - Go to `/account/`.

    - Scroll to the **Two-Factor Authentication** section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Disable the toggle" icon="minus" title-type="p">
    - Switch the 2FA toggle to **Off**.

    - Confirm the change if prompted.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify access" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    - Sign out and sign back in to confirm that you are no longer asked for a 2FA step.

    - If you did not intend to disable 2FA, return to `/account/` and turn it back on immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  If you lose access to your mobile phone and cannot complete 2FA, contact an internal Campium admin or support channel defined by your organization to regain access.
</Callout>

### Passkeys

Passkeys let you sign in with the same face, fingerprint, or device PIN you use to unlock your phone or computer, instead of typing a password. They are built on the WebAuthn standard and are tied to your device, so there is no code to remember or phish.

You manage passkeys from the **Passkeys** tab in your account settings.

#### Add a passkey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Passkeys tab" icon="key" title-type="p">
    - Click on your name in the top right and then click on **My Settings.**

    - Open the **Passkeys** tab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the passkey" icon="plus" title-type="p">
    - Choose **Add a passkey**.

    - Follow your browser or device prompt to confirm with your fingerprint, face, security key, or device PIN.

    - The new passkey is saved and appears in your list of passkeys.
  </Step>
</Steps>

#### List and remove passkeys

The Passkeys tab shows each passkey you have registered so you can review them across your devices.

- Review the list to confirm which devices can sign in with a passkey.

- Use the **Remove** action next to a passkey to delete it, for example when you replace or stop using a device.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Register a passkey on more than one device so you are not locked out if one device is lost or unavailable.
</Callout>

#### Require a passkey to sign in

Turn on **Require a passkey to sign in** to make your login password-less. With this setting on, Campium no longer accepts your password for sign-in; you complete every sign-in with a passkey instead. Google and Apple sign-in remain available on your account.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Before you turn on **Require a passkey to sign in**, register a passkey on at least one device you reliably have access to. If you lose access to all of your passkeys, use Google or Apple sign-in, or contact an internal Campium admin to regain access.
</Callout>

### Email Notifications

Use the **Email Notifications** tab to choose which optional staff emails you receive. Messages go to the email address on your staff account. Login, tickets, and billing emails always send.

Which types appear depends on your role and permissions. If a type is turned off for your organization, you see **This email is turned off for your organization.** and cannot enable it yourself.

#### Open Email Notifications

1. Click your name in the top right, then click **Settings**.
2. Open the **Email Notifications** tab.

Administrators can also open camp-wide defaults from **Admin → Email Notifications**, or from the link on this tab.

#### Optional email types

| Type | What it covers | How you control it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Activity digest** | Summary of registrations, logins, emails sent, revenue, payments, and discounts | Check **Daily**, **Weekly**, and/or **Monthly** |
| **Payment health digest** | Families with upcoming charge issues, open ACH returns, or a balance and no primary payment method | Toggle **Email me this digest** |
| **Form opening digest** | The day before a form’s **Start Date**, a summary of which forms become available on the Portal (Administrators only) | Toggle **Email me this digest** |
| **ACH returns digest** | Open ACH returns for your active seasons | Set **Frequency** to **Off**, **Daily**, or **Weekly (Mondays)** |
| **Wait list signup alerts** | An email when someone new joins a wait list | Toggle **Email me when someone joins a wait list** |
| **Campium product emails** | Optional product and announcement emails from Campium | Toggle **Email me Campium product updates** |

Activity digest schedules:

- **Daily** - Sent every morning covering the previous day.
- **Weekly** - Sent Sunday mornings covering the prior Sunday through Saturday.
- **Monthly** - Sent the 1st of each month covering the prior calendar month.

If you enable more than one Activity digest frequency, you receive a separate email for each schedule.

#### Save and preview

1. Adjust the toggles or frequencies you want.
2. Click **Save Preferences**.
3. Use **Send me a sample** under any type to send that email to your own address so you can see what it looks like before you rely on the schedule.

#### Turn a type off for everyone

Administrators can turn optional staff email types on or off for the whole organization:

1. Go to **Admin → Email Notifications**.
2. Use the switch next to each type (**Activity digest**, **Payment health digest**, **Form opening digest**, **ACH returns digest**, **Wait list signup alerts**, **Campium product emails**).
3. Click **Save**.

When a type is on for the organization, each person still chooses their own preferences under **Settings → Email Notifications**. Login, tickets, and billing emails always send.

#### Preview an email before you turn it on

Every optional type has a preview button that sends that email to your own address. Nothing is sent to anyone else.

| Where | Button | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Settings → Email Notifications** | **Send me a sample** | Available for any type your account is eligible for. If the type is turned off for your organization, the sample is blocked too. |
| **Admin → Email Notifications** | **Send test** | Administrators only, and it works even while the type is switched off for everyone, so you can read the content before deciding to turn it on. |

A sample uses your real data when there is any to show. When there is nothing to report, it falls back to clearly labeled placeholder rows such as **Sample Family** or **Sample Registration**, so the preview always shows you the finished layout.

Keep your account settings up to date and encourage other admins with sensitive access to:

- Use strong, unique passwords

- Turn on two-factor authentication

- Choose **Email Notifications** schedules that match how closely they monitor activity
