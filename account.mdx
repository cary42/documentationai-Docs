## Account overview

Use the Campium account section to manage your own login, security, and communication preferences. All settings here apply only to your staff account.

You can access these pages from the admin navigation:

- Click your name in the top right, then click **Settings**.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Changes you make in the Account section affect only your user login and messages. They do not change other admins or family accounts.
</Callout>

---

## Account Settings

Account Settings cover:

- Changing your password
- Choosing your default season
- **Name Format** for how names appear in reports (for you)
- **Sign-in security** (two-step sign-in, recovery codes, remembered browsers, passkeys)
- **Email Footer** (your own footer when you send from your address)
- **Email Notifications** (optional staff digests and alerts)

### Email Footer

Use the **Email Footer** tab to set wording that appears at the bottom of emails you send from your own address.

1. Click your name in the top right, then **Settings**.
2. Open **Email Footer**.
3. Edit **Your footer** in the HTML editor.
4. Click **Save Footer**.

Helper text explains that this footer is used instead of your camp's footer when you send from your own address, that email sent from your camp's address keeps the [camp footer](/automated-emails), and that leaving it blank uses the camp footer everywhere. Profile one-off emails and other staff sends follow the same From-address rule.

### Change your password

Use this form when you know your current password and want to update it.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  If you do not remember your current password, use the reset-password link on the login page instead of the in-account change form.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the password form" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    - Go to `/account/` from the admin navigation.

    - Find the **Change Password** section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your current and new password" icon="lock" title-type="p">
    - Enter your **current password** in the `Old password` field.

    - Enter your **new password** in the `New password` field.

    - Re-type the same new password in `Confirm password`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review password requirements" icon="info" title-type="p">
    - Make your password long and unique to Campium.

    - Longer is stronger. A short phrase of a few unrelated words beats a complicated short password. Uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols are optional.

    - Avoid common phrases, names, or passwords you use on other sites.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and confirm" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    - Submit the form to save your new password.

    - If the current password is correct and the new passwords match, you see a success message and your new password takes effect immediately.

    - If there is an error (mismatch or invalid current password), fix the highlighted fields and submit again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  After changing your password on a shared computer, sign out of Campium when you are done.
</Callout>

### Set your default season

Your default season controls which season Campium shows first in many admin screens (for example, forms, financials, and reporting). You can still switch seasons on individual pages; this setting picks your starting point.

#### From the left sidebar

On every admin page, the left sidebar includes a **Season** control above the navigation. On a desktop, hover it for: **This is the default season when you go to reports, forms and other pages.** Choose a season from the list to save it as your default.

On a phone or tablet, tap **Season** once to open your device's season list. The first tap opens it (no hover tip in the way).

If Campium picked a season for you because you had not saved one yet, a hint under the control says **Picked for you. Choose one to save it.** A first-run banner can also appear: **Season - you have not picked a season yet, so we are showing you [season name]. Pick the one you are working in and your forms and reports will open to it.** Use the banner's season list and **Save**, or dismiss it with **Not now** (the × control).

When the sidebar is collapsed to the rail, use the calendar button (**Change your season, currently [season name]**) to open the season picker.

#### From Account settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open default season setting" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    - Open your name menu and choose **Account**, or go to `/account/`.

    - Open the **Default Season** tab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your preferred season" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    - Open the dropdown to see the available seasons.

    - Select the season you work in most often (for example, `Summer 2025`).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your choice" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    - Save or apply the change (the page may auto-save when you choose a season, depending on your setup).

    - On pages that support it, Campium now opens with this season pre-selected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Helper text on the Account tab matches the sidebar purpose: the season selected when you go to profiles, financial reports, forms, and other pages throughout the site.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Changing your default season affects only what you see when you first open pages. It does not change the current season for families or your public portal.
</Callout>

### Name Format

Choose how camper and guardian names appear in reports and exports for your login only.

1. Click your name in the top right, then **Settings**.
2. Open the **Name Format** tab.
3. Under **Show names in reports as**, pick **Use camp setting (First Last)** or **Use camp setting (Last, First)** (whichever your camp uses), **First Last (Jane Smith)**, or **Last, First (Smith, Jane)**.
4. Click **Save Name Format**.

Helper text: applies to camper and guardian names in reports and exports, for you only. **Last, First** also sorts by last name. Mailing labels and parent emails are unchanged.

Camp-wide default lives under **Admin → Site Settings → Basic → Name Format in Reports**. See [Site Settings](/settings).

### Sign-in security

**Sign-in security** is the tab for two-step sign-in, recovery codes, remembered browsers, and passkeys. It replaces the older separate **Two-Factor Authentication** and **Passkeys** tabs.

Two-step sign-in applies to **campiumdb.com** staff accounts only. The parent portal does not use two-step sign-in.

#### Who needs two-step sign-in

Staff with payment, refund, or health permissions are asked to set it up. That includes roles that can process payments, use finance features, or open medical information, medications, or immunization. Super users are always covered.

One enrollment covers every camp you can sign into with the same account.

Until **November 25, 2026**, you can still sign in without finishing setup. A banner reminds you to set it up by that date to keep access to payments and health records. In the last few days before the cutoff, the banner stays until you finish. From **November 25, 2026**, covered staff who have not enrolled are held at the setup wizard until they complete it.

#### Open Sign-in security

1. Click your name in the top right, then **Settings**.
2. Open the **Sign-in security** tab.

You see heading **Two-step sign-in** and helper text: after your password, Campium asks for a second step (a passkey, a code from an authenticator app, or a code by text message).

Status badges include **Two-step sign-in is on**, **Text message only**, or **Not set up**. When you still have time before the cutoff, a grace note can say to set it up by the date to keep access to payments and health records.

#### Add a method

1. Click **Add a method** to open the setup wizard.
2. Choose a method:
   - **Passkey** (recommended when your browser supports it): your fingerprint, face, or device PIN
   - **Authenticator app**: Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, and similar apps
   - **Text message** under **Other options** (labeled less safe). Super users are not offered text message as their only method
3. Follow the on-screen steps for that method.
4. If you chose **Passkey** and you do not already have a text message or authenticator app, the wizard opens **Add a backup** before you finish. See [Add a backup for your passkey](#add-a-backup-for-your-passkey).
5. On **Save your recovery codes**, download or copy the codes, then confirm **I saved them** (or **I saved these codes**) and finish.

The methods table lists **Method**, **Added**, and **Last used**. You can add more than one method later from the same tab.

#### Add a backup for your passkey

A passkey often stays on the computer where you created it. Campium asks for a **Text message** or **Authenticator app** backup so you can still sign in on a phone or another computer.

- After you create a passkey with no backup yet, the setup wizard shows **Add a backup**. Helper text: **Your passkey may only work on the computer where you created it. Add a text message or authenticator app so you can sign in on your phone or another computer.**
- Choose **Text message** (badge **Easiest**) or **Authenticator app**, then finish that method's steps.
- If you already have a passkey and no backup, the next successful sign-in holds you on that same **Add a backup** step until you add one.
- You cannot remove your last text message or authenticator app while a passkey remains (when two-step policy covers your account). You also need a backup before you can generate recovery codes.

#### Recovery codes

Use a recovery code if you lose your phone or passkey. Each code works once. The tab shows how many of **10** are left. Click **Generate new codes** when you need a fresh set, then **Download** or **Copy**. Generate codes only after you have a text message or authenticator app backup for your passkey.

#### Remembered browsers

On the sign-in challenge after your password, you can check **Remember this browser for 30 days** (checked by default). That browser skips the second step for 30 days.

On **Sign-in security**, **Sign out all browsers** clears every remembered browser for your account.

#### Require a passkey to sign in

Turn on **Require a passkey to sign in** to stop password sign-in for your account. Google and Apple sign-in still work. You need at least one passkey before you can turn this on.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Before you turn on **Require a passkey to sign in**, register a passkey on at least one device you reliably have access to, and add a **Text message** or **Authenticator app** backup. If you lose access to all of your passkeys, use a backup method, Google or Apple sign-in, or contact Campium support to regain access.
</Callout>

#### Sign-in challenge

After your password (or Google sign-in, when two-step is on), Campium asks for the second step. Other first-step options (**Sign in with a passkey**, **Sign in with Google**, **Sign in with Apple**) stay hidden on this challenge screen.

- If you have a passkey and this browser supports it, the challenge leads with **Use a passkey**. Subtitle: **Confirm with a passkey**. Helper text explains that passkeys often stay on the computer where you created them, and that on a phone or another computer you should use a text message or authenticator app instead.
- The primary button is **Use a passkey** or **Verify** (for a code). Alternate methods appear as full-width buttons below it: **Use your authenticator app**, **Text me a code**, **Use a passkey**, and **Use a recovery code**. For a text code, use **Verify** and **Send a new text**.
- Leave **Remember this browser for 30 days** checked (it stays available when a passkey leads) so this browser can skip the second step for 30 days.

#### Require two-step for all staff

Administrators can widen coverage beyond payment, refund, and health roles:

1. Go to **Admin → Roles & Permissions**.
2. Turn on **Require two-step sign-in for all staff**.
3. Helper text: staff with payment, refund, or health permissions always need it. Turn this on to ask everyone with a staff role here.

When you grant a covered permission to a role, Campium can warn that everyone with that role will be asked to set up two-step at their next sign-in.

### Email Notifications

Use the **Email Notifications** tab to choose which optional staff emails you receive. Messages go to the email address on your staff account. Login, tickets, and billing emails always send.

Which types appear depends on your role and permissions. If a type is turned off for your organization, you see **This email is turned off for your organization.** and cannot enable it yourself.

#### Open Email Notifications

1. Click your name in the top right, then click **Settings**.
2. Open the **Email Notifications** tab.

Administrators can also open camp-wide defaults from **Admin → Email Notifications**, or from the link on this tab.

#### Optional email types

| Type | What it covers | How you control it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Activity digest** | Summary of registrations, logins, emails sent, revenue, payments, and discounts | Check **Daily**, **Weekly**, and/or **Monthly** |
| **Payment health digest** | Families with upcoming charge issues, open ACH returns, or a balance and no primary payment method | Toggle **Email me this digest** |
| **Form opening digest** | The day before a form’s **Start Date**, a summary of which forms become available on the Portal (Administrators only) | Toggle **Email me this digest** |
| **ACH returns digest** | Open ACH returns for your active seasons | Set **Frequency** to **Off**, **Daily**, or **Weekly (Mondays)** |
| **Wait list signup alerts** | An email when someone new joins a wait list | Toggle **Email me when someone joins a wait list** |
| **Campium product emails** | Optional product and announcement emails from Campium | Toggle **Email me Campium product updates** |

Activity digest schedules:

- **Daily** - Sent every morning covering the previous day.
- **Weekly** - Sent Sunday mornings covering the prior Sunday through Saturday.
- **Monthly** - Sent the 1st of each month covering the prior calendar month.

If you enable more than one Activity digest frequency, you receive a separate email for each schedule.

#### Save and preview

1. Adjust the toggles or frequencies you want.
2. Click **Save Preferences**.
3. For any type that shows **Send me a sample**, use that button to email a sample to your staff address (when the type is available and not turned off for your organization). The sample subject starts with `[Sample]`.

#### Turn a type off for everyone

Administrators can turn optional staff email types on or off for the whole organization:

1. Go to **Admin → Email Notifications**.
2. Use the switch next to each type (**Activity digest**, **Payment health digest**, **Form opening digest**, **ACH returns digest**, **Wait list signup alerts**, **Campium product emails**).
3. Optionally click **Send test** on a type to preview that email at your address (works even when the type is turned off for the organization).
4. Click **Save**.

When a type is on for the organization, each person still chooses their own preferences under your name (top right) → **Settings → Email Notifications**. Login, tickets, and billing emails always send.

Keep your account settings up to date and encourage other admins with sensitive access to:

- Use strong, unique passwords
- Finish **Sign-in security** / two-step sign-in by the cutoff if they handle payments or health
- Choose **Email Notifications** schedules that match how closely they monitor activity
