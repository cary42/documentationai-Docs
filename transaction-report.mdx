Open **Financials → Transaction Report** in the left menu.

## What the Transaction Report shows

Use the Transaction Report to list the **successful credit card payments** recorded for a season, one row per payment. Each row gives the processor's transaction ID, so you can match payments in Campium against your card processor's statements.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  This item appears only for camps that have it turned on. Contact Campium support if you want it. You also need the **Access To financial reports and financials in profiles** permission (in the **Financial** group on **Admin → Roles & Permissions**), and the **Financials** menu is hidden on the free plan.
</Callout>

### How it differs from Transaction Detail

The [Transaction Detail report](/transaction-detail) has one row for each **session added to an account**, with its cost, revenue, payments split by method, and balance. The Transaction Report has one row for each **credit card payment**. It does not include checks, cash, ACH, or other payment types, and it does not show sessions or balances.

## Filters

<ParamField body="Season" param-type="selector" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Use the season picker at the top of the page to choose the season. The page title, **Transaction Report for** followed by the season name, updates to match. The report covers one season at a time; if no single season is chosen, it uses your camp's default season.
</ParamField>

There is no date range filter. To review a specific window, export the rows and filter by the **Date** column.

## Columns

<ParamField body="Date" param-type="date" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The date and time the payment was recorded.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Last Name" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The last name recorded on the payment.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Amount" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The payment amount. The footer shows the total of all payments listed.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Transaction ID" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The transaction ID returned by your card processor.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Family ID" param-type="number" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The family account the payment belongs to.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Budget Code" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The name of the season the payment was recorded against.
</ParamField>

## Run the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the season" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    Choose the season with the season picker at the top of the page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Match against your processor" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Use the **Transaction ID** column to find each payment in your card processor's records.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Export" icon="download" title-type="p">
    Use the table export buttons to copy or download the rows.
  </Step>
</Steps>
