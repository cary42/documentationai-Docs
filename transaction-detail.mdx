## What the Transaction Detail report shows

Use the Transaction Detail report to audit money at the line level. It lists **one row each time a session is added to an account**, along with what that session cost, the revenue recorded, how much was paid by each method, and the balance still owed.

Where the Revenue Summary rolls totals up by product and payment type, this report keeps every session line separate, so you can reconcile individual charges and trace a specific family's activity.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Each row represents a session added to an account within your selected date range, not a single payment. A family that registers for several sessions appears on several rows.
</Callout>

## Filters and date range

Set the scope at the top of the report with the season picker and the date range.

<ParamField body="Season" param-type="selector" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Season or program group whose sessions you want to review. The report title updates to match your selection.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Date Range" param-type="date range" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Start and end dates that limit which session lines are included, based on when each session was added to the account.
</ParamField>

## Columns

Each row breaks a session line into cost, revenue, payments by method, and balance.

<ParamField body="Date" param-type="date" required="true" deprecated="false">
  When the session was added to the account.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Family ID" param-type="number" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Identifier for the family account the session belongs to.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Name" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The camper (or other person type) the session is registered to. Links to that profile.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Grade" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  The person's grade, when on file.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Primary Name" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The primary contact on the family account.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Session" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The session or product that was added.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Cost" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The list price of the session before discounts.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Revenue" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The revenue recorded for the line after discounts.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Refunds" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Amounts refunded against the line.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Check / Credit Card / CC Surcharge / ACH / Cash / Other" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Payments applied to the line, split by method. **CC Surcharge** is the card surcharge collected; **Other** covers manual or miscellaneous payment entries.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Total" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Total paid toward the line across all methods.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Balance" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  What still remains owed on the line.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  A few columns appear only when your account uses them, such as **Student ID**, **Plan Name** (for payment-plan lines), and **Subproduct** (for add-ons under a parent session).
</Callout>

## Choose which columns to show

Select **Display Columns** above the table to open a checklist of every column. Toggle any column on or off, then **Save Display Columns** to store the view for your account so it loads the same way next time.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Turn off payment-method columns you do not use to make the table easier to scan and export.
</Callout>

## Run the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the season" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    Choose the season or program group whose session lines you want to review.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set a date range" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Enter the start and end dates for the reconciliation window you care about.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and export" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">
    Scan the rows, adjust the visible columns if needed, and use the table export buttons to copy or download the data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Looking for a simpler list of individual payment transactions (date, name, amount, transaction ID, family ID, and budget code) rather than session lines? Your account may also offer a **Transaction report** view for that.
</Callout>
