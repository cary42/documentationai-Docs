## How families pay, and how you record the rest

Payment methods are the ways money lands on a family account. Families pay online with a card or, when you allow it, a bank account. You record cash and check yourself when someone pays at the office.

Which options appear at checkout depends on **Admin → Site Settings → Payment Info**, and on the [payment processor](/payment-processors) your camp uses.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Families never enter cash or a paper check during online checkout. Those methods exist so you can record a payment that already happened in person.
</Callout>

## Methods families use at checkout

At registration checkout, canteen add-funds, and other parent-portal payments, families choose from the electronic methods you have turned on.

| Method               | When it appears                                                                                            |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Credit card**      | Always available for online checkout. Visa, MasterCard, and Discover do not have a separate on/off switch. |
| **American Express** | Only when **Accept AMEX** is on.                                                                           |
| **ACH / e-check** (or **Bank account (PAD)** / **Pay by PAD** when your camp is set up for Canada on Finix) | Only when **Accept ACH Payments** is on. Families enter a bank account, or pick a saved one. US camps use routing and account numbers. Canada Finix camps use **Transit number** and **Institution number**, with a **Pre-authorized debit (PAD) agreement** at checkout. When a credit card surcharge is configured, the bank option can show **No Fee** next to your bank label, and the credit card option shows the surcharge rate. Selecting ACH on a US camp can show a sample check diagram that labels **Routing number** (9 digits), **Account number**, and **Check number** (marked **not needed**). |

Debit, prepaid, and HSA/FSA cards run through the same card option as credit cards. They are not a separate choice on the checkout screen.

Families can also pay in full or choose a [payment plan](/payment-plans) when the season has one. The plan controls _when_ charges run. The method on file controls _how_ they run.

### Saved methods on the parent portal

Logged-in families manage cards and bank accounts from **Account** or **My Account**, then **Payment Methods**.

- They can add, update, or replace a card or bank account.
- Scheduled charges and retries use a method on file. See [Retry Failed Scheduled Payment](/retry-failed-scheduled-payment).
- You can find households with nothing on file using [Families Without Primary Payment Method](/system-report-families-without-primary-payment-method).

When your camp uses **Finix**, the **Accounts on File** list can also show billing address for each method. Cards can show **No billing address on file.**, a ZIP-only note, or the full address, with **Add address** or **Edit**. The **Billing address** form asks for **Street address**, optional **Apt, suite**, **City**, **State**, **ZIP / postal code**, and **Country**, then **Save**. Saving checks the address with the card issuer without charging the card. Success: **Billing address updated.** When the address does not match what the bank has on file, the family sees **This payment could not be completed because your billing address did not verify. Check that the address matches what your bank has on file, then try again or use a different payment method.** After a good address save, Campium can put that card back on unfunded scheduled payments that were waiting on a verified address. Bank accounts show the address when present but are not editable there. Camps on other processors do not see this billing-address editor.

From **Scheduled Payments** or **Scheduled Payments Potential Issues**, you can send **Card Billing Address Did Not Verify**. That email tells the family to open **Payment Methods**, find the card, and use **Edit** next to the billing address. The [Scheduled Payment Email Log](/system-report-scheduled-payment-email-log) can filter those sends as **Address Did Not Verify**.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  If a family says checkout would not take their card, confirm **Accept AMEX** if it is an American Express card, and confirm they are not hitting a surcharge or processor decline. The last four digits appear as `x####` (for example, `Visa x1234`) on failed-attempt lists.
</Callout>

## Methods you record by hand

When a family pays at the office by cash or check, record it on their profile **Sales** tab with **Add Payment**. Use **Adjustment** there to give a family a credit. Your card or ACH processor does not process these entries. For the steps, see [Record a payment or credit](/profiles#record-a-payment-or-credit).

| Method         | What it is                                       | Where it shows up later                                                                                                 |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cash**       | Currency received in person                      | [Payments](/payments), [Revenue Summary](/revenue-summary), and undeposited rows in [Deposit Summary](/deposit-summary) |
| **Check**      | A paper check you will deposit                   | Same reports. The **Type** on undeposited rows in Deposit Summary shows **Check**.                                      |
| **Adjustment** | A manual balance change, not new money collected | [Revenue Summary](/revenue-summary) as **Adjustment**. Do not treat it as a bank deposit.                               |

Cash and check payments wait in **Financials → Deposit Summary** under **Pending cash and check payments** until you group them into a deposit. Card and ACH settlements follow your processor instead. See [Deposit Summary](/deposit-summary).

Canteen deposits taken at the office have their own **Add Funds** flow. The **Account** dropdown can include **Cash** (when **Accept cash** is on in canteen settings), a saved card or bank account, or **Adjustment** (for staff with canteen financial access). See [Adding canteen funds](/adding-canteen-funds).

## ACH and American Express

Turn these two options on or off in **Admin → Site Settings → Payment Info**. Changes save as soon as you toggle them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Site Settings" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    From the left sidebar, click **Admin**, then **Site Settings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Payment Info tab" icon="credit-card" title-type="p">
    Click **Payment Info**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Accept AMEX and Accept ACH Payments" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    - **Accept AMEX** controls whether American Express cards are accepted.
    - **Accept ACH Payments** controls whether families can pay by bank transfer (ACH / e-check) in addition to card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When **Accept ACH Payments** is off, families do not see a bank-account option at checkout, and no new ACH rows appear in [Payments](/payments) or [ACH Returns](/ach-returns).

When **Accept AMEX** is off, checkout rejects an American Express card with **We do not accept American Express**, even if other cards work.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  ACH timing, returns, and refund rules depend on your processor (Payabli, USIO, Finix, Payconex, Paysafe, NMI, or Stripe). See [Payment Processors](/payment-processors) and [What is an ACH return?](/what-is-an-ach-return).
</Callout>

## Credit card surcharge

**Credit Card Surcharge** on the same **Payment Info** tab is the percentage added when a family pays with a **credit** card, to help offset your card-processing cost.

- Enter the percentage as a number with or without the percent sign.
- Debit, prepaid, and HSA/FSA cards, plus ACH / e-check, are **never** surcharged.
- Most camps show a **Credit Card Surcharge** line and a label such as **(2% Surcharge)** or, when a small fixed amount is also configured for your processor, **(2% + $0.15 Surcharge)** next to the credit card option.
- In New York (and any other state that requires two-tier pricing), checkout shows two totals before a method is chosen: **Total with ACH/e-check or debit card** and **Total with credit card (includes the X% surcharge)**.
- On the parent **Pay** page (**Pay Now**), when a card on file is already selected, the **Credit Card Surcharge** line appears as soon as the page loads. Families do not need to click the card first to see the fee before they pay.
- With **Paysafe**, some older saved cards were never marked credit or debit. The Pay page shows the credit card surcharge for those cards in the preview, so the total matches what is charged. Finix and Payabli already classify cards when they are saved.

When you refund a card payment that included a surcharge, the refund form includes **Also Refund Surcharge**, checked by default. On Finix there is no checkbox: the surcharge is always refunded. The surcharge refund prorates the fee that was charged on the original payment. See [Processing Refunds and Voids](/processing-refunds-and-voids#refunds-and-credit-card-surcharges).

## After you change these settings

Site Settings save as soon as you change them. There is no separate Save button.

- Turning **Accept ACH Payments** off hides the bank-account option at checkout. Scheduled ACH charges can fail until the family uses a card instead.
- Turning **Accept AMEX** off stops American Express at checkout. Other card brands are unchanged.
- Changing **Credit Card Surcharge** changes the percentage used on **new** card charges. Refunds of an earlier payment still prorate the surcharge that was recorded on that payment.

**Payment Method Updated (families with failed payments)** on the same tab is the address that gets a notice when a family with failed or locked scheduled payments updates their card. Leave it blank to use the **Failed Scheduled Payment Email Address (for admin)** instead.

## Troubleshooting

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Families do not see ACH at checkout" default-open="false">
    Confirm **Accept ACH Payments** is on in **Admin → Site Settings → Payment Info**. If it is on and the option is still missing, the family may be on a flow that only accepts a card, or your processor may not be set up for ACH. See [Payment Processors](/payment-processors).
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="An American Express card is declined" default-open="false">
    Confirm **Accept AMEX** is on. If it is on, the decline is from the processor or the card issuer, not from that toggle.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="A family was charged a surcharge on a debit card" default-open="false">
    Debit, prepaid, and HSA/FSA cards are never surcharged. If they see a surcharge line, they likely paid with a credit card. The checkout label such as **(2% Surcharge)** or **(2% + $0.15 Surcharge)** sits next to the credit card option.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="A cash or check payment is not in Deposit Summary" default-open="false">
    Open **Financials → Deposit Summary** and expand **Pending cash and check payments**. That list is not filtered by the page date range. If the payment is already grouped into a deposit, look in the deposited batches instead. Confirm the payment exists on the profile **Sales** tab first.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="A scheduled payment has no method on file" default-open="false">
    Ask the family to add a card or bank account under **Account → Payment Methods**, or use [Families Without Primary Payment Method](/system-report-families-without-primary-payment-method) to find households that still need one.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
