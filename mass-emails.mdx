## Access the Mass Emails page

Open the Mass Emails tool from the main navigation.

- Go to **Communications → Mass Emails**.

- The Mass Emails list opens, showing all your active mass emails.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Add the Mass Emails page to your browser bookmarks or admin shortcuts so you can jump back to it quickly when you need to send updates.
</Callout>

## Understand the Mass Emails list

The Mass Emails list helps you review past emails, manage drafts, and track performance.

Key parts of the list:

- **Columns**

  - **Subject** - The email title and main link to open or edit the email.

  - **Status** - One of:

    - **Draft** - Not sent yet; still editable.

    - **Scheduled** - Set to send at a future date/time (the badge can include the scheduled date).

    - **Sent** - Fully sent to all selected recipients.

    - **Auto-Sending** - Ongoing email that automatically sends to new users who match its recipient filters.

    While a send is still running, the Status column can also show a live chip: **Preparing…**, **Sending X of Y**, or **Did not send** (if Campium could not build the recipient list).

  - **Opens / Sends** - At-a-glance open and send counts for emails that have gone out. Drafts and unsent rows show **Not sent**. While a send is still delivering, the cell shows **Sending**. After a send finishes with nobody delivered, it shows **None delivered** (not **Not sent**) next to the **Sent** status.

  - **Date Sent** - When the email went out. Unsent rows show **Not sent**.

- **Filters and search**

  - Status buttons: **All**, **Sent**, **Scheduled**, **Drafts**, **Auto-Sending**.

  - **Active** / **Archived** dropdown to switch between active emails and archived history.

  - **Search subject or body...** to find emails by text in the subject or body.

- **Common actions**

  - **Open / Edit** - Click the **Subject** to open the email composer and edit settings, recipients, or content.

  - **Copy** - Duplicate an existing email to reuse content and recipient filters.

  - **Delete** - Remove an email you no longer need.

  - **Archive** / **Unarchive** - Hide older emails from the Active view without deleting their stats or logs.

## Create and send a new mass email

Use these steps to send a new email to a group of families or users.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new email" icon="plus" title-type="p">

    - From **Communications → Mass Emails**, click **New Email**.

    - The email composer opens on the **Settings** tab with a new Draft.

    - The status stays **Draft** until you send or schedule it.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the Settings tab" icon="settings" title-type="p">

    - Fill in the sender address, subject, and email body.

    - Choose whether this is a marketing email and set when it should send.

    - Optionally add attachments such as PDFs or images.

    - If the email will send people to register, use **Campaign tracking** to generate a tracked registration link (see [Track registrations from a mass email](#track-registrations-from-a-mass-email)).

    Draft emails auto-save while you edit (see [Draft auto-save](#draft-auto-save)). You can still click **Save** any time.

    See the [Email settings reference](#email-settings-reference) below for each field.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose recipients" icon="users" title-type="p">

    - Go to the **Recipients** tab.

    - Build or load filters to target the right families or users.

    - Confirm the **Matches** count before sending.

    If you skip this step, the **Recipients** tab shows a warning icon and Campium will not send.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Test and preview your email" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">

    - In the action bar at the bottom of the **Settings** tab, click **Send preview** to open **Send Test Email** and send a copy to yourself or a colleague.

    - Click **Preview** to see the email in a new tab.

    Confirm that links, merge fields, and formatting look correct before sending to families.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Send or schedule the email" icon="rocket" title-type="p">

    - To send now, leave **When to send** on **Send immediately**, then click **Send**.

    - To send later, choose **Schedule for a date and time**, set **Send at** to a future date and time, then click **Save**. The email shows as **Scheduled** until it is sent.

    - After an email has been sent, the **Send** button becomes **Send to updated recipients**.

    After sending, the email moves to **Sent** (or continues as **Auto-Sending** if you enabled auto-send).

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Before you send, double-check the **Matches** count and the **When to send** setting. Mass emails cannot be unsent once they have gone out.
</Callout>

## Composer tabs: Settings, Recipients, Logs

Use the tabs at the top of the composer to move between content, audience, and history.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Settings" icon="settings">

    The **Settings** tab controls the content, sender, timing, and email options.

    On this tab you:

    - Set the **From email address**.

    - Enter the **Subject** line.

    - Use **Insert template** if you want a starting point, then edit the **Email body**.

    - Check **Marketing email** to include an unsubscribe link.

    - Set **When to send**, and use **Preview** and **Send preview**.

    - Add an **Attachment** if needed.

    - Use **Campaign tracking** to generate a tracked registration link for this email.

    See [Email settings reference](#email-settings-reference) for field-by-field details.

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Recipients" icon="users">

    The **Recipients** tab defines who receives the email.

    On this tab you can:

    - Use the filter builder to target specific groups (by session, status, payment state, and other criteria).

    - Load one of your **Saved Filters**.

    - See the **Matches** count and click **View** to review the list.

    Until you set recipients, the tab shows a warning: **You must set the filter below or there will be no recipients.**

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Logs" icon="bar-chart">

    The **Logs** tab shows who received the email and who opened it. Columns include **Name**, **Email**, **Sent**, **Opened** (when available), and **View**. To follow up with people who did not open it, see [Resend a mass email to non-openers](/resend-email-to-non-openers).

    The **Sent** column can show:

    - A send timestamp when that recipient was actually emailed.

    - **Already emailed** when this person was already covered in the same send for another camper, so Campium did not send a second copy.

    - **Failed** when delivery did not succeed (hover the badge for the reason when one is available).

    - **Pending** or **Not Yet Sent** while the message is still waiting to go out.

    Empty state: **No emails sent yet**, with the note that delivery and open details appear here after you send.

    When people were unchecked in the **Recipients** list (**View** on the **Recipients** tab) before send, **Logs** also shows **Skipped (N)** with **Name**, **Unchecked by**, and **When**. Helper text: **Unchecked in the recipient list, so they did not get this email and are not counted as sent.**

    **Sent**, **Opened**, **Clicked**, and **Registered** appear on the **Settings** tab under **How this email performed** after the email is sent (see [Review send and campaign stats](#review-send-and-campaign-stats)).

    When some recipients did not open the email, the **Sent** card shows **Resend to N non-openers**, which follows up only with people who did not open the first message.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Draft auto-save

While an email is still a **Draft**, Campium saves your edits automatically after you pause typing. The note in the bottom action bar shows the status:

- Resting: **Changes save automatically. Send yourself a preview before sending it out.**
- While you have edits waiting: **You have unsaved changes.**
- While saving: **Saving…**
- After a successful save: **Saved at** followed by the local time (for example **Saved at 3:42 PM.**)
- If auto-save fails: **Couldn't save automatically. Click Save to see why.** or **Couldn't save automatically. Check your connection, then click Save.**

Auto-save runs only when all of these are true:

- The email is still a draft (not already sent)
- **Auto-send** is off
- **When To Send** is empty
- Both **Subject** and the email body have content

If you turn on **Auto-send**, fill in **When To Send**, or open a sent email, the note goes back to asking you to save yourself, and you use **Save** (with its usual confirms) before changes stick. Buttons such as **Preview**, **Send preview**, **Save**, and **Send** stay available either way.

## Track registrations from a mass email

Use **Campaign tracking** on the **Settings** tab when the email sends people to register and you want clicks and signups credited to that send.

1. Open the email from **Communications → Mass Emails**.
2. On the **Settings** tab, confirm the draft has saved (see [Draft auto-save](#draft-auto-save)), or click **Save** if you are on a scheduled or Auto-send draft.
3. Under **Campaign tracking**, click **Generate campaign link**.
4. Choose **Website** or **Parent portal**, then click **Insert into email** (or copy the link).
5. If you have access to Campaigns, open **View campaign results** to review performance.

**Parent portal** links track without website code. **Website** links require the website tracking code. Full campaign setup is covered in [Campaigns](/campaigns#generate-a-campaign-link-from-a-mass-email).

## Review send and campaign stats

After you send an email, open it from **Communications → Mass Emails** and stay on the **Settings** tab. **How this email performed** appears at the top:

- **Sent** - How many recipients received the message.
- **Opened** - How many unique recipients opened it, with the open rate.

The **Delivery** panel on the right shows **Sent at**, the time the email went out.

If you generated a campaign link for that email, two more cards appear:

- **Clicked** - How many different visitors used your tracked link. The line under the number shows total clicks (for example, **12 total clicks**).
- **Registered** - How many people who clicked the link finished registering. The line under the number shows total registrations. Hover the info icon for the matching rule: a registration counts when someone who clicked your link finishes registering within 30 days on the same device. If they clicked links from more than one campaign, the registration counts toward each.

Use **View campaign results** under **Campaign tracking** for the full campaign analytics page. The **Opens / Sends** column on the Mass Emails list shows opens and sends only.

## Email settings reference

Use these field descriptions as a quick reference while you work on the **Settings** tab.

### From email address (required)

The address that appears to recipients as the sender. Use a camp address families recognize.

### Subject (required)

The email subject line shown in recipients' inboxes. Keep it clear and specific, for example, `Session 2: Bus Schedule and Packing List`.

### BCC (optional)

Enter one or more email addresses to blind-copy on the send, separated with commas. Recipients do not see the BCC addresses. Use this to copy an internal mailbox, a colleague, or an archive address without exposing them to families.

### Template (optional)

A saved template to start from. Choose one to reuse a standard header, footer, or structure, then edit the message body.

### Insert Template

Use **Insert template** above the email body to start from a saved subject and body. Choose a template from the dropdown (**Search or choose a template**), then edit the message before sending. **Manage templates** opens **Admin → Email Templates** in a new tab.

The visual **Template Library** (or **Library**) thumbnail browser is temporarily off. Per-template **Preview** on **Admin → Email Templates** still works, and you can still insert from the dropdown.

### Import HTML

Click **Import HTML** near the email body to paste (or upload) HTML from another tool, such as Mailchimp or Constant Contact. Campium keeps the layout, inlines styles for Gmail and Outlook, and maps common name and email tags to Campium merge fields. After import, use **Preview** and **Edit HTML** to review the full design. The same flow is available under **Admin → Email Templates**. See [Email Templates](/email-templates#import-html-from-another-email-tool).

### Save as template

Opposite the **Email body** heading, click **Save as template** to store the current subject and body in your templates.

1. Enter a **Template name** (prefilled from the subject) and optional **Category** (first option **No category**).
2. Helper text: **Saves the subject and body as they are now. Recipients and send settings are not included.**
3. Click **Save template**. Success toast: **Saved "..." to your templates.**
4. If that name already exists, Campium asks you to try a different name.

If you inserted one of your organization's templates (not a Campium system template), the next **Save as template** click asks **Choose how you want to save changes to "..."** with **Overwrite "..."**, **Save as new**, or **Cancel**. Overwrite toast: **Updated "..." in your templates.**

See also [Email Templates](/email-templates#mass-emails).

### Show Default Header (optional)

In the composer the checkbox label is **Show default header**, with the hint **Puts your default header and logo at the top of the email.** Turn it on so the message matches your official documents.

Use it when you want the email to look formal or transactional, such as billing notices or policy updates.

<Callout kind="tip">
  After you turn on **Show default header**, use **Preview** or **Send preview** to check the layout, since the header can change spacing.
</Callout>

### Display Login Button? (optional)

Adds a login button to the email. In the composer the checkbox label is **Display login button**, with the hint **Adds a button that takes families straight to the parent portal.** New mass emails start with it on (including drafts started from group email). Keep it on when the message asks families to sign in and act, such as completing a form or making a payment. Turn it off when the message does not need a login button.

### Account setup merge fields

When someone has never signed in, you can email them a setup link from a mass email.

In the composer, use **Insert merge field** and choose **Account Setup URL**. It inserts `{magic_login}`, which is the bare setup link (the token is attached when the email sends). Wrap that URL in your own link text or an **Insert button** label. There is no separate merge field that auto-writes **Set up your account** link copy.

These merge fields are available on mass emails. They are not on **Admin → Email Templates**. Recipients open the link on **Set up your account**, create a password (or continue with Google or Apple), and finish setup. When your payment processor has a self-service payment methods page, they land there next; otherwise they land on the portal home. Setup links from welcome-style sends stop working after 14 days; the expired page lets them request a new one.

You can also send a one-off setup email from a profile. See [Profiles](/profiles#send-an-account-setup-link).

### Also send to campers (optional)

Turn on **Also send to campers** when campers who have their own email address should get a copy, not only their guardians.

- Campers in the recipient list deliver to the Guardian 1 and Guardian 2 email addresses by default.
- With this checkbox on, Campium also sends a copy to each camper who has an email on their profile.
- After the email is sent, the **Delivery** panel on **Settings** shows **Campers** as **Included** or **Guardians only**.

Leave it off when only parents or guardians should receive the message.

### Insert button

In the email body toolbar, use **Insert button** to place a styled call-to-action link in the message.

1. Click in the email body where you want the button.
2. Open **Insert button**.
3. Enter **Button label** (default **Learn more**) and **Link URL**.
4. Pick a **Button color** (**Blue**, **Green**, **Red**, **Orange**, **Dark**, or **Custom color**).
5. Confirm **Insert button**. The link opens in a new tab when someone clicks it in their email.

### AI Rewrite

In the email body toolbar (right side), open **AI Rewrite** (tooltip **Rewrite the email body with AI**). Choose a rewrite style such as **Improve**, **Shorten**, **Expand**, **Simplify**, **Fix Grammar**, **Professional**, **Friendly**, or **Formal**, review **Original** vs **Rewritten**, then **Use This Text** or **Cancel**. It changes only the message body, not the subject, recipients, or send settings.

### Marketing Email (optional)

Turn on for promotional or marketing emails. In the composer the checkbox label is **Marketing email**, with the hint **Adds an unsubscribe link at the bottom and honors opt-outs. Required for anything promotional.**

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Check **Marketing email** for newsletters, promotions, or announcements families might want to opt out of. Leave it off for operational notices where unsubscribing does not apply.
</Callout>

### When To Send (optional)

Choose **Send immediately** to send when you click **Send**. Choose **Schedule for a date and time**, then set **Send at** to a future date and time and click **Save**. The email shows as **Scheduled** until that time. A time in the past is rejected with **Sent Date must be in the future.**

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Send times shown on the composer, the Mass Emails list, and the Logs tab are displayed in your configured send timezone, not in each recipient's local time. Set your scheduled time against that timezone.
</Callout>

### Auto-send Emails To New Matching Recipients (optional)

In the composer the checkbox label is **Auto-send to new matching recipients**. When it is on, Campium keeps sending this email to new people who match the recipient filters later. The email status changes to **Auto-Sending** and stays active until you turn it off or archive the email.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  If you turn on **Auto-send to new matching recipients**, treat this email as an ongoing campaign. Review the filters so new families do not receive outdated content.
</Callout>

After the email has already gone out with Auto-send still on, the **Recipients** tab locks the filters and shows **Recipient filters are locked.** The banner explains that Auto-send is on and new people who match an edited list would get this email within about 2 minutes. Click **Change recipient filters**, then confirm **Change the recipient filters anyway?** to unlock editing for your session. Unlock only when you intend new matching people to receive this same email.

### Attachments (optional)

Under **Attachment**, click **Choose file** to attach files such as JPG, PNG, or GIF images, PDF documents, or QuickTime videos. Each file can be up to **10MB**, and you can attach several files per email.

## Work with recipients and filters

The **Recipients** tab is where you decide who receives your message.

- **Filter builder**

  - Pick a field under **Choose a Field**, then click **Add Another Filter** for more conditions (for example, enrolled in a session, outstanding balance, camper age).

  - Join filters with **and** or **or** to narrow to the group you need.

  - Click **Save as New** to save filters you reuse.

- **Saved Filters**

  - Choose a saved filter from **-- Select a Saved Filter --**, then click **Load Template**.

  - Useful for regular sends like "All current-season families" or "Staff members."

- **Recipient count and list**

  - Review the **Matches** count for your current filters.

  - Click **View** to see each recipient when you need to verify who will receive the email.

  - On that list, use the **Send** column to uncheck anyone who should not get this email. Helper text: **Uncheck anyone who should not get this email. Unchecking a camper skips both guardians. Use the Send box in the header to check or uncheck everyone, then pick who should get it. Changes save right away.** (For SMS the helper says **text** instead of **email**.) The header **Send** checkbox (aria-label **Send to everyone in this list**) checks or unchecks the whole list. Checking everyone asks **Check all N people? Everyone in this list will get it.** with **Yes, check all**. The summary shows how many people are unchecked and will be skipped. Unchecks also apply when a scheduled email or text sends later.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Check the **Matches** count and, when sending to a sensitive group, click **View** to spot-check the list before sending.
</Callout>

Campium shows a **Warning** on the Recipients tab if no recipients are set. Resolve it before you schedule or send the email.

### Recipients locked from a Send Email button

When you start from a report or roster **Send Email** / **Email this group** control and choose **Open in full editor**, Mass Emails opens on **Settings** with that audience already set.

On **Recipients**, filters stay hidden so the list cannot change by accident. You see a banner such as **This email goes to the [count] [people] from [label].**, which explains that the **Send Email** button you used set this list.

When that message was started from a report and you schedule it for later, Campium rechecks who still appears on that report at send time. People who left the report are dropped. Nobody new is added.

To pick a different audience, click **Replace with a filter**, then confirm **Replace with a filter**. The filter builder then comes back. Until you replace the list, you cannot change recipients another way.

You need the **Manage Emails** permission for these group sends. The same full-editor handoff is used from bunks, sessions, wait list, tipping reminders, retention **Email This List**, and other **Email this group** entry points.

### Preview before a group send

From those same **Email this group** modals, **Send Email** opens a confirm step with the recipient count, subject, and one sample message filled in for a random recipient (banner notes it is test data). Use **Pick another** to see a different sample, **Back to editing** to change the draft, or **Yes, send it** to queue the send.

### Add people to a sequence from Email this group

When Campium has enabled **Sequences** for your camp and you have at least one **Added from a list** sequence, the **Email this group** modal can show **Or add them to a sequence** with a select and **Add to Sequence**. That enrolls the same audience starting today; anyone already in the sequence is skipped. **Send Email** remains the primary action. See [Sequences](/email-sequences#add-people-from-a-list).

### Replies we could not deliver

When **Sequences** is on for your camp, the top of **Mass Emails** can show the same undelivered-reply panel as the Sequences list: a title such as **1 reply we could not deliver**, then **Read**, **Open sequence**, and **Done** on each row. See [Sequences: Undelivered replies on the list](/email-sequences#undelivered-replies-on-the-list).

## Sending, testing, and resending

Use these actions in the composer to control how and when your email goes out.

### Send Test Email

- In the action bar on the **Settings** tab, click **Send preview**. The **Send Test Email** dialog opens.

- Enter one or more test addresses (often your own or a colleague's).

- Review formatting, links, and merge fields in a real inbox before sending to families.

### Preview Email

- Click **Preview** to see the email in a new tab.
- Merge fields are filled from **one random recipient** in your audience when recipients are selected. A notice at the top of the preview says it is test data and names that sample person. Use **Pick another** when you want a different sample. If nobody is selected yet, merge fields use your own profile (or another fallback) instead.
- The notice can also say who the real send would address (for example a guardian) when that differs from the sample recipient.
- Addresses you enter for a test send still only go to those addresses. The sample person is for fill-in only.
- Use the preview to catch spacing issues, long subject lines, or missing content.

### Send Now vs scheduled send

- **Send**

  - Use when you are ready to send immediately.

  - Leave **When to send** on **Send immediately**.

  - After you confirm (**Yes, Send!**), Campium queues recipients, then delivers messages in batches. You can leave the page; delivery keeps going.

  - On the **Settings** tab, a **Sending this email** panel shows progress while the send is in flight:

    - **Preparing recipients** - Campium is still building the recipient list (**Working out who this email goes to.**). No X of Y count yet.

    - **Delivering** - Shows **X of Y messages delivered**, plus tallies for **failed** and **skipped** when those apply.

    - **Retry N failed** - Requeues only failed delivery rows. Opted-out, archived, and already-emailed (skipped) recipients stay skipped.

  - If the queue job fails before any messages go out, the panel shows **This email did not send**. Check the **Recipients** tab, then send again.

- **Scheduled**

  - Under **When to send**, choose **Schedule for a date and time**, set **Send at**, and click **Save**.

  - The email status shows as **Scheduled** on the list page.

  - Campium sends the email at the scheduled time.

While delivery is in progress, **How this email performed** stays hidden so the page shows one send readout at a time. After delivery finishes, it returns with **Sent**, **Opened**, and related metrics.

### Resend to non-openers

After the first send, the **Sent** card under **How this email performed** shows **Resend to N non-openers** when some recipients did not open the email:

- Sends a follow-up only to recipients who did not open the first message.

- Useful for reminders about forms, payments, or upcoming dates.

Use the **Logs** tab and the **Opens / Sends** column on the list page to decide whether to resend.

## Attachments best practices

Use attachments to share schedules, forms, or flyers.

- Supported file types:

  - JPG, PNG, or GIF images.

  - PDFs for documents, forms, and handouts.

  - QuickTime videos for short clips.

- Size limit:

  - Each attachment can be up to **10MB**.

  - For several large files, link to a shared folder or website instead of attaching them.

Open the attachments in your test email (**Send preview**) before you send to families.

## After sending: stats and logs

After your email is sent (or while it is auto-sending), check results from the list and the composer.

- On the Mass Emails list, use **Opens / Sends** and **Date Sent** for a quick read.

- While a send is still delivering, open the email to watch the **Sending this email** panel, or watch the live **Sending X of Y** chip on the list.

- After delivery finishes, the composer **Settings** tab shows **How this email performed** (Sent, Opened, and campaign click metrics when tracking is on).

- Use the **Logs** tab for per-recipient delivery detail (timestamps, **Already emailed**, **Failed**, **Pending**, and related statuses), plus **Skipped (N)** when you unchecked people before send.

Use these tools to decide whether to resend to non-openers or adjust your next campaign.
