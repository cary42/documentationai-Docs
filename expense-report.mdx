## What the Expense report shows

Use the Expense report to record and review money your camp spends. Each row is a single expense, with the vendor, description, amount, budget code, category, who spent it, and the season it applies to. A total at the bottom sums the amounts in the current filtered list.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  This report tracks expenses you enter into Campium. It is a spending ledger, not a feed from your bank or accounting system.
</Callout>

## Filters

Set the scope at the top of the report, then select **Apply filters**.

<ParamField body="Season" param-type="selector" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Season or program group whose expenses you want to review. Leave it unset to see **All Seasons**.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Date Range" param-type="date range" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Start and end dates that limit which expenses are included.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Category" param-type="selector" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Limits the list to a single expense category.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Vendor" param-type="selector" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Limits the list to a single vendor.
</ParamField>

## Columns

<ParamField body="Date" param-type="date" required="true" deprecated="false">
  When the expense was recorded.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Vendor" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Who the money was paid to.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Description" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  A short note describing the expense.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Amount" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  How much was spent. The footer totals this column for the filtered list.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Budget #" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  The budget code assigned to the expense.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Category" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  The expense category.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Spent By" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  The staff member who recorded or made the purchase.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Season" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  The season or program group the expense is tied to.
</ParamField>

## Add, edit, or delete an expense

Select **Add New Expense** to record a new one. To change or remove an existing expense, use the menu at the end of its row and choose **Edit** or **Delete**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter to the expenses you want" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Set the season, date range, category, or vendor, then apply the filters.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add or update expenses" icon="plus-circle" title-type="p">
    Record new expenses with **Add New Expense**, or edit and delete existing ones from the row menu.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the total and export" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">
    Check the total at the bottom of the list and use the table buttons to copy or export the data.
  </Step>
</Steps>
