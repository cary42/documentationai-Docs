## What tipping does

Tipping gives your camp a public page where parents and visitors can leave tips for your staff. Logged-in families tip from their saved payment methods; visitors enter a card or bank account on the page. You control how the page looks and behaves in **Tip settings**, and you track everything that comes in with the **Tip report**.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Tipping is an optional module. If the tipping module is not turned on for your account, the Tip settings and Tip report pages redirect back to the financial reports. Separately, **Enable tipping on your portal** must be on for families to open the tipping page or see **Tipping** in the parent portal menu.
</Callout>

## Tip settings

Open Tip settings to configure the tipping page. The page lives at your camp's tipping URL, shown at the top of the settings.

### Availability

<ParamField body="Enable tipping on your portal" param-type="toggle" required="false" deprecated="false">
  When on, the tipping page is live at your tipping URL and logged-in families see **Tipping** in the parent portal navigation. When off, visitors are redirected away, cannot leave tips, and the **Tipping** menu item is hidden even if the tipping module is enabled.
</ParamField>

### Page content

<ParamField body="Page title" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  The heading shown at the top of the tipping page.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Intro copy" param-type="rich text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Text shown above the tip form.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Thank-you copy" param-type="rich text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Text shown after a tip is submitted.
</ParamField>

### Tip amounts

<ParamField body="Minimum amount per tip line" param-type="currency" required="false" deprecated="false">
  The smallest amount allowed for each tip.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Suggested amounts" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Comma-separated values shown as quick-pick buttons on the form (for example, 20, 40, 60, 100).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Allow tippers to split by counselor name" param-type="toggle" required="false" deprecated="false">
  When on, tippers can add a name to each tip line. When off, only a single bulk tip is collected.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Staff names" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  One name per line. When split tips are on, these appear as a dropdown so tippers can pick a name or type their own.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Email a receipt to the tipper" param-type="toggle" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Sends an emailed receipt after a successful tip.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Offer a whole family option" param-type="toggle" required="false" deprecated="false">
  When on, logged-in tippers can choose "Not specific / whole family" instead of a single camper.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Select **Save settings** after any change. The intro and thank-you fields support basic formatting.
</Callout>

## Tip report

The Tip report lists tips received and lets you manage each one. A summary bar at the top shows the number of tips, the tip total, surcharge collected, total charged, any refunds, and how much has been paid out.

### Two views

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Tips received" icon="list">
    Every tip collected in the date range, one row per tip, with the tipper, who it was on behalf of, the amounts, and payout status.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Haven't tipped yet" icon="users">
    Enrolled families with no tip for the season, with a manual reminder email flow to prompt them.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Filters

<ParamField body="Date Range" param-type="date range" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Limits which tips are shown by date.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Tipped person" param-type="selector" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Filter to tips left for one or more specific staff members.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Break up by" param-type="selector" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Group the report by a profile field (for example, cabin). When grouped, a **Print by** button opens a print-ready view with one group per page.
</ParamField>

### Columns

Each row shows the **Date**, **Tipper**, **Email**, who the tip was **on behalf of**, the individual **tip lines and payout** toggles, **Season**, a **Paid out** status, and the **Tip total**, **Surcharge**, and **Total charged** amounts. Select **Display Columns** to add profile fields as extra columns.

### Manage tips

- **Record manual tip** – log a tip received offline by cash, check, or another method.

- **Paid out toggle** – mark each staff member's tip line as paid out; the status badge updates as lines are paid.

- **Void** – cancel a charge before it settles, or remove a manually recorded tip.

- **Refund** – return all or part of a settled charge to the tipper.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Voids and refunds reduce your collected totals. The summary bar shows refunded and net-collected figures once any refunds exist, so use those when reconciling to deposits.
</Callout>

## Set up tipping

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on and configure the page" icon="toggle-right" title-type="p">
    In Tip settings, turn on **Enable tipping on your portal**, set your page title and copy, and choose your minimum and suggested amounts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Share your tipping URL" icon="link" title-type="p">
    Give parents and visitors the tipping URL shown at the top of the settings page. Logged-in families can also open **Tipping** from the parent portal menu when the portal toggle is on.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track and pay out tips" icon="dollar-sign" title-type="p">
    Use the Tip report to watch tips come in, mark lines paid out, and handle any voids or refunds.
  </Step>
</Steps>
