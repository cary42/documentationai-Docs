## Managing multiple email addresses

Users can have multiple email addresses associated with a single Campium account so families and staff do not need separate, duplicate profiles for each email.

<Callout kind="tip">

Using multiple email addresses on one profile reduces duplicate accounts and keeps a user's information, registrations, and payments in one place.

</Callout>

## Add additional email addresses to a user

Use the **Manage emails** link on a user's profile to add secondary addresses.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the user's profile" icon="users" title-type="p">

  - Go to the relevant user's profile in Campium.
  - Confirm you are on the correct person or family before changing email addresses.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Manage emails" icon="edit" title-type="p">

  - In the profile header contact area, click **Manage emails** (next to the primary email, or alone if there is no email yet).
  - The modal title is **Manage Emails**. Review the **Primary** address first, then any **Additional** addresses.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Add additional email addresses and save" icon="plus" title-type="p">

  - Use **Add Another** for each extra address.
  - After adding or editing addresses, click **Save** to apply your updates.

  </Step>
</Steps>

After you save, the new email addresses are linked to the same user account.

## Set an additional address as primary

Use **Set Primary** on an **Additional** address when that address should become the main one for login and messages.

1. Open **Manage emails** on the profile.
2. Helper text: **Set Primary on an additional address to make it the main one for login and messages. Your previous primary stays on file as an additional address.**
3. Click **Set Primary** next to the additional address.
4. Confirm: **Set [email] as the primary email address?**

The former primary remains listed as an additional address (it does not disappear from the list).

<Callout kind="info">

Whenever you update the email addresses on a profile, Campium sends a verification email to the user so they can confirm the changes.

</Callout>

## Remove an email address from a user

Remove an email if it no longer belongs to the user or should not receive communication.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage emails on the profile" icon="users" title-type="p">

  - Go to the user's profile.
  - Click **Manage emails** in the header contact area to see all email addresses on the account.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Remove the email address" icon="trash" title-type="p">

  - Click **Remove** next to the address. **Additional** addresses remove immediately after you confirm.
  - The **Primary** row also has **Remove**. Helper text: **Removing this promotes the next address on file, or leaves this person with no email.**
  - If other addresses remain, confirm: **Remove the primary email address? The next address on file becomes the primary one.** Success names the new primary (for example **[email] is now the primary email address.**).
  - If it is the only address, confirm: **Remove the primary email address? This person will have no email address on file.** Success: **Primary email address removed.**
  - You cannot remove your own primary email while signed in. The message is: **You cannot remove your own primary email address - it is how you sign in.**

  </Step>
</Steps>

Clearing the **Primary** field and clicking **Save** also clears the primary address, with the same self-guard.

The removed email can no longer be used for that account or receive communication tied to this profile.
