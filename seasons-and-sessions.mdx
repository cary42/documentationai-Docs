## Overview

Seasons group your sessions so families register for the right offerings in the right time frame. Some screens call sessions "products". A session can also have subproducts (options) for variants like time slots or age groups, each with its own price, deposit, capacity, and wait list.

This page covers how seasons, sessions, and subproducts fit together, and how to set capacity, dates, and wait lists.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  You can rename sessions with **Product Name (ie. Session)** under **Admin → Site Settings → Basic**. If you do, some labels below show your term (for example "Program" or "Class") instead of "Session".
</Callout>

## Key definitions

### Season

A **Season** represents a registration period, such as "Summer 2025" or "School Year 2025–26". Each Season contains:

- Sessions/Products families can register for

- Associated forms

- Payment plans

When you copy a season, Campium copies its sessions, forms, and payment plans into a new season.

### Session (Product)

A **Session** is the main thing a family registers for (for example, "Week 1 Day Camp"). If you renamed sessions under **Admin → Site Settings → Basic**, the admin shows your term instead (for example, "Program" or "Class").

Sessions:

- Live inside a Season

- Have their own name, public name, price, deposit, capacity, and dates

- Can have one or more Subproducts/options beneath them

- Link to registration and waitlist reports

### Subproduct / Option

A **Subproduct/Option** is a variant under a Session, for example:

- Morning vs afternoon track under a week-long camp

- Age group options under one program

- Add-ons that appear and register separately

Subproducts/options:

- Belong to their parent session

- Have their own name, description, price, deposit, capacity, and status

- Can have their own staff assignment

- Have their own registration and wait list links in the **Subproducts** panel

### Capacity

**Capacity** is the maximum number of campers a session or option accepts, sometimes called an enrollment limit or a cap on registrations. Once a session reaches capacity, new registrations go to the wait list instead (see **Waitlist** below).

- For Sessions (Products): set **Capacity** in the **Add New** or **Edit** session modal.

- For Subproducts/options: set **Capacity** on each option to cap that option separately.

Helper text under **Capacity** reads **0 or blank = unlimited.** Use a positive number to set a limit.

### Waitlist

A session or option puts new registrations on the wait list in two cases:

- It has a **Capacity** above 0 and every spot is taken.
- Its **Status** is **Closed**. The **Edit** session modal labels this field **Status - closed forces wait list**, with the options **Open** and **Closed**.

Wait-listed registrations are added at $0 with no deposit, so the family is not charged at checkout. To see who is waiting, click the **Wait listed** link on the **Sessions** table, or open the [Waitlist Report](/waitlist), where you can also invite families when a spot opens.

## Work with Seasons

### Copy an existing Season

Copying a Season reuses all of the sessions, forms, and payment plans from one Season in another. Use it when you run the same structure each year.

To fill an empty season that Guided setup already created, use **Copy last season's setup** on the Guided setup **Sessions** step instead. That path does not create a second season. See [Guided Season Setup](/guided-season-setup#copy-last-seasons-setup).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Copy Season modal" icon="folder" title-type="p">

    - In the left menu, open **Seasons**.
    - Find the Season you want to copy.
    - Open that Season's row menu and select **Copy Season**. In season setup, **New season** → **Copy of** the current season opens the same modal.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the copy note" icon="info" title-type="p">
    The Copy Season modal opens with this note:

    > **Note:** This feature will create a copy of all sessions, Forms, and Payment Plans from the selected season to a new season. This operation may take a few moments to complete. Please do not close this window until the process is finished.

    The word "sessions" shows your camp's term from **Admin → Site Settings → Basic**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in copy fields" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    The Copy Season modal includes:

    <ParamField body="Copy From Season" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
      Read-only. Shows the name of the Season you are copying from.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="New Season Name" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
      The name for the new Season that receives the copied sessions, forms, and payment plans. For example, `Summer 2026`.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Move Dates" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
      Radio group that shifts session dates, form windows, and payment plan dates together. Choose one:

      - **Same week next year** (default): moves each date forward by 364 days so it stays on the same weekday.
      - **Same dates next year**: keeps the same month and day one year later.
      - **Keep dates as they are**: copies dates unchanged.

      A preview line under the options shows how the season end date will look. Helper text notes that the shift applies to your products, forms, and payment plans, and that **Same week next year** keeps each one on the same weekday.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="New Season Start Date" param-type="date" required="true" deprecated="false">
      The start date for the new Season. Helper text: **When the first registration begins.** Required together with **New Season End Date**.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="New Season End Date" param-type="date" required="true" deprecated="false">
      The end date for the new Season. It follows the **Move Dates** option you picked. Helper text: **Follows the option above. Change it if this season ends on a different date.** Must come after **New Season Start Date**.
    </ParamField>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start the copy" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">

    - Click **Save** to start copying the Season.
    - Leave the window open until the copy finishes.
    - Click **Cancel** if you decide not to copy.

    When the copy completes, the new Season appears in your Seasons list with copied sessions, forms, and payment plans that you can then adjust.

    Form layout groups copy with their session assignments. Each group on the new season’s registration forms keeps the same sessions (in the same order) as the season you copied from, matched to the new season’s copied sessions. A session that has no matching copy in the new season is left out of the group rather than carried over as a broken link.

    After a copy, open the registration form’s **Sessions Layout** tab (it uses your camp's term for sessions) and confirm every session families should see is in the right group. Sessions left in the holding area do not appear on the form for families. On that tab you can also set **How this group appears** to a dropdown when a group has many options. See [Forms](/forms#how-a-layout-group-appears).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Work with Sessions (Products)

You create and manage sessions on the season's **Sessions** step in season setup. In the left menu, open **Seasons** and click the season name. The steps below work the same whatever you call your sessions.

On the **Sessions** table, click **Dates**, **Capacity**, **Price**, and **Minimum deposit** to edit them in place (empty capacity, price, or deposit shows **None set**). After you save from **Edit session**, those click-to-edit controls stay on the row, so you do not need to reload the page. Food / per-day sessions also keep the **Weeks** button under the dates. See [Guided Season Setup](/guided-season-setup#sessions).

### Create a new Session/Product

Use the new session modal to define a session that families can register for.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the new session modal" icon="plus" title-type="p">

    - Open the season's **Sessions** step.
    - Click **Add session**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill required naming and pricing fields" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    The create modal has the following fields. Labels that name the session use your camp's term from **Admin → Site Settings → Basic**.

    <ParamField body="Session/Product Name" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
      Labeled **Session Name** (with your term), marked with a red asterisk. Placeholder `e.g. Week 1`. Helper text: **Internal name shown in CampiumDB.** Staff see this name in the admin and in reports.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Public Name" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
      Labeled **Public Name**, with placeholder `Shown to families and on invoices`. Helper text: **Appears on the portal and invoices. Defaults to the name above.** If you leave it empty, Campium fills it with the session name when you leave the name field. Change it when you want families to see a different name.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Description" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
      Text area with placeholder `e.g. June 16 - 20`. The description appears on the parent site to help families understand the session details.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Price" param-type="number" required="true" deprecated="false">
      Labeled **Price** (required) with placeholder `e.g. 100.50`. Enter the full price for the session.

      If you change the price after families have registered, the new price applies to new registrations only. Each existing registration keeps the price it was sold at. To reprice one family, use **Switch** or an adjustment on their profile **Sales** tab. See [Profiles](/profiles#record-a-payment-or-credit).
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Min. Deposit" param-type="number" required="true" deprecated="false">
      Labeled **Min. Deposit** (required) with placeholder `e.g. 25`. On the **Edit** modal the tooltip reads `Fixed amount like $100`. Enter the minimum amount a family must pay at registration when deposit-based payment plans apply.
    </ParamField>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set capacity and purchase rules" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    Control how many registrations you accept and whether a child can register more than once.

    <ParamField body="Capacity" param-type="integer" required="false" deprecated="false">
      Labeled **Capacity**, with helper text **0 or blank = unlimited.** Use a positive whole number to cap registrations. On the season setup **Sessions** table, unlimited capacity shows as **None set**.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Allow discounts to be applied?" param-type="boolean" required="false" deprecated="false">
      Yes/No field labeled **Allow discounts to be applied?**, default **Yes**. Choose **Yes** to let discount rules apply to this session; choose **No** to keep discounts off it.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Can this be purchased twice for the same child?" param-type="boolean" required="false" deprecated="false">
      Yes/No field labeled **Can this be purchased twice for the same child?**, default **No**. Set it to **Yes** if a child may register for this session more than once (for example, multiple weeks).
    </ParamField>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure schedule and stats fields" icon="bar-chart" title-type="p">
    These fields set the session's schedule and internal statistics. Unless noted, they do not change how the session appears on the parent site.

    <ParamField body="Staff" param-type="array" required="false" deprecated="false">
      Multi-select field labeled **Staff**. Helper text: **Staff you pick can only open profiles of campers registered in this session.** (On an option: **…in this option.**) Staff with no session assignments remain unrestricted. Admins are not limited by this list.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Days of Week" param-type="array" required="false" deprecated="false">
      Multi-select dropdown labeled **Days of Week**. Use it to pick which days of the week this session runs. On the **New** and **Edit** session forms, if you set both a start and end date, you must also set **Days of Week**. Campium shows: **You must set the Days of Week if you set a start and end date.** Inline date edits on the sessions table do not require days of week (those cells have no Days of Week control).
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Number of Weeks" param-type="integer" required="false" deprecated="false">
      Labeled **Number of Weeks**. Enter how many weeks this session spans. Add-ons priced by the week and Special Weeks registration both read this value. If Special Weeks is on and this number is higher than the count of dated weeks for the season, you see a mismatch alert until you date more weeks or use **Lower Number of Weeks to N…**. See [Special Weeks](/special-weeks#session-weeks-vs-dated-weeks-warning).
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Start Date" param-type="date" required="false" deprecated="false">
      Labeled **Start Date** under the **Schedule** heading. Helper text: **For attendance.** Set the day this session begins.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="End Date" param-type="date" required="false" deprecated="false">
      Labeled **End Date** under the **Schedule** heading. Use it with the start date to define the session's date range. If the end date is before the start date, Campium shows **End date must be after start date for attendance.**
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Hide from attendance" param-type="boolean" required="false" deprecated="false">
      Checkbox labeled **Hide from attendance**, with helper text **Keep this item off attendance sheets and rosters.**
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Non-revenue session" param-type="boolean" required="false" deprecated="false">
      Checkbox labeled **Non-revenue session**, under the **Options** heading next to **Hide from attendance**. Helper text: **Charges still run as usual. Revenue Summary lists this separately and subtracts it from net.** Check it to exclude the session from **Net Revenue** in the [Revenue Summary report](/revenue-summary) while leaving pricing and charges unchanged.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Expiration Date" param-type="date" required="false" deprecated="false">
      Labeled **Expiration Date - can't be purchased after**, with a date and a time field. Families cannot purchase or register for the session after this date and time.
    </ParamField>

    <Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
      Marking a session as **Non-revenue session** does not change what families are charged. It only affects how qualifying registration lines are presented in the Revenue Summary report.
    </Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the new Session/Product" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">

    - Review all fields for accuracy.
    - Click **Save** to create the session.
    - Click **Cancel** to close without saving.

    After saving, the new session appears on the season's **Sessions** table, with links to its registration and wait list reports.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Copy an existing Session/Product

Copying a session reuses most settings from an existing session while you change the name or Season.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the copy session modal" icon="copy" title-type="p">

    - On the **Sessions** table, find the session you want to copy.
    - Open its row menu and select **Copy session**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter copy details" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    The copy modal includes:

    <ParamField body="New Session/Product Name" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
      Labeled **New Session Name** (with your term). Enter the name for the copied session.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Season" param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false">
      Dropdown labeled **Season**. Choose which Season should contain the copied session.
    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="Move Dates" param-type="string" required="false" deprecated="false">
      Radio group for shifting the session's dates when you copy into another season. Choose one:

      - **Keep dates as they are** (default)
      - **Same week next year**
      - **Same dates next year**

      A preview line updates under the options. Helper text: **Pick a shift when you are copying into next year's season.**
    </ParamField>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the copy" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">

    - Click **Copy** to create the new session with the name you entered in the selected Season.
    - Click **Close** to exit without copying.

    The new session then appears on the chosen Season's **Sessions** table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cancellation fees

Cancellation fees let you create a dedicated fee session inside a Season and reuse it whenever you charge the same cancellation amount. You manage them from the **Cancellation Fees** panel on the season's **Sessions** step: enter a **Name**, **Amount**, and **Description**, then select **Save Cancellation Fee**. To change an existing fee, select **Edit** on its row, update the fields, and select **Save Cancellation Fee** again, or **Cancel Edit** to leave without saving.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Cancellation fees are **not** applied automatically when a registration is cancelled. Creating a fee here only makes it available to reuse. To charge it, open the family's profile and add the fee from the **Sales** tab.
</Callout>

For the full walkthrough, including deleting fees, naming conventions, and troubleshooting, see the [Cancellation Fees](/cancellation-fees) guide.

## Manage Subproducts/options

Subproducts/options let you offer one session in different ways (by grade, age band, time slot, or another profile answer), each with its own price, deposit, capacity, wait list, and status. On the season's **Sessions** step, open a session's row menu and select **Subproducts** (or **Add subproducts** when it has none). A panel opens under the sessions table. Use **Add subproducts** to create options, click **Price**, **Minimum deposit**, or **Capacity** on a row to edit it in place, or click the pencil (**Edit subproduct**) for name, description, staff, and status changes. Drag the handle (**Drag to reorder**) to set display order. Empty price, deposit, or capacity cells show **None set**. The wait list column is labeled **Wait listed**.

Each option's **Status** uses **Open** and **Closed**: leave it **Open** to accept registrations, or set it to **Closed** to place new users on the wait list instead.

For the full walkthrough of setting up and using subproducts, see the [Subproducts](/subproducts) guide.

## View registrations and waitlists

Use the links on the **Sessions** table and in the **Subproducts** panel to open registration and wait list reports.

### Session-level registrations and waitlist

From the **Sessions** table:

- **# registered** link:

  - Opens a report showing campers registered for that session.

- **Wait listed** link:

  - Opens the [Waitlist Report](/waitlist) filtered to that session.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The **Wait listed** column appears only when the **Wait List Module** is on under **Admin → Site Settings → Modules**.
</Callout>

### Subproduct/option registrations and waitlist

From the **Subproducts** panel:

- **Wait listed** column:

  - Each option row links to the Waitlist Report.

  - The report opens filtered to the parent session, not to the single option.

- **# registered** column:

  - Each option row links to a registration report.

  - The report shows campers registered for that specific option.

These links take you from configuring options to checking who is registered or waiting, at both the session and subproduct level.

## Season management overview

You create and manage Seasons from **Seasons** in the left menu, including adding Seasons for upcoming registration periods and closing older ones after they end.

- **Create a new Season** with **Add New Season** on the Seasons page (or **New season** → **Blank season** in season setup), then enter the Season name and dates.

- **Set a Season to Inactive** after it has ended. Open the season's row menu, select **Edit Season**, and set **Status** to **Inactive**. Choosing **Inactive** opens the **Close season** page, which lists everything a close does and who it reaches before anything changes.

- **Freeze a season's profile data early** when you open next season before this one ends. In **Edit Season**, select **Freeze this season's data**. Campium builds the snapshot in the background while the season stays active, and you can choose it under Profile data when you build a custom report.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Only close a season after it has ended. Closing sets every active camper and staff member to Inactive unless they are in another Active Season.
</Callout>

## Summary

- **Seasons** group sessions, forms, and payment plans, and you can copy them with the Copy Season modal.

- **Sessions/Products** are created and copied with modals that set names, prices, deposits, capacities, and schedule fields.

- **Subproducts/options** add variants to a session. You manage them in the **Subproducts** panel, with inline editing for price, deposit, and capacity.

- **Registrations and waitlists** open from the same report links on the **Sessions** table and in the **Subproducts** panel.
