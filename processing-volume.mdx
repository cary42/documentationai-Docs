## What the Processing volume report shows

Use the Processing volume report to see how much money your camp processed through credit card and ACH payments over a date range. Summary tiles show the totals at a glance, a **By period** table (and chart) tracks volume over time, and a **card type** table breaks card volume down by brand.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  This report is for camp administrators. It focuses on **processed payment volume** by method, not on revenue, discounts, or balances.
</Callout>

## Filters

Set the scope at the top of the report, then select **Apply**.

<ParamField body="Start / End" param-type="date range" required="false" deprecated="false">
  The date range to measure. Defaults to the last year.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Group by" param-type="selector" required="false" deprecated="false">
  How the **By period** table and chart are bucketed: **Day**, **Month**, or **Year**.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Seasons" param-type="selector" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Optionally limit totals to one or more active seasons. Leave unset to include everything.
</ParamField>

## Summary tiles

<ParamField body="Total processed" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Combined credit card and ACH volume for the selected range.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Credit card" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Card volume, with the transaction count beneath it.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="ACH" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  ACH volume, with the transaction count beneath it.
</ParamField>

## By period

Each row is one time bucket (day, month, or year, per your **Group by** choice). A line chart above the table plots card and ACH amounts over the same periods.

<ParamField body="Period" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The time bucket. When grouped by month or year, select a period to drill into its days.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Total" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Combined card and ACH volume for the period.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="CC amount / CC # / CC txn %" param-type="mixed" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Card volume, card transaction count, and card transactions as a share of the period.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="ACH amount / ACH # / ACH txn %" param-type="mixed" required="true" deprecated="false">
  ACH volume, ACH transaction count, and ACH transactions as a share of the period.
</ParamField>

A footer row totals the columns across all periods.

## Credit card by card type

This table breaks card volume down by brand so you can see the mix.

<ParamField body="Card type" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The card brand.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Transactions" param-type="number" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Number of card transactions of this type.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="% of CC txns" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  This card type's share of all card transactions.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Amount" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Total volume for this card type. The footer totals the table.
</ParamField>

## Run the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the date range" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Choose the start and end dates you want to measure.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose how to group and scope" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Pick Day, Month, or Year, and optionally limit to specific seasons, then apply.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the trend and mix" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">
    Read the summary tiles, follow the trend in the By period chart and table, and check the card type breakdown.
  </Step>
</Steps>
