---
title: "Medications & health"
description: "Record a camper's medications, dispense prescribed and OTC doses, manage medication seasons, and print single or bulk health reports."
---

The medication and health tools let you keep each camper's (or staff member's)
medications on file, record every dose that gets handed out, and print a clean
health report for the nurse's station or a doctor's visit. Families can enter and
maintain their own camper's medications from the parent portal, so the list is
ready before opening day.

## Where it lives

| Where | Who uses it | What they do |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| **Profile → Health tab** (admin) | Camp staff, health office | Add/edit prescribed medications, dispense a dose, view the dispense history, download a health report |
| **Medical → Medications** (admin) | Health office | Dispense over-the-counter (OTC) doses for a camper or staff member, and dispense prescribed meds from the report |
| **Parent portal → Medications** (`/medication/`) | Families | Add/edit their camper's medications for a season, carry meds forward, certify the list |
| **Mobile app → Medications** | Families | Same medication management as the parent portal when the Medication Module is on |
| **Medical → Medication Log** (admin) | Health office | See every prescribed and OTC dose recorded across camp |
| **Combined Health Reports** (admin) | Health office | Print one PDF of health reports for everyone in a season |

<Callout type="info">
The medication features only appear when the **Medication module** is turned on
for your camp. Until it is enabled, the Health tab, the parent Medications page,
and the medication settings are hidden.
</Callout>

## Before you start

Two things control who sees what:

<ParamField path="Medication module" type="setting">
  Turns the whole feature on. Enable it under your camp's medical settings
  (**Medical → Settings**). Without it, the medication pages return
  "Medication module is not enabled."
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Medical information permission" type="permission">
  Staff need the **Medical information** permission to open the profile Health
  tab and to run the health reports. Gate this to your health office and
  directors, since medications are sensitive.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Reports permission" type="permission">
  Required (together with **Medical information**) to open **Combined Health
  Reports**.
</ParamField>

## Recording a camper's medications

A medication record captures what the camper takes, how much, when, and who
prescribed it. The same record is shared between the admin Health tab and the
parent portal — a med a family enters shows up for your staff, and vice versa.

### The fields you capture

<ParamField path="Medication" type="text" required>
  The name of the medication (for example, "Albuterol" or "Children's Tylenol").
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Dosage" type="text" required>
  How much to give — "1 tablet", "5 mL", "2 puffs", and so on.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="When to administer" type="text">
  Free-text timing, such as "After breakfast" or "As needed for headaches." On
  the parent portal this can be paired with reusable **When** options (see below)
  and specific **days of the week**.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Days of the week" type="checkboxes">
  On the parent portal, families can check the specific days a dose is given.
  When all seven are checked, the record simply reads "Every day."
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Only as needed, not on a schedule" type="checkbox">
  On the parent portal (and mobile **Medications**), families can mark a medication
  as **Only as needed, not on a schedule**. An optional **How often, at most**
  note covers limits such as every 4 hours or no more than 3 a day.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Doctor / prescriber" type="text">
  The prescribing doctor's name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Phone" type="text">
  The prescriber's phone number.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Start date / End date" type="date">
  The window the medication is active. If both are set, the end date must be on
  or after the start date.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Reason" type="text">
  Why the camper takes it (for example, "Seasonal allergies").
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Notes" type="text">
  Anything else staff should know — storage instructions, side effects to watch
  for, and so on.
</ParamField>

### Adding a medication as an admin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the camper's profile">
    Search for the camper (or staff member) and open their profile, then click
    the **Health** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the medication">
    Choose the person, enter the **Medication** and **Dosage** (both required),
    and fill in any of the other fields above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The medication is added to the list. Use the row's menu to **Edit** or
    **Delete** it later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Adding a medication as a parent

Families open **Medications** from the parent portal menu for the camper they
want. The parent form captures the same fields, plus the reusable **When**
options, the day-of-week checkboxes, and **Only as needed, not on a schedule**.
Parents can add, edit, and delete meds on any season that is currently open
(active) for your camp.

The same **Medications** area is available in the Campium mobile app when the
**Medication Module** is on. Parents manage their camper's list with the same
rules as the portal (active season edit, certify, soft delete).

<Callout type="info">
The **When** dropdown options (for example, "Breakfast," "Lunch," "Bedtime")
are managed by your camp under **Medical Settings** with the **New Option for
'When' Dropdown** button. Setting these up gives families consistent timing
choices instead of only free text.
</Callout>

## Medication seasons

Every medication belongs to a **season** (your camp's product group / year, such
as "Summer 2026"). This keeps each summer's medication list separate — a camper's
allergy meds for last year don't clutter this year's list.

- You pick which season is the **medication season** under **Medical Settings**.
  If you leave it unset, the feature falls back to your default season.
- The configured season only decides the **default** season shown. Families can
  still switch to and edit **any season that is currently open (active)** for
  your camp using the season selector on the Medications page.
- On the parent page, the default season is the active season the camper is
  actually enrolled in (usually exactly one), falling back to your configured
  medication season.
- Older medications that were saved before seasons existed have no season
  attached; those legacy records always show up in whichever season is being
  viewed, so nothing gets lost.

<Callout type="warning">
When a season is **closed** (inactive), its medication list becomes read-only —
families see "Medication entry for [season] is closed" and can no longer add or
edit meds for it. Reopen the season if entries still need to change.
</Callout>

### Carrying medications forward

So families don't re-type the same meds every year, the parent Medications page
offers a **carry-over** tool.

<Steps>
  <Step title="View the new season">
    Switch the season selector to the season you're filling in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a previous season to copy from">
    The carry-over card lists the earlier seasons where the camper has meds on
    file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the meds to copy">
    Select one or more medications and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each copied medication becomes a **fresh record** in the new season with its own,
empty dispense history. Any med that already exists in the target season (same
name and dosage) is skipped so you don't get duplicates.

### Certifying the list (parent portal)

For each season, a family can **certify** that their camper's medication list is
complete and correct — a checkbox on the Medications page records who certified
it and when. Your staff see this on the profile Health tab under **Medication
certification**.

<Callout type="info">
Adding a medication (or carrying one over) **clears** an existing certification,
because the list changed and should be reviewed again. Simply editing or removing
a med does **not** clear it.
</Callout>

## Dispensing and the medication log

When a dose is actually handed out, staff record it so you keep a running
**medication log** of every dose given. Prescribed medications use **Dispense**
on the profile **Health** tab (and on **Medical → Medications**). Over-the-counter
doses use **Dispense OTC** on **Medical → Medications**.

### Dispense a prescribed medication

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Dispense">
    On the camper's Health tab, find the medication and click **Dispense**. The
    dosage is pre-filled from the medication record, and the most recent dispense
    history is shown so you can see when it was last given.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the details">
    Adjust the **Dosage** if needed, set the **Dispensed at** date/time (defaults
    to now), and add any **Notes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally notify the guardians">
    Check **Send email notification to guardians** and/or **Send SMS to the
    guardians** to let the family know the dose was given.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The dose is written to the medication log with your name as the recorder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
Dispense records store the medication name, dosage, and notes encrypted at rest,
since they are protected health information.
</Callout>

### Dispense an over-the-counter (OTC) medication

Use **Dispense OTC** when you give a medication that is not on the camper's
prescribed list (for example acetaminophen for a headache). You need the
**Medication Module** on, plus the **Medication** permission that also unlocks
the profile **Health** tab.

#### Set up parent-approved OTC options (recommended)

1. Go to **Admin → Profile Fields**.
2. Add or edit a multi-select (**Choose Many**) field whose name includes **OTC**
   (or whose question includes "over-the-counter"). A common library label is
   **OTC Medications Approved**.
3. Add the medications you offer as options on that field.
4. Families (or staff) mark which options are approved for that person.

If no matching OTC field exists, staff can still type a medication name when
dispensing.

#### Record an OTC dose

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Medical → Medications">
    In the left navigation, open **Medical → Medications**. Click **Dispense OTC**
    in the page header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who is receiving it">
    Under **Who are you giving it to?**, search by name (at least two letters) and
    pick a camper or staff member. Staff results show **(Staff)** after the name.
    Use **Change person** if you need to pick someone else without closing the
    dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the dose">
    Choose **Medication** (grouped as **Approved by parent** and **Not approved by
    parent** when an OTC field exists), enter **Dose** and **Reason**, and
    optionally set **Given at** and **Notes**. Campium warns when the person has
    had other OTC doses in the last four hours, and when the medication is not on
    their approved list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally notify guardians">
    For campers, check **Email the guardians** and/or **Text the guardians**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The dose appears under **Recent OTC doses** in the dialog and
    on **Medical → Medication Log** with **Type** **OTC**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### The Medication Log report

**Medical → Medication Log** lists every recorded dose across camp for the
selected season, with columns for the person, medication, dosage, date and time,
type (**Prescribed** or **OTC**), whether guardians were notified, the notes, and
who recorded it. Use it for end-of-day reconciliation or an audit of what was
given. On a profile **View Log**, OTC rows show **(OTC)** next to the name.

## Health reports

A **health report** gathers everything a nurse or doctor needs about one person
onto a single, print-friendly PDF.

### What's on the report

- **Guardian contact blocks** for the camper (or the staff member's own contact
  line).
- **Health information** — the profile fields you've flagged for the health
  report (see below), plus an **Insurance information** section when those fields
  exist.
- **Medications** — every active medication on file, with dosage, schedule, days,
  prescriber, dates, reason, and notes.
- **Latest medical notes** — the most recent medical notes from the profile.
- **Emergency information** — any profile field whose name or label mentions
  "emergency."

### The "Include on health report" flag

Health reports don't show every profile field, only the ones you choose. To add
a field from **Profile Fields**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the field">
    Go to **Admin → Profile Fields** and edit the field you want on the report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the flag">
    Enable **Include on health report** and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also manage the same set from a profile **Health** tab with **Manage fields**. That dialog updates which fields appear on the Health tab and health report for that user type across your camp. See [Health Report](/health-report#choose-health-tab-fields-from-a-profile).

The report shows each person's **latest** answer for the flagged fields. If a
person's user type has no flagged fields, the report says so instead of showing
a blank section.

### One camper's report

From the profile **Health** tab, click **Download health report (PDF)**. The file
downloads immediately, named for the person. The PDF uses a two-column layout for
profile and health fields so each page stays compact.

### Combined Health Reports (bulk)

To print health reports for a whole group at once, go to **Combined Health
Reports** (requires the **Reports** and **Medical information** permissions).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a season">
    Use the season selector at the top of the report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a user type">
    Switch between campers, staff, volunteers, and so on. The list reloads for
    the selected season and type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select who to include">
    Everyone active for that season and type is listed and pre-checked. Uncheck
    anyone you want to leave out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate one PDF">
    Click **Generate One PDF**. Because a large batch can take several minutes,
    the finished combined PDF is emailed to you as a download link rather than
    generated on the spot.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
Every person's page in the combined PDF uses the same two-column layout as the
single-camper report, so the two always match. Combined batches omit the recent
dose and visit history so a full-group binder stays shorter.
</Callout>

## Permissions at a glance

| Capability | What's required |
|------------|-----------------|
| See the profile Health tab, add/dispense prescribed meds, download a health report | **Medical information** permission (and the **Medication module** enabled) |
| **Dispense OTC** on **Medical → Medications** | **Medication Module**, plus the same medication permission used for the Health tab |
| Configure the medication season and **When** options | **Medical Settings** (module enabled) |
| Run **Combined Health Reports** | **Reports** **and** **Medical information** permissions |
| Add/edit meds and certify the list from the portal | The family, on any open (active) season |
