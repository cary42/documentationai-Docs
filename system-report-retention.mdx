The **Retention** system report lists everyone registered in a prior season, split into who registered again (**Retained**) and who did not (**Lost**). You can change which earlier season you compare against, add profile field columns, and email or text the list on screen.

You need the **Retention card on the dashboard** permission (under the **Dashboard** permission category; Administrators get it by default). Your camp also needs at least two seasons in retention.

## Open the report

1. Go to **Reports → System Reports**.
2. Under **Enrollment**, open **Retention**.
3. Or from the dashboard **Retention** card drill-in, click **Open full report**.

The page title is **Retention -** followed by the current season name.

Description on System Reports: **Everyone registered in the previous season, split into who registered again (Retained) and who did not (Lost). You can email or text either list.**

## Filters and actions

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Lost** / **Retained** | Toggle which list you are viewing. Badges show the counts. |
| **Compared to** | Choose an earlier season in retention. Defaults to the immediate previous season in that set. |
| **Grade** | When your camp has a Grade field, pick **All grades** or one grade. The filter uses the grade they had in the **Compared to** season (the value saved when that season closed, when a snapshot is ready). Tooltip examples: **The grade saved when [season] closed.** or **[season] has no saved answers, so this is today's grade.** Choosing a grade keeps those people on the list even when their current grade is normally left out of retention. |
| **Only campers who paid** | Leaves out people whose charges came to $0 after discounts in the prior season (uses your camper plural label). |
| **Display Columns** | Opens **Profile fields to show**. Filter with **Type to filter fields...**, check fields, then **Save Columns**. |
| **Send Text** / **Send Email** | Reach the list currently on screen. You need **Manage Email**. Audiences carry the compare season, grade, and paying filter. |

When there is no earlier season to compare, the page explains that there is no earlier season in retention for the current season.

Intro text matches the dashboard card: everyone registered in the prior season, split by whether they registered again. People whose current profile status is **Archived**, **Deleted**, or **Removed** are left out of both seasons, so they count as neither retained nor lost. It also says **Grade is the grade they had in** the compare season. When that season has no saved answers, it notes that the grade shown is today's grade. When grades are left out of retention and **Grade** is **All grades**, a note can list those grades. With a specific grade selected, the note can say the list includes people whose current grade is normally left out.

## Columns

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Camper (your singular label) | Profile name |
| **Status** | Current profile status |
| **[season] grade** or **Grade** | When grades exist. Header uses the compare season name (for example **Summer 2025 grade**). Same tooltip as the **Grade** filter. |
| Prior season products | Sessions or products from the compare season |
| Current season products | Sessions or products for the report season, or **Not registered** |
| **Years** | Years at camp |
| Saved profile fields | Any fields you enabled under **Display Columns** |

## How this relates to the dashboard card

The dashboard **Retention** card uses the same season pool and grade rules. The card's **Email This List** drill-in is a quick composer for the retained or lost list. **Open full report** from the drill-in keeps the compare season and paying filter you were viewing, so the report opens with the same **Compared to** and **Only campers who paid** choices. The full report also adds the **Grade** filter, **Display Columns**, **Send Text**, and a standing page you can bookmark.

Configure seasons and grades from the card's **Configure retention** gear, or from **Reports → System Reports → Product YoY Comparison**. See [Chart Reports](/system-report-charts#retention-dashboard-card).
