Use the **Scheduled Payment Email Log** to confirm scheduled-payment emails went out, who received them, and what they said. The table is titled **Future Payment Email History**.

You need **Reports** and **Financial** access, with the financial module on. It is not available on the free plan.

## Open the report

1. Go to **Reports → System Reports**.
2. Under **Money**, open **Scheduled Payment Email Log**.

You can also open it from the **Scheduled Payments** dashboard card.

Dates come from the shared date picker in the page header (default last 30 days). The page title includes that range.

## Filters

Use the **Filters** card, then **Apply Filters**. **Reset** clears filters back to defaults.

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Email Type** | **All Types**, **No Account**, **Payment Issue**, **Address Did Not Verify**, **Reminder** |
| **Sent By** | **Everyone**, individual staff, or **Automated** (system sends such as upcoming payment reminders) |

Search placeholder: **Name, email or account**. Empty state: **No emails match these filters and dates.**

Automatic upcoming charge reminders appear as type **Reminder**, with subject **Upcoming payment reminder**, and **Sent By** **Automated**. Staff-sent issue emails keep the staff name under **Sent By**.

## Future Payment Email History

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Date/Time** | When the email was sent or queued |
| **Recipient** | Who received it |
| **Type** | **No Account**, **Payment Issue**, **Address Did Not Verify**, or **Reminder** |
| **Subject** | Subject line (automatic reminders show **Upcoming payment reminder**) |
| **Payment Info** | Installment details for that email. **Payment removed** when the scheduled payment row is gone but the log entry remains |
| **Sent By** | Staff name, or **Automated** |
| **Status** | **Sent**, **Queued**, or **Not sent** (with a reason such as no email address, **Unsubscribed from these emails**, or **Recipient is archived**) |
| **Opened** | Whether an open was recorded |
| **Message** | **View** opens the full email body |

The card header can show an open rate such as **N% open rate · X of Y opened** for emails that can report an open. Some mail apps load images on their own, which also counts as an open.
