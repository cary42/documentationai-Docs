## What the Payments report shows

Use the Payments report to review the individual payments recorded in your account. Each row is one payment, with the date, who paid, the amount, how it was paid, its settlement status, the account balance, and transaction details.

By default the report shows **successful payments only**. Switch to **All outcomes** to also include declined cards, returned ACH debits, and other failures.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The legacy "all payments" link opens this same report with **All outcomes** already selected.
</Callout>

## Open the report

1. Sign in to your Campium admin portal.
2. Open **Financials** in the left navigation.
3. Select **Payments**.

## Filters

Set your filters at the top of the report, then select **Apply**.

Your filter choices stay in the page URL, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view. PDF, XLS, and HTML exports use the same filters as the table.

When filters are active, an **Active filters** row of removable chips appears above the filter cards. Select **Clear all** to reset every filter to its default.

<ParamField body="Season" param-type="selector" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Season or program group to limit payments to.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Date Range" param-type="date range" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Start and end dates that limit which payments are shown.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Outcome" param-type="selector" required="true" deprecated="false">
  **Successful only** (the default) or **All outcomes**, which adds declined cards, returned ACH debits, and other failures.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Last 4" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Narrow to a specific card by its last four digits.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Amount" param-type="currency range" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Minimum and/or maximum amount. Matching uses the payment magnitude, so a payment and a refund of the same size both appear. Leave either bound blank for an open-ended range.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Deposit #" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Limit to payments settled into a specific deposit or batch ID.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Payment type" param-type="checkboxes" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Any of **Credit card**, **ACH**, **Discount**, **Adjustment**, **Check**, and **Cash**. A **Canteen** option appears when your account uses it.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Transaction type" param-type="checkboxes" required="false" deprecated="false">
  **Payment / credit**, **Refund / debit**, or **Void**.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Card brand" param-type="checkboxes" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Narrow to one or more card brands (for example Visa, MasterCard, Amex, or Discover). Brands are grouped so older processor codes for the same brand stay together. This section stays empty until your account has card transactions.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Recorded by" param-type="selector" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Who entered the payment. Choose a staff member, or **Online / system** for self-service checkout and automatic charges.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Discount" param-type="selector" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Limit to payments tied to one or more specific discount codes.
</ParamField>

## Columns

<ParamField body="Date" param-type="date" required="true" deprecated="false">
  When the payment was recorded.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Name" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Who the payment is associated with.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Amount" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The payment amount. The footer shows the page total.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Settlement status" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Where the payment is in processing and settlement.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Enrolled family members" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  The enrolled members on the family account.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Email" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  The contact email on the account.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Payment type" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  How the payment was made (card, ACH, cash, and so on).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Season" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  The season or program group the payment applies to.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Balance" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The remaining account balance. The footer shows the grand balance.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Transaction / Transaction info" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Transaction identifiers and processor details for reconciling.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Extra columns appear when your account uses them, including **Payment result** (shown with All outcomes), **Tier**, **Deposit #**, and a sessions column.
</Callout>

## Export

Above the table, use the **PDF**, **XLS**, or **HTML** export buttons to download the **full filtered list**. The table buttons inside the report copy or export only the current page.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Confirm your outcome, amount, deposit, card brand, recorded-by, payment type, and date filters before exporting. The export reflects exactly the filtered list you have set.
</Callout>

## Run the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Payments" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    Go to **Financials** → **Payments**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the season and date range" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Choose the season and dates you want to review.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose an outcome and refine" icon="filter" title-type="p">
    Keep **Successful only** for collected money, or switch to **All outcomes** to investigate declines and returns. Then narrow by **Last 4**, **Amount**, **Deposit #**, payment type, transaction type, **Card brand**, **Recorded by**, or discount. Select **Apply**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and export" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">
    Review the rows and page totals, then export the full filtered list if you need it elsewhere. Use the **Active filters** chips or **Clear all** when you want to start over.
  </Step>
</Steps>
