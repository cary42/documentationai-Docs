## Overview

Use the **Family Ledger** report to see a transaction-by-transaction history of what each family was charged, what they paid, and any credits applied, over a date range you choose. Transactions are grouped by family, and each family block ends with a subtotal row summarizing its revenues and credits.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The date range filters by the **date each transaction was recorded in Campium**. If you leave the dates unset, the report defaults to the **last 30 days**.
</Callout>

## Filters

<ParamField body="Season / program group" param-type="selector" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Limit the ledger to a single season or program group. Leave it unset to include transactions across all seasons.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Date Range" param-type="date range" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Start and end dates that control which transactions appear, based on when each was recorded. Defaults to the last 30 days.
</ParamField>

## Columns

Each row is one transaction (a charged product or a cancellation), grouped by family and sorted by last name.

<ParamField body="Date" param-type="date" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The date the transaction was recorded.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Family Name" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The primary contact on the family account. Select it to open the family's profile.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Camper" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The camper the transaction belongs to. (This column uses your camp's configured term for a person.)
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Comment" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Any note attached to the transaction. Cancelled products show a **PRODUCT CANCELLED** note with the cancellation date.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Transaction ID" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  The identifier for the transaction.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Category" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The product, and its price level when applicable, that the transaction is for.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Amount" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The dollar amount of the transaction.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Balance" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The running balance for the family as each transaction is applied.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Some camps also see a **Member #** column or a **Guardian** column, depending on your account setup.
</Callout>

## Family subtotal rows

After each family's transactions, a highlighted subtotal row summarizes that family:

- **Revenues** – total charges for the family in range.

- **Credits** – total credits/discounts applied.

Use these subtotals to reconcile a single family without adding up individual lines.

## Run and export

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Family Ledger report" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    Open the **Financials** area and choose the **Family Ledger** report.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the date range and season" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Choose a start and end date, and optionally a season or program group. Leave the dates blank to use the last 30 days.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and export" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">
    Review the ledger family by family. Use the export buttons above the table to copy, download to Excel or CSV, save a landscape PDF, or print.
  </Step>
</Steps>
