## What this report does

**Transfer Season Balances** moves eligible family season balances from a source season into a destination season. Use it when you are rolling leftover credits (or carrying balances owed) forward, instead of transferring only from [Credits](/negative-balance#transfer-a-credit-to-another-season).

Campium shows a live preview as you change seasons or transfer type. You can transfer every ready family at once, or use **Transfer** on one family row. Families already transferred for that season pair are skipped on a re-run.

## Who can open it

You need:

- Access to reports and financial tools (**Reports** and **Finance** permissions your roles grant)
- Financial features enabled for your organization

You do not need an Administrator login. Staff with report and finance access can run the transfer the same way they can open other Money reports.

The report is listed under **Reports → System Reports** in the **Money** group. It is not available on free plans.

## Open and set the seasons

1. Go to **Reports → System Reports**.
2. Under **Money**, open **Transfer Season Balances**.
3. In **Seasons and transfer type**, choose:
   - **From season (source)**
   - **To season (destination)** (must be a different season)
   - **Transfer**: **Credits only**, **Amounts owed only**, or **All nonzero balances**
4. Wait for the preview to load. **Transfer balances** stays disabled until at least one family is ready.

## Read the preview

The summary cards show:

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready to transfer** | Families that match your transfer type, with totals for credit and owed |
| **Already transferred** | Families skipped because they already have a transfer for this season pair |
| **Skipped** | Families outside the transfer type or with a zero balance |

The **Families** table lists **Primary Contact**, **Transfer Type**, **Source Balance**, **Transfer Amount**, **Status**, and **Actions** for the ready rows. Eligible rows show **Transfer** under **Actions**.

Above the table, the export toolbar offers **Copy**, **Excel**, **CSV**, **PDF**, **Print**, and **Email**. Exports omit the **Actions** column.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Transferring balances creates real **Balance Transfer** ledger rows and zeros the source season balance for each family that moves. Review the preview carefully before you confirm.
</Callout>

## Run the transfer

### All ready families

1. Click **Transfer balances**.
2. Confirm the dialog (it names the seasons, how many families will move, **Credit to move**, and **Balance owed to carry**). The confirm button is labeled **Transfer balances**.
3. When it finishes, Campium reports how many families transferred and how many were skipped.

### One family

1. On that family's row, click **Transfer**.
2. Confirm: **Transfer [amount] credit/owed for [family] from [source] to [destination]?**
3. Campium posts the transfer for that family only.

Afterward, you can review individual transfers on [Credits](/negative-balance), in **Financials → Payments** filtered to **Balance Transfer**, or with [Balance Transfer Utilization](/balance-transfer-utilization).
