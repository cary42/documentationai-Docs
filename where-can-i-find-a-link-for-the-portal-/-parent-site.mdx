---
title: Where can I find a link for the Portal / parent site?
description: Open or copy your parent portal Sign in, Create account, and Staff account links from the top bar Portal menu.
---

At the top right of any Campium admin page, open **Portal**. The menu lists:

- **Sign in** - your portal home page
- **Create account** - opens the portal on the **Create account** tab (`/new`), when **Allow New Registrations** is on under **Admin → Site Settings → Modules**
- **Staff account** - opens **Create account** with Staff selected (`/staff`), when new registrations are on

Each row has **Open** (new tab) and **Copy** (clipboard). If copy is blocked, your browser may show a warning such as **Your browser blocked copying.**

You can also find the portal subdomain under **Admin → Business Info & Logo**.

See [Authentication](/authentication#portal-login-sign-in-and-create-account) for what families see on those pages.
