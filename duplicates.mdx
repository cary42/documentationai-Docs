## Overview

Use the Potential Duplicate Users Report to spot pairs of accounts that share the same first and last name, compare them side by side, and clean them up. Duplicate accounts split a person's history, payments, and communication across two profiles, which makes reporting and follow-up harder.

Campium also catches an exact first-and-last-name match when you add a camper:

- **Admin:** saving a new child can ask to confirm with a link to the existing profile and **Add anyway**.
- **Dashboard → New Campers:** a matching row can show **Possible duplicate of** the other name, with **Merge** when you can open Merge Users.
- **Parent portal:** the family can see a message about another family account, with **Continue**, a login link, and **Send a sign-in link** to a masked address when that path applies.

<Callout kind="info">

Matching names are a signal, not proof. Always compare email, phone, birth date, and sessions before you merge. A pair whose families also share another member's name shows **Higher confidence** and sorts first.

</Callout>

## Open the Potential Duplicate Users Report

1. Sign in with an admin account.
2. Go to **Reports → System Reports**.
3. Under **Data Check**, choose **Potential Duplicates**.
4. Confirm the page title reads **Potential Duplicate Users**.

The report lists one row per pair. Columns: **Name**, **User Type**, **Older account**, **Newer account**, and actions.

## Compare a pair

Each side of the pair shows:

- Name and status (linked to the profile)
- Email and cell when on file
- Active sessions line
- **Born** date (when known) and **Added** date

When both families include another shared name, the row shows badge **Higher confidence** and a line such as **Both families include** that name.

## Resolve a pair

Actions on the row (you need the matching permission):

| Action | What it does | Permission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Merge** | Opens [Merge Users](/merge-users) with the newer account into the older one | **Merge Users** |
| **Not a duplicate** | Confirms **Mark these two as different people?** They leave this list and stop showing as a possible duplicate (including the dashboard chip) | Anyone who can open the report |
| **Delete family** | Archives an empty family (tooltip: **Nothing on this family: no sales, payments, forms or email addresses**). Confirm with **Delete family** | **Family Actions** |

### Recently deleted families

When you have deleted empty families from this page, a **Recently deleted families** card lists **Family of**, **People**, **Deleted**, **By**, and **Undo**. **Undo** restores each archived member to the status they had before the delete.

<Callout kind="alert">

Matching names alone do not confirm a duplicate. Always open both profiles (or use **Merge**) and compare email, phone, birth date, and sessions before you combine two people.

</Callout>
