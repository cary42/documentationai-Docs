## Log in as another staff member

**Log in as** on a staff profile opens Campium as that person so you can see what they see in admin. It is separate from **Login**, which opens the parent portal.

You need the **Log in as staff** permission under the **Administration** category in **Admin → Roles & permissions**. It is off by default. Grant it only to roles that should troubleshoot other staff accounts.

### Who you can sign in as

- Someone at your own camp who has a staff answer on their profile (parents who are also staff count).
- Their role must not hold any permission yours lacks. You cannot escalate into a higher role.
- You cannot sign in as yourself, while you are already signed in as someone else, or while you are previewing a role.
- Campium staff accounts are not available.

### How to do it

1. Open the person's profile.
2. In the action row under their photo, click **Log in as** followed by their first name. The button opens a new tab.
3. That tab signs you in on a separate Campium host so your own session stays open in the first tab.
4. A banner reads **Signed in as** their name and notes that everything you change is recorded under your name. Click **End session** on the banner when you are done (closing the tab alone leaves their signed-in session until it ends).

While signed in as them, you cannot open their password, two-step, or passkey pages, and password or account-settings saves are blocked. The session stays locked to your camp. The sign-in appears on their profile **Logins** tab.

For the parent portal instead, use **Login**. See [Can I log in as a parent?](/help-center/faq/can-i-login-as-a-parent) and [Profiles](/profiles#log-in-as-this-person).
