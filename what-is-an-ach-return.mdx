## Short answer

<Callout kind="info">

An ACH return occurs when a bank sends back an electronic check instead of completing the payment, usually because of an issue with the bank account details. When this happens, Campium sends you an email letting you know that an ACH return occurred.

</Callout>

## Where to see ACH returns

Review ACH returns in the **ACH Returns & Rejects** report.

- Go to **Financials**.
- Click **ACH Returns & Rejects**.

On this report you see:

- The original transaction date.
- The return date.
- The reason the payment was returned.

A common return reason is **No account/unable to locate**, which means the bank could not find an account matching the account number that was used.

## What families see on the portal

When a bank returned a recent payment, signed-in families see a danger banner on home, **My Account → Account History**, account pay, and Payment Methods. Headlines look like **Your bank returned your $540.00 payment** or **Your bank returned 2 of your payments**. Detail explains the date and account ending, may include a plain-language reason (never the raw bank code), and ends with **Add a new payment method so your payments go through.** The button is **Update payment method**.

If the family has a payment plan with a balance but no primary method, they can see **Your payment plan needs a payment method** instead (one banner at a time; ACH return wins when both apply). Custom plans and plans you exclude from **Families Without Primary Payment Method** do not trigger that plan banner. See [Payment Methods](/payment-methods#payment-method-alerts-on-the-parent-portal).

## Optional: email the family

If you want Campium to email the family when an ACH charge fails:

- Go to **Admin → Site Settings**.
- Open **Payment Info**.
- Enable the setting to send an email about failed ACH charges.

After you turn this on, families receive an email automatically when an ACH return occurs. The portal banner still appears whether or not that email is on.
