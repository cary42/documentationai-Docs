Training Videos is a read-only page where your camp's administrators can watch recordings that Campium has published for your organization.

## Who can open it

You need access to the **Admin** menu (the same access used for other Admin tools such as Staff Hub). The **Training Videos** item appears under **Admin** in the left navigation only when at least one active video exists for your camp.

If you do not see **Training Videos**, either no videos have been published for you yet, or your role cannot open Admin pages. Contact Campium support if you expected a recording to appear.

## Open Training Videos

1. Open **Admin** in the left navigation.
2. Click **Training Videos**.

The page title is **Training Videos**, with the subtitle **Watch the training sessions recorded for your camp.**

## Watch a video

Each active video appears as its own card with:

- The video **title**
- An in-page player (play, pause, and other browser controls)
- An optional description under the player

Use the player to watch without leaving Campium. If your browser cannot play the file, the card includes a download link.

## Share a video link

Under each video you will see **Video link**:

1. The URL field is read-only.
2. Click **Copy** to put the link on your clipboard (you see **Link copied.** when it works).
3. Or click **Open in new tab** to play the file outside Campium.

Helper text under the controls: **Copy this link to share the video, or open it in a new tab.**

If the browser blocks clipboard access, Campium selects the link and asks you to copy it with Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on a Mac).

## Empty state

When no videos are published yet, the page shows **No training videos yet** and explains that recordings for your camp will appear here. Reach out to Campium support if you are expecting one.
