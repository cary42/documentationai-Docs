## Spread fees over time with seasonal payment plans

Payment plans let families spread camp fees over time instead of paying everything at once. When a season has at least one payment plan, families also see an automatic pay-in-full option, so do not create a plan named `Pay In Full`.

<Callout kind="info">

Payment plans belong to a **season**. Before you create, edit, or copy a plan, confirm the season selector shows the season you want.

</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert">

You cannot use `Pay In Full` as a payment plan name. Campium adds that option automatically.

</Callout>

## Open the Payment Plans page

Set up payment plans from the Admin menu.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Payment Plans" icon="settings">

  Go to **Admin → Payment Plans**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the season" icon="calendar">

  Use the season selector at the top of the page to pick the season.

  The list shows only that season's plans.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="warning">

If no registration form in that season has payment plans turned on, the page shows: **Plans will not show on the portal until a form has payment plans enabled. Open the form's Settings and turn on payment plans.**

</Callout>

When the season has no plans yet, the page shows **No payment plans yet**, with: **Payment plans are optional. Add one if parents should pay over time instead of only paying in full.** The button is **Add New Payment Plan**.

## Preview a sample schedule on the list

In the plans table, the **Preview for** column header has a `$` sample balance box (default `$1,000`). Hover the label to see **What a family owing this sample balance would be charged, and when**. The same control appears on the **Payment plans** step of a season's **Guided setup** (from **Seasons**).

Each row under that column shows the sample schedule for that plan, Standard and Even Split alike. Rows can show **per camper** or **balance** badges. Long schedules use **Show N more** / **Show fewer**.

If a plan cannot be previewed yet, the cell explains why, for example a missing final payment date, no plan details, every charge date in the past, or nothing due on that sample balance. Changing the sample balance refreshes every row.

Use **Copy All To Another Season** to copy every plan in the current season. When the page shows **Preview Cart**, it opens a cart preview in a new tab so you can try real payment plans and paying-today choices without signing in as a parent. That button is separate from the **Preview for** column. The preview needs one season that has a registration form; otherwise you see **Cart preview unavailable.** with a note to open Payment Plans for a season that has a registration form. The built-in **Custom** plan is left out of the preview list.

## Create a new plan

Create a plan after you confirm the correct season.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new plan" icon="plus">

  Click **Add New Payment Plan**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the plan form" icon="edit">

  Choose **Type of plan**, enter **Plan Name**, set the dates, and fill in the fields for that plan type.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the plan" icon="check-circle">

  Click **Save**. The plan appears in the list for that season.

  </Step>
</Steps>

### Plan fields

Use these fields when creating or editing a plan.

<ParamField body="Plan name" param-type="string" required="true" show-location="false">
The **Plan Name** field. Helper text: **Visible on the portal.** You cannot use `Pay In Full`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Type of plan" param-type="string" required="true" show-location="false">
The **Type of plan** field when you create a plan: **Standard** or **Even Split**. Standard plans use charge lines you define. Even Split plans divide the remaining balance across charges between the start and end dates. Each season also has a built-in **Custom** plan (see below). You do not create that type from **Add New Payment Plan**.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Description" param-type="string" show-location="false">
Optional text for **Standard** plans. Helper text: **Visible on the portal.** Use it to explain the timing in plain language.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Charge cadence" param-type="string" show-location="false">
The **Charge cadence** field, for **Even Split** plans only. Choose **Monthly**, **Weekly**, **Bi-weekly**, or **1st and 15th each month**. The list's **Type** column shows the last one as **Even Split (1st & 15th)**.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Day of month for charges" param-type="integer" show-location="false">
The **Day of month** field, for **Monthly** Even Split plans. Choose the day of the month charges run by default.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Let families choose their charge day" param-type="boolean" show-location="false">
Available only for **Monthly** Even Split plans. When checked, checkout shows **Charge day of the month** so the family can pick a day from the **1st** through the **28th**. The plan's **Day of month** stays the default until they change it. After the checkout that creates their schedule, the day locks for the family. You can still change it on their profile. Plans with this option show a **Family picks** badge in the **Day of month** column.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Start date" param-type="date" required="true" show-location="false">
The **Start Date** field. For **Standard** plans, the day the plan becomes available to select. For **Even Split** plans, the first charge is on or after this date.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="End date" param-type="date" required="true" show-location="false">
The **End Date** field. For **Standard** plans, the plan cannot be selected on or after this date. For **Even split** plans with **Final payment** set to **On the end date**, this is the last date a charge can happen on or before. When **Final payment** is **Before each session starts**, helper text says: **Families can't pick this plan after this date.**
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Final payment" param-type="string" show-location="false">
Available for **Even split** plans when per-session scheduling is available. Choose **On the end date** (charges run through the end date) or **Before each session starts**. When you choose the per-session option, set **Days before start**. Helper text: **Each session's share of the balance is paid off this many days before it starts. Deposits count toward every session by price.** Plans that use this option show a list type such as **Even Split (…, per session)**. At checkout, families see wording that each session is split evenly and paid off at least N days before that session starts (or by the day the session starts when N is 0).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Season" param-type="season" required="true" show-location="false">
The season the plan belongs to. Once a family is using the plan, this field no longer appears.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Require paid by session start" param-type="boolean" show-location="false">
When checked, the plan is offered only when its last charge is on or before the session start date, so the plan is paid off before camp begins. If there is too little time left for that, families do not see the plan.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Show Only For These Sessions" param-type="multi-select" show-location="false">
Restricts the plan to specific sessions. When you select one or more sessions, the plan is offered only when the cart includes at least one of them. Leave it blank to offer the plan for all sessions in the season. The label uses your camp's term for products, for example **Show only for these Sessions**.
</ParamField>

## Choose between Standard, Even Split, and Custom

The plan type changes how charges are created and managed.

| Plan type | How charges are set | What you configure | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | You define each charge manually | Schedule lines with dates and amounts or percentages | Plans with fixed installments or custom timing |
| Even Split | Campium divides the remaining balance automatically | Charge cadence, dates, and monthly day if needed | Plans that should spread the balance evenly over time |
| Custom | No automatic charges | Plan Name and Description only (staff rename) | Families you bill by hand on the Sales tab |

### Custom (staff only)

Each season gets a built-in **Custom** plan. It shows a **Staff only** badge. Tooltip: **Parents never see this plan. Choose it on a family's Sales tab when they pay on their own schedule. Families also move here when you edit one of their scheduled payments.**

- Parents never see it at checkout. When a family is already on Custom, checkout shows a note that the camp office arranged a custom schedule and they should contact the office to change it.
- On the plans list, the preview cell says there are no automatic charges and that you manage these families' payments.
- You can rename **Plan Name** and **Description**. You cannot delete it, copy it, or turn its status off the way you do for Standard and Even Split plans.
- Assign it from a family's profile **Sales** tab (**Modify Payment Plan**). Editing a scheduled payment by hand, or removing one with **Recalculate remaining scheduled payments** on an Even Split family, can also move the family onto Custom.
- When you change a family onto **Custom**, **Modify Payment Plan** shows a **Note** field (placeholder **Why is this family on a custom plan?**). Helper text: **This note is added to the Notes tab.** The saved note starts with **Payment plan changed to** the plan name and season.

### Standard plans

Standard plans give you full control over each charge. After you save the plan, you add a plan detail line for each installment.

### Even split plans

Even Split plans do not use schedule lines. Campium calculates the charge amounts from the balance, the date range, and the cadence you choose.

#### Let families choose their charge day

On a **Monthly** Even Split plan, you can check **Let families choose their charge day** below **Day of month**.

- At checkout, families see a **Payment plan** card with each plan as a choice, then **Charge day of the month** with shortcut buttons for **1st**, **15th**, and **28th**, plus a dropdown for any day from the **1st** to the **28th**, and helper text: **Your payments will run on this day each month. You will not be able to change it after you check out.**
- The **How much today?** card shows **Full amount**, plus pay-later, minimum, and **Another amount** options as the form allows, and **Total today**.
- The schedule preview updates when they change the day.
- After the checkout that creates their scheduled payments, the portal shows locked text such as **Charges on the 15th of each month.** with **To change this, contact the camp office.**
- Weekly, bi-weekly, and 1st and 15th Even Split plans do not offer this option. Neither do Standard plans.

This is a per-plan setting and works with any payment processor.

<Callout kind="tip">

Use the **Preview for** sample balance on the payment plans list to check how Standard and Even Split charges fall between the start and end dates before families use a plan.

</Callout>

## Add schedule details to a Standard plan

A Standard plan needs plan detail lines before families can use it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Expand the plan row" icon="chevron-right">

  Find the Standard plan in the list and expand its row.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a plan detail line" icon="plus">

  Click **Add New Plan Detail** under the plan name to create each installment.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the charge date and amount" icon="edit">

  For each line, choose the **Date**. Then decide whether the line charges the entire remaining balance or only part of it.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save additional lines as needed" icon="check-circle">

  Add one line for each installment in the schedule.

  Click **Save and add another** to keep the window open for the next installment, or **Save** to close it.

  You can edit a line with the pencil icon or remove it with the trash icon.

  </Step>
</Steps>

### Plan detail line fields

Each payment line has these settings:

- **Date** - The day the charge runs.
- **Charge Entire Balance** - Set to **Yes** if the payment should collect whatever is still unpaid at that point. The schedule line then shows **Remaining Balance**.
- **Percent or Amount** and **Amount** - Use these when the line charges only part of the balance: choose a percentage or a fixed dollar amount, then enter the value.
- **Per Child?** - Whether the payment applies separately to each child or once for the registration.

<Callout kind="info">

Even Split plans do not use plan detail lines. Campium calculates those charges from the balance and dates.

</Callout>

### Last payment isn't the balance

On **Admin → Payment Plans** (and on the **Payment plans** step of a season's **Guided setup**), a **Standard** plan can show a warning badge: **Last payment isn't the balance**.

Hover the badge to see **The last payment is a set amount or percentage, not the remaining balance. Anything the earlier payments don't cover is never charged.**

Next to the badge, click **Fix last payment** to set the last schedule line to **Charge Entire Balance**. Its tooltip reads **Sets the last payment to Charge Entire Balance. Payments already scheduled for families on this plan do not change.** While it runs, the button reads **Fixing...**. Plan details stay expanded so you can confirm the change.

You can also open the latest-dated plan detail line yourself and set **Charge Entire Balance** to **Yes**. Even Split plans do not show this badge.

## Turn plans on or off

Each plan has an on/off switch in the **Status** column. You can turn a plan on or off regardless of its start and end dates.

- **Active** (on) plans can be offered on forms when the other requirements are met.
- **Inactive** (off) plans are not offered on forms.

If a plan shows **Not showing on forms**, hover the badge to see the reason.

### Why a plan may not show on forms

The **Not showing on forms** badge lists one or more of these reasons:

- The plan is inactive.
- No season is assigned.
- No form in the season has payment plans enabled.
- For a **Standard** plan: today is outside the plan's start and end dates, no plan details have been added, or the latest plan detail date is in the past.
- For an **Even Split** plan: the end date is in the past.

A plan without the badge can still be hidden from a family at checkout:

- **Require paid by session start** is on and there is no longer enough time for the last charge to land on or before the session start date.
- **Show only for these** sessions is set and the cart does not include one of the selected sessions.

## Edit or delete a plan

Update a plan from its row in the list.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan options" icon="edit">

  Open the row menu, then choose **Edit Payment Plan**.

  You can also click the underlined plan name.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes" icon="settings">

  You can change the name, dates, description, and type. For Even Split plans, you can also change the cadence and day of month.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the plan" icon="check-circle">

  Click **Save**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

### Delete availability

**Delete Payment Plan** appears in the row menu only if no family is using the plan.

<Callout kind="alert">

If families are already using a plan, you cannot delete it. Turn it off in the **Status** column instead.

</Callout>

## Copy plans to another season

Copy plans when a new season uses the same plan structure.

### Copy one plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan menu" icon="copy">

  Find the plan, open the row menu, and choose **Copy Payment Plan**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the destination" icon="folder">

  Enter a **New Plan Name**, then choose the season in **Copy To Season**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose whether to shift dates" icon="calendar">

  To shift the dates, check **Increase dates by one year**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the copy" icon="check-circle">

  Click **Create Copy**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

### What gets copied

- **Standard** plans copy their schedule lines.
- **Even Split** plans copy the type, cadence, and day-of-month settings.

### Copy all plans for a season

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the season copy" icon="folder">

  Click **Copy All To Another Season**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the destination season" icon="calendar">

  In **Copy To Season**, pick the season that receives the copied plans.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the optional date shift" icon="edit">

  To shift the dates, check **Increase dates by one year**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the copy" icon="check-circle">

  Click **Copy**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">

**Copy Payment Plan** will not copy a plan into a season that already has a plan with the same name. **Copy All To Another Season** does not check names, so check the destination season for duplicates.

</Callout>

## Change a family's payment plan

Change a family's assigned plan from the Sales area on their profile.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the family profile" icon="users">

  Go to the family's profile and open the **Sales** tab.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the season plan link" icon="chevron-right">

  In the left column, find the season. If that season has a payment plan assigned, click the plan name below the season name.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a different plan" icon="edit">

  In **Modify Payment Plan For** that person, select another plan in **Payment Plan**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Scheduled Charges" icon="list">

  **Scheduled Charges** shows what the chosen plan would charge for this family's current balance (for example **Based on a balance of $X…**). For **Custom**, the preview explains that the plan schedules nothing automatically. When you are moving the family onto **Custom**, fill in **Note** if you want a reason on the **Notes** tab.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust Charge day when needed" icon="calendar">

  For a monthly Even Split plan, use **Charge Day** to keep the family's day, pick **Use plan default**, or choose another day (**1st** through **31st**). Changing the day rebuilds their scheduled payments. When the family chose the day at checkout, the helper text says so.

  <Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cary42/documentationai-Docs/main/images/walkthroughs/modify-payment-plan-charge-day.png" width="600" height="815" alt="Modify Payment Plan with Even Split - Monthly, Charge Day set to the 10th, and the scheduled charges it would create" />

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the change" icon="check-circle">

  Click **Save Plan**.

  If the family has no primary payment method on file, Campium still saves the plan and warns that the scheduled payments will not be charged until one is added. Those families also appear on **Reports → System Reports → Scheduled Payments Potential Issues**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Remove a family's payment plan

A family's payment plan drives their automatic billing (autopay). To take a family off automatic billing or stop their scheduled payments, remove the plan from their profile. You can keep or delete the future scheduled payments when you do.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the family's current plan" icon="users">

  Open the family's profile and go to the **Sales** tab. Find the season, then click the payment plan name below the season name. This opens **Modify Payment Plan**.

  <Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cary42/documentationai-Docs/main/images/walkthroughs/sales-tab-plan-name.png" width="982" height="175" alt="The season on the Sales tab, with the payment plan name under it" />

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a removal option" icon="minus">

  Under **Remove Plan**, pick the button that matches what you want to happen to scheduled payments, then confirm.

  <Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cary42/documentationai-Docs/main/images/walkthroughs/modify-payment-plan-remove.png" width="600" height="815" alt="Modify Payment Plan, with the two Remove Plan buttons at the bottom" />

  </Step>
</Steps>

### Removal options

<ParamField body="Remove and delete scheduled payments" param-type="action" show-location="false">
Removes the plan and deletes its future scheduled charges. Nothing more is charged automatically for that season.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Remove, keep scheduled payments" param-type="action" show-location="false">
Removes the plan but leaves future charges scheduled, so they still run.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Remove Plan" param-type="action" show-location="false">
Shows instead of the two buttons above when the family has no scheduled payments left.
</ParamField>

Removing a plan does not change what the family owes. Payments already made are not touched. Collect any remaining balance as a one-time payment, or assign a different plan from the same window. Repeat for each season that has a plan.

## Even split: pay off before each session

On an **Even Split** plan, **Final payment** can be **Before each session starts** instead of **On the end date**.

1. Open **Admin → Payment Plans** and create or edit an Even Split plan.
2. Set **Final payment** to **Before each session starts**.
3. Enter **Days before start** (required). Example: `7` means each session's share is paid off seven days before that session starts.
4. Set **End Date** as the last day families can still pick the plan.
5. Set **Start Date** no later than the final payment date of your last session. If the Start Date is after that cutoff, Campium refuses to save with: **The Start Date is after …, the final payment date of the last session. Charges begin on the Start Date, so move it earlier.**
6. Save the plan. Use **Preview for** on the list to check a sample schedule. Preview notes can mention how many sessions start in the sample window.

When a family signs up late relative to a session's final-payment date, the preview can note that the session's share is **charged in full** on the late-charge date (or today once that date has passed), for example: **Final payment due … so that share is charged in full on ….**

Plans never create installments before the plan **Start Date**. When the season balance is already paid or on a credit ($0 or less owed), Campium does not create zero or negative scheduled charges.

This is not limited to a specific payment processor. The option appears when per-session Even Split columns are available on your account (they are live for camps that use Payment Plans).

## Even split cadence behavior

Even Split plans calculate charges from the cadence and date range.

<ParamField body="Monthly" param-type="cadence" show-location="false">
Charges on the day of the month you set, or on the family's chosen day when **Let families choose their charge day** is on for that plan.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Weekly" param-type="cadence" show-location="false">
Charges every 7 days. The start date acts as the anchor, and charges continue every 7 days through the end date.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Bi-weekly" param-type="cadence" show-location="false">
Charges every 14 days. The start date acts as the anchor, and charges continue every 14 days through the end date.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="1st & 15th" param-type="cadence" show-location="false">
Charges on the 1st and 15th of each month when those dates fall within the date range. In the plan editor this option reads **1st and 15th each month**.
</ParamField>

## How schedules adjust when the balance changes

When a family's season balance changes (for example you add or remove a session, or apply a discount), remaining scheduled payments update as follows:

- **Even Split** (plan-based): remaining installments re-spread evenly across the rest of the schedule. Early installments use whole dollars; any leftover cents land on the last installment. The list **Preview for** sample and **Recalc Even Split** use the same rounding. Per-session Even Split plans re-spread within each session's payoff window.
- **Standard** plans where every non-balance line is the same **Percent** (for example 25% / 25% / 25% / balance): remaining installments re-divide the new balance the same way (whole dollars, cents on the last row). Recording an extra payment on an equal-percent Standard plan also re-spreads the leftover the same way (toast title **Payment plan updated**). Hand-edited installment rows stay as you left them.
- **Standard** plans with unequal percents or fixed dollar amounts: leftover balance still flexes on the final **Charge Entire Balance** row when that row is set to **Yes**.

## Remove a scheduled payment

On a family's profile **Sales** tab, remove one upcoming installment when you confirm **Remove this scheduled payment?**

- Checkbox **Recalculate remaining scheduled payments** (on by default). Helper: **Spreads this season's balance across what is still scheduled. Turn off to remove this row only.**
- Confirm with **Yes, remove it**.

When recalculate is on for an **Even Split** family, Campium moves them onto that season's **Custom** plan and spreads the remaining balance evenly across the installments that are left. It does not rebuild the deleted row from the old Even Split dates. Families already on **Custom** get the same even spread across what remains. Turn recalculate off when you only want that one row gone.

## Recalculate an even split schedule

When your camp has it turned on, a **Recalc Even Split** link appears on the family profile's **Sales** tab.

Use it after the family's balance or plan assignment changes to rebuild the Even Split schedule from the current balance and dates.

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="When does Recalc Even Split appear?">

  You see **Recalc Even Split** only if the setting is turned on for your camp. If you do not see the link, your camp does not have it.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="When should staff use it?">

  Use it after a balance or plan change when the Even Split schedule needs rebuilding from the family's current balance and the plan dates. Plan-based Even Split schedules re-spread on their own when you add or remove a session. Use **Recalc Even Split** when you still need a manual rebuild.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Quick reference

Use this table when you need to decide which plan type to build.

| Feature | Standard | Even Split |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amounts | Defined manually for each charge | Calculated automatically from the remaining balance |
| Extra setup | Add and manage plan detail lines | Set cadence and, for monthly, the day of month |
| Charge timing | Based on your schedule lines | Based on start date, end date, and cadence |
| Copy behavior | Copies all schedule lines | Copies cadence and related settings |

## Clarifications and troubleshooting

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Why do families still see a pay-in-full option when payment plans exist?">

  Campium adds a pay-in-full option whenever the season has at least one payment plan. You do not create a separate pay-in-full plan.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Why is my Standard plan not available yet?">

  Check that the plan is active, today is between its start and end dates, it has plan detail lines with at least one future date, and a form in that season has payment plans enabled. Hover the **Not showing on forms** badge for the reason.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Why does my even split schedule not match what I expected?">

  Check the plan's start date, end date, and cadence. Weekly and bi-weekly plans repeat from the start date, monthly plans use the day of month, and 1st and 15th plans use those dates within the range.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
