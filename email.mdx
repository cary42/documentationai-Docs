## Overview

Campium sends email in a few different ways, and each one has its own place in the admin site. Use this page to understand the difference and jump to the right guide.

- **Mass Emails**: emails you compose and send to a group of recipients, either right away or on a schedule.
- **Sequences**: a series of emails you write once; Campium sends each one on its day after registration, a new lead, or when you add people from a list (when Campium has enabled Sequences for your camp).
- **Email Templates**: reusable email designs you build once and use across your communications.
- **Automated Emails**: messages Campium sends on its own when something happens, such as a form reminder or a scheduled payment receipt. Edit the wording in one place.

## Mass Emails

Mass emails are one-off or scheduled messages you send to families and other recipients. You choose who receives the message with recipient filters, write your content, optionally attach files, and send now or schedule for later. You can also review basic delivery stats afterward.

## Sequences

Sequences are drip-style emails under **Communications → Sequences**. You write each email on the sequence page; Campium sends them at 9am your time on the day you choose. Available when Campium has turned on **Email Sequences (beta)** for your camp and your role includes **Manage Email**.

## Email Templates

Email templates let you save a reusable design and content so you do not have to rebuild the same email every time. Create a template once, then use it for forms, mass emails, and other profile-based communication.

<Card title="Email Templates" href="/email-templates" icon="layout" horizontal="false">
  Create, organize, and reuse email designs across your communications.
</Card>

## Automated Emails

Automated emails are transactional messages Campium sends when a specific event happens, for example when a scheduled charge is coming up, a form reminder is due, or a wait list spot opens. You do not send these one at a time; they are triggered by activity on your site. Open **Admin → Automated Emails** to review the full list, edit wording, and send a test to yourself.
