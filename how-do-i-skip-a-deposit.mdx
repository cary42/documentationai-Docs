---
title: How do I skip the deposit?
---
### **Steps**

1. Open **Admin → Discounts**.

2. Click **Add New Discount**, or open a discount's action menu and choose **Edit Discount**. Find **Bypass payment at portal checkout?**.

3. Set it to **Yes**.

4. Click **Save**.

### **What this does**

- When a family applies the code in the parent portal, they can submit checkout without paying a deposit and without entering a card or ACH account.

- You collect payment later.

- After the code is applied, choosing a payment plan or switching between card and bank keeps the bypass. For a code with no amount or percent, the cart shows a line ending in **payment deferred**. Removing the discount ends the bypass.

### **Quick test**

1. Open your parent portal.

2. Start a test registration and enter the bypass code.

3. Confirm checkout no longer requires a deposit.

4. If you offer payment plans, pick one and confirm the deposit is still deferred.

### **If it still asks for deposit**

- Confirm you saved the setting on the discount the family is using.

- Confirm **Bypass payment at portal checkout?** is **Yes**.

- Confirm the discount is still on the cart (removing it clears the bypass).

- Confirm the code passes its other rules. The bypass still respects **Allow On Portal?**, the start and end dates, **Limit**, and **Allow Duplicate?**.

- Retry in a private or incognito window to start a fresh session.
