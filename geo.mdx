## Overview

Use the Family Map to see where your families live at a glance. Each active camper with an address on file is plotted as a marker, so you can spot how far people travel, find clusters to target for outreach, and plan regional events or transportation.

Your camp's own location appears as a blue marker near the center of the map.

## What it shows

Select any marker to open an info window with that family's details.

<ParamField body="Name" param-type="link" show-location="false">
The camper's name, linked to their profile so you can open the full record in a new tab.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Address" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The primary address on file, used to place the marker on the map.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Mobile" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The mobile phone number on file, when one is available.
</ParamField>

## Filter by season

Use the season selector at the top of the map to limit it to families registered for a specific season. Leave it on the default to plot everyone in your account.

<Callout kind="info">
  Markers are placed by geocoding the primary address on file. A family with no address, or an address that cannot be located, will not appear on the map.
</Callout>

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Family Map from Reports" icon="folder" titleType="p">
    Go to **Reports** &gt; **System** and select **Family Map**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a season (optional)" icon="calendar" titleType="p">
    Use the season selector at the top to focus the map on one season's families.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

- **A family is missing from the map**: They may have no address on file, or their address could not be located. Add or correct the primary address on their profile.
- **The map looks centered on the wrong place**: The center point uses your camp's saved location. Update your business location if it is off.
