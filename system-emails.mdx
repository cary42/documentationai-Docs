## Use Automated Emails for your camp's wording

Campium still sends transactional messages for you, such as form reminders, payment notices, wait list updates, and birthday wishes. To review that list, change the wording, and send a test to yourself, open **Admin → Automated Emails**.

See [Automated Emails](/automated-emails).

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The older **System Emails** screen is not available in your camp's admin menu. Platform-wide templates are managed by Campium. Anything you can customize for your camp lives under Automated Emails, Forms, Email Templates, or the Profile Photo Report.
</Callout>
