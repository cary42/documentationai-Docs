## Not currently available

**Email Sequences** (scheduled drip steps after registration) are not currently available in the left navigation. The older sequence editor is not offered for camps right now.

For messages you compose and send on a schedule, use [Mass Emails](/mass-emails).

For messages Campium sends on its own (payment notices, form reminders, wait list updates, birthday wishes, and more), use [Automated Emails](/automated-emails).

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  If you previously used Email Sequences, contact Campium support about your options. Do not rely on this page for a current setup path.
</Callout>
