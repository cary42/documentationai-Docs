A **sequence** is a series of emails you write once. Campium sends each one on its day: after a family registers for a session, before or after the session, after someone becomes a lead, or after you add people from a list.

## Who can use Sequences

Sequences appear under **Communications → Sequences** only when:

1. Campium has turned on **Email Sequences (beta)** for your camp.
2. Your role includes **Manage Email** (the same permission used for **Email this group** and Mass Emails).

You cannot turn the module on yourself under **Admin → Site Settings → Modules**. If you do not see **Sequences** in the left menu, ask Campium support whether it is available for your camp.

You also need a valid **Contact Email Address** under **Admin → Site Settings → Basic** before you can turn a sequence on, so replies can reach you.

For one-off or scheduled blasts, use [Mass Emails](/mass-emails). For messages Campium already sends on its own (payment notices, form reminders, and similar), use [Automated Emails](/automated-emails).

## Open the list

1. Go to **Communications → Sequences**.
2. The page title is **Sequences**. Subtitle: **Emails Campium sends for you on a schedule**.
3. Click **New Sequence**, or open an existing row by its name.

The list panel **Your sequences** shows columns **Sequence**, **Starts when**, **Status**, **Emails**, **In progress**, and **Sent**. Use **Archived** (or **Back to your sequences**) to switch views.

Empty state: **A sequence is a series of emails you write once. Campium sends each one on its day: after a family registers for a session, before the session starts, after it ends, or after someone becomes a lead.** Then **New Sequence**.

When a sequence has replies that could not be forwarded, its row shows a chip such as **1 reply not delivered** or **N replies not delivered**. See [Undelivered replies on the list](#undelivered-replies-on-the-list).

## Create a sequence

1. Click **New Sequence**.
2. Enter a **Name** (required).
3. Choose **Starts when**:
   - **Session registration** - Families get these emails when they register for the sessions you pick.
   - **New lead** - People get these emails when their status is set to Lead, including inquiries from your website form.
   - **Added from a list** - You add people from a report or a group, and they get these emails from that day.
4. Save. The sequence opens as a **Draft**. Nobody gets these emails until you turn it on.

## Work on one sequence

The sequence page uses tabs:

| Tab | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Emails (N)** | Write and edit each email in place |
| **People (N)** | Who is enrolled, their status, and **Remove from sequence** |
| **Sent** | What the sequence has already sent |
| **Settings** | Name, description, sessions (for session sequences), stop on reply |

Status badges: **Draft**, **Active**, **Paused**, **Archived**.

The bottom bar shows what the status means, then actions such as **Copy to another season** (session sequences) or **Make a copy**, **Archive** / **Restore**, **Pause**, **Turn On**, or **Resume**.

### Emails tab

Each email is a card with its timing, optional **Only** badge naming one session, subject, and a preview of the message. Cards that have already sent show a line such as **N sent · N opened · N clicked** (people, not scanners). Click **N clicked** to open the **People** tab filtered to people who clicked a link in that email. Use **Edit** to change it, or **Add Email** under the list.

The side **Progress** panel can show **Clicked a link** among its counts.

If a session sequence has no sessions yet, a panel above the emails says **Pick the sessions this sequence covers…** with **Choose sessions**. Nobody joins until you pick sessions.

When you edit:

- **When it goes out** - Days plus one of: **days after they are enrolled**, **days before the session starts**, **days after the session starts**, or **days after the session ends** (session sequences). Lead and list sequences only use days after enrollment.
- For session sequences, choose **for every session** or a single session option labeled **for** that session **only**.
- **Subject** and **Message** (required). Optionally **Start from a saved template**.
- Next to **Message**, **AI Rewrite** opens rewrite styles, then **Original** vs **Rewritten**. Choose **Use This Text** or cancel. It does not save until you click **Save Email**.
- **Send test to me**, **Cancel**, **Save Email**, or **Delete**. Leaving with unsaved edits asks **Discard your changes to this email?** (**Yes, discard** / **Keep editing**).

The side column **How it runs** shows **Starts when**, **Sessions** (or **Pick sessions**), **Sends at** (**9am your time**), **Replies go to**, and **Stop when they reply**. **Progress** counts link into the **People** tab. **Merge fields** insert tags such as `{first_name}`, `{campers}`, `{session_name}`, `{session_start_date}`, `{session_end_date}`, `{days_until_session}`, `{agency_name}` (your camp's name), and `{magic_login}` where you are typing.

Helper note: if someone joins after an email's day has passed, they skip that email. Changes apply to everyone's future emails.

If a reply could not be forwarded, **Replies we could not deliver** appears above the emails with **Read** and **Done**.

### People tab

Shows who is in the sequence: **Name**, optional **Session**, **Status**, **Enrolled**, **Emails sent**, and **Remove from sequence** while in progress.

Statuses include **In progress**, **Finished**, **Converted** (lead sequences), **Replied**, **Stopped (no longer registered)**, and **Removed**.

For **Added from a list** sequences, helper text points you to **Add to Sequence** in the **Email this group** window on a report or group.

### Sent tab

Columns: **Sent**, **To**, **Email**, **Status**, **Opened**, **Clicked**, **Replied**.

Above or beside the send log, **Links people clicked** lists which tracked links people opened. Helper text: people, not clicks. Link scanners are not counted. Columns: **Link**, **Email**, **People**, **Clicks**.

### Settings tab

- **Name** and **Description** (staff only).
- **Starts when** is fixed after create; helper text explains that trigger.
- Session sequences:
  - **Season** (required). Placeholder **Choose a season**.
  - **Sessions** (required) with **Choose sessions**. The checklist modal filters with **Filter, like Week**, offers **Select all (N)** and **None** per season group, shows **N sessions picked**, and applies with **Use 1 session** or **Use N sessions**.
  - Helper text: one sequence can cover many sessions (for example every week of summer). Each email set to **for every session** goes out for each session, timed to that session. Families join when a camper's registration for one of those sessions is active. Waitlisted and pending campers join once moved to active.
- **Stop sending to someone who replies** - Replies are noted and forwarded to your **Contact Email Address** when reply tracking is on. Automatic replies (such as out of office) do not count.
- **Save Settings**.

**Copy to another season** uses the same **Choose sessions** checklist (same-named sessions can be pre-checked).

## Turn a sequence on

1. Finish at least one email with a subject and message.
2. For session sequences, choose a **Season** and sessions on **Settings** (or **Choose sessions** from the Emails tab).
3. Confirm **Contact Email Address** under **Admin → Site Settings → Basic**.
4. Click **Turn On**.

The confirm modal explains that matching people join automatically from now on. If people already match, you can check **Also enroll the N families who already match** (or **leads** / **people**, depending on how the sequence starts). They skip any email whose day has already passed.

Problems before the modal opens (shown as a warning):

- **Add at least one email first.**
- **Pick the sessions that start this sequence on the Settings tab.**
- **Add a parent contact email in Site Settings, so replies reach you.**

**Pause** holds pending sends. Emails that came due while paused are skipped when you **Resume**. **Archive** stops the sequence; **Restore** brings it back.

## Undelivered replies on the list

When reply tracking cannot forward a reply to your contact email, Campium keeps it for you to read in admin.

- On **Communications → Sequences**, a sequence row can show **1 reply not delivered** or **N replies not delivered**.
- A camp-wide panel (title such as **1 reply we could not deliver**) lists recent undelivered replies with **Read**, **Open sequence**, and **Done**. Overflow text can say **And N more…**.
- The same panel also appears at the top of **Communications → Mass Emails** when Sequences is on for your camp (not on the Archived sequences view).

Read replies here and answer from your own email. Each undelivered reply is removed after a set number of days.

## Email sequences on a profile

Open a person's profile **Emails** tab. When they (or their family) are in a sequence, the **Email sequences** card lists **Sequence**, **Status**, **Joined**, and **Emails sent**. While enrollment is active, use **Remove from sequence**. If the sequence is paused, you see **Sequence paused** next to the status.

## Add people from a list

1. Create a sequence with **Starts when** set to **Added from a list**, write its emails, and turn it on.
2. From a report or roster, open **Email this group** (or **Send Email**).
3. When your camp has a manual sequence, the modal shows **Or add them to a sequence** with a select and **Add to Sequence**.
4. They get that sequence's emails starting today. Anyone already in it is skipped.

**Send Email** stays the primary action. Adding to a sequence does not replace sending a one-off email.
