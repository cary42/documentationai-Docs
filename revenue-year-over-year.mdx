Use **Revenue Year Over Year** when you want the dashboard year-over-year revenue card as its own report: pick a current season and a compare season, then see a chart and week table side by side.

You need the financial module on, plus the **Financial Snapshot** and **Charts** permissions that unlock the other chart money reports. The report is not on the free plan.

## Open the report

1. Go to **Reports → System Reports → Money → Revenue Year Over Year**, or open it from the chart reports button row on a related chart page.
2. Choose **Current season**.
3. Choose **Compare to** a different season.

If both picks are the same, Campium asks you to **Select two different seasons to compare.** When neither season has revenue in the shared pace window, you see **No revenue in this range.**

## How the comparison works

Campium lines the seasons up by days from each season's own start, not by the same calendar date a year earlier. The note under the chart explains that the compare season stops the same number of days after its own start as the current season has so far.

The table columns are **Week**, the current season name, the compare season name, and **Difference**.
