## Purpose of this report

Use **Paying vs Non-paying** to see, for each season, how many families and campers are paying versus enrolled at no net cost after discounts. Click a count to open the list of people in that group.

A person is **paying** when their charges in that season still net above $0 after discounts. A family is **paying** when at least one enrolled person in that season does.

<Callout kind="info">

The report appears under **Reports → System Reports** in the **Money** group when your account includes finance reporting. It is not available on free plans. You need report and finance access.

</Callout>

## Run the report

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the report" icon="folder">

1. Go to **Reports → System Reports**.
2. Under **Money**, open **Paying vs Non-paying**.
3. Confirm the page title is **Paying vs Non-paying**.

</Step>

<Step title="Read the Seasons table" icon="bar-chart">

The **Seasons** panel lists every season with enrollments. Columns:

| Column group | Columns |
| --- | --- |
| **Families** | **Paying**, **Non-paying**, **Enrolled** |
| Your camper or member plural label | **Paying**, **Non-paying**, **Enrolled** |

**Enrolled** counts are plain numbers. Non-zero **Paying** and **Non-paying** counts are buttons you can click.

If nobody is enrolled in any season, the page shows: **No seasons have anyone enrolled yet.**

</Step>

<Step title="Drill into a group" icon="users">

1. Click a **Paying** or **Non-paying** count for families or for people.
2. A second panel opens with a title such as **Paying families, [season]** or **Non-paying [people], [season]** (using your person plural label).
3. For a family drill-in, columns are **Family**, **Enrolled**, and **Paying**.
4. For a person drill-in, the table lists the people in that group.
5. Use the export toolbar on the detail table (**Copy**, **Excel**, **CSV**, **PDF**, **Print**, **Email**) when you need a list off the screen.

Empty detail: **No families in this group.**

</Step>

</Steps>
