## Overview

Use Season Stats for a quick, visual read on how a season is performing. It brings your recent payments, revenue, new registrations, and card-versus-bank mix together on one page so you can see momentum at a glance.

Use the season selector at the top to switch which season the charts reflect.

## What it shows

Chart titles change when you pick a season that has **Season Start Date** and **Season End Date**. Without a season window, recent charts use a rolling last-7-days label and monthly charts use a longer rolling window.

<ParamField body="Payments Processed - Last 7 days" param-type="chart" show-location="false">
Daily total of successful card and bank payments. With a season selected, the title becomes **Payments Processed - 7 days to** the end of the season window (or today if the season has not ended yet).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Payments By Month - Last 18 Months" param-type="chart" show-location="false">
Monthly payment totals. With a season selected, the title becomes **Payments By Month -** followed by the season start and end dates (for example `Jun 1, 2026 - Aug 15, 2026`).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Revenue By Day - Last 7 days" param-type="chart" show-location="false">
Registration revenue booked each day. Follows the same last-7-days versus season-window title pattern as payments.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Revenue By Month - Last 18 Months" param-type="chart" show-location="false">
Monthly revenue totals. With a season selected, uses the season start and end dates in the title.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="New Children In System" param-type="chart" show-location="false">
How many new campers were added each day over the past week.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="CC vs ACH - # of Transactions" param-type="chart" show-location="false">
A pie chart comparing how many payments came in by credit card versus bank (ACH) over the last 12 months.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="CC vs ACH - Transaction $ Amount" param-type="chart" show-location="false">
A pie chart comparing the dollar amount paid by credit card versus bank (ACH) over the last 12 months.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info">
  Payment and revenue figures count only successful, processed payments. A chart may be hidden when there is not enough recent activity to plot.
</Callout>

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Season Stats" icon="folder" titleType="p">
    Go to **Reports** &gt; **System** and select the season stats dashboard, or reach it from the main dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a season" icon="calendar" titleType="p">
    Use the season selector at the top to change which season the charts reflect.
  </Step>
</Steps>
