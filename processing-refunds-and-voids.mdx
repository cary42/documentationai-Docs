## Overview

Process refunds and voids for credit card and ACH payments from the **Sales** tab in an individual user’s profile. You can also remove scheduled or future payments from the same place.

Refunds are available only after a payment settles. Before settlement, you may see a **Void** or **Remove** option instead of **Refund**, depending on whether the payment has already run or is still scheduled.

## When refunds are available

Refunds become available once the original charge has fully settled to your merchant account.

- For **credit card payments**, this is typically the next day.

- For **ACH payments**, settlement typically takes **3-5 business days**. Until an ACH payment settles, Campium does not offer **refund**. Use **void** to cancel the debit instead.

Before settlement, the payment is still pending and you will see a **Void** option instead of **Refund**. Voiding cancels the payment before it completes.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  If you open **refund** on an ACH payment that has not settled yet, Campium shows: **This ACH payment has not settled yet. Void it to cancel the debit. You can refund only after it settles.** Use **Void this payment** (or **Close**), then issue a refund after settlement. The same message appears on profile payments and on **Canteen** → **Payments**.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  If the transaction has not settled yet, you see a **Void** option that cancels the pending payment. Depending on your daily batching cutoff, you may see a message indicating you can no longer void the transaction. When that message appears, wait for the payment to settle, then issue a refund instead of a void.
</Callout>

Once the payment settles, the **Void** option is no longer available and a **Refund** option appears for that transaction.

## How to issue a refund

Use the Sales tab in a user’s profile to refund completed credit card or ACH payments.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the user's Sales tab" icon="users" title-type="p">
    - Go to the relevant user’s profile.

    - Click the **Sales** tab to view their charges and payments.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the completed payment" icon="search" title-type="p">
    - In the payment list, find the completed **credit card** or **ACH** payment you want to refund.

    - Hover over the payment row until a **Refund** link appears.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter refund details" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    - Click the **Refund** link.

    - Enter the **amount** you want to refund. You can issue a full or partial refund.

    - Enter a **reason** for the refund.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the refund" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    - Review the refund amount and reason, then confirm the refund.

    - The refund appears on the family’s invoice along with the reason you entered, so they can clearly see why the adjustment was made.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  As of December 2, 2025, the **Also Refund Surcharge** checkbox is available when refunding credit card payments that included a surcharge. See the sections below for full details on how this works.
</Callout>

## Canceling scheduled or future payments

Cancel scheduled or future payments from the same Sales tab in the user’s profile.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the user's Sales tab" icon="users" title-type="p">
    - Go to the user’s profile.

    - Click the **Sales** tab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Remove the future payment" icon="trash" title-type="p">
    - Find the scheduled or future payment you want to cancel.

    - Follow the same steps you use to access refunds and voids. Instead of a **Refund** or **Void** link, you see a **Remove** option.

    - Click **Remove** to cancel the scheduled or future payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The payment will no longer process on the scheduled date once you remove it.

## Refunds and credit card surcharges

When you refund a credit card payment that included a surcharge, the **Amount** field represents the camp balance portion only, not the full amount charged to the card. By default, **Also Refund Surcharge** is checked so the fee is returned proportionally with the refund. Uncheck it only when you intentionally want to keep the surcharge (not available on Finix; see below). A **Void** works differently: it cancels the full original card charge before settlement and always reverses both the payment amount and the surcharge.

## How a credit card payment is stored

Campium stores each credit card payment as two separate parts so the camp balance and card processing fee stay distinct in payments, refunds, and reporting.

| Part | What it is | Example ($100 camp fee + 2.5% surcharge) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Applied amount (camp balance) | What reduces the family's balance | $100.00 |
| Surcharge | Card processing fee added on top | $2.50 |
| Total charged to card | Applied amount + surcharge | $102.50 |

On the profile **Payments** tab, you see the applied amount on the main payment line and **Surcharge** as a separate line underneath when a fee was charged. ACH and debit card payments usually do not include a surcharge, so the **Also Refund Surcharge** checkbox does not appear for those payments.

## Refund vs void

Use a void for same-day or not-yet-settled card payments when you need to cancel the entire transaction. Use a refund after settlement or any time you need to return only part of the camp amount.

| Action | When to use it | What it does | Surcharge behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Void | Same day or before settlement | Cancels the entire original transaction | Always reverses the full surcharge automatically; no checkbox |
| Refund | After settlement or when returning only part of the payment | Sends money back after the original transaction has settled | **Also Refund Surcharge** is checked by default; on Finix it is required and locked |

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">

The **Amount** you type is always the camp balance portion. Whether the surcharge is also returned depends on **Also Refund Surcharge** (checked by default, and required on Finix).

</Callout>

## Also Refund Surcharge: what it does

The **Also Refund Surcharge** checkbox appears only when you are issuing a refund, not a void, and only if the original payment included a surcharge. If the payment had no surcharge, there is no fee to return and the checkbox does not appear. ACH and debit card payments usually do not include a surcharge.

When the checkbox is checked (the default), Campium calculates an additional surcharge refund using the current surcharge rate from **Settings**, then credits the card for the entered **Amount** plus the surcharge refund. When you uncheck it (processors other than Finix), Campium refunds only the amount you enter and your organization keeps the surcharge.

### Finix

On Finix, card-network rules require the surcharge to be refunded with the payment. The checkbox shows as **Also Refund Surcharge (required)**, stays checked, and cannot be turned off. A note on the refund form explains that the surcharge is included automatically.

### Other processors

On Payabli, USIO, and similar processors, the checkbox starts checked with a note that the surcharge is refunded proportionally by default. Uncheck it only if the surcharge should be kept.

The calculation is:

`surcharge refund = Amount × (surcharge rate ÷ 100)`

Campium rounds the surcharge refund to the nearest cent. The surcharge rate is read from settings at the time of the refund, not recalculated from the original payment record. If your surcharge percentage changed after the family paid, the refunded fee may differ slightly from the original surcharge amount.

## Examples (2.5% surcharge rate)

Assume a family paid **$100.00** toward camp and **$2.50** in surcharge, for a total card charge of **$102.50**. On Finix, only the **Checked** rows apply (the checkbox cannot be turned off).

### Full refund of the camp portion

| Amount entered | Also Refund Surcharge | Total returned to card | Camp balance credit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $100.00 | Checked (default) | $102.50 | $100.00 |
| $100.00 | Unchecked (non-Finix only) | $100.00 | $100.00 |

### Partial refund (50%)

| Amount entered | Also Refund Surcharge | Total returned to card | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $50.00 | Checked (default) | $51.25 | $50.00 + $1.25 surcharge refund |
| $50.00 | Unchecked (non-Finix only) | $50.00 | Half of the camp amount only |

### Another partial example

If the original camp portion was **$200.00** and the surcharge was **$5.00**, the total card charge was **$205.00**. Refunding **$80.00** with the checkbox checked (the default) returns **$82.00**, because 2.5% of $80.00 is $2.00. On a non-Finix processor, unchecking the box returns **$80.00** only.

## What families see

- The **Reason** you enter appears on the family invoice.
- Refund rows show the applied amount and, when applicable, a separate **Surcharge** line.
- Parent portal terms may state that processing fees are non-refundable. Your staff choice with **Also Refund Surcharge** (or Finix's required inclusion) controls whether the fee is returned on that refund.

## Reporting and QuickBooks

Revenue Summary reporting includes **Refunds - Credit Card Surcharge (Refunds & Voids)** so you can track surcharge income that was reversed. When QuickBooks sync is enabled, refund rows are treated as the principal refund amount for camp charges plus a separate surcharge or convenience fee reversal when the surcharge was refunded.

## Canteen payments

Canteen refunds use a separate workflow in **Canteen** → **Payments**. The canteen refund form does not include **Also Refund Surcharge**, so a partial refund returns only the amount you enter and does not add any surcharge refund. A void still reverses the full amount and the full surcharge automatically.

## Common staff questions

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="If I refund the full amount shown on the card, do I get the surcharge back?">

The **Amount** field is the camp portion, not the full card total. For a $102.50 card charge made up of $100.00 camp charges and a $2.50 surcharge, enter **$100.00**. With **Also Refund Surcharge** checked (the default, and required on Finix), the family receives the full $102.50.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="If I refund 50% of the total, is only 50% of the surcharge refunded?">

Refund 50% of the camp amount, not 50% of the full card charge. For a $100.00 camp amount, enter **$50.00**. With **Also Refund Surcharge** checked, Campium refunds $1.25 in surcharge at a 2.5% rate. If the rate has not changed, that matches half of the original $2.50 surcharge.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What if I leave the checkbox unchecked?">

On processors other than Finix, the family receives only the **Amount** you entered and your camp keeps the surcharge. On Finix you cannot uncheck the box.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Does void always refund the surcharge?">

Yes. A void cancels the entire original transaction and automatically reverses the full surcharge. There is no separate checkbox for voids.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Why don't I see the checkbox?">

The original payment probably did not include a surcharge. This is common for ACH, debit card, cash, check, or card payments processed without a fee. The checkbox appears only for refunds of credit card payments that recorded a surcharge.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Refund amount seems too high | **Also Refund Surcharge** is checked by default. Uncheck it on non-Finix processors if you meant to keep the fee. |
| Refund amount seems too low | Confirm you entered the full camp portion you intended. On Finix the surcharge is always included when the original payment had one. |
| Refunded fee doesn't match original fee | Check whether the surcharge rate in settings changed after the original payment. Refunds use the current rate at refund time. |
| Can't refund | The payment may be outside the processor's refund window or may not be settled yet. If it is still pending, try a void. Otherwise contact support. |
| ACH refund warning (USIO) | Follow the instructions in the warning modal. Large ACH refunds may be issued as a separate credit. |

## Related settings

- **Credit Card Surcharge** in [Site Settings](/settings): the percentage used when charging cards and when calculating **Also Refund Surcharge**.
- Debit, prepaid, and HSA/FSA cards and ACH/e-check are never surcharged, so those payments do not show the checkbox.
