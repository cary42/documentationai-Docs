## Overview

You refund and void credit card and ACH payments from the **Sales** tab of a user’s profile. You also remove scheduled or future payments there.

Use a refund after a payment settles. Before then, a payment that has already run shows **void**, and a payment that is still scheduled shows **remove**.

### Who can refund or void

On **Admin → Roles & Permissions**, staff need:

- **View the 'Sales' tab** (**Users** category) to open the profile **Sales** tab
- **Process Payments** (**Financial** category) to see **refund** and **void**, and to use **Add Payment**

Without **Process Payments**, those payment action links stay hidden. If someone still reaches a refund or void action without that access, Campium shows: **You do not have permission to record payments, refunds or voids.** Removing a session from **Sales** only needs **View the 'Sales' tab**; without it, Campium shows: **You do not have permission to remove sessions.**

## When refunds are available

Refunds become available once the original charge has fully settled to your merchant account.

- For **credit card payments**, this is usually the next day.

- For **ACH payments**, settlement usually takes **3-5 business days**. Until an ACH payment settles, the row shows **Refund (after settle)** in place of the **refund** link. Use **void** to cancel the debit instead.

Before settlement, the payment is pending and the row shows **void**. Voiding cancels the payment before it completes. A card payment can show **void** and **refund** side by side during this window.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  If you open **refund** on an ACH payment that has not settled yet, Campium shows: **This ACH payment has not settled yet. Void it to cancel the debit. You can refund only after it settles.** Use **Void this payment** (or **Close**), then issue a refund after settlement. The same message appears on profile payments and on **Canteen** → **Deposits**.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The **void** link disappears after your daily batch cutoff. If you open **void** on an ACH payment that has already settled, Campium shows **This transaction has already been settled and can't be voided. You must refund at this point.** Click **Refund this payment instead**.
</Callout>

Once the void window closes, only **refund** remains for that transaction.

## Imported payments

Payments brought in from another system (for example a CampMinder import) do not show **refund** or **void** on the profile. Those rows have no processor transaction behind them, so Campium cannot reverse them the usual way.

If someone still tries to refund or void an imported payment, Campium shows: **This payment was imported from another system, so it can't be refunded or voided here. Record a credit instead.**

## How to issue a refund

Refund completed credit card or ACH payments from the **Sales** tab of a user’s profile.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the user's Sales tab" icon="users" title-type="p">
    - Go to the relevant user’s profile.

    - Click the **Sales** tab to view their charges and payments.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the completed payment" icon="search" title-type="p">
    - In the payment list, find the completed **credit card** or **ACH** payment you want to refund.

    - Hover over the payment row until a **refund** link appears.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter refund details" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    - Click the **refund** link.

    - Enter the **Amount** you want to refund. You can issue a full or partial refund.

    - Enter a **Reason** for the refund.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the refund" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    - Review the amount and reason, then click **Refund**.

    - The refund and the reason you entered appear on the family’s invoice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  When a credit card payment included a surcharge, the refund form also handles the surcharge: an **Also Refund Surcharge** checkbox on most processors, and an automatic refund on Finix. See the sections below.
</Callout>

## Canceling scheduled or future payments

Cancel scheduled or future payments from the same **Sales** tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the user's Sales tab" icon="users" title-type="p">
    - Go to the user’s profile.

    - Click the **Sales** tab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Remove the future payment" icon="trash" title-type="p">
    - Find the scheduled or future payment you want to cancel.

    - Hover over the payment row. A scheduled payment shows a **remove** link instead of **refund** or **void**.

    - Click **remove**, then confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After you remove it, the payment does not run on its scheduled date.

## Refunds and credit card surcharges

When you refund a credit card payment that included a surcharge, the **Amount** field represents the camp balance portion only, not the full amount charged to the card. By default, **Also Refund Surcharge** is checked so the fee is returned proportionally with the refund. Uncheck it only when you want to keep the surcharge (not possible on Finix; see below). A **Void** works differently: it cancels the full original card charge before settlement and always reverses both the payment amount and the surcharge.

## How a credit card payment is stored

Campium stores each credit card payment as two separate parts so the camp balance and card processing fee stay distinct in payments, refunds, and reporting.

| Part | What it is | Example ($100 camp fee + 2.5% surcharge) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Applied amount (camp balance) | What reduces the family's balance | $100.00 |
| Surcharge | Card processing fee added on top | $2.50 |
| Total charged to card | Applied amount + surcharge | $102.50 |

In the **Payments** list on the profile **Sales** tab, the main payment line shows the applied amount, and a **Surcharge:** line underneath shows the fee when one was charged. ACH and debit card payments are never surcharged, so the **Also Refund Surcharge** checkbox does not appear for them.

## Refund vs void

Use a void for same-day or not-yet-settled card payments when you need to cancel the entire transaction. Use a refund after settlement or any time you need to return only part of the camp amount.

| Action | When to use it | What it does | Surcharge behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Void | Same day or before settlement | Cancels the entire original transaction | Always reverses the full surcharge automatically; no checkbox |
| Refund | After settlement or when returning only part of the payment | Sends money back after the original transaction has settled | **Also Refund Surcharge** is checked by default; on Finix there is no checkbox and the surcharge is always refunded |

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">

The **Amount** you type is always the camp balance portion. Whether the surcharge is also returned depends on **Also Refund Surcharge** (checked by default). On Finix the surcharge is always returned.

</Callout>

## Also Refund Surcharge: what it does

The **Also Refund Surcharge** checkbox appears only on a refund, not a void, only if the original payment included a surcharge, and only on processors other than Finix. If the payment had no surcharge, there is no fee to return and the checkbox does not appear. ACH and debit card payments are never surcharged.

When the checkbox is checked (the default), Campium calculates an additional surcharge refund from the **original surcharge** on that payment, then credits the card for the entered **Amount** plus the surcharge refund. When you uncheck it (processors other than Finix), Campium refunds only the amount you enter and your organization keeps the surcharge.

Under the checkbox, a live helper line updates as you change the **Amount**. Examples:

- **Returns $1.25 of the $2.50 surcharge.**
- **Returns the full $2.50 surcharge.**
- **Keeps the full $2.50 surcharge.**
- **Enter an amount to see how much of the $2.50 surcharge comes back.**

While the surcharge is included, a breakdown below the **Amount** shows **Refund amount**, **Surcharge refund**, and **Total refund**.

### Finix

On Finix, card-network rules require the surcharge to be refunded with the payment. The refund form has no checkbox. A note at the top reads **Card-network rules require the surcharge to be refunded with the payment, so it is included automatically.**, and the breakdown shows the surcharge added to the total.

### Other processors

On Payabli, USIO, and similar processors, the checkbox starts checked, with the note **The surcharge is refunded proportionally by default. Uncheck it only if the surcharge should be kept.**

The calculation is:

`surcharge refund = original surcharge × (Amount ÷ original camp portion)`

Campium rounds the surcharge refund to the nearest cent. The fee comes from what was charged on the original payment, not from re-applying today's **Credit Card Surcharge** percentage. Changing the percentage in settings after the family paid does not change how much surcharge is returned on that payment.

## Examples (original payment had a 2.5% surcharge)

Assume a family paid **$100.00** toward camp and **$2.50** in surcharge, for a total card charge of **$102.50**. On Finix, only the **Checked** rows apply, because the surcharge is always included.

### Full refund of the camp portion

| Amount entered | Also Refund Surcharge | Total returned to card | Camp balance credit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $100.00 | Checked (default) | $102.50 | $100.00 |
| $100.00 | Unchecked (non-Finix only) | $100.00 | $100.00 |

### Partial refund (50%)

| Amount entered | Also Refund Surcharge | Total returned to card | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $50.00 | Checked (default) | $51.25 | $50.00 + half of the original $2.50 surcharge |
| $50.00 | Unchecked (non-Finix only) | $50.00 | Half of the camp amount only |

### Another partial example

If the original camp portion was **$200.00** and the surcharge was **$5.00**, the total card charge was **$205.00**. Refunding **$80.00** with the checkbox checked (the default) returns **$82.00**, because $5.00 × ($80.00 ÷ $200.00) is $2.00. On a non-Finix processor, unchecking the box returns **$80.00** only.

## What families see

- The **Reason** you enter appears on the family invoice.
- Refund rows show the applied amount and, when applicable, a separate **Surcharge** line.
- Parent portal terms may state that processing fees are non-refundable. **Also Refund Surcharge** (or, on Finix, the automatic inclusion) decides whether the fee is returned on that refund.

## Reporting and QuickBooks

The Revenue Summary report includes a **Refunds - Credit Card Surcharge (Refunds & Voids)** row, so you can track surcharge income that was reversed. When QuickBooks sync is on, a refund syncs as the principal refund for camp charges plus a separate surcharge or convenience fee reversal when the surcharge was refunded.

## Canteen payments

Canteen refunds use a separate workflow in **Canteen** → **Deposits**. The canteen refund form does not include **Also Refund Surcharge**, so a partial refund returns only the amount you enter and does not add any surcharge refund. A void still reverses the full amount and the full surcharge automatically.

## Common staff questions

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="If I refund the full amount shown on the card, do I get the surcharge back?">

The **Amount** field is the camp portion, not the full card total. For a $102.50 card charge made up of $100.00 camp charges and a $2.50 surcharge, enter **$100.00**. With **Also Refund Surcharge** checked (the default, and automatic on Finix), the family receives the full $102.50.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="If I refund 50% of the total, is only 50% of the surcharge refunded?">

Refund 50% of the camp amount, not 50% of the full card charge. For a $100.00 camp amount with a $2.50 surcharge, enter **$50.00**. With **Also Refund Surcharge** checked, Campium refunds $1.25 in surcharge (half of the original fee).

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What if I leave the checkbox unchecked?">

On processors other than Finix, the family receives only the **Amount** you entered and your camp keeps the surcharge. On Finix there is no checkbox: the surcharge is always refunded.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Does void always refund the surcharge?">

Yes. A void cancels the entire original transaction and automatically reverses the full surcharge. There is no separate checkbox for voids.

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Why don't I see the checkbox?">

The original payment probably did not include a surcharge. This is common for ACH, debit card, cash, check, or card payments processed without a fee. The checkbox appears only for refunds of credit card payments that recorded a surcharge. On Finix it never appears, because the surcharge is always refunded.

  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Refund amount seems too high | **Also Refund Surcharge** is checked by default. Uncheck it on non-Finix processors if you meant to keep the fee. |
| Refund amount seems too low | Confirm you entered the full camp portion you intended. On Finix the surcharge is always included when the original payment had one. |
| Refunded fee doesn't match original fee | Surcharge refunds prorate the **original** fee on that payment. Confirm the original surcharge line on the payment, and that you entered the camp **Amount** you intended. |
| Can't refund | Payments more than 365 days old cannot be refunded: the form shows **This transaction is more than 365 days old and can not be refunded.** An ACH payment that has not settled yet cannot be refunded; void it instead. Otherwise contact support. |
| ACH refund warning | On USIO, Finix, and Paysafe, **Heads up before refunding this ACH** means the debit already went to the bank, so the refund posts as a separate ACH credit. Read the warning before you click **Refund**. |

## Related settings

- **Credit Card Surcharge** on **Admin → Site Settings → Payment Info** ([Site Settings](/settings)): the percentage used when charging cards. Surcharge refunds use the fee recorded on the original payment, not a fresh percentage from settings.
- Debit, prepaid, and HSA/FSA cards and ACH/e-check are never surcharged, so those payments do not show the checkbox.
- [Payment Methods](/payment-methods) for which methods families can use at checkout and which ones you record by hand.
