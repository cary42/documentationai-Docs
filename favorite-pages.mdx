title: "Favorite pages"
description: "Save admin pages you use often with the heart in the top bar, organize them in folders, and open them again from Favorites."
---

Pin the admin pages you return to most often. Favorites live in the top bar heart menu and are private to your signed-in user.

## Find Favorites

1. Sign in to CampiumDB.
2. Look at the top bar (right side, near other shortcuts such as your todo list).
3. Click the **heart** button (**Your favorite pages**).

The menu title is **Favorites**.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Favorites are available to every signed-in staff user. There is no separate module toggle. Each person only sees their own list.
</Callout>

## Save the page you are on

1. Open the page you want to keep.
2. Click the heart to open **Favorites**.
3. Click **Save this page**.

The heart turns solid (filled) when the current page is already saved. In the menu, the same row reads **Saved - remove**. Click it again to remove the page from your list.

If the current URL cannot be favorited, the menu shows **This page can't be saved as a favorite.** instead of the save row.

When you have not saved anything yet, the empty state says **Nothing saved yet. Use "Save this page" above to keep the page you are on.**

## Open a saved page

1. Click the heart.
2. Click a page name in the list.

Pages that are not in a folder appear at the top. Folders appear below and start collapsed. Click a folder name to expand or collapse it.

## Manage favorites (rename, folders, reorder)

1. Open **Favorites**.
2. Click **Manage favorites**.

In the manage dialog:

- Helper text: **Drag to reorder or to move a page between folders.**
- **New folder** creates a folder.
- **Ungrouped** holds pages that are not in a folder. You cannot rename or delete **Ungrouped**.
- Drag the handle next to a page to reorder it or move it into a folder.
- Drag the handle next to a folder name to reorder folders.
- Use the pencil control to rename a page or folder.
- Use the trash control to remove a page or delete a folder.

Empty folders show **Drag a page here.** If you have no favorites yet, the dialog says **You have not saved any pages yet.** and **Tap the heart in the top bar to save the page you are on.**

## Tips

- Favorites store the page URL you reached, so they are a shortcut for pages you already have permission to open.
- Renaming a favorite only changes the label in your list. It does not rename the page itself.
