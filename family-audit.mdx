Use **Family Audit** when you need a season snapshot for auditors or your bookkeeping team: every family that had activity in that season, with money totals as of a date you choose.

You need **Financials** access (financial module on). The report is not on the free plan. It also appears under **Reports → System Reports → Money**.

## Open the report

1. Go to **Financials → Family Audit** (next to **Family Ledger**), or open **Family Audit** under **Reports → System Reports → Money**.
2. Pick a **Season** in the page header.
3. Set **As of** to the date you want. Totals include charges, payments, refunds, and credits recorded through the end of that day.
4. If no season is selected, the page shows **Pick a season to run the audit.**

The page title includes the season name. The subtitle reads like **Every family's charges, refunds, credits, payments and balance as of** the long date.

## Columns

Each row is one family.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Family** | Last name(s) for the household. Click to open the primary guardian's profile. |
| **Adults** | First names of the primary and secondary guardians. |
| **Children** | First names of children in the family. A sibling with no enrollment in that season shows **(Not Enrolled)** after their name. Enrolled children have no extra label. |
| **City/State** | City and state from the primary guardian. |
| **Charges** | Sale lines for the season that were active as of the date (not yet cancelled by then). |
| **Refunds** | Card or bank refunds recorded by that date. |
| **Credits** | Credits, discounts, and adjustments applied by that date. |
| **Paid Non-CC** | Payments other than card (cash, check, ACH, and similar) by that date. |
| **Paid CC** | Card payments by that date. |
| **Paid Total** | **Paid Non-CC** plus **Paid CC**. |
| **Balance** | What the family still owed for that season as of the date (charges minus credits and payments, plus refunds). |

A **Totals** footer sums the money columns across every family on the list.

## Export

Use the table toolbar **Copy**, **Excel**, **CSV**, **PDF**, or **Print** controls. PDF and Print include the as-of date and page numbers. The list is built for a short PDF of the whole season.
