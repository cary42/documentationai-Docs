## Overview

Use Transportation in CampiumDB to manage buses, assign campers as riders, track buses on a map, and review pickup privileges for dismissal.

Transportation has two main parts:

- **Buses**: manage the bus list, assign and order riders, view a live map, and email riders.
- **Pickup privileges**: see who is allowed to pick up a camper, block guardians when needed, and run the **Pickup Report**.

<Callout kind="info">

Transportation appears in the admin portal when the Transportation module is on for your camp and your role has the **Transportation Management** permission.

</Callout>

## Who uses this

<Callout kind="info">

Transportation involves three groups:

- **Transportation coordinators / office staff**: set up buses and riders; monitor routes.
- **Bus staff**: track the bus, mark riders, and log notes from **Bus Tracking** in the Campium mobile app.
- **Admins / super users**: choose which profile field or fields represent buses and control who can access Transportation.

</Callout>

## Getting to Transportation

Access Transportation features from the admin portal:

- **Transportation**: click **Transportation** in the left menu to open the **Bus Information** page, with the **Buses** and **Map** tabs and each bus's Riders page.
- **Reports**: open **Reports → System Reports → Attendance → Pickup Report** when you need authorized pickups or blocked guardians.

## Buses: basics

### What is a bus in CampiumDB

A bus is a named route or vehicle you assign campers to as riders and, optionally, track live on a map.

You use buses to:

- Assign campers as **riders** for transportation.
- Order riders in the sequence they will be picked up or dropped off.
- Track the bus live on a map when tracking is on.

### The Bus list page

The **Buses** tab on the **Bus Information** page summarizes each bus. Its columns are:

- **Vehicle Name**
- **ETA**
- **Tracking**: **Tracking On** with a **map** link and **Turn off** while the bus is tracking; blank otherwise
- **# Riders**
- **Arrival Time** (optional)
- **Default Destination** (optional)

Use the Bus list to confirm buses exist, review rider counts, and open a bus's Riders page or the **Map** tab.

#### Print Roster

On the Bus Information page (Buses tab) and on a bus **Riders** page, click **Print Roster**. The **Print Options** modal offers:

- **Condensed: one line for each rider** (on by default)
- **Print Without Columns**
- **Print With Check-In / Check-Out**

The roster opens as a PDF in a new tab, in the same layout as Attendance Sheets.

The **Tracking** column refreshes every 30 seconds. The **Map** tab refreshes bus locations every 15 seconds, so a marker can lag slightly behind the bus. After 20 minutes without activity on the map, updates pause with **Live map updates paused after 20 minutes of inactivity.** Click **Resume updates** to start them again.

<Callout kind="info">

When tracking is on, families can view their child's assigned bus in the Campium mobile app or parent portal.

</Callout>

### The Riders page

The Riders page lists the riders assigned to one bus.

Open it from **Transportation** in the left menu, then click a bus name.

On the Riders page, staff can:

- Review the rider list for that bus.
- Check riders in as they board (**Check-in** column, when Attendance is on).
- See which riders bus staff marked **Passed** in the app, and when (**Marked At**).
- Reorder riders.
- Edit stop times and addresses.
- Email the riders on that bus with **Email Riders**.

#### Filter by attendance date

When the **Attendance Module** is on in **Admin → Site Settings → Modules**, the Riders page shows a **Filter by attendance date** switch. Flipping it refreshes the riders list without reloading the page.

- **On** (default when you last left it on): the list follows the selected attendance date, with session-today and check-in style columns as before. Your preference is saved for your staff login.
- **Off**: the page shows all active riders on this bus. Helper text reads **When off, shows all active riders on this bus.**

When the filter is on and some riders match the date, a summary line appears above the table, for example **Showing 14 of 41 riders for Fri, Sep 11.**

When the filter is on and nobody on that bus has an active session that day, the table is replaced with:

- Heading: **No riders have an active session on this date.**
- Detail such as **41 active riders are on this bus. Pick another date above, or show all active riders.**
- Button: **Show all active riders**, which turns the filter off.

If Attendance is off for your camp, the switch does not appear.

The rider statuses mean different things: **Check-in** confirms the rider boarded, **Passed** shows the bus has gone past that stop, and **Absent** shows the rider was not present for that run. Bus staff mark **Passed** and **Absent** from **Bus Tracking** in the Campium mobile app.

### Change a rider's stop address

On the **Riders** page, open a rider's address to change where they are picked up or dropped off for a given attendance day. The dialog title is **Change Address**.

Door-to-door riders:

1. Under **Pick up and drop off at**, choose **Parent 1**, **Parent 2**, or **Other address**.
2. For **Other address**, type the full stop (placeholder: **Street, city, state, zip**).
3. Optionally check weekdays under **Repeat every week on**. Leave them unticked to change that day only.
4. Click **Save** (or **Cancel**).

Express riders (when the bus field uses Express stops):

1. Choose an **Express stop**, or pick **Parent 1**, **Parent 2**, or **Other address** the same way door-to-door riders do.
2. Optionally set **Repeat every week on**, then **Save**.

If no Express list is linked yet, the dialog explains that you need to pick one under **Express Stops** in **Bus Settings** on the bus page. On the Bus Information page, **Express Stops** maps each bus field to the profile field that lists its stops. **No Express stops** means that bus field has none.

When a weekly rule already exists, the dialog can show:

- **Stop repeating on** a weekday
- **Change the weekly rule**
- **Remove the change for** a one-day override

A new weekly rule starts on the day you save it (it does not rewrite earlier roster days). Unticking every weekday under **Change the weekly rule** asks you to confirm, then stops the rule. Removing a one-day override can toast **Change removed**.

If a typed **Other address** cannot be placed on the map, the address still saves and Campium warns: **We couldn't find this address on the map. Check the spelling.**

Saving a weekly rule over one-day changes asks you to confirm **Yes, replace** when those one-day stops would be overwritten. Press Escape to cancel a replace confirmation without leaving the dialog. Enter saves when the form is ready.

You need the **Transportation** module and the **Transportation** permission.

### Bus Notes

Bus staff can log notes from **Bus Tracking** in the Campium mobile app. In admin, open **Transportation**, then click **Bus Notes** at the top of the **Bus Information** page.

Filter with **From**, **To**, **Bus** (**All buses** or one bus), and **Type** (**All types**, **Rider behavior**, **Driver issue**, **Other**, or **For Parents**), then click **Show notes**.

The table columns are **Date**, **Bus**, **Type**, **Rider**, **Note**, **Logged By**, and **Sent**. Under **Sent**:

- **Emailed** when an alert email went out for that note
- **Parents notified** when a **For Parents** note was pushed to guardians

Bus names link to that bus's riders page; rider and author names open profiles when available.

#### Email for bus note alerts

When staff bus tracking is on, set **Email for bus note alerts** under **Admin → Site Settings → Mobile App**. Bus staff can choose to email an alert to that address when they log a rider-behavior or driver-issue note. Separate several addresses with commas. Leave the field blank to turn alert emails off.

**For Parents** notes are about one rider. Guardians can see them in the Campium app (and may get a push). They are separate from the alert-email setting.

If nothing matches, you see: **No bus notes match these filters. Bus staff add notes from Bus Tracking in the Campium app.** When many notes match, a warning can say newest notes are shown and to narrow the dates or pick a bus.

You need the Transportation module and the **Transportation Management** permission, the same access as the rest of **Transportation**.

## Day-to-day workflow

Use this workflow for a season and each bus day, from setup through communication.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Set up buses for the season" icon="settings" title-type="p">

  Open the **Bus list** to confirm your buses for the season.

  - Check that each expected bus appears.
  - Confirm the **Vehicle Name** matches what staff expect to see on their lists.
  - For each bus, optionally click into:
    - **Arrival Time**
    - **Default Destination**

  If you do not see the buses you expect, go to the Troubleshooting section at the end of this page.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Add riders to a bus" icon="users" title-type="p">

  From the **Bus list**, click a bus to manage its riders on the **Riders** page.

  - Click **Add Riders**.
  - Select the campers who should be on this bus.
  - Click **Add**.

  The Riders list then shows each camper assigned to that bus.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Order riders by pickup or dropoff sequence" icon="users" title-type="p">

  Use the **Riders** page to put riders in the order the bus will serve them.

  - Drag the sort handle next to each rider.
  - Arrange riders so the list matches your planned pickup or dropoff sequence.

  Riders with no position show **Unset**. When some riders have no position, the page shows **Stop order incomplete:** with a count.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Add stop times and addresses (recommended)" icon="map" title-type="p">

  Still on the **Riders** page, fill out per-rider details.

  - Enter the **Pickup/Dropoff time** (the stop time) for each rider.
  - Open **Change Address** to set the stop for a day: **Parent 1** / **Parent 2**, **Other address**, or an **Express stop**, with optional **Repeat every week on** weekdays. See [Change a rider's stop address](#change-a-riders-stop-address).

  Tips:

  - Use a consistent time format for all riders on a route.
  - Prefer parent addresses or Express stops when you can, so staff see the same place families expect.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn tracking on before the route starts" icon="monitor" title-type="p">

  Bus staff turn tracking on from **Bus Tracking** in the Campium mobile app when the bus is ready to begin the route.

  - On the **Buses** tab, confirm the bus shows **Tracking On**.
  - Click **map**, or open the **Map** tab, to watch location updates.
  - Use **Turn off** to stop tracking from admin.

  While tracking is on, the map refreshes the bus's location every 15 seconds.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Check riders in and update rider status on bus day" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">

  On the day of transportation, open the **Riders** page with the correct date selected.

  - Find each camper as they arrive or board.
  - Click **Check-in** for each rider as they get on the bus.
  - If you check in the wrong rider, click **undo** next to the check-in time.
  - Bus staff mark **Absent** and **Passed** from the Campium mobile app.

  The Riders page then shows who is on the bus for that date and which stops the bus has passed.

  </Step>

  <Step title="View the map for live tracking" icon="monitor" title-type="p">

  Use the **Map** tab to see live location for buses with tracking enabled.

  - On the **Bus Information** page or a bus's Riders page, click the **Map** tab, or click **map** next to **Tracking On** on the Buses tab.
  - Confirm that tracking is on for the bus you want to monitor.

  The map shows each tracking bus's location and refreshes every 15 seconds. Only buses with tracking on show as live on the map.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Email riders for a bus (optional)" icon="mail" title-type="p">

  Use **Email Riders** to send updates or reminders to the riders on one bus and their families.

  - On the **Riders** page, click **Email Riders**.
  - Fill in:
    - **From Email Address**
    - **Subject**
    - **Email Body**
  - Click **Preview Email** to check the content and formatting.
  - Enter an address in **Send test to** (it defaults to your login email), then click **Send Test Email**.
  - When everything looks right, click **Send to All Riders** and confirm with **Yes, Send**.

  The email body supports the placeholders `{first_name}`, `{last_name}`, and `{email}`.

  </Step>

</Steps>

## Pickup Privileges (Dismissal): basics

### What are pickup privileges

Pickup privileges define which people are authorized to pick up a camper.

Use pickup privileges for:

- End-of-day dismissal verification.
- Front desk and security confirmation when adults arrive.
- Reporting and audit trails for who is allowed to pick up each camper.

### Add or update pickup people

Pickup people are managed from the camper profile (**More → Pickup Info**) or from the family's pickup page on the parent portal.

When you add or update a pickup person, you enter:

- **Name**
- **Relationship** to the camper
- **Phone** number

Keep these fields accurate so staff can confirm identity and contact information at dismissal.

On **Pickup Info**, use the **Guardians** table to mark a guardian **Allowed** or **Not allowed** for a camper. Turning pickup off asks for a staff-only **Reason** and records the change under **Pickup changes**. Blocked guardians do not appear at check-out. On the parent portal, families see **Parents and Guardians** with **Allowed** or **Not allowed for pickup**. See [Block a guardian from picking up a camper](/attendance#block-a-guardian-from-picking-up-a-camper).

When you remove a pickup person, CampiumDB keeps or deletes the record depending on whether it has been used. If the person is tied to past attendance activity, CampiumDB hides them but keeps the record for history. Otherwise, CampiumDB deletes them.

### Pickup Report

Open **Reports → System Reports**. Under **Attendance**, open **Pickup Report**.

Use it to:

- Review **Authorized pickups** for each camper.
- Review **Blocked guardians** (staff **Reason**, who turned pickup off, when, and **Pickup history**).
- Filter by season or session.
- Export or print from the report tools.

Details: [Pickup Report](/pickup-report). Use printed or exported copies at dismissal points where live access to the admin portal is limited.

## Troubleshooting

<ExpandableGroup>

  <Expandable title="I do not see the Bus area in navigation" default-open="false">

  If you do not see **Transportation** in the admin navigation, the Transportation module may be off for your camp, or your role may not include the **Transportation Management** permission.

  Ask an admin to review your role and grant that permission if appropriate.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="A bus is missing from the Bus list" default-open="false">

  When a bus you expect is missing from the Bus list, common causes are:

  - No bus field is set up for your camp.
  - The bus exists as a profile field option, but that field is not selected as a bus field.

  Ask a super user or admin to review Bus settings and confirm which profile fields represent buses.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="A camper does not appear in the Riders list" default-open="false">

  If a camper does not show on the Riders page for a bus, check:

  - The camper is actually assigned to that bus.
  - The camper is active in your system.
  - The camper has an **active session on that date**. Some views hide riders who are not scheduled for the selected day.

  Update the camper's bus assignment or session dates as needed, then refresh the Riders page.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="The map is blank or does not show my bus" default-open="false">

  When the **Map** tab is blank or does not show the bus you expect, check whether:

  - Tracking is off for that bus.
  - The bus is not sending location updates right now.
  - Updates paused after 20 minutes of inactivity. Click **Resume updates**.
  - Network or browser restrictions, such as ad blockers, are blocking map tiles.

  Try these steps:

  - Confirm the bus shows **Tracking On** on the Bus list.
  - Refresh the page and reopen the **Map** tab.
  - If the issue persists, check with your IT team about browser extensions or network filters.

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Email sending is failing or formatting looks wrong" default-open="false">

  If Email Riders messages do not send or look incorrect:

  - Use **Send Test Email** first to see exactly what families will receive.
  - Avoid pasting content directly from Word or other rich editors. Paste as plain text if formatting becomes messy, then reapply simple formatting in the editor.

  When a test email looks right and arrives, send to all riders again.

  </Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>

## Glossary

- **Bus**: a route or vehicle you assign riders to.
- **Rider**: a camper assigned to a bus.
- **Stop time**: the planned pickup or dropoff time for a rider.
- **Tracking**: live location sharing for a bus, displayed on the Map tab.
- **ETA**: estimated time of arrival for a bus at a stop, calculated when tracking is on.
- **Passed**: a per-rider status showing the bus has already gone by that rider's stop.
- **Absent**: a per-rider bus status showing the rider is not present for that bus run.
- **Pickup privileges**: the list of authorized pickup people for a camper.
