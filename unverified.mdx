## Overview

Use the Unverified Email Addresses Report to see parent accounts that have not clicked **Verify** in the verification email sent after they register or change their email address. Until an account is verified, that email address may not receive your important messages, so this report helps you follow up.

<Callout kind="info">

This report lists parent accounts with an email on file that are still unverified and have signed in within the last three years. It does not include filters or export options.

</Callout>

## Open the Unverified Email Addresses Report

Access the report directly from the Data Check reports area.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Go to the Unverified Email Addresses Report" icon="folder">

  - Sign in with an admin account.
  - In the main navigation, go to **Reports** and select **System**.
  - Under **Data Check**, choose **Unverified Email Addresses**.
  - Confirm the page title reads `Unverified Email Addresses`.

  When the page loads, you see a table with the columns **Name**, **Home Phone**, **Cell Phone**, **Email**, **Status**, **Last Login**, and **Resend**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Resend a verification email" icon="mail">

  - Find the account you want to prompt.
  - In the **Resend** column, select **Resend** to send a fresh verification email to that account's address.
  - A green banner reads `Verification email sent successfully.` when the message goes out.

  The account clears from the list once the owner clicks **Verify** in the email they receive.

  </Step>

</Steps>

## Report columns

Each row is a parent account with an email on file that has not been verified.

### Name

Account name, shown as `Last, First`. This is a link; select it to open the profile.

### Home Phone

Home phone number on file, so you can call the contact if the email keeps bouncing or is ignored.

### Cell Phone

Cell phone number on file, useful for a quick text reminder to verify.

### Email

The unverified email address. Confirm this address is correct with the contact, since a typo is a common reason an account never verifies.

### Status

Account status, such as Active, Inactive, Pending, Archived, Removed, Deleted, or Lead. The badge is color coded so you can quickly spot which accounts are still live.

### Last Login

The date and time the account last signed in. More recent activity means the person is engaged and more likely to respond to a resent verification email.

### Resend

A **Resend** link that sends the verification email again to the address in the **Email** column.

<Callout kind="alert">

An unverified account may miss confirmations, receipts, and reminders. If a resend does not work after a few tries, contact the account another way and correct the email address on the profile.

</Callout>
