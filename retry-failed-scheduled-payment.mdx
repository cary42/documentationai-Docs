## Retry a failed scheduled payment

Retry a failed scheduled payment when a payment-plan installment or other automatic charge did not go through. Families can retry from the parent portal. Staff can retry from a family profile or from the **Scheduled Payments** report.

A retry runs the same scheduled charge again right away with the payment method you choose. It does not create a new installment or change the payment plan schedule.

## What this is

Campium runs scheduled payments on the dates in a payment plan. If a charge fails because of a declined card, insufficient funds, an expired card, a missing payment method, an ACH return, or a similar processor error, Campium marks the scheduled payment as failed and shows an error message.

When you retry, Campium submits that failed scheduled charge again right away. You can retry with the same payment method or choose a different saved card or bank account.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled payment** | An upcoming or due automatic charge on a family account |
| **Failed scheduled payment** | A charge that was attempted and did not succeed and shows an error |
| **Retry** | Run that failed installment again now with a selected card or bank account |

<Callout kind="info">

Retry applies to the existing failed scheduled payment only. Retry does not add a new payment, move the due date, or rewrite the payment plan.

</Callout>

## Who can retry

Families only see and retry their own failed payments. Staff can retry for any family they can open in the admin portal.

| Where | Who |
| --- | --- |
| **Parent portal** → **Account** → **Payment Methods** | Logged-in family member on the account, including a primary or authorized guardian |
| **Admin portal** → family **Profile** → **Sales** tab | Staff with profile and financial access |
| **Admin portal** → **Reports** → **System Reports** → **Scheduled Payments** | Staff with report access |

## Families: retry from the parent portal

Use the parent portal when a family fixes a declined or returned scheduled payment on their own account.

### Find failed payments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Payment Methods" icon="users">

  Sign in to your camp's parent portal.

  Open **Account**, then follow the link to your saved accounts (**Add Account** or **Manage Accounts**) to reach **Payment Methods**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Failed Payments section" icon="alert-triangle">

  If your account has failed scheduled payments, a **Failed Payments** section appears below your saved payment methods.

  The table has **Date**, **Amount**, **Error**, **Method**, and **Action** columns. **Method** shows the payment method on the original attempt, or **None**. On Finix and Payabli, the **Error** column can also show a short next-step line under the processor message (for example, update the card on file, then retry).

  </Step>
</Steps>

On Finix and Payabli accounts, the parent **Account** page can also show failed scheduled payments in the payment list **Status** column (**Failed** badge, reason, optional next-step text, and a **Manage payment methods and retry** link). USIO billing shows the **Failed Payments** section with the raw error message and does not add the guided next-step line.

### Before you retry

You need at least one valid payment method on file before you can retry: a credit card or bank account, depending on what your camp accepts.

<Callout kind="tip">

If the **Retry** button is disabled, add a payment method in **Payment Methods** first. Then return to the **Failed Payments** section and refresh the page if needed.

</Callout>

If the saved card is expired or was declined, add a new method or update the existing one before you retry. Retrying with the same invalid method fails again. When Campium blocks repeats of the same decline, follow the on-screen message to use a different method or contact the camp office.

### Retry the payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a payment method" icon="credit-card">

  In the failed payment row, use the **with** dropdown to pick the payment method to charge.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the retry" icon="play-circle">

  Click **Retry**, then confirm. The prompt names the amount and the method you selected.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result" icon="check-circle">

  The page shows **Payment Retry Successful!** or **Payment Retry Failed**.

  - **Success** - the charge went through, and the row disappears after the page refreshes.
  - **Failure** - the processor declined the charge again. Read the error and any next-step line, update the payment method if needed, and retry again or contact your camp office.

  </Step>
</Steps>

You can retry with a different method than the one that originally failed. For example, you can switch from a declined card to a bank account.

## Staff: retry from a family profile

Use the family profile to retry one family's failed scheduled charge.

### Find the failed charge

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the family profile" icon="users">

  Sign in to the admin portal, then open the family's **Profile**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Sales tab" icon="folder">

  Open the **Sales** tab to view payments and scheduled charges.

  Failed scheduled charges show a **Failed:** message under the line item and a **retry** link. A badge such as **Declined, no automatic retry** means Campium will not retry that charge on its own. You can still click **retry**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

### Retry from the profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the retry modal" icon="refresh-cw">

  Click **retry** on the failed scheduled charge.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a payment method" icon="credit-card">

  In **Select Payment Method**, choose a payment method on file for that family.

  If there are none, the window shows **No payment methods on file. Please add a payment method before retrying.** Add one, or ask the family to add one in the parent portal.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Process the payment" icon="play-circle">

  Click **Retry Payment**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the outcome" icon="check-circle">

  Review the result in the window or the refreshed sales list.

  - **Success** - the payment processes and the sales list refreshes.
  - **Declined** - the window shows the processor message. Choose another method or ask the family to update their card or bank account.

  </Step>
</Steps>

### Related profile tools

These tools help you troubleshoot. They are separate from retry.

| Tool | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Recent failed payment attempts** | Shows the last three declined attempts in the profile sidebar, with the processor response |
| **Send payment notification** | The mail icon on the failed line item. Emails the family about the failed charge |

<Callout kind="info">

If the family has no payment methods on file, the retry window asks you to add one first. Use **Payment Methods on Portal** on the profile when it is available, or walk the family through adding a method in the parent portal.

</Callout>

## Staff: retry from the Scheduled Payments report

Use the **Scheduled Payments** report to work through failed charges across many families.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report" icon="bar-chart">

  In the left menu, go to **Reports → System Reports**.

  Under **Money**, choose **Scheduled Payments**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Find failed rows" icon="alert-triangle">

  Failed rows are highlighted.

  On a failed row, the first column shows a **Retry payment** icon and a **Delete This Record** icon.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry the payment" icon="play-circle">

  Click the **Retry payment** icon, select a payment method, and click **Retry Payment**.

  This is the same window as the retry on the family profile.

  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also delete a failed scheduled payment with the **Delete This Record** icon when the family will pay another way. Deleting removes the scheduled row only and does not charge the family.

| Related report | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled Payments Potential Issues** | Find families whose scheduled charges may need attention |
| **Card Updates – Families with Failed Payments** | Find families who updated payment methods after failures |

## What happens when you retry

Retry charges the selected payment method right away for the original scheduled amount.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Campium attaches the selected method">

  Campium attaches the payment method you selected to the failed scheduled payment.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Campium submits the charge immediately">

  The charge is sent to your payment processor right away for the original scheduled amount.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Campium updates the result">

  - **If approved**, the payment posts to the family balance like a normal successful charge and the item moves off the failed list.
  - **If declined**, the scheduled payment stays failed, shows the new error message, and keeps its original payment method.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">

Retry charges immediately. Campium does not wait for the original scheduled date before submitting the payment again.

</Callout>

Additional notes:

- Clicking retry does not send the family another automatic payment-declined email. Decline emails come from the automatic nightly processing run.
- Credit card surcharges follow your normal rules for scheduled charges.

## Troubleshooting

Use these checks when retry is unavailable or the result is not what you expect.

### Retry button disabled in the parent portal

- Add at least one payment method on file.
- Refresh **Payment Methods** and return to the **Failed Payments** section.

### Message says to select a payment method

- In the parent portal, **Please select a payment method.** means no method is chosen in the **with** dropdown. Choose one, then click **Retry**.
- In the admin portal, select a payment method in **Select Payment Method** before clicking **Retry Payment**.

### Payment declined again

- Read the processor error text, such as insufficient funds, expired card, or do not honor.
- Ask the family to update or replace the payment method.
- Retry with the new method after the update.
- For ACH returns, use a different bank account if needed.

### Failed payment is not listed

- Check whether the scheduled payment is still pending and has not reached its processing date yet.
- Check the **Scheduled Payments** report for the current status.
- Confirm the issue is a failed scheduled payment and not a returned completed payment. ACH returns on already-posted payments appear differently on the profile, and retry does not apply to them.

### Retry succeeded but the balance looks wrong

- Refresh the profile **Sales** tab.
- Confirm that the successful payment line appears and that the balance updated.
- Check the season filter if your camp uses more than one season.

### Repeated declines on the same card

Campium can temporarily block repeated charge attempts on a saved card after several declines. Campium support can click **Clear lock** under **Recent failed payment attempts** on the profile to remove the block so retry can run again.

<Callout kind="alert">

Camp staff do not see **Clear lock**. If retries keep failing without a clear processor message, contact Campium support.

</Callout>

## Quick reference

| Goal | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Family retries their own failed charge | Parent portal → **Account** → **Payment Methods** → **Failed Payments** |
| Staff retries one family's failed charge | Admin portal → **Profile** → **Sales** → **retry** on the failed line |
| Staff retries from a list of failed charges | Admin portal → **Reports** → **System Reports** → **Scheduled Payments** → **Retry payment** icon |
| Staff reviews families who updated cards after failures | **Reports** → **System Reports** → **Card Updates – Families with Failed Payments** |
| Staff emails a family about a failed charge | Profile or **Scheduled Payments** → send email icon |
