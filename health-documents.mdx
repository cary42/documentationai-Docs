Health documents keep doctor's orders, prescription label photos, physicals, allergy action plans, and care plans with the camper and medication, separate from the profile **Files** tab. Families upload from the portal **Health Center** button. Your health staff upload from the Health Center chart, Med Pass, Today, or the profile Health / Medication tab.

Uploading is always optional. Health documents are **on** for every camp that uses the **Medication Module** or the **Health Center**, until you turn them off.

## Turn Health documents on or off

1. Open **Admin → Site Settings → Modules**.
2. Find **Health documents**.
3. Leave it on, or turn it off if you do not want uploads.

Helper text on the switch: **Lets families and your health staff upload doctor's orders, prescription label photos, physicals, and care plans with each camper and medication. Families upload on the Documents tab of the Health Center button in the Portal. Works with the Medication Module or the Health Center. On unless you turn it off.**

Turning it off hides the Documents tab, paperclips, and upload buttons. Files already uploaded stay stored and come back if you turn the switch on again.

## Who can manage documents

- When **Health Center** is on, staff need Health Center hub access (not lists-only kitchen access).
- When **Health Center** is off, administrators can manage documents, and staff with the medication tab permission can too.
- Without access, staff see **You do not have access to health documents.**

## Staff: upload and review

Open a camper from **Health Center** (chart, **Med Pass**, or **Today**) or from the profile **Health** / Medication area.

- Card title: **Documents**
- Button: **Upload**
- Each medication row can show a paperclip with a count. Use **Documents** in the row's More menu when you need the list for that medication.
- On **Med Pass** and **Today**, the paperclip is read-only so you can open files without leaving the pass.

### Upload a document

1. Click **Upload** (or open **Documents** for a medication).
2. The modal title is **Upload a document**, or **Upload to** the medication name when you started from a med.
3. Choose a type, optional note, and file.
4. Turn **Show to parents** on when the family should see this file on the portal.
5. Save.

Default types: **Doctor's order**, **Prescription label**, **Physical exam**, **Allergy action plan**, **Care plan**, and **Other**.

Accepted files: PDF, JPG, PNG, or iPhone photo (HEIC), up to **15 MB**.

Parent uploads you have not opened yet show a **New** badge. From the list you can **Open**, **Download**, or **Remove**. Confirm with **Remove this document? You can't undo this.**

Printed health charts include a **Documents on file** section with names and dates (not the file itself).

## Document types

Edit the type list when Health documents are on:

- With **Health Center** on: **Health Center → Settings**, heading **Document types**. Use **Add type** and **Remove**. Placeholder: **New type, e.g. Seizure plan**.
- With **Health Center** off: **Medical → Settings**, card **Health document types**.

Renaming a type updates every document that uses it. Removing a type only hides it for new uploads; existing files keep the old type name.

## Parent portal

Families need the portal **Health Center** button (Medication Module and/or Health information on). That button opens tabs such as **Medications**, **Health information**, and **Documents**.

**Documents** appears only when Health documents are on and at least one other Health Center tab is available.

1. On the portal home, open **Health Center** for the camper.
2. Open the **Documents** tab.
3. Title: **Health Center for** the camper. Card: **Health documents for** the first name.
4. Click **Upload a document**. Choose type, optional note, **Which medications is this for?**, and the file. Helper text covers physicals and allergy plans, plus **PDF, JPG, PNG or iPhone photo, up to 15 MB.**
5. Use **Upload** or **Cancel**.

Parents see their own uploads and staff uploads marked **Show to parents**. Camp uploads can show **Added by camp**. Parents can **Download** and can **Remove** only their own uploads.

On the **Medications** tab, a paperclip count links to Documents. When adding or editing a medication, **Attach a document (optional)** can include a doctor's order or a photo of the prescription label.
