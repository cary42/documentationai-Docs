Portal News lets you post updates that families see in the parent portal. Use it for reminders, documents, videos, and time-sensitive alerts.

## Where to find portal news

1. Go to **Communications → News For Portal**.
2. Review the list, or click **New News** to create a post.

## Understand the News list

The list matches what the portal actually shows, not a separate “live” flag.

### Filters and search

- Status buttons: **All**, **Showing now**, **Scheduled**, **Not showing**, **Hidden**.
- **Active** / **Archived** dropdown to switch between current posts and archived history.
- **Search title or content...** to find posts by text.

### Columns

- **Title** - Opens the editor. Shows **Pin** and attachment hints when those apply, plus when the item was created.
- **Type** - How the item is delivered (for example portal news, files, video, or push).
- **Status** - **Showing now**, **Scheduled**, **Not showing**, or **Hidden**.
- **Shown** - The start and end window, or **Not set** when dates are missing.

### Row actions

Open the menu to the left of a row for **Copy**, **Delete**, **Archive**, or **Unarchive**.

Archived news is available from the **Archived** dropdown. Parents do not see archived items.

## Create and publish a news item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new item" icon="plus" title-type="p">

    - From **Communications → News For Portal**, click **New News**.

    - The editor opens on the **Settings** tab with a live **Portal preview** on the right.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the title and type" icon="edit" title-type="p">

    - Enter a short **Title** parents will recognize.

    - Choose **Type**:

      - **Post news to Portal** - Standard text announcement.

      - **Post files to Portal** - Share a downloadable document as the main purpose.

      - **Video Embed URL** - Show an embedded video (use the share or embed link).

      - **Push Notification** - Send a push when your camp uses the mobile app.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Write the content" icon="edit" title-type="p">

    - Under **Content**, write the body in the editor.

    - Merge variables such as `{first_name}`, `{last_name}`, and `{email}` fill in per family.

    - For video type, paste the **Embed URL**.

    - Optionally add **Attachments** (PDF or image, up to 10MB each).

    The **Portal preview** updates as you type so you can check layout before you save.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the publishing window" icon="calendar" title-type="p">

    - Set **Start date** and **End date** (both required for the portal to show the item).

    - The portal only shows the item while today is inside that window.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose visibility options" icon="settings" title-type="p">

    - **Pin to top** - Keeps this item above the rest of the news feed while it is showing.

    - **Show full content** - Shows the whole item instead of collapsing behind a Read More link.

    - **Hide from the portal** - Keeps the item off the portal even inside its dates. Use this to pull something down without losing the schedule.

    A visibility note above the preview explains why an item is not showing (hidden, missing dates, empty body, scheduled, expired, or a type that skips the news feed).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Set recipients and save" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">

    - Open the **Recipients** tab and choose who should see the item.

    - Click **Save news**. The Save button stays inactive until you change something from the last saved version.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  After you save, confirm the item in the parent portal for a family that matches your Recipients filters. If it does not appear, check **Hide from the portal**, the date window, and whether the body is empty.
</Callout>

## Field reference

### Title

Parents see **Title** first in the portal news list. Keep it short and specific.

### Type

**Type** controls how the item behaves:

#### Post news to Portal

Standard text announcements. Parents read the content in the portal news feed.

#### Post files to Portal

Best when the main purpose is a downloadable file (packets, schedules, packing lists).

#### Video Embed URL

Paste the share or embed URL (for example `https://www.youtube.com/embed/UWlzMIl7E48`), not the watch page URL.

#### Push Notification

Sends a push notification when your camp uses the mobile app. Use this for urgent, time-sensitive updates.

### Content and attachments

Write the body under **Content**. Attach supporting PDFs or images under **Attachments** when parents need a download.

### Start date and End date

Both dates define the visibility window. Without them, the item cannot show on the portal.

### Pin to top

Keeps the item above unpinned posts while it is showing.

### Show full content

Shows the full body in the portal feed instead of collapsing behind Read More.

### Hide from the portal

Turns the item off without clearing its dates. Uncheck it when you want the schedule to take over again.

## Manage existing items

From **Communications → News For Portal**:

- Click a **Title** to edit.
- Use the row menu for **Copy**, **Delete**, **Archive**, or **Unarchive**.
- Switch to **Archived** to find older posts and bring them back.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Prefer **Hide from the portal** or **Archive** when you may need the wording or dates later. **Delete** removes the item from your list and from the portal.
</Callout>

## Tips for clear communication

- Use short, specific titles so families can scan the feed.
- Group related information and files in one item instead of many separate posts.
- Put deadlines in the body as well as in the date window.
- Link to forms parents need, and attach files when everything should live in one place.
