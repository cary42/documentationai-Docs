## What the Invoiced Revenue report shows

Use the Invoiced Revenue report to see every product line invoiced for a season, one row per sale line. It lists the date the line was recorded, the enrollee it belongs to, the product and its description, the price, and the family ID, with a running total at the bottom.

This report only includes active sale lines for the season you pick, so it reflects what has actually been billed rather than what has been paid.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Each row is a single invoiced line. If a family bought several products, you see a separate row for each one.
</Callout>

## Filters

<ParamField body="Season" param-type="selector" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Season whose invoiced lines you want to review. Use the season picker at the top of the page. If you do not choose one, the report uses your default season.
</ParamField>

## Run the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a season" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    Pick the season from the picker at the top of the page. The report loads every active invoiced line for that season.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the lines and total" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Scan the table for the products, prices, and families you expect, then check the total at the bottom of the Price column.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Columns

<ParamField body="Date" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Date and time the sale line was recorded.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Season" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Season the line belongs to.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Name" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Enrollee the product was invoiced to.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Product Name" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Name of the product that was sold.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Description" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Longer description of the product, when one is set.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Price" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Price of the line. The total at the bottom of this column is the sum of every line shown.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Family ID" param-type="number" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Identifier for the family the enrollee belongs to, useful for grouping lines by household.
</ParamField>

## Export

Export buttons above the table let you save the list as PDF, Excel, or CSV, or print and copy it. The export reflects exactly the season you have selected.
