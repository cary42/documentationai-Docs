Ask applicants to name people who will vouch for them. Each person they name is emailed a short form to fill out. You track who was invited, who replied, and whether a message bounced, without waiting on the reference to finish before the applicant continues.

## How it works

1. On the application form **Settings**, turn on **Ask for References?** and pick one **Reference Form**.
2. The applicant names each reference (first name, last name, email) on the application.
3. Campium emails each person a link to that shared form.
4. Staff review status on the applicant's profile **Forms** tab. Applicants can check progress from the portal home card or on **My References**.

Everyone the applicant names fills out the **same** reference form. You no longer link two separate forms for two references.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Applicants are not held up waiting for references to reply. **References Required** only controls how many people they must name before they can continue.
</Callout>

## Turn on references for a form

Open **Forms** in the left navigation, open the application form, then open the **Settings** tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for references" icon="users" title-type="p">

  Set **Ask for References?** to **Yes**. Helper text explains that applicants name people who will vouch for them and each person is emailed a short form.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the Reference Form" icon="folder" title-type="p">

  Under **Reference Form**, choose the form each reference fills out. The first option is **-- Choose a form --**. Pick an existing form (for example a recommendation questionnaire). Forms from another season show **(different season)** after the name.

  Saving **Ask for References?** as **No** clears the reference form and turns the feature off for this application.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Set how many are required" icon="hash" title-type="p">

  Under **References Required**, choose **Optional** (0) or **1** through **10**. This is how many people the applicant must name before they can submit. It does not wait for those people to reply.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where the block appears" icon="layout" title-type="p">

  Under **Where References Appear**, choose:

  - **At the top of the form**
  - **At the bottom of the form**
  - **After a section** (then pick the section; the block goes directly after it)

  If you choose **After a section** and the form has no sections yet, the block goes at the bottom.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add instructions (optional)" icon="edit" title-type="p">

  Use **Reference Instructions** for copy shown above the reference questions, such as who counts as a good reference.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Set reminders (optional)" icon="bell" title-type="p">

  Under **Remind References After**, choose **Do not send reminders**, or a number of days from **1** to **90** to nudge someone who has not replied.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">

  Click **Save Settings**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

### Older two-form setup

If a form still uses the older **Reference One Form** / **Reference Two Form** setup, Settings shows a yellow notice:

**Action needed: this form uses the older reference setup.**

Applications already submitted still show their references, and any link already emailed still opens. **This form will not send any new reference requests until you choose a Reference Form above.** Pick one form for everyone to fill out (or combine the two into a single form), then save.

## What the applicant sees

On the application, a **References** section appears where you placed it.

- Required count intro: **Please give us one reference** (or **N references**), with a note that you will email each person a short form and the applicant can carry on while they wait.
- Optional intro when none are required: they can still add a reference if they want.
- Each row is labeled **Reference 1**, **Reference 2**, and so on, with **First name**, **Last name**, and **Email address**.
- **Add another reference** adds a row. **Remove** deletes one (with a confirm if that person may already have been emailed).
- As they type, a live line confirms: we will email that address asking that person to complete a reference.
- Self-references and duplicate emails are refused. A reference has to be someone else on a different address.

After an invitation goes out, the row shows status such as **Invitation sent**, **Reminder sent**, **Completed**, **We could not deliver to this address**, or **Not sent yet**. Invitation and reminder badges can include the send date.

## What the reference sees

The person you emailed opens the shared **Reference Form** from the invite link. They do not need a portal account.

Invite links expire **60 days** after they are sent. An expired link shows: **This invitation has expired. Please ask the applicant to send you a new one.** Other broken or removed links still show the generic **This link is not valid. Please ask for a new one.** From the profile **References** card, use **Resend invitation** (or **Send again** on **My References**) to issue a fresh link.

- Banner: **You have been asked to give a reference for** the applicant's name.
- Helper: **Your answers go straight to the camp. You do not need an account, and you can close this page once you have submitted it.**
- After submit: **Thank you for taking the time. You can close this page.**

The reference form uses the same form experience as other portal forms (including question types such as file upload and signature), so build the questionnaire the same way you build any other form.

## Review reference responses on the form

Open the **Reference Form** → **Responses**. When the form has reference submissions:

- A **Reference For** column shows the applicant and links to their profile.
- **Primary Email** shows the email used on the invitation.

You can change status from the **Status** value and open a response from the **View** column, the same as on other forms. See [Forms](/forms#responses-tab).

## My References on the portal

Applicants (and others who named references) can open **My References** at `/references/` on your portal. There is still no separate top-nav menu item. The usual way in is a progress card on the portal **home** page.

### Home page progress card

When a logged-in family has at least one active reference request, the portal home shows a card that summarizes status and links to `/references/`. The card is hidden when every request was removed, or when all references were received more than 30 days ago.

| Situation | Typical headline | Button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invitation bounced | **We could not deliver your reference request to** the address | **Update and send again** |
| Not sent yet | **Your reference request to** the person **has not been sent yet.** | **Send the request** |
| Waiting on replies | **Your reference request has been sent.** or **N of M references received.** | **See your references** |
| All received (within 30 days) | **Your reference has been received.** or **All N references received.** | **See your references** |

On the **My References** page itself:

- The page lists each request grouped by form, with status and **Sent** / **Completed** dates.
- **Send again** resends the invitation when allowed.
- During a cooldown, the button is disabled with **You can send again in N minutes** (or minutes wording for the remaining wait).
- After the self-send cap is reached, the page tells them to ask the camp to send it again.
- **Change email address** opens a small form to correct a typo. Existing emailed links keep working; the new address is used the next time the invitation is sent.

Staff remove a reference from the profile card, not from this page.

## Track references on a profile

Open the person's profile → **Forms** tab. When references exist, a **References** card appears above the forms list.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Reference** | Name |
| **Email** | Address on the invitation |
| **Status** | Same plain-language status the applicant sees |
| **Sent** | How many times the invitation was sent |
| **Last sent** | Most recent send |

Row menu actions:

- **Resend invitation**
- **Change email address**
- **Remove** (confirm if they may already have been emailed; their link stops working)

Reference-form submissions on the same **Forms** tab can show a **Reference** badge on the row.

Access follows the **Forms** tab permission. There is no separate references permission.

## Reference Request email

Campium seeds a **Reference Request** email template the first time your camp sends a reference invite. Edit it under **Communication → Emails** (template name **Reference Request**).

The default subject names the applicant. The body includes a **Complete the reference** link for the recipient.

## Tips

- Build the reference questionnaire as its own form first, then select it under **Reference Form** on the application.
- Keep **References Required** at the number you actually need. Higher counts add more outbound email and follow-up.
- If a family says a code or invitation failed, check status on the profile **References** card for **We could not deliver to this address** before resending.
- Cloning a form for a new season copies these reference settings. Confirm **Reference Form** still points at the form you want for the new season.
