## Overview

Use the Bounced Email Addresses Report to see addresses that permanently bounced when your camp emailed them. A permanent bounce means the message could not be delivered, so the person is not receiving your confirmations, receipts, or reminders until the address is fixed.

<Callout kind="info">

A bounce is archived automatically once the account owner updates their email address, since changing the address re-verifies the account. The **Active** tab shows bounces that still need attention; the **Resolved** tab shows the ones that have cleared.

</Callout>

## Open the Bounced Email Addresses Report

Access the report directly from the Data Check reports area.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Go to the Bounced Email Addresses Report" icon="folder">

  - Sign in with an admin account.
  - In the main navigation, go to **Reports** and select **System**.
  - Under **Data Check**, choose **Bounced Email Addresses**.
  - Confirm the page title reads `Bounced Email Addresses`.

  When the page loads, you see the **Active** and **Resolved** tabs above a table with the columns **Name**, **Email**, **Subject**, **Reason**, and **Bounced**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Follow up and resolve bounces" icon="check-circle">

  - Stay on the **Active** tab to work the bounces that still need attention.
  - Select a **Name** to open the profile, then confirm and correct the email address with the contact.
  - Switch to the **Resolved** tab to see bounces that have already cleared, along with the date each was archived.

  When an address is updated, that bounce moves to **Resolved** on its own. A green banner tells you how many bounces were archived because the account owner has since updated their email address.

  </Step>

</Steps>

## Report columns

Each row is an email address that permanently bounced.

### Name

Account name linked to the profile. If the bounce is not tied to an account, this reads `(no account)` and is not a link.

### Email

The address that bounced. Confirm this with the contact and update it on the profile to stop future bounces.

### Subject

Subject line of the message that bounced, so you can tell which email failed to deliver.

### Reason

The delivery error reported back for the bounce, which helps you understand why the message could not be delivered.

### Bounced

The date and time the bounce was recorded.

### Archived

Shown only on the **Resolved** tab. The date and time the bounce was archived, which happens automatically once the address is updated.

<Callout kind="alert">

A permanently bounced address will keep failing until it is corrected. Fix the email on the profile so the account owner starts receiving your messages again and the bounce clears to the **Resolved** tab.

</Callout>
