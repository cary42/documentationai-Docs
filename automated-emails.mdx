Automated Emails is where you review and edit the messages Campium sends on its own, such as upcoming charge notices, form reminders, wait list updates, birthday wishes, and your email footer.

You need the **Site Settings** permission. Open **Admin → Automated Emails**.

## What you see on the list

The page groups every automated email Campium sends for your camp. Helper text: **These are the emails Campium sends for you on its own. Edit the wording of any marked Edit. Some belong to one form or one season, so you change those where that form or season is set up.**

Each row can show:

- **On** or **Off** when the email has a switch
- **Your wording** when you have customized it
- **Edit** and **Send Test** for emails you edit here
- Links such as **Forms** or **Email Templates** when the wording lives elsewhere
- **Standard wording** (and sometimes **Preview**) when Campium keeps fixed text

Footer helper: **Send Test emails the saved wording to you, filled in with sample details. To turn payment emails on or off, go to Site Settings > Payment Info.**

### Payments

| Row | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Upcoming scheduled charge** | Sent before each scheduled payment on the days you choose under **Admin → Site Settings → Payment Info**. Turn **Charge Coming Email** on there to send it. |
| **Scheduled payment receipt** | Sent after a scheduled payment goes through when **Send Scheduled Payment Receipt** is on under Payment Info. |
| **Scheduled payment failed** | Standard wording when a scheduled payment is declined. |
| **ACH payment returned** | Standard wording when the bank returns an ACH payment after it settled. |

### Wait list

| Row | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Wait list: accepted** | Sent when you move a camper off the wait list and enroll them. |
| **Wait list: spot available** | Sent when you offer a wait list spot and let the family accept or decline. |

### Forms

| Row | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Form approved, pending, or rejected** | Edited per form. Links to **Forms** and **Email Templates**. |
| **Reference request** | Sent to references an applicant lists, and again as a reminder. |
| **Form reminder: due soon** | Sent on a form's reminder days before its due date. |
| **Form reminder: past due** | Sent on a form's reminder days after its due date. |

On a form's **Settings** tab, **Preview Before-Due Email** / **Preview Past-Due Email** stay next to **Edit Wording**, which opens the matching row here.

### Campers and staff

| Row | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Happy birthday** | Sent on each camper or staff birthday when the switch is on. The Birthday Report also has **Edit email**. |
| **Profile photo reminder** | Edited per season on the **Profile Photo Report**. |

### Donations and tips

These rows appear only when the matching module is on:

- **Donation receipt** (standard) when donations are on
- **Tip receipt** (standard) when tipping is on

### Every email

| Row | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Email footer** | Added to the bottom of emails you send from Campium and most automated ones. **Admin → Site Settings → Basic** also links here with **Edit in Automated Emails**. |

## Edit an automated email

1. Open **Admin → Automated Emails**.
2. Click **Edit** on the row you want, or open a direct link such as **Edit Wording** on a form.
3. The editor shows **Subject** (when the email has one) and **Message** or **Footer text**.
4. Use **Merge fields** on the right: click in the subject or message, then click a field to insert it. Fields marked **Required** must stay in the message.
5. Click **Save**.
6. Optionally click **Preview**, **Send Test**, or **Reset to default**.

**Send Test** emails what is on screen to you, filled in with sample details. It does not save. Helper text under the buttons says the same.

**Reset to default** puts back the standard subject and message. When you are on custom wording, a note says: **You are using your own wording. Reset to default puts back the standard email.**

Use **All automated emails** at the top to return to the list.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Turning payment emails on or off still happens under **Admin → Site Settings → Payment Info**. Automated Emails is where you change the wording.
</Callout>

## Send a test from the list

On an editable row, click **Send Test** without opening the editor. Campium emails the saved wording to you with sample details.
