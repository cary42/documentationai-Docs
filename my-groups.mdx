## Set up My Groups

My Groups lets staff take attendance for their assigned group in the mobile app and on CampiumDB.com, even if they do not have a CampiumDB login.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Designate the Group Field" icon="settings">

  Visit [Site Settings](https://campiumdb.com/admin/site-settings.php#basic) and open the **Basic** tab. Near the top of the page, select the appropriate **Group Field** option for your site.

  This field determines which group assignment CampiumDB will use for My Groups attendance.

  <Callout kind="alert">

  The Group Field must be a **Choose One** or **Choose Many** (multiple choice) profile field, **Active**, and set to **All Users**, or to **Camper**, **Parent**, and **Staff** together (Campium treats that the same as All Users). Site Settings help also answers **Why isn't my field listed?** when the field is a different type, **Inactive**, limited to only some user types, or a built-in field such as **Status**. If your field does not meet these requirements, edit the profile field first, then return to this setting. Saving **Group Field** refreshes My Groups on the web and in the app so the new choice shows right away.

  </Callout>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign campers to the Group" icon="users">

  Assign campers to the group you selected in the Group Field setting. Each camper must have the correct group value so the roster appears accurately for staff.

  After this step, the group should include all campers whose attendance will be taken together.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign staff to the Group" icon="check-circle">

  Assign staff members to the same group. Staff must be linked to the group before My Groups appears for them.

  Once assigned, staff can take attendance for that group from the mobile app or on CampiumDB.com.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Show My Groups on the mobile app" icon="smartphone">

  To make My Groups appear in the mobile app, turn on the **Display My Groups menu item** toggle in your mobile app settings. When this toggle is off, staff will not see My Groups in the app even if they are assigned to a group.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">

Once staff members are assigned, they will see a **My Groups** option in the navigation. This displays a roster of group members along with the attendance taker.

</Callout>

## When My Groups looks empty

Opening **My Groups** lists only your own groups. If the list is empty:

- **Your camp hasn't picked a Group Field yet.** Admins also see **Choose it in Settings**, which opens **Admin → Site Settings → Basic**.
- Otherwise: **You are not assigned to any groups. Ask your director to add you to a group in your** Group Field name **field.**

The same empty messages can appear in the mobile app when it asks why the list is empty.

## Open group camper profiles

Staff who are tied to a session (the **Staff** picker on a session or subproduct) can normally open only families registered in those sessions. When the same person is also assigned to a My Groups group, they can open profiles for campers who share that group answer, even if those campers are not in their assigned sessions. Staff with no session ties are not limited this way. Roster name links and the mini-profile hover follow the same access rules: a camper name links only when that staff member can open the profile.

## Extended care badges

On a session product under **Admin → Products** (or when you add a product), set **Extended care** to **Not extended care**, **Early care**, **After care**, or **Early and after care**.

When a camper is enrolled in an extended-care product, the My Groups roster can show an **EC** badge (title **Early care**) and/or an **AC** badge (title **After care**) next to their name. Badges appear for every camp that has at least one product flagged this way (or when a product name still matches the older early/after-care naming).

## Restrict a login to My Groups only

You can put a staff login into a **my groups only** mode. In this restricted mode, the user signs in and goes straight to their assigned group's attendance roster, without access to the rest of the admin tools. This is useful for seasonal helpers or volunteers who only need to take attendance for their own group and nothing else.
