---
title: "Pricing Mismatches"
description: "Find registrations whose subproduct no longer matches the person's profile answers, then update the ones you pick."
---

Use **Pricing Mismatches** when a family's profile answers change after they register (for example their **Grade**), and their session line is still on the old priced option. The report lists those lines so you can move them to the matching subproduct.

## Who can see it

Open **Reports → System Reports**. Under **Money**, choose **Pricing Mismatches**.

The report appears only when:

- Your camp is not on the free plan, and
- At least one session uses answer-based subproduct pricing (a profile answer such as grade picks the priced option).

You need access to system reports and permission to update sales lines when you use **Update selected** or **Fix**.

## What the report shows

The card title is **Lines to update**. Helper text: **Updating moves the line to the subproduct that matches their answers, at its price on the signup date. A line not at its list price keeps its amount.**

Empty state: **Every registration in your active seasons matches the person's answers.**

Only **active** registration lines in **active, unfrozen** seasons appear.

## Columns

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Person** | Who the line belongs to |
| **Season** | Season of the registration |
| Session (your product wording) | The parent session |
| **Current subproduct** | Option on the sale today |
| **Current answer** | Profile answer that drives pricing |
| **Current amount** | Amount on the line now |
| **New amount** | Amount after you fix it |
| **Change** | Difference, or **Not the list price, so it stays** when the line keeps its current amount |

## Show filter

Above the table, use **All**, **New price**, or **Amount stays** to narrow the list.

## Update a line

1. Check the rows you want to move, then click **Update selected**, or click **Fix** on one row.
2. Confirm the update when prompted.
3. Campium moves each line to the subproduct that matches the person's answers, at that option's price on the signup date. A line that was already not at list price keeps its amount.

You can also fix a mismatched line from the person's profile **Sales** tab when Campium shows that the line does not match their profile.
