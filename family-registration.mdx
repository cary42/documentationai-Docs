## Overview

Use the Family Registration Report to review per-family registration details for a season: which campers are in the family, parent contact info, how many campers registered, and the total value of their registration.

## What it shows

Each row is a family's registration for the selected season.

<ParamField body="Campers in Family" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The campers in the family. Where a profile is available, this links to it.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Parent First Name" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The parent's first name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Parent Last Name" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The parent's last name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Parent Email" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The parent's email address.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Parent Phone" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The parent's phone number.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Zip Code" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The family's zip code.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Season" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The season the registration is for.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Registration Date" param-type="date/time" show-location="false">
When the family registered.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="# Campers" param-type="number" show-location="false">
How many campers the family registered.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Total Registration Value" param-type="number" show-location="false">
The gross value of the family's registration.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Net Revenue" param-type="number" show-location="false">
The net revenue from the family's registration.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Returning Family" param-type="text" show-location="false">
Whether the family is returning or new.
</ParamField>

## Choose the season

Use the season picker in the header to choose one or more seasons. The report title updates to reflect your selection.

## Where to find it

In Campium, go to **Reports** &gt; **System** and select the Family Registration Report.

## Troubleshooting

- **No families listed**: No families are registered for the selected season. Try a different season.
- **Total or net revenue looks off**: Values reflect the registration on file; confirm the family's charges and payments on their profile.
