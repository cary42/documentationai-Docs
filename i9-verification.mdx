---
title: "I-9 verification"
description: "Turn on I-9 verification, request Section 1 from staff, complete Section 2, and download records for inspection."
---

Collect electronic Form I-9s from hires: staff complete Section 1 on the portal, and you complete Section 2 after you examine their documents. Use **I-9 Records** for retention, Supplement B, and inspection downloads.

## Access you need

1. Turn on the paid **I-9 verification** module under **Admin → Site Settings → Modules** (Staff section). An admin turns it on the same way as [W-4 collection](/w4-collection#turn-on-w-4-collection). Paying a **Billing** balance invoice for the add-on also turns the module on.
2. Grant the **I-9 Records** permission (Admin category) under **Admin → Roles & Permissions** to the roles that handle hiring paperwork. Staff Hub alone is not enough. Without this permission, the Staff Hub I-9 column, **Request I-9**, and **Open I-9 records** stay hidden even when the module is on.

## Open I-9 records

From the Modules card (when the module is on), click **Open I-9 records**, or open Staff Hub and use an I-9 cell when you have permission. The page title is **I-9 Records**.

Tabs include **Needs action**, **Complete**, **Past retention**, and **All**. Toolbar actions include **Settings** and **Download for inspection**.

### Settings

Under **Settings**, set **Employer name on Section 2**, **Employer address on Section 2**, **We use E-Verify**, and **Keep copies of the documents for every hire**, then **Save settings**.

## Request an I-9

### Automatically

1. Open **Admin → Staff Hub → Staff Hub Settings**.
2. Under **Request an I-9 automatically**, pick **Application Status** values that should trigger a request. Empty control text is **By hand only**.
3. **Save Settings**.

### By hand

1. Select staff on **Staff Hub**.
2. Click **Request I-9**.
3. Confirm with **Request I-9**. Only do this after the person has accepted your job offer. Each selected person gets an email asking them to complete Section 1 by their first day.

## Track status

When I-9 is visible, the roster shows an **I-9** column (or **W-4 & I-9** when both are on). Badges include states such as **Waiting for Section 1**, **Section 1 overdue**, **Ready for Section 2**, **Section 2 overdue**, **Complete**, **Past retention**, and **Section 1 correction requested**.

## Complete Section 2

1. Open the person's I-9 from **I-9 Records** or Staff Hub.
2. After you examine their documents, use **Sign Section 2**.
3. Use **Ask for a Section 1 correction** or **Cancel this I-9** when needed.
4. Use **Supplement B: reverification and rehire** when you rehire or reverify.
5. Use **Download PDF** for a single form, or **Download for inspection** for a package.

## What staff see on the portal

Staff get a home card such as **Complete Section 1 of your I-9** and open **Form I-9, Section 1** to finish and sign their portion.
