The **Upcoming Charge Emails** system report shows the next upcoming charge reminders that have not been sent yet: who will receive each one, and a preview of what it will say. The report does not send or queue email.

You need **Reports** and **Financial** access, with the financial module on. It is not available on the free plan.

System Reports description: **The next upcoming charge reminders that have not been sent yet: who will receive each one, and a preview of what it will say.**

## Open the report

1. Go to **Reports → System Reports**.
2. Under **Money**, open **Upcoming Charge Emails**.

You can also open it from:

- **Scheduled Payments** (dashboard **Payment Issues** card): header button **Upcoming Charge Emails**
- **Admin → Site Settings → Payment Info**: **See Who Gets It Next** next to **Charge Coming Email**, or **See who gets each reminder** next to **Days Before Charge For Email Notification**
- **Admin → Automated Emails → Upcoming scheduled charge** editor: **See who gets the next reminder**

## When upcoming charge email is off

If **Charge Coming Email** is off under Payment Info, the report shows **Upcoming charge email is off** and explains that nobody will receive an upcoming charge email while it is turned off. Use **Turn on the upcoming charge email** to open the Automated Emails editor for **Upcoming scheduled charge**.

Turn the email on under **Admin → Site Settings → Payment Info**, and choose the reminder days under **Days Before Charge For Email Notification**. See [Site Settings](/settings) and [Automated Emails](/automated-emails).

## Pick the email date

When the email is on, use **When the email goes out** under **Email date**. Each option is a send date tied to a charge date and reminder day (for example, 12 days before a charge). The page defaults to the next send date after today.

Changing the dropdown reloads the list for that send.

## Summary and table

Summary tiles:

| Tile | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Emails to send** (or **People** when the send is not due yet) | How many reminder emails are waiting |
| **Scheduled payments** | Payments those emails cover |
| **No payment method** | Rows where the email will say there is no payment method |

Beside the **Filter** box, chips filter the table (export follows the chip):

- **All**
- **Will not send** (no email address on the recipient)
- **No payment method**

The **Upcoming charge emails** table columns:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Charge date** | When the payment is scheduled |
| **Family** | Family name (links to the profile when available) |
| **Recipient** | Person and email. **Will not send** when there is no email address, with a **Fix** link to that person's profile |
| **Reminder** | Which reminder day this is |
| **Amount** | Payment amount. When several payments for the same person share that send day, the amount cell can show **One email, N payments, $X** so you see they ride in one combined message. |
| **Season** | Season for the payment |
| **Payment method** | Method on file, or **No payment method** with a **Fix** link to the family profile at **Payment Methods on File** |
| **Preview** | Eye button opens **Email preview** with the subject and message for that row (including combined same-day lines when they apply) |

Each guardian gets at most one upcoming charge email per send day. When more than one payment for that person shares the send day, Campium combines them into that one email.

If payments were scheduled after today's email already went out, a note titled **Added after today's email** explains that nothing has been sent for them and that this page does not send email.

When a family already got today's upcoming charge email for another payment on the same day, a note titled **A second payment for a family already emailed today** (or **Second payments for families already emailed today**) explains that each guardian gets one upcoming charge email a day, so the email for that later payment goes out with tomorrow morning's emails. This page does not send email.

Empty states: **No upcoming charge emails are waiting for this day.** or **The upcoming charge email for this day has already been sent.**
