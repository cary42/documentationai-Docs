## What the Donor module does

The Donor module adds a complete fundraising workspace to Campium. Once it is on, your camp can publish public donation pages, collect one-time and recurring gifts online, email receipts automatically, and follow your results from a dedicated dashboard.

Everything lives under a **Donor** section in the left navigation, next to Financials. That section includes:

- **Donor dashboard** – headline totals and charts for your giving.

- **Donations** – a list of every gift received, with the option to resend receipts.

- **Donor list** – your contacts (both donors and prospects) and their giving history.

- **Newsletter** – a simple email tool to reach your donor list.

- **Donation pages** – the public pages people use to give.

- **Email log** – a record of newsletters sent, with open rates.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Online donations require a supported payment processor. The donor experience works with **Payabli**, **USIO**, or **Finix**. If your camp uses a different processor, the public donate page shows a notice instead of a payment form.
</Callout>

## Turn the module on

The Donor module is off by default. An administrator enables it from your settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your module settings" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    Go to your settings and find the module toggles. Look for **Donor Module** in the list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable Donor Module" icon="toggle-right" title-type="p">
    Switch **Donor Module** on and save. This adds the **Donor** section to your navigation with the dashboard, donations, donor list, newsletter, donation pages, and receipts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm your payment processor" icon="credit-card" title-type="p">
    Make sure your camp is set up with Payabli, USIO, or Finix so the public donate page can accept payments. If donations show as unavailable, this is usually why.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How donations are collected

People give through a public donation page hosted on your camp's portal, at a web address like `/donate/?slug=annual-fund`. You can run one page or many — see the [Donation pages](/donation-pages) guide.

On a donation page, a giver:

- Picks a suggested amount ($25, $50, $100, $250, $500) or types a custom amount.

- Enters their name, email, optional phone, and billing address.

- Enters card or bank (ACH) details in a secure payment form and completes the gift.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  With Finix, givers can also check **Make this a recurring gift** and choose **Monthly** or **Annually**. The first gift is charged right away, then automatically on that schedule until it is stopped.
</Callout>

Each completed gift is recorded in your **Donations** list, and the giver is added to your **Donor list** so you can follow up later. A receipt is emailed automatically, and you can resend it at any time.

## The donor dashboard

The donor dashboard is the quickest way to see how fundraising is going. At the top you can filter by a specific **donation page** (or all pages) and by a **date range** using the date picker.

### Headline numbers

<ParamField body="Donations YTD" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Total value of gifts received so far this calendar year.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="All-time donations" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Total value of every gift ever recorded for the selected donation page filter.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="One-time gifts" param-type="number" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Count of gifts that are not part of a recurring schedule.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Recurring gifts" param-type="number" required="true" deprecated="false">
  Count of gifts tied to a recurring schedule.
</ParamField>

### Charts

- **Donations by month** – a bar chart of gift revenue for each month in the date range you selected.

- **Projected recurring (next 12 months)** – an estimate of upcoming income from active recurring gifts, projected month by month for the year ahead.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The projected recurring chart appears when you are viewing **all donation pages**. It is an estimate based on each active recurring gift's amount and next charge date, so actual income can differ if gifts are changed or stopped.
</Callout>
