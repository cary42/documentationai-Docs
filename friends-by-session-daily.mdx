## Overview

Use this daily report to see, for a chosen day, the campers attending each session, along with their parent contact info and allergy details. It is useful for day-of planning, roster checks, and knowing who to call.

## What it shows

Each row is a camper attending on the selected day.

<ParamField body="Camper" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The camper attending that day. This is a link to their profile.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Grade" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The camper's grade.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Teacher" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The camper's teacher, when on file.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Allergies" param-type="text" show-location="false">
Any allergies noted for the camper, so staff can plan accordingly.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Session" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The session the camper is attending, plus any special note.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Primary Contact" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The camper's primary contact, with a link to that person's profile.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Contact Info" param-type="text" show-location="false">
Home and cell phone numbers and email for the contact, so you can reach them quickly.
</ParamField>

## Choose the day and season

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a date" icon="calendar" titleType="p">
    Use the date picker in the header to choose the day you want. The report reloads for that date.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a season" icon="folder" titleType="p">
    Use the season dropdown to focus the report on a single season.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where to find it

In Campium, go to **Reports** &gt; **System** and select this daily campers-by-session report.

## Troubleshooting

- **No campers listed**: No one is attending on the selected day for that season. Try another date.
- **Allergies or contact info are blank**: That information has not been entered on the camper's profile.
