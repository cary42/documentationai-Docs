**Health Center** is one left-nav home for your nurses and health staff: today's work, med pass, visits, camper charts, printable lists, and settings. It is labeled **Health Center (beta)** in Site Settings.

When the hub is on, it replaces the **Medical** menu. Medications, the medication log, medical notes, and related pages stay available from **Reports** and **Settings** inside the hub. See [Medications & health](/medications) for dispensing details that still apply.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Admin → Site Settings → Modules**.
2. Turn on **Health Center (beta)**. Helper text: **One place for your nurses to run med pass, log health center visits, review medications families add, and print allergy lists for the kitchen and counselors. Replaces the Medical menu.**
3. Save your Site Settings changes.
4. Grant roles the Health Center permissions your staff need (below), then reload so **Health Center** appears in the left navigation.

You can turn the module on yourself. It is no longer limited to Campium staff.

## Who can open it

| Permission (Roles) | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| **Use the Health Center: med pass, visit log, camper health charts, and settings** | Full hub tabs for med pass, visits, charts, and settings |
| **Everything in the Health Center, plus the director summary and the health access log** | Director cards and the health access log |
| **See and print allergy and dietary lists (no medications, visits, or diagnoses)** | Kitchen and counselor lists only |

Camp administrators can open the whole hub when the module is on. Staff without any of these permissions do not see **Health Center** in the menu.

**Medication Module** still controls family medication entry and med-pass content. **Incident Reports** stay a separate top-level item when that module is on.

## Tabs in the hub

Open **Health Center** in the left navigation. Tabs:

| Tab | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | Today's health work and director summary cards (when you have director access) |
| **Med Pass** | Dispense by medication time; switch **Today** / **Yesterday** and pick a time block |
| **Dose log** | Recorded doses |
| **Visits** | Log and close health center visits (**New visit** sits in the header) |
| **Campers** | Search and open camper health charts |
| **Reports** | Shortcuts such as **Kitchen list**, **Counselor list**, plus links to Medications, Medication log, Medical notes, and Incident reports when those modules are on |
| **Settings** | Medication times, who counts as present, allergy and health flags, family Health page options, and related hub settings |

**Reports → Kitchen list** is allergies, dietary needs, and EpiPens for everyone here today (printable). **Counselor list** is the kitchen list plus conditions you chose to share with counselors.
