## What the Rolling Revenue report shows

Use the Rolling Revenue report to compare how revenue builds day by day across your seasons. The report is a grid: each row is a calendar day and each column is a season, so you can read down a column to follow one season's pace or across a row to compare seasons on the same day.

Alongside each day's figure, the report shows the change from the previous day as a percentage, with a green up arrow when revenue rose and a red down arrow when it fell.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  This is a specialized comparison view built around your seasons. The season columns come from your season list, so which columns appear depends on the seasons set up for your camp.
</Callout>

## Reading the grid

<ParamField body="Day" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The calendar day (month and day) the row represents. Read down a column to follow a single season across days.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Season columns" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  One column per season. Each cell shows that season's revenue for the day, followed by the day-over-day change: a green up arrow for an increase, a red down arrow for a decrease.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Use the arrows to spot the days when registrations sped up or slowed down, then compare those turning points across seasons to plan your outreach.
</Callout>
