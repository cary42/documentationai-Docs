## Purpose of this report

Use **Membership Recurring Charges** to see upcoming automatic dues for everyone enrolled in a membership season: the next charge date, amount, how many charges are left, and which card or bank account will be charged.

These charges come from each session's recurring billing settings. They do not appear under **Scheduled Payments**.

<Callout kind="info">

The report appears under **Reports → System Reports** in the **Money** group only when you have at least one season marked as a membership season, and when your account includes finance reporting. See [Memberships](/memberships).

</Callout>

## Run the report

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the report" icon="folder">

1. Go to **Reports → System Reports**.
2. Under **Money**, open **Membership Recurring Charges**.
3. Confirm the page title is **Membership Recurring Charges**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose a membership season" icon="settings">

1. In the **Season** panel, use **Membership season**.
2. Leave **All membership seasons** selected, or pick one membership season.

The summary cards and **Upcoming charges** table refresh for that choice.

</Step>

<Step title="Review the summary" icon="bar-chart">

When there are upcoming charges, three summary cards appear:

- **Next charge run** - the next date any enrollment will be charged.
- **Charges on that date** - how many enrollments charge on that date, with the total amount.
- **No payment method on file** - how many enrollments would be skipped for want of a card or bank account. When every enrollment can be charged, the note says **Every enrollment can be charged**.

Click a summary card to filter the **Upcoming charges** table to that group. Click the active card again, or use **Show all** next to the row count, to clear the filter. A card with zero rows is not clickable.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Columns in Upcoming charges

Each row is one enrollment with an upcoming recurring charge.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Next Charge** | The next date this enrollment will be charged. |
| **Family** | The guardian or primary contact, linked to their profile when available. |
| Member name | The enrolled person (uses your camper or member singular label), linked to their profile. |
| **Membership** | The membership session or product. |
| **Season** | The membership season. |
| **Amount** | The recurring charge amount. |
| **Schedule** | How often the charge runs (for example monthly on a day of the month, or weekly). |
| **Charges Left** | How many charges remain. An open-ended schedule can show a count with `+`. |
| **Total Left** | Remaining dollar total, or **Ongoing** when there is no end date. |
| **Payment Method** | The card or bank account on file, or **None on file**. |
| **Last Charge** | The most recent charge date and status (**Paid**, **Declined**, and similar), or **Not charged yet**. |

## Empty states

- **You have no membership seasons. Recurring charges only apply to sessions in a season marked as a membership season.** - Mark a season with **Membership season** set to **Yes**, then configure recurring billing on a session. See [Memberships](/memberships).
- **No upcoming recurring charges. Either nobody is enrolled, or recurring billing is turned off or has ended for these memberships.** - Check enrollments and each session's recurring settings.
