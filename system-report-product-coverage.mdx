Use **Who Has Sessions By Season** to check, for a single season, who already has sessions (or other products) and who does not. Every active sale product in that season counts, including add-ons and fees.

The report name uses your product wording. Most camps see **Sessions**; your list may say **Products** or another label in the same place.

## Where to find it

1. Open **Reports** in the left navigation.
2. Choose **System Reports**.
3. Under **Enrollment**, select **Who Has Sessions By Season** (or **Who Has** your product plural **By Season**).

You can also open it from the dashboard **System Reports** card when it appears there.

## Choose a season

Use the **season** dropdown in the page header. The report opens on your default season when one is set.

If no season is selected, Campium asks you to choose a season before it can show who has sessions and who does not.

## What each row shows

Each row is one person (camper or staff) who is not archived or removed.

| Column | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Person name | Links to their profile |
| **Type** | Camper (or your singular user label) or **Staff** |
| **Status** | Their user status (**Active**, **Pending**, **Inactive**, or **Lead**) |
| **#** | How many products they hold in the selected season |
| Sessions (or your product plural) | Product names they hold, or **None** |

## Filter the list

Above the table, three button groups work together (AND). Counts on each button update with the full list. Your choices stay in the browser for next time.

### Sessions (or your product plural)

- **Everyone**: all people in the report
- **Has Sessions**: only people with at least one product in the season
- **No Sessions**: only people with none

### Type

- **All**
- Campers (your plural user label)
- **Staff**

### Status

- **All**
- One button for each status that appears in the report (**Active**, **Pending**, **Inactive**, **Lead**), with counts

Archived and Removed people are not included.

## Export and print

Use the table export buttons (for example PDF or spreadsheet options on the report table) after you set your filters. Filters apply to what you export.

## Tips

- Use **No Sessions** with **Type** set to campers when you need a follow-up list for people who are not enrolled yet.
- Use **Has Sessions** when you want a quick roster of who is covered for the season, including staff with season products.
- Switch seasons from the header dropdown; the note under the filters shows which season you are viewing.
