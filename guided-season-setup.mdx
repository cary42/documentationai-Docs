## Overview

**Guided setup** walks you through preparing a season for registration in six steps: **Season**, **Sessions**, **Form**, **Payment plans**, **Discounts**, and **Review**. It is marked **Beta** on the Seasons list and is available to every camp.

Use it when you want one workspace that ties sessions, the registration form, plans, and discounts together, then checks what families will see before you open registration.

## Open Guided setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Seasons" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    In the left navigation, open **Seasons**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start Guided setup" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">
    On the season row, select **Guided setup** (with the **Beta** label), or open the row menu and choose **Guided setup**. From **Seasons**, that link opens the **Sessions** step, because the season already exists.

    From a season's products page you can also select **Try the new setup workspace**. From the products page or the dashboard **Setup** button, you still land on the **Season** step first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use **Back** and **Next** at the top of the workspace to move between steps. The stepper across the top shows which step you are on.

## Season

The **Season** step opens **Season details**.

1. Set **Season name**.
2. Set **Status** to **Active** or **Inactive**.
3. Set **Membership season** to **Yes** only when this season is for membership-style enrollment with recurring weekly or monthly charges. Leave **No** for a standard camp season.
4. Set **Start date** (required). Helper text: **When the first registration begins.**
5. Set **End date** (required) to help you close out the season later. It must come after **Start date**.
6. Set **Display on dashboard snapshot** to **Yes** if this season should appear in the financial snapshot on your dashboard.
7. Set **Show in season menus** to **Yes** to list the season in admin season menus and filters.
8. Select **Save and continue**.

If an active season is missing its start or end date, the admin header can show a nudge: **At least one active season is missing its Season Start Date or Season End Date.**

## Sessions

On the **Sessions** step you manage the season's sessions in **Table** or **Calendar** view.

- Select **Add session** to create a session with dates, capacity, price, and minimum deposit.
- Use **Table** / **Calendar** to switch how sessions are listed.
- In **Calendar** view, each day shows session chips. When a day has more than three, a **+N more** / **Show less** control lets you collapse or expand the extras. The same control appears on the season's products calendar outside Guided setup.
- If **Cancellation Fees** appears, expand it to define reusable cancellation-fee sessions for the season. Fees are not charged automatically; you still add them from a profile **Sales** tab. See [Cancellation Fees](/cancellation-fees).
- If **Special Weeks** appears for your camp, open it to manage week schedules. See [Special Weeks](/special-weeks).

### Copy last season's setup

When this season has no sessions, forms, or payment plans yet, and you already have another season with setup, the empty Sessions step also offers **Copy last season's setup**. Helper text explains: **Copying brings a past season's sessions, forms, and payment plans across, with their dates moved forward.**

1. Select **Copy last season's setup**.
2. In the **Copy season setup** window, choose **Copy from** (the season whose sessions, forms, and plans you want).
3. Under **Move dates**, pick **Same week next year** (default), **Same dates next year**, or **Keep dates as they are**. Session dates, registration windows, price tiers, and payment plan dates all move together. **Same week next year** keeps each date on the same weekday.
4. Select **Copy setup**.

After a successful copy, check the dates on the sessions, forms, and payment plans before you open registration. Copying is only offered while the season is empty. A camp's first season never shows this button.

This is different from **Copy Season** on the Seasons list, which creates a brand-new season. Here you fill the season Guided setup already created. See [Seasons & Sessions](/seasons-and-sessions#copy-an-existing-season).

### Sessions table

The sessions table shows **Session**, **Dates**, **Capacity**, **Price**, **Minimum deposit**, **Form status**, and an actions menu. From each row you can choose **Edit session**, **Copy session**, or **Delete session**. Sessions that already have registrations may disable delete. Next to each session name, a smaller **Subproducts** link opens that session's subproducts outside the guided workspace.

#### Edit values in place

On sessions without subproducts, click the blue **Price**, **Minimum deposit**, or **Capacity** values to edit them in the table (tooltips: **Edit price**, **Edit minimum deposit**, **Edit capacity**). Press Enter or click away to save. Empty price, deposit, or capacity shows **None set** (capacity blank or `0` means unlimited).

**Dates** are always editable inline. Click the date range to change start and end without opening **Edit session**.

When a session has subproducts, **Price**, **Minimum deposit**, and **Capacity** stay read-only on this table (ranges or summed capacity). Change those amounts on the session's **Subproducts** page instead. Dates still edit inline.

#### Price, deposits, and tiers

**Price** and **Minimum deposit** show the amounts families are charged. When a session has subproducts with different prices or deposits, the cell shows a range (for example `$4,200 - $5,800`). Hover the range for a tooltip such as **Range across N subproducts.** When every subproduct matches, you see a single amount.

In the **Price** cell, select **Tiers** (or **Tiers (n)** when tiers already exist) only when the session has **no** subproducts. That opens the same pricing-tier editor as on the season's session list (**Seasons**, then click the season name). If the session already has subproducts, Guided setup hides **Tiers** here. Open **Subproducts** and use **Tiers** on each option instead. The products list can still show a parent **Tiers** button for older parent-level tiers.

If an active subproduct under a session has a **$0** minimum deposit, a warning icon appears next to **Minimum deposit** in the table (and next to **Min. Dep.** in **Calendar** view). Hover the icon to see **A subproduct has a $0 minimum deposit.** Open **Subproducts** to correct the deposit before families register.

## Form

The **Form** step builds or links the registration form for the season. Use it to set who can register, when the form opens and closes, which sessions appear, and form settings such as discounts and payment plans.

Choose a form from the toolbar dropdown, or select **New form** to create one without leaving Guided setup. The **New Form** dialog asks for **Form name**, shows the season you are setting up, and lets you pick **Form type**:

- **Registration (with sessions for sale)**
- **Standard (nothing for sale, just a form)**
- **Allow an upload of a file**

Then select **Create**.

The Form toolbar also shows the form's availability chip and a **Test Mode** switch next to it. Turn **Test Mode** on so staff and admin users can open the form on the parent portal before families can. When the form is already **Open**, Test Mode turns off automatically and the switch stays locked until the form is no longer in its live window. See [Test Mode](/test-mode).

After you choose a form, open **Preview** for a menu of parent-facing pages:

- **Form page** – the form as families fill it out
- **Sessions selection page** and **Cart header** – only for registration forms that sell sessions

On a wide screen (about 1400px and up), choosing a Preview item opens a side drawer beside the form editor instead of leaving Guided setup. The drawer includes:

- **Phone** / **Desktop** width toggles
- **Refresh** to reload the preview
- **New tab** if you still want the page on its own tab

After you save a change on the Form step, an open drawer refreshes on its own. If the portal cannot load inside the panel, you see **This preview cannot be shown here** with **Open in new tab**. On narrower screens, or when you use a modifier click (for example Ctrl/Cmd+click), Preview keeps opening each page in a new tab the way it did before.

For full form editing outside Guided setup, see [Forms](/forms).

## Payment plans

The **Payment plans** step is optional. Add or edit plans families can choose at checkout.

- With at least one plan listed, families also see a **Pay in Full** option on the portal.
- Use **Copy all to another season** when you want the same plans on a different season.
- Use **Preview cart** when a preview link is available.
- If you see a warning that plans will not show on the portal, open the **Form** step, then **Settings**, and turn on payment plans.

For payment plan details outside Guided setup, see [Payment plans](/payment-plans).

## Discounts

The **Discounts** step is optional. Discounts are shared across seasons. A code can apply to all sessions, or only to the sessions you select.

- Create or edit discounts from this step, including a **Sibling discount** when you offer automatic multi-child savings. See [Discounts](/discounts#create-a-sibling-discount).
- Use **More discount tools** to open the full **Admin → Discounts** page.
- If you see a warning that discount codes will not show on the portal, open the **Form** step, then **Settings**, and turn on discounts.

## Review

The **Review** step shows whether registration is ready and what families will see.

- Status banner and **Opens** / **Closes** dates (and times when set) for the registration window
- **Recipients** summary when the form has audience rules, with **Edit recipients** to jump back to the Form step
- A checklist of blockers and warnings, with jump buttons to the step that needs attention
- **Preview the form** when a preview link is available

Fix blockers before families register. Warnings do not block registration by themselves once blockers are cleared.
