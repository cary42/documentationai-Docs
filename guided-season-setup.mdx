## Overview

Season setup takes you through six steps to get a season ready for registration: **Season**, **Sessions**, **Forms**, **Payment plans**, **Discounts**, and **Review**. Every camp uses this workspace. Older separate session and subproduct pages redirect into the **Sessions** step.

It ties sessions, the registration form, plans, and discounts together in one place, then checks what families will see before you open registration.

## Open season setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Seasons" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    In the left navigation, open **Seasons**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open a season" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">
    Click the season name to open that season's setup page (title ends with **setup**) on the **Sessions** step.

    You can also open the row menu and choose **Guided setup**, which lands on the same **Sessions** step. From the dashboard **Setup** button you still start on the **Season** step first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click a step name in the stepper across the top (**Season**, **Sessions**, **Forms**, **Payment plans**, **Discounts**, or **Review**) to move between steps. There is no separate **Back** or **Next** button.

If an active season is missing its start or end date, a banner can ask you to **Add dates so reports can order this season.** It explains that a season with no start date is counted as your oldest season in Retention and other comparisons. Enter **Start date** and **End date**, then **Save dates**. Someone who cannot edit seasons sees: **Someone who can edit seasons can add them on the Season step.**

## Season

The **Season** step opens **Season details**.

1. Set **Season name**.
2. Set **Status** to **Active** or **Inactive**.
3. Set **Membership season** to **Yes** only when this season is for membership-style enrollment with recurring weekly or monthly charges. Leave **No** for a standard camp season.
4. Set **Start date** (required). Helper text: **When the first registration begins.**
5. Set **End date** (required). Helper text: **Helps you close out your season when the time comes.** It must come after **Start date**.
6. Set **Display on dashboard snapshot** to **Yes** if this season should appear in the financial snapshot on your dashboard.
7. Set **Show in season menus** to **Yes** to list the season in admin season menus and filters.
8. Optionally enter **Invoice footer**, which is printed at the bottom of every invoice for this season. Leave it empty for no footer.
9. Select **Save and continue**.

## Sessions

On the **Sessions** step you manage the season's sessions in **Table** or **Calendar** view.

- Select **Add session** to create a session with dates, capacity, price, and minimum deposit.
- Use **Table** / **Calendar** to switch how sessions are listed.
- In **Calendar** view, each day shows session chips. When a day has more than three, a **+N more** / **Show less** control lets you collapse or expand the extras. Use **Print calendar** to print **This month** or **All season months**.
- On **Edit session** or **Add session**, use **Ask when picked** to require profile answers under that item when a family selects it. See [Ask when picked](/ask-when-picked).
- If **Cancellation Fees** appears, expand it to define reusable cancellation-fee sessions for the season. Fees are not charged automatically. You add them from a profile **Sales** tab. See [Cancellation Fees](/cancellation-fees).
- If **Special Weeks** appears for your camp, open it to manage week schedules. See [Special Weeks](/special-weeks). When a session's **Number of Weeks** is higher than the dated week count for the season, a red mismatch alert appears on this step with **Set special week dates** and **Lower Number of Weeks to N on…**. See [Session weeks vs dated weeks warning](/special-weeks#session-weeks-vs-dated-weeks-warning).

### Copy last season's setup

When this season has no sessions, forms, or payment plans yet, and you already have another season with setup, the empty Sessions step also offers **Copy last season's setup**. Helper text: **Copying brings a past season's sessions, forms, and payment plans across, with their dates moved forward.**

1. Select **Copy last season's setup**.
2. In the **Copy season setup** window, choose **Copy from** (the season whose sessions, forms, and plans you want).
3. Under **Move dates**, pick **Same week next year** (default), **Same dates next year**, or **Keep dates as they are**. Session dates, registration windows, price tiers, and payment plan dates all move together. **Same week next year** keeps each date on the same weekday.
4. Select **Copy setup**.

After a successful copy, check the dates on the sessions, forms, and payment plans before you open registration. Copying is only offered while the season is empty. A camp's first season never shows this button.

This is different from **Copy Season** on the Seasons list, which creates a new season. Here you fill the season you already created. See [Seasons & Sessions](/seasons-and-sessions#copy-an-existing-season).

### Sessions table

The sessions table shows **Session**, **Status**, **Dates** (or **Cadence** on a membership season), **Capacity**, **# registered**, **Wait listed** (only when the **Wait List Module** is on), **Price**, **Minimum deposit**, **Form status**, and an actions menu.

**Status** badges include **Open**, **Closed**, **Closed - Waitlist**, and **Expired**. Hidden sessions also show **Archived**. **Form status** is **On form** or **Off form**. Food / per-day sessions show a **Food** badge and a **Weeks** (or **Weeks (n)**) control to manage weeks.

From each row's actions menu you can choose **Edit session**, **Copy session**, **Delete session** (sessions already used cannot be deleted), **Subproducts** or **Add subproducts**, and **Email session** when that session has an emailable group. **Subproducts** / **Add subproducts** opens an in-page panel so you stay in season setup.

#### Edit values in place

On sessions without subproducts, click the blue **Price**, **Minimum deposit**, or **Capacity** values to edit them in the table (tooltips: **Edit price**, **Edit minimum deposit**, **Edit capacity**). Press Enter or click away to save. Empty price, deposit, or capacity shows **None set** (capacity blank or `0` means unlimited).

**Dates** (or **Cadence**) are always editable inline. Click the date range to change start and end without opening **Edit session**.

When a session has subproducts, **Capacity** is read-only on this table because it is the total of the subproduct capacities. **Price** and **Minimum deposit** stay editable while every subproduct has the same amount: the tooltip reads **Edit price for all N subproducts** (or **Edit minimum deposit for all N subproducts**), and the value you enter is saved to every subproduct. When the subproducts differ, the cell shows a range. Click the range to open the **Subproducts** panel and change each one there. Dates still edit inline.

#### Price, deposits, and tiers

**Price** and **Minimum deposit** show the amounts families are charged. When a session has subproducts with different prices or deposits, the cell shows a range (for example `$4,200 - $5,800`). Hover the range for a tooltip such as **Range across N subproducts.** When every subproduct matches, you see a single amount.

In the **Price** cell, select **Tiers** (or **Tiers (n)** when tiers already exist) on every session, including sessions that already have subproducts. Open **Subproducts** and use **Tiers** on each option when you price options separately.

If an active subproduct under a session has a **$0** minimum deposit, a warning icon appears next to **Minimum deposit** in the table (and next to **Min. Dep.** in **Calendar** view). Hover the icon to see **A subproduct has a $0 minimum deposit.** Open **Subproducts** to correct the deposit before families register.

#### Subproducts panel

**Subproducts** or **Add subproducts** on a session row opens a panel under the sessions table (you do not leave season setup).

- Eyebrow **Subproducts** and the session name as the heading
- Helper text: families see the session's portal name, not the subproduct name. To tell families more about a subproduct, add a description.
- Buttons: **Add subproducts**, **Delete selected** (when rows are checked), and **Close**
- Columns: **Subproduct** (shown to staff, not on the portal), **Description** (shown on the portal and invoices), **Matches**, **Status** (plus **Admin only** when set), **Price** with **Tiers**, **Minimum deposit**, **Capacity**, **# registered**, and optional **Wait listed**
- Empty state: **No subproducts yet. Add them to price this session by a profile answer, such as grade or bus stop.**
- Drag the handle (**Drag to reorder**) to set display order

Manage subproducts only from this panel. See [Subproducts](/subproducts).

## Forms

The **Forms** step builds or links the registration form for the season. Use it to set who can register, when the form opens and closes, which sessions appear, and form settings such as discounts and payment plans.

Choose a form from the toolbar dropdown, or select **New form** to create one without leaving season setup. The **New Form** dialog asks for **Form name**, shows the season you are setting up, and lets you pick **Form type**:

- **Registration (with sessions for sale)**
- **Standard (nothing for sale, just a form)**
- **Allow an upload of a file**

Then select **Create**.

The Forms toolbar also shows the form's availability chip and a **Test Mode** switch next to it. Turn **Test Mode** on so only the form's recipients can see it from a staff login before the form opens to families. Helper text names how many recipients that is and links **Check a person**. When the form is already **Open**, Test Mode turns off automatically and the switch stays locked until the form is no longer in its live window. See [Test Mode](/test-mode).

On form **Settings**, under **Files and records**, you can upload optional files (PDF or image, 25 MB max) with **Choose file to upload**. Families can download them from the portal. A just-uploaded file gets the same trash control as the others.

After you choose a form, open **Preview** for a menu of parent-facing pages:

- **Questions** - what people see when they open this form
- **Sessions selection** and **Cart** - only for registration forms that sell sessions

On a wide screen (about 1400px and up), choosing a Preview item opens a side drawer beside the form editor instead of leaving season setup. The drawer includes:

- **Preview as** so you can type a person's name and see the form as they would
- **Phone** / **Desktop** width toggles
- **Refresh** to reload the preview
- **New tab** if you still want the page on its own tab

On a sessions preview, **Show all sessions** (your product wording) is on by default so every session on the layout appears. Turn it off to hide sessions the previewed person's answers do not qualify for. The alert explains which view you are in.

After you save a change on the Forms step, an open drawer refreshes on its own. If the portal cannot load inside the panel, you see **This preview cannot be shown here** with **Open in new tab**. On narrower screens, or when you use a modifier click (for example Ctrl/Cmd+click), Preview opens each page in a new tab.

On the form's **Responses** tab, use **Email** (title **Email the people on this form**) when your role includes **Manage Email**. That opens the same status and session audience picker as the Forms list **Email** action, then the shared **Email this group** composer. See [Email users that have not completed a form](/email-users-who-have-not-completed-a-form).

For full form editing outside season setup, see [Forms](/forms).

## Payment plans

The **Payment plans** step is optional. Add or edit plans families can choose at checkout.

- With at least one plan listed, families also see a **Pay in Full** option on the portal.
- Use **Copy all to another season** when you want the same plans on a different season.
- Use **Preview cart** when a preview link is available.
- If you see **Plans will not show on the portal until a form has payment plans enabled**, open the **Forms** step, then **Settings**, and turn on payment plans.

For payment plan details outside season setup, see [Payment plans](/payment-plans).

## Discounts

The **Discounts** step is optional. Discounts are shared across seasons. A code can apply to all sessions, or only to the sessions you select.

- Create or edit discounts from this step, including a **Sibling discount** when you offer automatic multi-child savings. See [Discounts](/discounts#create-a-sibling-discount).
- Use **More discount tools** to open the full **Admin → Discounts** page.
- If you see a warning that discount codes will not show on the portal, open the **Forms** step, then **Settings**, and turn on discounts.

## Review

The **Review** step shows whether registration is ready and what families will see.

- Status banner and **Opens** / **Closes** dates (and times when set) for the registration window
- **Recipients** summary when the form has audience rules, with **Edit recipients** to jump back to the Forms step
- A checklist of blockers and warnings, with jump buttons to the step that needs attention
- **Preview the form** when a preview link is available

Fix blockers before families register. Warnings do not block registration by themselves once blockers are cleared.
