title: Deposit Summary report
description: Review deposit batches, summary totals, estimated bank deposit dates, and match a bank-statement amount to a batch.

## Overview

The Deposit Summary report shows your payments in two parts:

- **Pending cash and check payments** - individual payments that are ready to be deposited but are not in a deposit yet.

- **Deposit summary** - existing deposit batches, each with a total and, on Payabli and Finix, the fees and net amount.

Use this report to reconcile check and cash activity, group payments into deposits, and compare what Campium shows to your bank and processor statements.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  On Payabli, USIO, and Finix, the Deposit Summary uses the **date the payment was entered in Campium**, not the date funds moved at the bank or processor. On other processors, card and ACH batches use the settled date, and cash and check deposits use the deposit's **Effective Date**.
</Callout>

## Sections in the Deposit Summary

The page contains two main areas:

- **Pending cash and check payments**\
  A **collapsed card** that lists check, cash, and manual adjustment payments that **are not in any deposit yet**, with a count badge beside the heading. Click the heading or **Show payment list** to expand it. The card appears only when at least one payment is waiting, and it is hidden in the **All transactions** view. When the canteen module is on, eligible canteen payments are included too.

- **Deposit summary**\
  Shows deposits that have already been grouped and sent through your normal processing. Each row is one deposit, with the total amount and, on Payabli and Finix, the fees and net amount.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The **Pending cash and check payments** card holds money you still need to group into a deposit. The **Deposit summary** table is your history of deposits to match against bank and processor reports.
</Callout>

### Switch between By-batch and All-transactions views

Above the table, a toggle switches between two views:

- **By batch** - One row per deposit batch. Use this view to match your bank and processor deposits.

- **All transactions** - One row per transaction, so you can review the underlying payments.

Use **By batch** to reconcile whole deposits and **All transactions** to review individual payments.

## Summary totals above the batch table

When the **By batch** view has rows for your date range, Campium shows summary totals above the table:

- **Gross** - sum of batch totals in the current date range.
- **Fees** and **Net** - shown when the batches have fee amounts (Payabli and Finix only).
- **Undeposited** *(Finix)* - net from Finix settlements in the current range that are not yet confirmed deposited to your bank. Includes **In transit**, **Overdue**, and **Sent to bank** rows; excludes **Deposited** batches and ACH returns. Hover the card for the same explanation, and click it, or **Undeposited only** above the table, to show only those batches. **All** shows every batch again.
- **ACH returns** - total returned ACH amount for the range, with the return count in parentheses. Hover the card for the ACH return rate against ACH volume when Campium can calculate it.

These cards use the same date range as the Deposit summary table. They appear only on Payabli, USIO, and Finix.

## Find a bank deposit

Use **Find a bank deposit** when you have a dollar amount from your bank statement and need the matching Campium batch.

1. Open **Financials → Deposit Summary** and stay on the **By batch** view.
2. Set the date range picker at the top of the page to cover the statement period.
3. In **Find a bank deposit**, enter the bank amount (for example `1,234.56`).
4. The table filters to batches whose **Net** or **Total** matches that amount. The text beside the field shows how many rows matched, such as **1 match**.
5. Clear the field with **×** to show the full list again.

If you see **No match. The bank amount may combine batches or reflect withheld fees.**, the bank line may combine more than one batch, or your bank may show a net that withholds fees differently than Campium.

## Filters and date range behavior

Choose the dates with the date range picker at the top of the page.

- The **date range applies only to the Deposit summary table** (batched deposits).

- The **Pending cash and check payments card always shows every payment that is not yet in a deposit**, no matter which dates you choose.

- The range defaults to the current month. After you pick a range, Campium keeps it for the rest of your session.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  On Payabli, USIO, and Finix, the date filter looks at **when the payment was entered in Campium**. It does not use bank settlement dates or processor cut-offs. If a payment was entered outside your chosen range, it does not appear in the Deposit summary for that period, even if it settled during those dates.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The **Pending cash and check payments** list is **independent of the date range**. It always shows the check, cash, and manual adjustment payments that are not yet grouped into a deposit.
</Callout>

## Columns in the Pending cash and check payments table

The **Pending cash and check payments** table shows individual payments that can be added to a deposit.

- **Select** - Checkbox for choosing which payments go into a new deposit. The **Toggle All** link in the header selects or clears every checkbox in the table.

- **Date Entered** - The date and time the payment was recorded in Campium, not when money moved between banks.

- **Type** - The payment method: **Check**, **Cash**, or **Manual Adjustment**.

- **Name** - The name attached to the payment, such as the payer, family, or account holder.

- **Description** - Extra details about the payment, such as notes or status text.

- **Total** - The amount of the individual payment.

- **Total (footer)** - The total of all payments listed in the table, beside the **Create Deposit #** button.

## Columns in the Deposit summary table

The **Deposit summary** table shows one row per deposit batch, with totals and, on Payabli and Finix, fees. The columns below are the Payabli, USIO, and Finix layout. On other processors the table shows **Date Settled**, **Type**, **Deposit #**, **Batch #**, **# of Items**, **Total**, and **Reconciled**, with no summary totals or **Find a bank deposit**.

- **Date** - The main date shown for the deposit. When available, this is the date the deposit settled; otherwise, it is the date the deposit was created. Match this date to your bank or processor report.

- **Type** - The tender for the batch, such as credit card or ACH.

- **Batch Number** - The deposit batch number. Click this link to open a detailed view of the payments included in that deposit.

- **# of Items** - How many individual payments are included in the deposit.

- **Total** - The full amount of all payments in the deposit, before fees.

- **Est. deposit** *(Finix)* - Estimated date funds arrive in your bank. Card funding is usually T+2 business days from settlement; ACH is usually T+4 (or T+3 after you qualify). Hover the date for the calculation detail. Status badges can appear next to the date:
  - **In transit** - Finix is transferring these funds to your bank.
  - **Sent to bank** - Finix reports the transfer has been sent; Campium has not yet marked the batch **Deposited**.
  - **Overdue** - The estimated date has passed and Campium has not confirmed the bank deposit yet. Weekends, holidays, or funding delays can push the real deposit later.
  - **Deposited** - Finix sent these funds to your bank.

- **Fees** *(Payabli and Finix)* - Processing fees for the deposit. On Finix, hover to see whether the figure is the actual settlement fee. On Payabli, hover to see the estimate breakdown.

- **Net** *(Payabli and Finix)* - The deposit amount after subtracting fees.

- **Reconciled** - Mark the batch reviewed against your bank activity.

- **Grand totals (footer)** - Totals for **Total**, and when shown, **Fees** and **Net**, across deposits in the current date range.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Use the **grand totals** in the footer to reconcile an entire period against your accounting system, bank deposits, or processor statements.
</Callout>

## Deposit Summary reconciliation

The Deposit Summary report includes a **Reconciled** column so you can track which deposits you have already reviewed against your bank activity.

With reconciliation, you can:

- Mark a deposit as **Reconciled** after you confirm it.
- Add an optional note, such as a bank reference or internal reminder.
- See which deposits still need review.
- Remove reconciliation if a deposit was marked by mistake.

### What changed in the report

Each row in the **Deposit summary** table includes a **Reconciled** column.

- **Reconcile** button - the deposit has not been reconciled yet.
- **Reconciled** button - the deposit has already been reconciled.
- **Note line** - if a note was saved during reconciliation, it appears below the button.

### Recommended workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Deposit Summary" icon="bar-chart" title-type="p">
    Go to **Financials → Deposit Summary**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the date range" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Choose the date range for the period you are reconciling.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Compare each deposit" icon="scale" title-type="p">
    Review each deposit row against your bank activity. Use **Find a bank deposit** with the statement amount when you need to locate the matching batch.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Mark confirmed rows" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    When a row is confirmed, click **Reconcile**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add an optional note" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    Enter a note if needed, then confirm.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish the review" icon="check-check" title-type="p">
    Continue until all confirmed rows show **Reconciled**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Mark a deposit as reconciled

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the deposit row" icon="search" title-type="p">
    Locate the deposit you want to confirm.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Reconcile" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    In the **Reconciled** column, click **Reconcile**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter a note if needed" icon="file-text" title-type="p">
    In the **Mark Deposit as Reconciled** pop-up, add a note if you want to save extra context. This is optional.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the reconciliation" icon="save" title-type="p">
    Click **Reconcile** to save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After saving:

- The button changes to **Reconciled**.
- The saved note appears under the button.

### Remove reconciliation

If a row was reconciled by mistake:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the reconciled button" icon="rotate-ccw" title-type="p">
    Click the **Reconciled** button on that row.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm removal" icon="trash-2" title-type="p">
    At **Remove reconciliation for this deposit?**, click **Yes, Remove**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After removal:

- The button returns to **Reconcile**.
- The note is cleared.

### Tips for cleaner reconciliation

- Reconcile in date order so review is easier to track.
- Use short notes for exceptions only, such as timing differences.
- Reconcile only after matching the amount and batch to your bank activity.
- If you are unsure, leave the row unreconciled and return later.

### Troubleshooting

#### I clicked Reconcile but nothing changed

- Refresh the page and try again.
- Confirm you still have access to the report.
- If it still fails, contact your admin or Campium support.

#### I reconciled the wrong row

- Click the **Reconciled** button for that row.
- Confirm removal, then reconcile the correct row.

#### My note is missing

- Notes are saved only when you click **Reconcile** in the pop-up.
- Re-open the row by removing reconciliation, then reconcile it again with a note.

### Glossary

- **Reconcile** - mark a deposit as reviewed and matched.
- **Unreconciled** - a deposit that still needs review.
- **Batch Number** - the processor identifier for a deposit group.

## Where the Fees and Net columns come from

Where the **Fees** and **Net** values come from depends on your processor:

- **Finix** - Campium reads the actual settled fee for each deposit from Finix settlement data. The column tooltip reads **From Finix settlement**.

- **Payabli** - Campium estimates fees from your card and ACH processing rates (a percentage plus a per-transaction fee). The column tooltip reads **Approximate fees**, and hovering a value shows the breakdown.

- The **Net** column is the **Total minus the Fees**.

USIO and the legacy deposit view used by other processors have no **Fees** or **Net** columns.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  On Finix the fee figures reflect what the processor charged. On Payabli they are estimates. For chargebacks, refunds, or special pricing, reconcile against your processor statement for final totals.
</Callout>

## Run the Deposit Summary report

Open the Deposit Summary from **Financials** to reconcile deposits.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Deposit Summary" icon="bar-chart" title-type="p">
    - Click **Financials** in the left sidebar.

    - Click **Deposit Summary**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your date range for deposits" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    - Use the date range picker at the top of the page to choose the period you want to review.

    - Keep the current month to review current activity, or set dates that match your bank or processor statement.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply and review the results" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">
    - Apply the date range to refresh the **Deposit summary** table.

    - Check that deposit totals, and the fees and net amounts when shown, look right for the period.

    - Expand **Pending cash and check payments** to see which payments are **not yet included** in any deposit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the page updates successfully, you see:

- A **Pending cash and check payments** card with the check, cash, and manual adjustment payments that still need to be grouped into deposits (when there are any).

- A **Deposit summary** table listing deposits whose payments were entered within your chosen date range.

## Create a deposit from new payments

Use the **Pending cash and check payments** table to group selected payments into a deposit and give it the date you deposit the funds.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select payments to include" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    - Expand **Pending cash and check payments** and use the checkboxes to select the payments you want to include.

    - Click **Toggle All** in the header to select or clear all listed payments.

    - Check the **Total** at the bottom of the table to confirm it matches what you expect to deposit.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Create Deposit window" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">
    - Click the **Create Deposit #** button in the table footer.

    - The **Check Deposit** window opens with an **Effective Date** field.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the deposit date and save" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    - Enter the **Effective Date** for the new deposit (for example, the date you bring the checks or cash to the bank).

    - Click **Save** to create the deposit.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Understand what happens to the payments" icon="info" title-type="p">
    - Campium groups the selected payments into deposits by type: cash and checks go into one deposit, and manual adjustments into another.

    - Each payment is now part of a deposit and no longer appears under **Pending cash and check payments**.

    - The deposits will appear in the **Deposit summary** section once they move through your normal processing and receive a batch number.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Return to the Deposit Summary" icon="arrow-right" title-type="p">
    - After saving, you return to the **Deposit Summary** page.

    - The payments you grouped no longer appear under **Pending cash and check payments**, and show in deposit views as processing continues.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Batch numbers (shown as **Batch Number** in the Deposit summary table) come from downstream Campium and processor workflows, not from the **Create Deposit #** action. Use the batch links in the Deposit summary when you need to review the payments inside an existing deposit.
</Callout>

## Export, copy, and print options

Both Deposit Summary tables include export and print buttons so you can share, save, or analyze the data outside Campium:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Copy" icon="copy">
    Copies the current table view to your clipboard. Use this when you want to paste rows directly into an email, document, or spreadsheet without creating a file.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Excel" icon="download">
    Downloads the table as an Excel file, for finance teams that work with formulas, filters, and formatting in a spreadsheet.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="CSV" icon="download">
    Exports the table as a comma-separated values file that you can open in Excel or Google Sheets, or import into other accounting tools.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="PDF (landscape legal)" icon="file-text">
    Creates a PDF of the table in landscape orientation on legal-size paper, for a fixed-layout copy for records or audits.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Print" icon="printer">
    Opens your browser’s print dialog with the table formatted for printing. Combine this with the PDF option if you need both electronic and paper copies.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Use the table’s page-size control to set how many rows appear before you export or print. A larger page size puts more rows in a single file or printed report.
</Callout>
