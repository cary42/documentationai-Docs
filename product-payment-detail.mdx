## Overview

Use Payment Detail to see how payments are landing across a season. Switch between a line-by-line camper view and a session rollup.

Payments are recorded against a season, not against a single line item, so each family's payments are split across their sessions in proportion to price. Charged minus Paid minus Discounts and Credits equals the Balance.

## Views

Above the summary cards, choose:

- **By Camper** (detail) – one row per camper and session, with family, charged, paid, discounts/credits, and balance
- **By Session** (rollup) – one row per session with **Registrations**, charged, paid, discounts/credits, and balance totals

Your camp's wording for people and products may replace **Camper** and **Session** on those buttons.

## Filters

<ParamField body="Season" param-type="filter" show-location="false">
The report always covers one season. Use the season selector at the top to choose it.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Date range" param-type="filter" show-location="false">
Paid and Discounts / Credits count only payments received in this range. When you first open the report for a season, the range defaults to that season's start and end dates (not the last 30 days). Change the dates if you need a narrower window.
</ParamField>

## Summary cards

<ParamField body="Total Charged" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
The full value of all active registrations in the season.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Total Paid" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
Payments received (card, bank, check, or cash) within the selected date range.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Discounts / Credits" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
Non-payment reductions such as discounts, credits, and adjustments applied within the date range.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Outstanding Balance" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
Charged minus what was paid and credited in the date range.
</ParamField>

## Table columns

### By Camper

<ParamField body="Family" param-type="link" show-location="false">
The family's name, linked to their profile.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Camper" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The person the line item is for.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Session" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The registered session or product.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Charged" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
The full price of that line item.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Paid" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
The share of payments allocated to that line, pro-rated by price.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Discounts / Credits" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
The share of discounts and credits allocated to that line.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Balance" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
Charged minus Paid minus Discounts / Credits for that line.
</ParamField>

### By Session

<ParamField body="Session" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The session or product name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Registrations" param-type="number" show-location="false">
How many active registration lines belong to that session.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Charged / Paid / Discounts / Credits / Balance" param-type="currency" show-location="false">
Sums of the matching detail rows for that session.
</ParamField>

<Callout kind="info">
  Because payments apply to a season rather than a single session, the Paid and Discounts / Credits figures are an allocation, not a record of a specific charge against one line item. Adjust the date range to change which payments are counted.
</Callout>

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Payment Detail from Reports" icon="folder" titleType="p">
    Go to **Reports** &gt; **System** and select **Payment Detail by Camper and Session**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a season and date range" icon="calendar" titleType="p">
    Choose the season at the top, then set the date range to control which payments are counted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

- **No active registrations were found**: There are no active registrations for the selected season. Confirm you picked the right season.
- **Paid looks lower than expected**: Only payments received inside the date range are counted. Widen the range to include earlier payments.
