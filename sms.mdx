## Overview

Use SMS in Campium to send short, one-way text updates to families when you need their attention quickly.

<Callout kind="info">

Campium sends SMS messages from `(551) 209-2312`. This is a one-way number: recipients cannot reply to your texts.

</Callout>

## Text message opt-in on forms

When a portal form asks for a cell phone number, Campium shows **Can we send you text messages?** with **Yes, text me** and **No thanks**. Anyone who enters a cell number must choose one before they can submit. If they skip it, they see **Choose Yes or No for text messages.** Nothing is pre-selected unless that person has already answered.

Customize the wording under that question on **Portal → Portal Site Text** with **Text Message Opt-in** and optional **Text Message Opt-in for Staff**. See [Portal Settings](/portal-settings#text-message-opt-in-wording).

Answers update **SMS Subscribed** on the profile, and Campium logs each answer as a consent record. Staff cannot opt someone in by changing **SMS Subscribed** alone; the next form with a cell phone asks again. Setting someone to **Unsubscribed** is always fine. Replies of **STOP** still turn texts off automatically.

## Change text preferences on the portal profile

Families and staff can change the same Yes / No choice on their portal **Profile**, under **Text messages**, without opening a form again.

- Question: **Can we send you text messages?** When a cell phone is on file, the question includes the number (for example **at (555) 123-4567**).
- Choices: **Yes, text me** and **No thanks**. **Yes** is pre-selected only when they have already opted in. A default subscribed flag alone does not pre-select **Yes**.
- No cell phone yet: **We don't have a cell phone number for you yet. Texts go to the cell phone you enter on a form.**
- After a carrier **STOP**: **Texts are off because your number replied STOP to one of our texts. To turn them back on, reply START to that text.**

## Where to find SMS

Open SMS from the main navigation:

1. Go to **Communications**.
2. Click **SMS**.

The SMS list opens. Each message you open has three tabs: **Settings**, **Recipients**, and **Logs**. You need the **Manage SMS** permission to see **SMS** in the menu.

## Create and send an SMS

Use this workflow to write a new text and send it now or schedule it.

<Steps>

<Step title="Open a new SMS" icon="play-circle">

On the **Communications → SMS** page, click **New SMS**, or open an existing draft to edit and send it. The tabs across the top are **Settings**, **Recipients**, and **Logs**.

</Step>

<Step title="Fill in Settings" icon="settings">

On the **Settings** tab, fill in the message, timing, and any extra phone numbers. Keep the text short so families understand the update at a glance. Use **Insert link** or **Insert account setup link** when you need a URL in the message (see [Insert link and account setup link](#insert-link-and-account-setup-link)).

</Step>

<Step title="Choose your recipients" icon="users">

Switch to the **Recipients** tab to choose who receives the SMS. Use the filters to narrow your audience, then confirm the list matches who you intend to contact.

</Step>

<Step title="Schedule or send now" icon="zap">

Return to the **Settings** tab and set **When to send**: **Send immediately**, or **Schedule for a date and time** with a **Send at** time. Your draft saves automatically. Click **Send**, then confirm with **Send now**.

</Step>

<Step title="Review delivery in Logs" icon="bar-chart">

After sending, open the **Logs** tab to see when each message was sent and to whom.

</Step>

</Steps>

### What success looks like

- The SMS appears in **Logs** with a sent time.
- The recipient list in **Recipients** matches your intended audience.
- Families report receiving the text on their phones from `(551) 209-2312`.

## Settings tab

The **Settings** tab controls the content of your SMS and when it goes out.

### SMS message

Type the text you want families to receive in the **SMS message** box.

<Callout kind="tip">

The helper text under the box reads: up to 160 characters sends as one message; longer text sends as multiple messages, each billed at $0.01. Emoji and special characters are removed, and curly quotes and long dashes become plain ones, because any of them would split the text into more messages. Include opt-out language such as "Reply STOP to opt out" so carriers do not filter your messages. A live character counter shows when the text will send as more than one message.

</Callout>

Keep your message focused on one topic, such as a schedule change, arrival reminder, or weather update.

### Insert link and account setup link

Above the **SMS message** box you can:

- **Insert link** - Opens **Insert a link**. Enter a full web address that starts with `https://`, then click **Insert**. Campium shortens long links to your parent portal address when the message sends, and the character counter already counts those shorter links.
- **Insert account setup link** - Inserts a personal setup placeholder. Each recipient gets their own short link that signs them in to set up their account. It works once and expires after 14 days.

Helper text under the message box always includes **Long links are shortened to your portal address when the message sends.** When the message includes an account setup link placeholder, an extra note explains that it becomes a personal short link for each recipient, works once, and expires after 14 days.

### Start with your camp name

On **Settings**, leave **Start with your camp name** checked (it is on by default) so each text begins with your camp name. The hint explains that families know who the text is from and that the character count includes the prefix. Uncheck it when the message already names your camp. The same checkbox appears when you text a group from a report. Campium adds the prefix when the message sends; it is not stored in the draft.

### Text families from a report

On **Reports → System Reports → No Primary Payment Method** and **Wait List Management**, use **Send Text** (or **Send Text To All Wait Listers**) to open a text modal for that report's list. Write the message, use **Show names** to review who has a cell phone, then click **Send Text** and confirm with **Yes, send it**. **Open in full editor** continues in **Communications → SMS**. You need the **Manage Emails** permission (the same permission used for **Send Email** on those reports). See [Families Without Primary Payment Method](/system-report-families-without-primary-payment-method) and [Waitlist Report](/waitlist#email-everyone-on-the-list).

When you schedule a text that started from a report, Campium rechecks who still appears on that report at send time. People who left the report are dropped. Nobody new is added. People you unchecked in **View** / **Send** stay skipped when the scheduled send runs.

### Rewrite with AI

Above the message box, use **Rewrite with AI** to open **AI Text Rewriter**. The modal opens already running **Shorten**, since length is what an SMS is billed on. Other actions include **Improve**, **Simplify**, **Fix Grammar**, **Friendly**, **Professional**, **Formal**, and **Expand**. Compare **Original** and **Rewritten** (with character and message counts), then click **Use This Text** to replace the draft, or close without applying.

### When to send

Use **When to send** to choose whether the SMS goes out now or later.

- Choose **Schedule for a date and time**, then set **Send at**. Times use your configured send timezone.
- Choose **Send immediately** and click **Send** to send as soon as you confirm.

Scheduling helps with early-morning reminders or evening updates you prepare ahead of time.

### Also send to

Use **Also send to** to add phone numbers that are not in your recipient list.

- Enter phone numbers separated with commas.
- Use this for staff or other contacts who should receive the same message.

Check extra numbers carefully so you do not text the wrong person.

### Also send to campers

Select **Also send to campers** when campers should receive the SMS as well as their guardians. The hint reads **Sends a copy to campers who have a phone number, not only their guardians.**

If you leave this unchecked, Campium sends the SMS only to guardians (Guardian 1 and Guardian 2 phone numbers for camper recipients) and any numbers you added under **Also send to**. After send, the **Delivery** panel on **Settings** shows **Campers** as **Included** or **Guardians only**.

### Save

There is no **Save** button. A draft saves automatically as you type; the action bar shows **Changes save automatically.**, then **Saving...** and **All changes saved.**

- Leave a draft and come back later to review, update, or send it.
- Click **Send me a preview** to text the current message to your own cell phone before you send.

### Send

Click **Send** when you are ready for the SMS to go out. Campium asks you to confirm with the recipient count (for example **Send this SMS to 120 recipients now?**) and a note when the text sends as more than one message. Click **Send now** to confirm.

- If you chose **Send immediately**, Campium begins sending right away.
- If you set **Send at**, Campium queues the SMS to send at that date and time.

<Callout kind="alert">

Before you click **Send**, confirm both the **SMS message** and the **Recipients** list. SMS cannot be unsent once it is delivered.

</Callout>

### Sending progress

After you send (or while a scheduled send is delivering), the **Settings** tab shows **Sending this SMS** with how many messages are delivered so far, a progress bar, and an estimated time left. Text messages go out in batches and keep going after you leave the page. When delivery finishes, that panel is replaced by **How this SMS performed** (how many were sent, plus a note when some were not delivered). Use the **Logs** tab for per-recipient detail.

## Recipients tab

Use the **Recipients** tab to control who receives your message. To text every parent in one session, filter the recipients by that session. Campium sends to each camper's guardians unless you also check **Also send to campers**.

On this tab, you:

- Use filters to narrow the audience to the right families or sessions.
- Check the **Matches** count and click **View** to review the list.

Target the smallest group you need, such as a single session, age group, or bus route, instead of the entire camp.

### Skip people without changing filters

When you click **View** to open the recipient list, a **Send** column lets you uncheck anyone who should not get this SMS. Helper text: **Uncheck anyone who should not get this text. Unchecking a camper skips both guardians. Use the Send box in the header to check or uncheck everyone, then pick who should get it. Changes save right away.** The summary shows how many people are unchecked and will be skipped. Skipped people are left out of the send and out of the sent count.

<Callout kind="info">

SMS messages from Campium are one-way. Families see them as coming from `(551) 209-2312` and cannot reply, so they contact you through your usual channels.

</Callout>

## Logs tab

The **Logs** tab lists each recipient with **Name** and **Sent**, plus **Status** when delivery status is available.

On this tab, you can:

- See when the SMS went to each recipient.
- See which recipients were included.
- Confirm that a message you expected to go out was sent.

When people were unchecked before send, **Logs** also shows **Skipped (N)** with columns **Name**, **Unchecked by**, and **When**. Helper text: **Unchecked in the recipient list, so they did not get this text and are not counted as sent.**

Use Logs when:

- A family asks whether a message was sent about a specific event.
- You want to check the timing of past alerts.
- You are reviewing communications for a particular day or session.

## Best practices for SMS

- **Be clear and concise.** Lead with the main point: what is happening, when, and what action (if any) families should take.
- **Use SMS for time-sensitive updates.** Save texts for information families need quickly, such as last-minute changes or urgent reminders.
- **Respect timing.** Avoid late-night or early-morning messages unless they are urgent.
- **Avoid over-sending.** If an update is not urgent, use email, or combine several minor updates into one SMS.
- **Respect opt-outs and preferences.** If a family asks not to receive texts, keep a note in your records and choose other communication channels for them. When someone replies **STOP** to the carrier, Campium turns off **SMS Subscribed** on matching profiles so they are not texted again. Turning SMS off yourself in Campium is separate from a carrier opt-out. A later **START** reply can resubscribe profiles that were turned off by a carrier STOP.
- **Match tone to the situation.** Use a calm, straightforward tone, especially for weather or safety-related updates.
