description: >-
  See combined revenue, paying campers, average, and retention across every camp
  in your organization on the dashboard and Organization Summary report.
---

When your camp is linked with other camps in the same organization, **Organization Snapshot** adds their money figures together for one season year so you can see the whole organization at once.

You need finance access at each camp that appears. The card and report show only when you can see at least two camps in the organization, including the camp you are viewing.

## Dashboard card

On the home dashboard, the **Organization Snapshot** card shows:

- The season year (for example **2026 seasons**) and the camp names included
- Tiles for **Total revenue**, **Paying** campers (your camper wording), **Avg per** camper, and **Retention**
- One row per camp with revenue, paying count, average, and retention
- The camp you are signed into marked **(this camp)**

Use **Full report** to open **Organization Summary**. Use the gear (**Change year** / **Season year**) to pick a year, then **Apply** or **Cancel**. Your year choice is saved for you. The card can show **Updated N min ago** with a refresh control.

## Organization Summary report

1. Go to **Reports → System Reports**.
2. Under **Money**, open **Organization Summary**.

The report has one row per camp and a combined **All camps** total. Columns include revenue figures, paying counts, average per camper, and retention for the selected season year. Use the year picker in the header to change years.

## How the figures work

- **Total revenue** matches each camp's Revenue Summary average-sale numerator (net of discounts and non-revenue sessions), added across camps.
- **Paying campers** counts people whose own lines net more than $0, once per camp.
- **Avg per camper** divides combined revenue by combined paying campers (not the average of camp averages).
- **Retention** follows each camp's Retention card rules for seasons in that year, then adds retained and lost across camps.

Seasons are matched by the four-digit year in the season name when present, otherwise by the season end or start date.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Single-camp organizations do not see this card or report. Campium links camps into an organization for you when you run more than one camp under the same parent.
</Callout>
