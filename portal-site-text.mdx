Open **Portal → Portal Site Text** in the left menu.

## What Portal Site Text controls

Portal Site Text is where you change the built-in wording on your parent portal. Each box on the page is one piece of text, with a short note under its label that says where it appears. Some boxes also show a screenshot of that spot on the portal, and a few show a **Live preview** that updates as you type.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Portal Site Text appears in the menu for users with the site text permission. The home page heading and the instructions under it are no longer on this page. They live on **Portal → Settings**, next to the home page colour and its preview. Use **Open Home page settings** in the blue note at the top of the page to jump there.
</Callout>

## Change portal wording

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Portal Site Text" icon="type" title-type="p">
    Go to **Portal → Portal Site Text**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Edit the boxes you want to change" icon="pencil" title-type="p">
    Type your wording in any box. Read the note under each label to confirm where the text shows up on the portal.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save" icon="save" title-type="p">
    Click **Save Text** at the top right. The button stays at the top of the screen as you scroll, so you can save from anywhere on the page. All boxes save together.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Text message opt-in wording

Both opt-in boxes start filled with the wording currently in use, so you can adjust it rather than start from a blank box. **Text Message Opt-in** starts with Campium's wording. **Text Message Opt-in for Staff** starts with whatever **Text Message Opt-in** says. A **Live preview** under each box shows the question, your wording, and the **Yes, text me** and **No thanks** choices as families see them.

Carrier rules for business texting require certain pieces in every opt-in. Your wording must include:

- Your camp name, or `{name}`, which is replaced with your camp name on the form
- "Message frequency varies" (or how often you text)
- "Message and data rates may apply"
- "Reply STOP to opt out"
- "Reply HELP for help"

Use `{terms}` where you want the **Terms and Privacy Policy** link to appear. If you leave it out, the link is added at the end.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  If either opt-in box is missing a required piece, **Save Text** shows a message listing what is missing and nothing on the page is saved. Add the missing wording and save again.
</Callout>

If you save an opt-in box without changing it from the wording it started with, Campium keeps following that default instead of storing a copy. That way, if Campium's default wording is updated later, your forms pick up the change. The staff box works the same way: left matching the general box, it keeps following the general box.
