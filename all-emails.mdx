## Overview

Use the Email Log to see the emails that have gone out from your account and check whether they were opened. It is the quickest way to confirm a message was sent, look up what a family received, and troubleshoot a "did you get my email?" question.

## What it shows

Each row is a single sent email. The table loads and filters as you type, so you can search a large history without waiting for the whole list.

<ParamField body="From" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The sender address the email went out from.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="To" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The recipient the email was sent to.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Subject" param-type="text" show-location="false">
The subject line of the email.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Sent" param-type="date/time" show-location="false">
When the email was sent. The list is sorted with the most recent first.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Opened" param-type="date/time" show-location="false">
When the recipient opened the email, when that information is available.
</ParamField>

## Search

Use the **Filter** box above the table to search across senders, recipients, and subjects. Results update automatically as you type.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Email Log from Reports" icon="folder" titleType="p">
    Go to **Reports** &gt; **System** and select **Email Log**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search for a message" icon="search" titleType="p">
    Type a name, address, or subject in the Filter box to narrow the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

- **No emails match the criteria selected**: Clear the Filter box or check your spelling. The log only includes emails sent from your account.
- **The Opened column is blank**: An open is only recorded when the recipient's email client loads the message; a blank does not always mean it was never read.
