Use the **Email Log** to confirm a message was sent, look up what a family received, and check whether it was opened.

You need permission for the **Email Log** report.

## Open the report

1. Go to **Reports → System Reports**.
2. Under **Misc**, open **Email Log**.

Dates come from the shared date picker in the page header (default last 30 days). The page title includes that range. The results card is titled **Email History**.

## Filters

Use the **Filters** card, then **Apply Filters**. **Reset** clears filters back to defaults.

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Sent By** | **Everyone**, individual staff, or **Automated** |
| **Opened** | **Opened or not**, **Opened**, **Not opened** |

Search placeholder: **Name, email or subject**. Empty state: **No emails match these filters and dates.**

## Email History columns

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Date/Time** | When the email was sent |
| **Recipient** | Who received it |
| **Subject** | Subject line |
| **Sent By** | Staff name, or **Automated** |
| **Opened** | Whether an open was recorded |
| **Email** | Opens the full message |

An open is only recorded when the recipient's email client loads the message. A blank **Opened** value does not always mean the email was never read.
