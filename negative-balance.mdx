## Overview

Use the **Credits** report to find families whose balance is **below zero** for a season, meaning they have a credit or overpayment on file. It shares its underlying data with the **Account Balance** (Accounts Receivable) report but keeps only the families in credit, so you can review and clean up overpayments.

Open it from **Financials → Credits**. On the dashboard **Financial Snapshot** card, switch to the **Credits** view, then select a season to open the same report for that season.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  A credit means the family has paid more than they owe, or a credit was applied. Use this report to spot credits that should be refunded or transferred to a future season.
</Callout>

## Filters

<ParamField body="Season / program group" param-type="selector" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Select the season or program group to review. When you select a **single** season, a per-row **Transfer** action becomes available so you can transfer a credit or balance to another season.
</ParamField>

### Filter by credit source

Above the table, use the source buttons to narrow the list. Each button shows a count:

- **All**
- **Balance transfer**
- **Adjustment**
- **Overpayment**

A family can match more than one source. Selecting a source keeps every row that includes that badge. The footer **Balance** total updates to match the filtered rows.

## Columns

Each row is a **family** with a credit balance.

<ParamField body="Primary Contact" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The family's primary contact. Select it to open the profile.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Users" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The campers on the family account for the selected season.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Products" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The sessions, add-ons, or other products tied to the family's registration.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Email" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The primary contact's email, with their phone number shown below it when available.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Reg. Date" param-type="date" required="true" deprecated="false">
  When the family registered for the season.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Revenue" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The family's total charges for the season.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Balance" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The outstanding balance. In this report the value is negative, representing the credit on file.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Source" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Where the credit came from. Badges can include:

  - **Balance transfer**: credit moved in from another season
  - **Adjustment**: a staff adjustment that created credit
  - **Overpayment**: payment exceeded charges (default when no other source applies)

  A row can show more than one badge when more than one source contributed.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Note" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  A free-text note you can edit inline on the row to track follow-up, such as a planned refund or transfer.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Actions" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Per-row shortcuts:

  - **Email this family** (mail icon) opens the family profile and starts the email composer.
  - **Transfer** appears when a single season is selected (see below).
</ParamField>

The report can also show optional **School** and **profile-field** columns, and the footer totals the sum of credit balances for the rows you are viewing.

## Transfer a credit to another season

When you have a **single** season selected, each row shows a **Transfer** button in **Actions**. Use it to open **Balance Transfer To Another Season** and transfer that family's credit or balance to another season, for example rolling a leftover credit forward to next summer.

1. Select **Transfer** on the family row.
2. In **Balance Transfer To Another Season**, confirm **From** the source season (the current balance shows under that label).
3. Choose **To This Season**.
4. Enter **Amount**. A positive amount moves a credit out of the source season. A negative amount carries a balance owed forward.
5. Review the **Preview**: ledger entries for each season, and **Season balance** before and after. Fix the amount or destination if a warning appears.
6. Select **Confirm Transfer of** the previewed amount (the button stays disabled until the preview succeeds).

You can also start the same modal from a family's **Sales** tab (tooltip **Balance transfer to another season**).

After you transfer a balance, the ledger records show payment type **Balance Transfer**, with reasons like **Balance Transfer to** and **Balance Transfer from** the other season. Filter to them in **Financials → Payments**, or open the linked **Balance Transfers** row from [Revenue Summary](/revenue-summary).

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Transferring a credit changes real financial records for a live family. Use the preview to confirm direction and balances before you confirm.
</Callout>

## Run the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Credits report" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    Open the **Financials** area and choose the **Credits** report.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a season" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    Select the season or program group. Choose a single season if you plan to transfer credits between seasons.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review credits and take action" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">
    Optionally filter by **Balance transfer**, **Adjustment**, or **Overpayment**. Review each family's credit and **Source** badges, email the family from **Actions**, add a note where useful, and transfer or refund credits as needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
