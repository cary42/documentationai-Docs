## Overview

Use the **Negative Balance** report to find families whose balance is **below zero** for a season, meaning they have a credit or overpayment on file. It shares its underlying data with the **Account Balance** (Accounts Receivable) report but keeps only the families in credit, so you can review and clean up overpayments.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  A negative balance means the family has paid more than they owe, or a credit was applied. Use this report to spot credits that should be refunded or moved to a future season.
</Callout>

## Filters

<ParamField body="Season / program group" param-type="selector" required="false" deprecated="false">
  Select the season or program group to review. When you select a **single** season, a per-row **Move** action becomes available so you can move a credit or balance to another season.
</ParamField>

## Columns

Each row is a **family** with a negative balance.

<ParamField body="Primary Contact" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The family's primary contact. Select it to open the profile.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Users" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The campers on the family account for the selected season.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Products" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The sessions, add-ons, or other products tied to the family's registration.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Email" param-type="text" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The primary contact's email, with their phone number shown below it when available.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Reg. Date" param-type="date" required="true" deprecated="false">
  When the family registered for the season.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Revenue" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The family's total charges for the season.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Balance" param-type="currency" required="true" deprecated="false">
  The outstanding balance. In this report the value is negative, representing the credit on file.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="Note" param-type="text" required="false" deprecated="false">
  A free-text note you can edit inline on the row to track follow-up, such as a planned refund or transfer.
</ParamField>

The report can also show optional **School** and **profile-field** columns, and the footer totals the sum of all negative balances.

## Move a credit to another season

When you have a **single** season selected, each row shows a **Move** button. Use it to open **Move Credit / Balance To Another Season** and move that family's credit or balance to another season, for example rolling a leftover credit forward to next summer.

1. Select **Move** on the family row.
2. In the modal, confirm **Move Credit / Balance From** the source season.
3. Choose **To This Season**.
4. Enter **Amount**. A positive amount transfers a credit. A negative amount carries an owed balance forward.
5. Select **Move Balance**.

You can also start the same modal from a family's **Sales** tab with **Move credit / balance to another season**.

After you move a balance, the ledger records show payment type **Balance Transfer**. Filter to them in **Financials → Payments**, or open the linked **Balance Transfers** row from [Revenue Summary](/revenue-summary).

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Moving a credit changes real financial records for a live family. Review the family, the amount, and the destination season carefully before confirming.
</Callout>

## Run the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Negative Balance report" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    Open the **Financials** area and choose the **Negative Balance** report.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a season" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    Select the season or program group. Choose a single season if you plan to move credits between seasons.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review credits and take action" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">
    Review each family's credit, add a note where useful, and move or refund credits as needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
