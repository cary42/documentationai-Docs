## What Camper Hub is for

Camper Hub is a dashboard for camper enrollment forms. It lists every camper with an active enrollment in the seasons you pick, with their account status, the profile fields you choose, and form progress.

Use Camper Hub to see who still needs forms, email guardians a reminder, open one camper's form detail, and approve, reject, or reset a form without opening each profile. For a spreadsheet-style report across many forms, use the [Form Status Summary](/form-status-summary) report instead. To email from one form's settings by completion status, see [Email users that have not completed a form](/email-users-who-have-not-completed-a-form).

Camper Hub is the camper version of [Staff Hub](/staff-hub). Staff Hub tracks people who started a staff application. Camper Hub tracks campers who already have an active enrollment for a season.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  You need the **Camper Hub** permission (under the **Admin** category) to open this page. Administrators get it by default. It appears under **Admin → Camper Hub** (the menu item may show a **New** badge).
</Callout>

## Open Camper Hub

1. Open **Admin** in the left navigation.
2. Click **Camper Hub**.

When it loads, you see a **Season** filter, **Camper Hub Settings**, summary tiles, and a roster table.

## Filter by season

Use the **Season** filter at the top to choose which seasons you want to work with, then click **Filter**. **Clear** returns you to the default view. The season list includes past and inactive seasons, not only active ones.

Campers with an active enrollment in those seasons appear below. When more than one season is selected, a camper appears once per enrolled season, and a **Season** column shows which season each row belongs to.

If you do not choose a season, Camper Hub uses your default season when one is set, or all available seasons.

## Read and filter with the summary tiles

Above the roster, four tiles summarize the current season filter (five when the Pending Registrations module is on):

<Card title="Summary tiles" icon="chart-bar" horizontal="false">
  - **Campers** or **Camper Seasons**: how many roster rows match the season filter. The label is **Camper Seasons** when more than one season is selected (a camper enrolled in two seasons counts twice). Click this tile to clear a completion filter and show everyone again.

  - **Required Forms Complete** or **All Forms Complete**: enrollment rows where every counted form is finished. The **Required** wording appears only when every selected season has required forms configured in settings. Click the tile to show only these rows; click it again to clear the filter.

  - **Missing Required Forms** or **Missing Forms**: enrollment rows where at least one counted form is still unfinished. Click to show only these rows; click again to clear.

  - **Pending Approval** (only when the Pending Registrations module is on): campers with a registration saved for later and waiting for your approval. Click to show only these rows. When the count is above zero, a **Review pending approvals** button above the roster opens the pending registrations queue.

  - **No Forms Started**: enrollment rows where the camper has not started any of the counted forms. This tile is a count only. Clicking it does not filter.
</Card>

The tile totals always reflect the full season filter. Searching by name narrows the table, but the tile numbers do not change. Profile-field filters (below) update the roster and the tile totals together.

When a completion filter is active, a short note under the tiles explains what the roster is showing (for example **Showing only those missing at least one required form. Click the tile again to show everyone.**). When nothing is filtered, the note says **Click a tile above to filter this list.**

## Filter by profile fields

Use **Filters** (next to **Search campers**) to narrow the roster by profile answers. The panel title is **Filter campers by profile fields**. Helper text: **Changes apply as you make them. Every filter must match. A Guardian 1 or Guardian 2 field matches campers whose guardian has that answer.**

1. Click **Filters**.
2. In a row, **Pick a field**, choose an operator, then enter or pick a value (**Pick one or more**, **Type to filter**, or a date).
3. The roster updates as you go (status shows **Updating...**). Add more rows with **Add filter**. Every complete row must match. Incomplete rows are ignored until you finish them.
4. Remove one row with the trash control, remove one chip with **×** when the panel is closed, or click **Clear all**.

Supported field types: Choose One, Choose Many, Ranked Choice, Text, and Date. Operators depend on the type (for example **is**, **contains any**, **contains all**, **contains**, **is on or after**, **is empty**). You can add up to 20 filter rows. Role-hidden fields do not appear in the picker.

**Save as...** stores a named filter that everyone at your camp who uses Camper Hub can load from **Saved filters**. Re-using an existing name replaces that filter. **Delete** removes a named one. Your last-used filter is restored the next time you open the page.

## Export the roster

Above the table, the export buttons (`Copy`, `Excel`, `CSV`, `PDF`, `Print`, `Email`) export every camper in the current filter (season, tiles, and profile-field filters), not just the page you are viewing. Tooltip: **Exports every camper in the current filter, not just this page**.

## Work the roster table

Each row is one camper for one season (or one camper when a single season is selected). From left to right, the roster shows:

- **Camper**: the person's name (a link to their profile) and email.

- **Status**: the camper's account status (Active, Pending, and so on). Change it in the table and it saves right away. When several seasons are shown, the change updates every row for that camper.

- **Season**: only when more than one season is selected.

- **Profile field columns**: any camper profile fields you chose to display. Text fields and dropdowns can be edited in the table; click **Save** when the value changes.

- **Forms**: **Started** and **Completed** counts for the forms Camper Hub is tracking. When every counted form is finished, a **Complete** pill appears. Click the counts to expand form details.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Use **Search campers** above the table to find people by name across the whole season filter. Choose **Rows per page** (50, 100, 200, 500, or **All**) and use **Previous** / **Next** to page through large rosters.
</Callout>

## Choose which fields and forms count

Open **Camper Hub Settings** on the page to control the dashboard:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick fields to display and edit" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Under **Fields to display and edit**, search and pick which camper profile fields appear as columns. You can edit these columns in the table for every camper on the roster.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set required forms by season" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    Under **Required forms by season**, choose the forms campers must complete for each season. The **Forms** column counts only the forms you pick here, and campers who finish all of them are marked **Complete**. Inactive forms are listed too, so you can set up a season before its forms open.

    You can also mark a form as required from that form's **Settings** tab (**Required** / **Every camper in this season must finish this form**). Both places edit the same list. See [Forms](/forms#required).

    Leave a season empty to count all of its active forms. Until you pick required forms for a season, a callout appears above the tiles titled **Choose which forms are required for** that season (or seasons). It explains that every form counts toward the totals, the Missing filter can include people who only have optional forms left, and reminder emails list those optional forms too. Use **Choose required forms** to jump into settings, or **Copy from another season** (when another season or a saved template has choices) to reuse a set you already made.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Show or hide other seasons" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    When your season filter covers only some seasons, click **Show other seasons (N)** to list the rest so you can set required forms for them too. Otherwise the control reads **Show inactive seasons (N)** and lists seasons whose end date has passed. Click **Hide other seasons** or **Hide inactive seasons** to collapse the list. Choices you make for those seasons are kept when you save.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Click **Save Settings**. Your choices apply the next time the roster loads.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## See form progress for one camper

Click the **Forms** cell in any row to expand a panel titled **Forms for** that camper (with the season name when more than one season is in the filter).

Each form shows its status (for example **Not started**, **In progress**, **Pending approval**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**). Forms you marked as required in settings show a **Required** badge. Finished forms also show a **Completed** date.

A form counts as completed when its status is **Pending approval** or **Approved**.

When the camper still has counted forms unfinished, **Send reminder** appears on the right side of that panel heading. Use it to email guardians for this camper only (see [Send a reminder for one camper](#send-a-reminder-for-one-camper)).

From the gear menu on a form you can:

- **Reset to In Progress**
- **Pending Approval**
- **Approve** (approving also sets the camper's account to Active, and may ask about a deposit if the form has **Charge Deposit On Approval** on)
- **Reject** (may offer to remove purchased sessions when the camper has them)
- **Delete** (the camper would need to start the form again)

These actions use the same confirmation prompts as the form tools on a camper's profile.

## See when a family was last reminded

After you send a reminder from Camper Hub, the roster remembers it for that camper and season.

- Under the **Forms** counts, a line such as **Reminded just now**, **Reminded 2 hours ago**, or **Reminded 3 days ago** appears once a reminder has gone out.
- The same wording appears next to **Send reminder** in the expanded **Forms for** panel.
- For the first **24 hours**, that line is highlighted and the **Send reminder** button is dimmed, so you can see the family was reminded recently without opening the dialog.

## Send reminders for missing forms

When at least one enrollment row is missing counted forms, **Send reminders** appears above the roster (to the right of the filter note).

1. Click **Send reminders**. Recipients are everyone in the current season filter who still has forms outstanding, even if you filtered the roster with a tile.
2. Review the **Send reminders** dialog: how many people still have forms outstanding, how many emails will go out, and a short sample of names and addresses.
3. Under **Send to**, choose **Primary guardian only** or **Primary and secondary guardian**. Changing the choice recounts recipients before you send.
4. When some of those campers were already reminded in the last **24 hours**, a checkbox such as **Skip the 2 who were already reminded in the last 24 hours** appears (checked by default). Leave it on to avoid a second email the same day, or clear it if you want those families included again. The summary notes how many were left out, or that they will be emailed again.
5. Confirm with **Send reminders**, or cancel.

Each recipient gets one email that lists the unfinished forms for that camper. Guardians without an email on file, and guardians who unsubscribed from form notifications, are skipped. The dialog shows those counts when they apply. If a season still has no required forms configured, the dialog notes that those reminders list every form for the season.

## Send a reminder for one camper

To remind one family (for example after a phone call), expand that camper's **Forms** panel and use **Send reminder** instead of the bulk button.

1. Expand the **Forms** cell for the camper. **Send reminder** appears only when counted forms are still unfinished.
2. Click **Send reminder**. The **Send reminder** dialog shows the camper name, how many forms are outstanding (and their names), and the email addresses that will receive the message.
3. Under **Send to**, choose **Primary guardian only** or **Primary and secondary guardian**. Changing the choice updates the **To** line before you send.
4. If the family was reminded in the last **24 hours**, the dialog warns **Already reminded** with how long ago, and the confirm button becomes **Send again**. Older reminders show a **Last reminded** note instead. Sending for one camper never skips a recent reminder; you decide whether to send again.
5. Confirm with **Send** (or **Send again**), or cancel.

If the family has no email on file, or nothing is outstanding anymore, Campium tells you instead of opening the send step.
