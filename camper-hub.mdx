## What Camper Hub is for

Camper Hub is the operations dashboard for camper enrollment forms. It lists every camper with an active enrollment in the seasons you pick, along with their account status, key profile fields, and form progress.

Use Camper Hub when you want to see who still needs forms, email guardians a reminder, open the form detail for one camper, and approve, reject, or reset a form without hunting through each profile. For a spreadsheet-style report across many forms, use the [Form Status Summary](/form-status-summary) report instead. To email from one form's settings by completion status, see [Email users that have not completed a form](/email-users-who-have-not-completed-a-form).

Camper Hub is the camper-side counterpart to [Staff Hub](/staff-hub). Staff Hub tracks people who started a staff application. Camper Hub tracks campers who already have an active enrollment for a season.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Only users with the **Administrator** role can open Camper Hub. It appears under **Admin → Camper Hub** (the menu item may show a **New** badge).
</Callout>

## Open Camper Hub

1. Open **Admin** in the left navigation.
2. Click **Camper Hub**.

When it loads, you see a **Season** filter, **Camper Hub Settings**, summary metrics, and a roster table.

## Filter by season

Use the **Season** filter at the top to choose which seasons you want to work with, then click **Filter**. **Clear** returns you to the default view.

Campers with an active enrollment in those seasons appear below. When more than one season is selected, a camper appears once per enrolled season, and a **Season** column shows which season each row belongs to.

If you do not choose a season, Camper Hub uses your default season when one is set, or all available seasons.

## Read and filter with the summary tiles

Above the roster, four tiles summarize the current season filter:

<Card title="Summary tiles" icon="chart-bar" horizontal="false">
  - **Campers** or **Camper Seasons**: how many roster rows match the season filter. The label is **Camper Seasons** when more than one season is selected (a camper enrolled in two seasons counts twice). Click this tile to clear a completion filter and show everyone again.

  - **Required Forms Complete** or **All Forms Complete**: enrollment rows where every counted form is finished. The **Required** wording appears only when every selected season has required forms configured in settings. Click the tile to show only these rows; click it again to clear the filter.

  - **Missing Required Forms** or **Missing Forms**: enrollment rows where at least one counted form is still unfinished. Click to show only these rows; click again to clear.

  - **No Forms Started**: enrollment rows where the camper has not started any of the counted forms. This tile is informational only (not a filter control).
</Card>

The tile totals always reflect the full season filter. Searching the roster by name narrows the table below, but the tile numbers stay put.

When a completion filter is active, a short note under the tiles explains what the roster is showing (for example **Showing only those missing at least one required form. Click the tile again to show everyone.**). When nothing is filtered, the note says **Click a tile above to filter this list.**

## Work the roster table

Each row is one camper for one season (or one camper when a single season is selected). From left to right, the roster shows:

- **Camper**: the person's name (a link to their profile) and email.

- **Status**: the camper's account status (Active, Pending, and so on). You can change it in the table and it saves immediately. Changing status for a camper updates every row for that person when multiple seasons are shown.

- **Season**: only when more than one season is selected.

- **Profile field columns**: any camper profile fields you chose to display. Text fields and dropdowns can be edited in the table; click **Save** when the value changes.

- **Forms**: **Started** and **Completed** counts for the forms Camper Hub is tracking. When every counted form is finished, a **Complete** pill appears. Click the counts to expand form details.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Use **Search campers** above the table to find people by name across the whole season filter. Choose **Rows per page** (50, 100, 200, 500, or **All**) and use **Previous** / **Next** to page through large rosters.
</Callout>

## Choose which fields and forms count

Open **Camper Hub Settings** on the page to control the dashboard:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick fields to display and edit" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Under **Fields to display and edit**, search and select which camper profile fields appear as columns. These columns are editable inline for everyone in the roster.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set required forms by season" icon="folder" title-type="p">
    Under **Required forms by season**, choose the forms campers must complete for each season. The **Forms** column counts only the forms you pick here, and campers who finish all of them are marked **Complete**.

    Leave a season empty to count all of its forms. Until you pick required forms for a season, a callout appears above the tiles titled **Choose which forms are required for** that season (or seasons). It explains that every form counts toward the totals, the Missing filter can include people who only have optional forms left, and reminder emails list those optional forms too. Use **Choose required forms** to jump into settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Show or hide past seasons" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    Click **Show inactive seasons** to reveal seasons whose end date has passed so you can still set required forms for them. Their choices are kept when you save. Click **Hide inactive seasons** to collapse that list again.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save" icon="check-circle" title-type="p">
    Click **Save Settings**. Your choices apply the next time the roster loads.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## See form progress for one camper

Click the **Forms** cell in any row to expand a panel titled **Forms for** that camper (with the season name when more than one season is in the filter).

Each form shows its status (for example **Not started**, **In progress**, **Pending approval**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**). Forms you marked as required in settings show a **Required** badge. Finished forms also show a **Completed** date.

A form counts as completed when its status is **Pending approval** or **Approved**.

When the camper still has counted forms unfinished, **Send reminder** appears on the right side of that panel heading. Use it to email guardians for this camper only (see [Send a reminder for one camper](#send-a-reminder-for-one-camper)).

From the gear menu on a form you can:

- **Reset to In Progress**: **Pending Approval**: **Approve** (approving also sets the camper's account to Active, and may ask about a deposit if your form charges a deposit on approval)
- **Reject** (may offer to remove purchased sessions when the camper has them)
- **Delete** (the camper would need to start the form again)

These actions use the same confirmation prompts as the form tools on a camper's profile.

## Send reminders for missing forms

When at least one enrollment row is missing counted forms, **Send reminders** appears above the roster (to the right of the filter note).

1. Click **Send reminders**. Recipients are everyone in the current season filter who still has forms outstanding, even if you filtered the roster with a tile.
2. Review the **Send reminders** dialog: how many people still have forms outstanding, how many emails will go out, and a short sample of names and addresses.
3. Under **Send to**, choose **Primary guardian only** or **Primary and secondary guardian**. Changing the choice recounts recipients before you send.
4. Confirm with **Send reminders**, or cancel.

Each recipient gets one email that lists the unfinished forms for that camper. Guardians without an email on file, and guardians who unsubscribed from form notifications, are skipped; the dialog notes those counts when they apply. If a season still has no required forms configured, the dialog notes that those reminders list every form for the season.

## Send a reminder for one camper

When you need to chase one family (for example after a phone call), expand that camper's **Forms** panel and use **Send reminder** instead of the bulk button.

1. Expand the **Forms** cell for the camper. **Send reminder** appears only when counted forms are still unfinished.
2. Click **Send reminder**. The **Send reminder** dialog shows the camper name, how many forms are outstanding (and their names), and the email addresses that will receive the message.
3. Under **Send to**, choose **Primary guardian only** or **Primary and secondary guardian**. Changing the choice updates the **To** line before you send.
4. Confirm with **Send**, or cancel.

If the family has no email on file, or nothing is outstanding any more, Campium tells you instead of opening the send step.
